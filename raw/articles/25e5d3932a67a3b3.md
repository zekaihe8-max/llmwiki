Folks have asked me with the decline in UXR roles, where are UXR folks going?

Of 2,779 US user researchers who changed jobs in 2024-25:
• 49% took another user research job
• 17% moved into design
• 9% left the field
• ~2% became PMs

User research jobs are also moving outside tech. Almost half of researchers who took another research job joined a company outside tech. Banks, financial firms, and insurers hired about 1 in 7.
