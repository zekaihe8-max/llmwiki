I've been looking into public benchmarks and am astounded by the poor quality vs. how much we rely on them as an industry.

[@xeophon](/xeophon) pointed me to this excellent site [epoch.ai/benchmarks/](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/) which is an independent third party which scrutinizes benchmarks and "looks at the data" 💪

For example, here are some popular benchmarks we all rely on.   Before trusting a benchmark its worth running them and inspecting examples the grader failed carefully, to get a sense of the quality.

I'm continuing to look more into this, and will write something up eventually once I've collected more data, but I think this is an important subject.
