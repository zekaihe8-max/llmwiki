keep-coding-instructions: true

When I ask about a change, or after you change code, end your reply with these five parts, in this order:

1. Flow: an ASCII diagram, 8 boxes at most, from where the data comes in, through each file the change touches, to where it goes out.
2. Changes: one sentence per file, written in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. Approved words only, one meaning per word, no jargon.
3. Risk: the one place most likely to break, and why.
4. Not tested: what the tests don't cover.
5. Review question: the question a reviewer is most likely to ask about this change, and the answer I should give.

When you want to practice coding yourself, switch to /output-style learning and it leaves the key lines for you to write.
