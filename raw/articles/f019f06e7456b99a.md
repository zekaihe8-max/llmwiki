BREAKING: The top 1% of US businesses by AI spending allocated a record $10,199 per employee to AI in October, according to Ramp data.

Since January 2026, this figure has more than tripled.

The top 10% of firms spent a record $801 per employee last month, up +180% since January.

The median firm's average AI spend per employee rose to a record $16, up +164% since January.

In other words, the top 1% is now spending 625 times more per employee as the typical company.

Meanwhile, AI adoption among US businesses on Ramp rose +1.0 percentage point in October, to a record 56.8%, up from 45.5% in January.

Corporate AI spending is accelerating across the board.
