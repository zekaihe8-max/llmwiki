Personal Intelligence in Gemini securely connects information from apps you already use to make your experience more personalized and helpful. Today, we’re adding Google Wallet to the list of connected apps so you can get more tailored responses in the [@GeminiApp](/GeminiApp) around personal finance questions, based on your spending habits, including:

📈 Generate dynamic charts tracking cash flow, asset allocation, and budgets directly in your chat to help you visualize your spending.
📅 Find forgotten subscriptions and recurring charges across your linked accounts, and Gemini will also flag any price increases.
🎁 Set a budget for the holidays by asking “How much did I spend on gifts and travel last year? Use that to help me set a holiday budget” and Gemini will look at your transactions from the last year to suggest a budget tailored for you.

You’ll also have the option to securely link your financial accounts from your credit cards and banks to Wallet via [@Plaid](/Plaid) to unlock deeper insights.
