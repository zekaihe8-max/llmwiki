New Anthropic research: progress on provable inference for large language models.

Here, we introduce FlashProve. Building on established cryptographic techniques, FlashProve allows developers to verify whether a given response came from a given AI model. It’s substantially more efficient than prior open-source methods for doing so.

These proofs have a range of applications. They can, for instance, make it harder for attackers to hijack a chat, or enable safety evaluators to verify that the model they’re testing is the same one being served to customers.

For our full write-up, see here: [anthropic.com/research/prova…](https://t.co/OIZWPb6QZa)
