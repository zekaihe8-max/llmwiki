Huge opportunity right now to build new services firms from the ground up, or transform existing ones, with agents at the center.

The expertise that services firms in theory should have is as important as ever because you still need deep domain understanding to get the most out of agents. But now the ability to deliver on projects has a 10X multiplier. This means that you can make clients happier, compete to win more of them, go after smaller customers not in the TAM before, or deliver outcomes that weren’t really conceivable.

This is going to happen in advertising, IT transformation, management consulting, finance, and a number of other fields that historically are just fully constrained by the speed of their workforce for output.

This is a huge opportunity right now for those starting these firms. I wouldn’t count out existing players that commit to the transformation either as existing customer trust goes a long way. But they have to move quickly to make it work.
