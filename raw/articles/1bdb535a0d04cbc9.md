## Overview

Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR-Flash is a large-scale model supporting efficient short speech recognition, designed for high-quality, multilingual, and cultural content transcription scenarios. The model supports multilingual and multi-regional Chinese dialect recognition, and is optimized for the rhythm, cadence, and classical Chinese expression of ancient Chinese poetry. It features context enhancement, punctuation prediction, and text normalization capabilities. This version also supports controllable ASR/AST output for multiple dialects. It includes native transcription and polishing capabilities, as well as industrial-grade multi-person dialogue role-based transcription capabilities.
