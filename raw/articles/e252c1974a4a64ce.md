Today we’re launching the Gemini Decisions API, powered by our new Gemini Decision 1 model in [@GoogleAIStudio](/GoogleAIStudio).

- 3 decision types: yes/no, multiple choice (up to 255 options) + rubric scoring
- $0.10 / 1M input tokens with $0 billed for output tokens (~5x cheaper than Flash-Lite)
- leads on several benchmark evals with lowest calibration error
