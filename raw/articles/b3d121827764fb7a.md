Tech jobs are concentrating in the Bay Area

15% of all open tech roles are now in the Bay Area, about one in seven and a record high.

It’s even more concentrated for product roles. One in four open PM and design roles is in the Bay Area, and one in five engineering roles. All three are at or near record highs, and each has nearly doubled its share since 2022.

Meanwhile, remote roles keep shrinking. Just 14% of open tech roles offer a remote option, the lowest on record and down from 21% in late 2022.

Remote share by role:
• Engineering: 14%, down from 25%
• Design: 19%, down from 34%
• PM: steady at about 21% for two years, still near its low

Put it all together: sometime last year, open PM, eng, and design roles in the Bay Area overtook remote roles worldwide. Three years ago, remote led almost 2 to 1.
