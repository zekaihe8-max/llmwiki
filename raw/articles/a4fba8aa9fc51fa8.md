# A library of behaviors for agents to build and drive the next generation of user interfaces.

/wait-what

Say we give an agent a Select component. It can build a Select. Makes sense, right?

But what if, instead, we gave it the pieces that make up a Select? The popup (createPopup), choosing from the list (createSelection), and finding an option by typing (createTypeahead).

Now not only can it build a Select, but it can build an autocomplete, a combobox or even a new component we haven’t thought of yet.

That’s.

We abstracted the complex interactions of user interfaces into reusable behaviors. And we taught agents how to build and how to use them.

## Behaviors? What are those?

They are the pieces that make a component work: keyboard navigation, focus, selection, popups, drag and drop, and screen reader support.

- 01createRovingFocusArrow keys move through a list, with one Tab stop.
- 02createTypeaheadType a few letters to jump to an item.
- 03createPopupOpens next to its button and stays on screen.
- 04createSelectionPick one or many, with Shift and Ctrl like a file manager.
- 05createReorderSort a list with a pointer or the keyboard.
- 06createResizeResize boxes from their handles.
- 07createViewportPan and zoom a canvas.
- 08createVirtualizerRenders only the rows you can see.

## How do I use behaviors to build components?

You don’t. Ask your agent. We made them really good at it. The agent picks the behaviors it needs and puts them together in your framework.
