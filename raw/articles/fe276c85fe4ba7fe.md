##
          What is DreamCoder?

            Keeping an eye on one agent is easy. Fifteen or fifty is not. A terminal
            leaves you flipping between tabs with no real feedback &mdash; squinting at
            slivers of text, losing track of what each agent is actually doing.

            DreamCoder gives every agent, terminal and review its own pane in a tiling
            workspace that runs in your browser. Drag them around, hide or show them,
            fork a chat into its own window. Lost that conversation from an hour ago?
            Open the thread in a pane &mdash; done. Want to watch progress? Hit
            track objectives and a pane keeps score.

            Tired of watching important conversations scroll off-screen as the agent is
            working? Just open the answer in a new pane; keep reading, or click
            review to edit and reply directly to the agent.

            Prefer the keyboard? DreamCoder is keyboard and mouse driven. A
            dedicated keyboard mode moves, swaps and resizes panes and jumps between
            workspaces without leaving the home row &mdash; and the workspace minimap puts
            a traffic light on every pane: running, needs you, or done.

            It never forgets where you were. Open DreamCoder on your phone, tablet or
            laptop and resume the exact layout you left. And the polish isn&rsquo;t skin
            deep: underneath is a state-of-the-art agent engine, so you get a first-class
            interface and a top-flight coding harness.

            Why so many agents? Because one or two no longer cut it. Conversations are
            often long-running, even if the agents are idle. Think of all the conversations
            you parked hours, days or weeks after you last spoke with them. Revisit them
            quickly and efficiently whenever you need it, and colocate them with other
            related work to keep things organized.

Here&rsquo;s what that looks like.
