Welcome to AAIF, Agent Skills, Open Responses, OpenEnv, and OpenSandbox!

These four projects support how developers share agent expertise, work across model providers, train agents, and run their tasks in isolated environments.

These additions bring AAIF to 10 hosted projects in its first year.

Thank you to the contributing teams and maintainers. We’re glad to provide a neutral home where your communities can develop this work together.
