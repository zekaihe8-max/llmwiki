### Local models: A Claude-class brain on a 12 GB GPU

[#local-models-a-claude-class-brain-on-a-12-gb-gpu](#local-models-a-claude-class-brain-on-a-12-gb-gpu)

[/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/local-inference.md](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/local-inference.md)

Efficient AI model–harness co-design brings a full local agent to consumer Macs and PCs. Our MoE inference engine, PolyStrata, keeps a model's busiest experts on your GPU and the rest in RAM or on SSD, so larger models run with less GPU memory: the 125B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next runs on a 12 GB card with 64 GB of RAM. The model runs on your own machine instead of a remote server, so there is no cloud bill and no token fee.

### Model Autopilot: The smartest model your machine can run

[#model-autopilot-the-smartest-model-your-machine-can-run](#model-autopilot-the-smartest-model-your-machine-can-run)

[/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/local-inference.md#planning](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/local-inference.md#planning)

One command detects your hardware, recommends the best model, installs it and checks it. In our lab we run an architecture search: 49 language models, two inference engines, quantizations from 1-bit to 8-bit and context windows from 64K to 256K, tested tier by tier on NVIDIA cards and Macs. On your computer, the installer reads your GPU, memory and bandwidth, keeps only what fits with at least a 64K context, picks the smartest model and then the fastest, and counts it as installed only after it answers a real question.

### Wiki and memory: It remembers your business

[#wiki-and-memory-it-remembers-your-business](#wiki-and-memory-it-remembers-your-business)

[/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/wiki-and-memory.md](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/wiki-and-memory.md)

Tell one agent, and every agent knows. Another Me reads back through your inbox, then each night files your mail, chats and meetings into wiki pages that every agent can search. It keeps the information, preferences and decisions that matter across conversations, on your device instead of in a provider's account.

### Proactive Another Me: It speaks up when it matters

[#proactive-another-me-it-speaks-up-when-it-matters](#proactive-another-me-it-speaks-up-when-it-matters)

[/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/proactive.md](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/proactive.md)

It reaches out first. Another Me watches your inbox, calendar and Slack, follows up and moves work forward, and messages you only when something needs you. And it improves itself recursively: every night it reads how its own messages landed and rewrites the rules it decides by, so every "not now" makes tomorrow's judgment sharper.

#### How it learns: recursive self-improvement, every night

[#how-it-learns-recursive-self-improvement-every-night](#how-it-learns-recursive-self-improvement-every-night)

Every message Another Me sends on its own is also a test. Each night, Dreaming reads how those messages landed and rewrites the rules Another Me decides by; the next day it decides with the new rules, and how those land feeds the next night. No retraining: what it learned stays a short list of rules.

Implicit reinforcement: push back on the same kind twice, and it becomes a rule

You never state the rule; Another Me reads it from how you react. Want it sooner twice, and it tells you right away; turn it down twice, and it stays quiet.

[/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/proactive.md#learning-rules](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/proactive.md#learning-rules)

[/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/proactive.md#learning-rules](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/proactive.md#learning-rules)

No second time needed. Name the kind and it changes whether you are told; name the hour and it changes when.

### More features

[#more-features](#more-features)

- [Desktop and browser](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/desktop-and-browser.md): Your apps, your Chrome, already signed in. Agents open the apps on your Mac, Windows PC or Linux desktop and click and type the way you do, and on a Mac or Windows PC they work in your everyday Chrome with the sign-ins you already have.

- [Agent teams](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/team-md.md): One plan for every agent. When a job needs more than one agent, Another Me brings in the agents you have, creates the ones it is missing, and writes one team.md that they all follow.

- [Task delegation](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/task-delegation.md): Long work runs in the background while the chat stays with you. An agent hands long work to background copies of itself and keeps talking with you while the work runs.

- [Model scheduling](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/model-scheduling.md): One model, many agents, your work first. Every agent, approval and background job shares the local model, so work you are waiting for starts at once and background work waits until the model is quiet.

- [Bring your own runtime](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/local-inference.md): Claude Code, Codex or Pi, on your GPU or your subscription. Each runs as it always does, on local models or on the plan you already pay for.

- [Voice](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/voice.md): Record a meeting with a live transcript, ask what was said, and hand the follow-ups to Another Me.

## Quick start

[#quick-start](#quick-start)

### Option 1: the desktop app

[#option-1-the-desktop-app](#option-1-the-desktop-app)

For one person on one computer. Choose your platform below. The app
opens Linger, connects this computer and runs its agents. Quick Install on its
setup screen sets up a Linger server on this computer too.

Platform
Package

[macOS 14.0 or later, Apple silicon](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/macos.md)

Linger-<version>-macos-arm64.dmg

[Windows x64](https://github.com/simplexai-labs/linger/releases/latest)

Linger-<version>-windows-x64-setup.exe, or -windows-x64-portable.zip to run without installing

[Linux x64](https://github.com/simplexai-labs/linger/releases/latest)

Linger-<version>-linux-x64.tar.gz (glibc 2.35 or newer)

There is no package for Intel Macs; use Docker Compose there. Check each
download against SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release.

On macOS, the package is not yet signed, so macOS blocks the first launch.
After dragging Linger to Applications, run this in Terminal, then open Linger:

```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Linger.app
```

If Terminal prints Operation not permitted, turn on Terminal in System
Settings › Privacy & Security › App Management, reopen Terminal and run it
again. [Start Linger on macOS](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/macos.md) shows how to allow the launch
without Terminal.

See [Install the desktop app](https://docs.heylinger.com/install/desktop-app)
for the Windows and Linux steps and for help when setup fails.

### Option 2: Docker Compose

[#option-2-docker-compose](#option-2-docker-compose)

For a server that several computers share, or to build from source. You need
Docker with Compose and at least 5 GiB of memory for its virtual machine.

```
git clone https://github.com/simplexai-labs/linger.git
<span class="pl-c1">cd</span> linger
cp .env.example .env
docker compose up --build --detach --wait
```

Create the owner account. As written, it signs in as owner@linger.local with
the test password test1234; replace either one to use your own:

```
<span class="pl-c1">printf</span> <span class="pl-s"><span class="pl-pds">'</span>%s\n<span class="pl-pds">'</span></span> <span class="pl-s"><span class="pl-pds">'</span>test1234<span class="pl-pds">'</span></span> <span class="pl-k">|</span> docker compose <span class="pl-c1">exec</span> -T teamhub \
  teamhub-account create-owner --email owner@linger.local --password-stdin
```

Change the test password in Settings › Security before other people can
reach the server.

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) and sign in. The app then shows the commands that
connect your computer. See
[Install with Docker Compose](https://docs.heylinger.com/install/docker-compose)
to serve other computers on your network and to turn on voice.

#### Local models

[#local-models](#local-models)

The Compose containers run only the Linger server; models and agents run on the
computer you connect. Run the guided installer on that computer:

```
<span class="pl-c"><span class="pl-c">#</span> macOS (Apple silicon) or Linux</span>
./scripts/install/install.sh
```

```
<span class="pl-c"><span class="pl-c">#</span> Windows (x64, ordinary non-elevated PowerShell)</span>
powershell <span class="pl-k">-</span>ExecutionPolicy Bypass <span class="pl-k">-</span>File .\scripts\install\install.ps1
```

It checks your hardware, installs and verifies a local model after your
approval, and connects linger-computer to your Linger server. Pass --help
for day-2 commands (--status, --up, --remove), or see
[Bring your own runtime](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/docs/local-inference.md) for other model setups.

If Compose ports are busy, the installer offers to select free ports. It saves
LiveKit's port mappings and matching RTC startup arguments in
.local/compose.ports.yml and enables the override through .env. This requires
Docker Compose 2.24.4 or later. Rerunning the installer reuses those ports.

## Acknowledgements

[#acknowledgements](#acknowledgements)

Linger is built on these open-source projects:

- [PolyStrata](https://github.com/simplexai-labs/PolyStrata): Runs local models, keeping the experts a model uses most on your GPU.

- [Strata](https://github.com/Niko1221/Strata): The inference engine PolyStrata is built on. It runs Qwen3.8-Flash-Next.

- [BlockNote](https://github.com/TypeCellOS/BlockNote), [Yjs](https://github.com/yjs/yjs) and [Hocuspocus](https://github.com/ueberdosis/hocuspocus): Power the documents that people and agents edit together.

- [Hindsight](https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight): Where agent memory's fact extraction and search come from.

- [Cua](https://github.com/trycua/cua): Lets agents use the apps on your desktop.

- [LiveKit](https://github.com/livekit/livekit): Carries the audio for voice and meetings.

- [River](https://github.com/riverqueue/river): Runs TeamHub's background jobs.

- [NOTICE](/simplexai-labs/linger/blob/main/NOTICE): Lists every third-party component, what we adapted from it, and the license that applies.

Thank you to the open-source community for libraries this powerful and this simple: they let us focus on the product itself, and we hope our projects will in turn give everyone an easier-to-use knowledge base.
