Anthropic ([ANT](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/ant)), one of the world’s leading artificial intelligence companies, began trading on the Nasdaq Stock Market on Thursday.

The San Francisco-based public benefit corporation opened for trading at $111.50 per share. Anthropic co-founders Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei marked the initial public offering with remarks during the opening bell ceremony at Nasdaq MarketSite.

“Our customers, the world's most ambitious companies, nonprofits, and individual people are already applying the intelligence we've built to stimulate economic growth, lift people out of poverty, protect democracy and free societies, even make everyday services like the DMV work better,” said Dario Amodei, the company’s CEO. “We're proud to work alongside them, and I'm extremely optimistic about what we can do together in the future.”

“The advent of machine intelligence is one of the seminal moments in human history, and we have a rare opportunity to shape it for the better,” he added.

Anthropic is the company behind the frontier intelligence Claude, which it delivers to customers through its models, platform, and applications. Since its commercial launch in the first quarter of 2023, Claude has seen significant enterprise adoption.

“We want Claude to be a trusted partner, one that’s honest with you, one that makes you more capable, but leaves you in charge. Plenty of technology is built to take up your time. We want Claude to give it back to you,” said Daniela Amodei, who serves as Anthropic’s president and Chairperson of its board. “Our customers are some of our best partners in that work. More than half of the Fortune 500 use Claude for everything from drug discovery to underwriting, to preventing financial fraud and abuse.”

Anthropic was started in 2021 by a team of seven co-founders and describes itself first and foremost as a research lab. The company is incorporated as a public benefit corporation, meaning it is required to balance the financial interests of its stockholders, the best interests of those materially affected by its conduct, and the specific public benefit purpose of its mission for “the responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.”
