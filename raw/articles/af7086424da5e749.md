I’m being pitched a new inference neocloud company on average every two days. No exaggeration. I take every entrepreneur seriously and I look deeply into each.

But it’s hard to fathom this being sustainable. At the same time, AI Gateway token demand on Vercel has been ~40% MoM compounded since the beginning of the year. No signs of stopping.

One way to reconcile this is that compute is the new economy, and tokens the new energy unit. There’s a new Industrial Revolution taking place. Every aspect of our lives is being replatformed on top of this new system that enhances everything. More like an “oil rush” than a “gold rush”: it’s durable, not a fad.

In the short term, I anticipate more expansion, followed by consolidation due to economies of scale, cybersecurity and enterprise requirements (ZDR and cyber being a hot topic for inference providers), capital requirements, and an evolving regulatory landscape.
