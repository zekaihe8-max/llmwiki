Through my wonderful time at NVIDIA and Stanford, one question has kept me up at night:

Should the future of AI be monolithic or plural?

To answer this question, we’ve raised $110M to get started, backed by [@MenloVentures](/MenloVentures), [@MadronaVentures](/MadronaVentures), [@NVIDIA](/NVIDIA), [@NEA](/NEA), [@KindredVentures](/KindredVentures),[@Wing_VC](/Wing_VC), [@aixventureshq](/aixventureshq), [@Principal_VC](/Principal_VC), [@Cisco](/Cisco), [@Snowflake](/Snowflake), among others.

Today, Carlos ([@guestrin](/guestrin)) and I are stoked to launch Noeri, a neolab to push the frontier of plural intelligence.

Noeri means “the plural of intelligence” or many intelligences.

The future of AI we root for is plural: a healthy ecosystem of many intelligences, in all sizes, strengths, and quirks.

Discovery, in particular, has always been plural: many curious minds, each devoted to their own question, often on roads less traveled.

Our path there is self-improving AI that:

-> learns continually on the job, closing the gap between what AI can do off the shelf and what the dynamic world demands

-> learns to use other AIs as tools and make new tools to overcome its own limitations

-> picks the right-sized model for your problem, at a fraction of the frontier cost

Then the power of intelligence, from the craftsmanship of making AI to ownership, can be in the hands of all.

[@noeri_ai](/noeri_ai) mission reflects who we are as the founding team. My career has been anything but linear, literally continual learning on the job. Carlos has devoted his career to building tools that empower people through AI.

We are all a bit contrarian, a bit adventurous, and above all, big believers in pluralism.

Join us to build this future together: [noeri.ai](https://t.co/oAAoe0lzEH)
