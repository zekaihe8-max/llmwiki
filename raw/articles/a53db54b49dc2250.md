# Traces is joining Vercel

Tarun Sachdeva
·Nov 16, 2026·1 min

Today, I wanted to share an important update: Traces is joining Vercel. At Vercel, our entire team will continue working on how teams build software with agents.

For existing Traces users, nothing changes on day one, and you can continue to share sessions on Traces and use the Traces CLI.

###
The Path Forward

Traces started in February as a simple way to share our work across different agents. In the last nine months, we’ve shipped: [Traces for Teams](https://traces.com/blog/traces-for-teams), adapters for 16 coding agents, [Git integrations](https://traces.com/blog/traces-git), [Handoffs](https://traces.com/blog/handoffs), [Traces MCP](https://traces.com/blog/traces-mcp), [Surfaces](https://traces.com/blog/make-your-own-surfaces), and so much more. Thousands of teams have joined Traces and shared nearly 500,000 sessions.

Today, Traces has become a hub to collaborate across agent ecosystems, an evolving team memory, and a core building block for teams to take ownership of their intelligence stack.

Existing Trace users can continue publishing and using Traces. You can continue adding users to your existing orgs. We will be reaching out to you and our support channels will remain open. This is your data, and we remain committed to handling it thoughtfully. As part of this transition, we have paused new org creation.

For anyone who has used Traces, shared sessions, and given us feedback: thank you. We’ve learned so much from you, and we’re grateful for your trust.

We're excited to continue working on these problems at Vercel.

Thank you for everything.

Tarun
