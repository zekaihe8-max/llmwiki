Alibaba has launched Qwen4 Max, gaining 9 points over Qwen3.8 Max on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and landing 3 points behind Kimi K3.1 Pro at ~11% lower Cost per Task

Qwen4 Max, using xhigh reasoning effort, scores 43 on the Intelligence Index, 9 points ahead of Qwen3.8 Max (xhigh) and 3 points behind Kimi K3.1 Pro (max, 46)

Key takeaways:

➤ Qwen4 Max improves on every evaluation in the Index vs Qwen3.8 Max (xhigh, 34): HLE-Diamond +21 points (24% to 45%), AA-Omniscience +18 points, AutomationBench-AA +17 points, CritPt v2 +13 points, Terminal-Bench-Science +11 points, Terminal-Bench 4.0 +9 points, GDP.pdf +3 points, FrontierCode 2.0 +3 points and AA-Briefcase +82 Elo.

➤ Qwen4 Max costs $3.27 per Intelligence Index task, ~3x less than Qwen3.8 Max ($10.02) and ~11% less than Kimi K3.1 Pro ($3.68): This is driven by 25% lower per-token pricing than Qwen3.8 Max, 70% cheaper cache hits and lower token usage. Tencent Hy4 costs ~3x less per task ($1.03) than Qwen4 Max but scores 8 points lower on the Index.

➤ Qwen4 Max uses 39% fewer output tokens per task than Qwen3.8 Max: ~105k output tokens per Intelligence Index task vs ~171k for Qwen3.8 Max, ~72k for Kimi K3.1 Pro and ~78k for Tencent Hy4. Qwen4 Max uses fewer tokens, while increasing 9 points on the Intelligence Index

➤ Improvements in AA-Briefcase (knowledge work): On AA-Briefcase, our evaluation of agentic work across real business files, Qwen4 Max reaches 1686 Elo vs 1674 for Kimi K3.1 Pro and 1446 for Tencent Hy4.

➤ Qwen4 Max trails Kimi K3.1 Pro on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and Tencent Hy4 on FrontierCode 2.0: Qwen4 Max scores 48% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 vs 67% for Kimi K3.1 Pro and 37% for Tencent Hy4. On FrontierCode 2.0, [@cognition](/cognition)’s private evaluation of real-world coding tasks, it scores 26%, level with Kimi K3.1 Pro (26%) and behind Tencent Hy4 (31%).

Key model details:

➤ Context window: 1M tokens

➤ Multimodal: Text and image input with text output

➤ Pricing: $1.50/$4.50 per 1M input/output tokens on Alibaba's first-party APIs, with a $0.075 cache hit price. This is lower than Qwen3.8 Max across the board ($2.00/$6.00, with a $0.25 cache hit price)

➤ Availability: [@Alibaba_Qwen](/Alibaba_Qwen) 's first-party APIs
