## What everyone does with it

[#what-everyone-does-with-it](#what-everyone-does-with-it)

Pick up where a teammate left off. Open a teammate's AI session in a team project and keep going: the whole conversation and every answer so far are there, and each new answer runs with the access of the person who asked. Share any other session with people or groups, like a file in Google Drive.

[/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/shared-sessions.gif](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/shared-sessions.gif)

Ask beside any room, send in one click. A side chat only you see; one click sends Trina's answer into the room.

[/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/room-share.gif](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/room-share.gif)

Each person's AI sees only what they may open. Mina can't open Finance, so neither can her AI. [How permissions work](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/PERMISSIONS.md)

[/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/folder-access.gif](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/folder-access.gif)

Files found by what they are. Every night the team's AI reads new and changed files in shared, group and project folders and keeps one line per file saying what it is, so a scan named temp.pdf or a photo named IMG_2031.jpg is found by its content. It uses the team's AI account and never reads personal folders. In a test with 2,238 made-up files, Claude Code found the right file for 181 of 184 questions with these lines, and for 172 without them.

Scheduled tasks. Ask once; Trina repeats it on your schedule, in your team's time zone.

[/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/scheduled-list.webp](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/scheduled-list.webp)

Guests. Invite people outside the company into a group chat or project, and choose whether they can use the AI.

[/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/guest-room.webp](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/guest-room.webp)

Usage and limits. Answers, tokens and estimated cost per person; token limits per person, group or team.

Search everything. ⌘K across chats, history, projects, files, skills and scheduled tasks.

Team chat like Slack, but with Trina AI. Channels for everyone and each group, private channels, direct messages, group chats, threads and @mentions, on the web, desktop and phone. Ask Trina beside any of them, in a side chat only you see.

[/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/team-chat.webp](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/images/readme/v4/team-chat.webp)

- Every change is kept. By a person or their AI; bring back any version.

- In-app browser. In the desktop app, Trina opens websites in a pane beside your chat, reads them, clicks and fills in forms while you watch; Stop ends it. It asks before a new site unless you allow all websites.

- Apps post into rooms. Sentry, GitHub, Stripe, Jira and HubSpot updates arrive as cards.

- Find work files. The desktop app finds recent work files on your computer and adds the ones you pick.

- Documents. Reads Hangul (HWP/HWPX) text; previews PDF and PowerPoint.

- Every device. Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, Android and any browser, in six languages.

## How it works

[#how-it-works](#how-it-works)

One server runs Claude Code and Codex as the engine. Every file request, from a person or their AI, goes through one permission check on the server. On a Linux server the engine runs as its own account inside a bubblewrap, Landlock and seccomp sandbox, with its shell and built-in file tools turned off: it reaches files only through Trina's tools. [Security and permissions](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/security-and-permissions.md)

Trina provides the workspace, not the AI: connect Claude or OpenAI accounts and plans authorized for your use. AI usage is billed by the account you connect.

Data, limits and what we don't promise

### What we don't promise

[#what-we-dont-promise](#what-we-dont-promise)

- Self-hosting is not offline: the context a request needs goes to the AI provider you connect, and configured integrations receive their requests. Each Codex web search sends the conversation so far to OpenAI's search service. With OpenRouter for speech, allowed by an admin, the sound of video and audio files goes to OpenRouter. The server asks updates.trina.app for its own public address every 30 minutes (MYCEL_EXIT_ADDRESS_CHECK=off turns that off). [Details](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/security-and-permissions.md)

- Server operators can read stored files and chats, including Trina operators for hosting. App admins don't get a browser for everyone's private conversations.

- Removing folder access does not erase earlier answers. The wider-sharing check is not universal data-loss prevention.

- No security certification, perfect answers, guaranteed savings or automatic legal, financial or HR decisions. Review outputs and deployment boundaries.

- Linux servers run no OCR: the AI sees photos and scanned pages as images, but they are not text-searchable. Office Macs cannot read video yet.

- HWP/HWPX text works for every team; [previews and making Hangul documents](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/resources/hwp/README.md) need the optional HWP Preview plugin (macOS, or Linux with glibc 2.39+; not Debian 12).

- Connections: a person's own eligible connection comes first, then one assigned to the person, a group, then the whole team. On fresh Trina teams, one company connection can serve the whole team by default; existing saved choices persist. This software setting does not grant permission under Anthropic's or OpenAI's terms. [Connection details](/trina-app/trina-ax/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md)

- Linux self-hosting needs Debian 12/13 or Ubuntu 24.04, systemd with cgroup v2, amd64 or arm64, 4 GB RAM and 20 GB disk. Your IT team installs, updates and restores backups.
