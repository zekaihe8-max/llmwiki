# One question at a time

When the problem is hugely underspecified and you really do not know what you are building, sequential one-question grilling beats a single Q&A round that could branch the work in massively different directions.[^mattpocock-grill-me]

## Synthesis

[Matt Pocock on /grill-me](../sources/articles/mattpocock-grill-me.md) describes still using `/grill-me about this, one question at a time` in that underspecified case—when one broad clarification pass could send the build down very different paths.[^mattpocock-grill-me] [Matt Pocock on /software-factory](../sources/articles/mattpocock-software-factory.md) shows the same grilling move aimed at a concrete domain: `/grill-me on how a /software-factory might help us` to decide whether factory infra is worth it.[^mattpocock-software-factory]
