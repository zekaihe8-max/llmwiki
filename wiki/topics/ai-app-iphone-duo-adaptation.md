# AI 应用适配 iPhone Duo

*AI apps adapting to iPhone Duo*

双屏/折叠机型（如 iPhone Duo）上，AI 应用的识别与界面适配可能滞后；一旦社区把识别情况公开，产品方可在很短周期（如隔天）内跟进适配。[^op7418-workbuddy-iphone-duo]

On dual-screen or foldable devices (e.g. iPhone Duo), AI apps’ recognition and UI fit can lag; once the community surfaces recognition issues, vendors can follow up with adaptation on a short cycle (e.g. the next day).[^op7418-workbuddy-iphone-duo]
