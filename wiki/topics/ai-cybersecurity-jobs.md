# AI 与网络安全岗位

*AI cybersecurity jobs*

网络安全是观察「AI 创造而非仅替代岗位」的前沿场景。防御工作更偏智能体化之后，组织仍需要更多人去指挥智能体、研判发现、打补丁与处置事件——人才与系统防护因此同为投资增长点。[^levie-ai-cybersecurity-jobs]

Cybersecurity is a leading place to see jobs AI creates rather than only displaces. As defensive work becomes more agentic, organizations still need more people to direct agents, judge findings, patch systems, and handle incidents—so talent and protecting systems both grow as investment areas.[^levie-ai-cybersecurity-jobs]
