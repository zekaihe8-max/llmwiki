# 开源工具装进 AI 助手做自然语言剪辑

*Open-source tools in AI assistants for natural-language editing*

把开源创作工具装进 AI 助手后，用户不必学复杂时间线或外包剪辑，可用自然语言选模板、放素材、导出成片，甚至复刻喜欢的片子；婚礼视频是早期消费场景之一。[^gorden-sun-vvv-wedding-edit]

Once open-source creative tools sit inside an AI assistant, users need not learn complex timelines or hire editors: natural language can pick templates, place assets, export a finished cut, and even replicate a liked video; wedding films are an early consumer case.[^gorden-sun-vvv-wedding-edit]

## 综合分析 / Synthesis

[Gorden Sun 谈开源 VVV 一句话剪婚礼片](../sources/articles/gorden-sun-vvv-wedding-edit.md) 以婚礼成片为例：质疑「还在花钱找人剪」，主张把开源项目 VVV 装进 AI 助手，说一句话完成选模板、放照片、出成片，并可复刻喜欢的片子。[^gorden-sun-vvv-wedding-edit] 原帖 <https://x.com/Gorden_Sun/status/2113902089978126354>。该主张把价值放在「开源工具 + 助手入口 + 自然语言」，而不是另搭一套封闭剪辑产品。

The value sits in open-source tool + assistant surface + natural language, not a new closed editing product.
