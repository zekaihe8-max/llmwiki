# Agent 产出直送 Notion 阅读

*Agent output routed to Notion for reading*

个人 Agent 不必只在聊天里读完生成物：用户可把 Grok bot、Muse 等产出直接发到 Notion，再在笔记应用里阅读；当 Notion 在折叠/双屏机型（如 iPhone Duo）上适配到位时，这条「生成 → Notion → 读」路径更说得通。[^op7418-notion-iphone-duo]

Personal agents need not finish reading only inside chat: users can send Grok bot, Muse, and similar output straight into Notion and read in the notes app; when Notion adapts well on foldable or dual-screen devices (e.g. iPhone Duo), the generate → Notion → read path becomes more plausible.[^op7418-notion-iphone-duo]
