# 社区实测对抗刷分碰瓷

*Community evals vs score hype*

榜单与付费吹分挡不住推友手测；刷分找 KOL 掩盖、跟 Opus 一类口碑标杆碰瓷，碰的是社区真实测评，不是实验室本身。水平可以不如旗舰，但忽悠高价与过度宣传会在实测曝光后被锤。[^fkysly-score-hype-opus-grave][^fkysly-overhype-backlash]

Leaderboards and paid score hype do not outrun peers’ hands-on tests; score-chasing KOL cover-ups and name-dropping a reputation bar like Opus confront community evals, not the lab itself. Trailing a flagship can be fine, but selling high on hype and over-promotion get hammered once real tests expose the gap.[^fkysly-score-hype-opus-grave][^fkysly-overhype-backlash]
