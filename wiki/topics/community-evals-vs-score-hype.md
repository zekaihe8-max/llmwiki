# 社区实测对抗刷分碰瓷

*Community evals vs score hype*

榜单与付费吹分挡不住推友手测；顶档上 AA 一类分数更像潜力，真用才是 hit / miss。刷分找 KOL 掩盖、跟 Opus 一类口碑标杆碰瓷，碰的是社区真实测评，不是实验室本身。水平可以不如旗舰，但忽悠高价与过度宣传会在实测曝光后被锤。另有声音点名某次英推 KOL 宣发：选人离谱、Paid Partnership 合规格局不该小，模型强就该让人用起来，别规定奇怪话术刷屏。还有声音指出：不投中推、花小钱买通英推上游让中推免费搬运，以及许多「海外」大号实为华人/港新假号与 MCN 互刷对接甲方，花钱无效。[^fkysly-score-hype-opus-grave][^fkysly-overhype-backlash][^shao-meng-kimi-en-kol-promo-weird][^dingyi-kimi-en-kol-fake-accounts][^natolambert-real-world-over-aa]

Leaderboards and paid score hype do not outrun peers’ hands-on tests; at the top end, AA-style scores are more like potential, and real usability is the hit-or-miss conversion. Score-chasing KOL cover-ups and name-dropping a reputation bar like Opus confront community evals, not the lab itself. Trailing a flagship can be fine, but selling high on hype and over-promotion get hammered once real tests expose the gap. Another voice names a specific English-Twitter KOL campaign: bad talent pick, Paid Partnership compliance that should not be petty, and that a strong model’s best promo is letting people use it—not prescribing weird talking-point spam. Still another notes skipping Chinese-Twitter spend, buying English-Twitter upstream so Chinese accounts free-carry, and that many “overseas” big accounts are Chinese/HK/SG fakes farmed by MCNs and pitched to clients—spend with no effect.[^fkysly-score-hype-opus-grave][^fkysly-overhype-backlash][^shao-meng-kimi-en-kol-promo-weird][^dingyi-kimi-en-kol-fake-accounts][^natolambert-real-world-over-aa]
