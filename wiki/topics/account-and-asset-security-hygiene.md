# 账号与资产安全卫生

*Account and asset security hygiene*

个人账号与支付工具的基本卫生包括：强密码与两步验证、停用不需要的信用卡并对在用卡设限额、注册前确认服务可信，以及不点不明链接、不装破解软件。[^oran-ge-account-asset-security]

Basic hygiene for personal accounts and payment tools includes strong passwords and two-factor authentication, stopping unused credit cards and capping cards in use, confirming a service is trustworthy before signup, and not clicking unknown links or installing cracked software.[^oran-ge-account-asset-security]
