# Millenarian apocalypse discourse

Communities that treat a near-term Apocalypse as axiomatic, scan for onset signs, feel both fear and excitement, and talk only with people who share the same beliefs fit a millenarian death-cult pattern. The claim is a cultural critique of that discourse shape, including radical AI-doom circles that match it.[^fchollet-millenarian-death-cult]

[François Chollet on millenarian death-cult signs](../sources/articles/fchollet-millenarian-death-cult.md) compresses the pattern into four linked signs—near-term certainty, omen-watching, fearful excitement, and a closed belief circle—without naming a specific prophecy calendar in the post itself.[^fchollet-millenarian-death-cult] Adjacent wiki pages that push back on fatalistic [AI-doom framing of open models and cyber](./open-models-cyber-defense.md) sit in the same broader skepticism of doom discourse, though they argue a different substantive claim.
