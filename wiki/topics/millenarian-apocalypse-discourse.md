# 千禧年末日话语

*Millenarian apocalypse discourse*

把近在眼前的 Apocalypse 当作公理、扫描开端征兆、同时感到恐惧与兴奋、且只与共享同一套信念的人交谈——这类共同体符合千禧年死亡崇拜的模式。主张是对该话语形态的文化批评，也包括与之吻合的激进 AI 末日圈。[^fchollet-millenarian-death-cult]

Communities that treat a near-term Apocalypse as axiomatic, scan for onset signs, feel both fear and excitement, and talk only with people who share the same beliefs fit a millenarian death-cult pattern. The claim is a cultural critique of that discourse shape, including radical AI-doom circles that match it.[^fchollet-millenarian-death-cult]
