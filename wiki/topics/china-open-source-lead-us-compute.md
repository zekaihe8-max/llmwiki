# 中国开源领先与美国算力优势

*China open-source lead and US compute advantage*

开源模型的「谁领先」不必绑成单一答案：可在开源赛道上判中国全面领先，同时承认美国有 10 倍以上算力优势；「蒸馏」不必自动当成瓶颈，而开源相对闭源前沿仍可保留一段约「3 到 5」的落后（单位随来源）。[^shao-meng-natolambert-harvard-open-models]

Who “leads” on open models need not be a single score: China can lead the open track while the US keeps a 10×+ compute advantage; distillation need not be assumed the bottleneck, and open can still lag the closed frontier by about “3 to 5” of a unit that depends on the source.[^shao-meng-natolambert-harvard-open-models]

## 矛盾与变化 / Contradictions

[邵萌转述 Nathan Lambert 哈佛开源模型讲座](../sources/articles/shao-meng-natolambert-harvard-open-models.md) 称开源模型落后闭源前沿大约 3 到 5（单位未完）。[^shao-meng-natolambert-harvard-open-models] [宝玉拆解 Kimi K3.1 Pro](../sources/articles/dotey-kimi-k3-1-pro.md) 等写入 [开放权重前沿模型](./open-weight-frontier-models.md) 的材料，则按官方表把某款中国开放权重写成代码接近 Claude Opus 5.5、多项自报第一。格局上的「仍落后一段」与单品发布侧「逼近闭源旗舰」并存，尚未用同一套尺度对齐；亦不必把「中国开源全面领先」读成「已追平闭源前沿」。

[Meng Shao on Nathan Lambert's Harvard open-models lecture](../sources/articles/shao-meng-natolambert-harvard-open-models.md) says open models lag the closed frontier by about 3 to 5 (unit incomplete).[^shao-meng-natolambert-harvard-open-models] Material such as [Dotey on Kimi K3.1 Pro](../sources/articles/dotey-kimi-k3-1-pro.md) filed under [Open-weight frontier models](./open-weight-frontier-models.md) reads a Chinese open-weight launch table as coding near Claude Opus 5.5 with several claimed firsts. Landscape “still lags some stretch” and product-launch “near closed flagship” coexist without a shared scale; “China comprehensively leads open source” also must not be read as “already tied with the closed frontier.”
