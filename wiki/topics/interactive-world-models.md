# 实时交互世界模型

*Real-time interactive world models*

世界模型可从「能生成一段画面」推进到更接近可交互环境：实时周围原声、更强的画质与物理体感，以及在生成过程中用提示词插入事件。实验室发布时，社区演示观感可显著领先于最高付费档（如 Ultra）当下是否真正可玩；同一实验室其他产品线「整体拉胯」的一年里，仍可出现被社区标成「目前最强 / 质感有希望」的世界模型亮点。[^op7418-genie-4-world-model][^oran-ge-genie-4-world-model]

World models can move from “generate a clip” toward a more interactive environment: real-time ambient audio, stronger visual and physics feel, and mid-generation promptable events. At launch, community demo impressions can clearly outrun whether the top paid tier (e.g. Ultra) can actually try the product yet; in a year when the same lab’s other models are said to underwhelm overall, a world-model release can still be framed as “currently strongest” with promising quality.[^op7418-genie-4-world-model][^oran-ge-genie-4-world-model]
