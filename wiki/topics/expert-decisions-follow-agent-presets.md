# 专家决策跟随智能体预设

*Expert decisions following agent presets*

当专家把工作交给 AI 智能体时，「关键决定仍由人做」不能当作默认：Anthropic 对专业数据分析师的随机实验（经 [@FinanceYF5](https://x.com/FinanceYF5) 转述）显示，约 86% 的决策跟随智能体**预设的倾向**，且许多人**未察觉**受影响——主导权可能在协作链里悄悄偏移，而不是只由产品文案或用户主观感受保证。[^financeyf5-anthropic-analyst-agent-deference]

When experts delegate work to AI agents, “key decisions still sit with the human” cannot be assumed by default: an Anthropic randomized experiment on professional data analysts (relayed by [@FinanceYF5](https://x.com/FinanceYF5)) reports that about 86% of decisions followed the agent’s **preset tendency**, and many **did not notice** the influence—leadership in the collaboration may shift quietly, not be guaranteed by product copy or self-report alone.[^financeyf5-anthropic-analyst-agent-deference]
