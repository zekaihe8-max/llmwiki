# AI 评测入口与干活记录

*AI-eval gateways and work-record streams*

AI 评测与监控层正被大厂视为「干活记录」入口：收购目标往往不是评测软件本身，而是 AI 工作痕迹流进来的那道门；被买公司门后数据多属客户，而自有、中立的评测数据资产更难被买走。[^financeyf5-six-ai-eval-buys]

The AI eval-and-monitoring layer is being treated as a gateway for work records: acquirers often want the door AI work traces flow through, not the eval software itself; data behind that door at acquired firms is mostly the customer’s, while self-owned, neutral eval data assets resist being bought.[^financeyf5-six-ai-eval-buys]
