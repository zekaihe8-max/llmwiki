# Embedded third-party evals

Independent evaluators can work on site at a frontier lab, with deep access to infrastructure, to assess the systems that monitor internal agents for misalignment. Results from such pilots can strengthen safeguards and shape later third-party research collaborations.[^openai-metr-embedded-eval]

## Synthesis

[OpenAI METR embedded evaluation](../sources/articles/openai-metr-embedded-eval.md) describes a three-week summer pilot in which METR and Redwood Research evaluated OpenAI's internal monitoring for agent misalignment.[^openai-metr-embedded-eval] OpenAI reports that the findings helped harden safeguards as the system expanded and will inform future work with METR and other third-party evaluators; the same fetched thread carries METR's note that two employees worked on premises and that a brief write-up is public.[^openai-metr-embedded-eval]
