# 桌面换装角色伴侣

*Desktop outfit-changing character companions*

创作者可以把热门角色做成**天天待在桌面上、并能换不同衣服**的伴侣形象；旁观者（含作者自述）常把这类成品框成「不小心整了个大活」，欲望句落在「谁不想天天在桌面上看穿不同衣服的某角色」。耐久主张是**桌面日常可见的换装角色陪伴欲与意外成活感**，不是模型品牌实体周边，也不是可玩小游戏地板。[^op7418-desktop-malfoy-outfits]

Creators can ship popular characters as **desktop companions with outfit variety** people want to see every day; observers (including the author) often frame the artifact as accidentally pulling off something big, with the desire line “who wouldn’t want character X in different outfits on the desktop daily.” The durable claim is **daily desktop outfit-character companionship desire plus accidental-big-ship feel**—not model-brand physical merch, and not a playable mini-game floor.[^op7418-desktop-malfoy-outfits]
