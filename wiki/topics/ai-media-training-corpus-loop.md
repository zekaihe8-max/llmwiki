# AI 媒体内容回灌训练语料

*AI media content looping into training corpora*

当电视等大众媒体用 AI 写稿、由主持人念出「AI 味」文本，而这些播出内容又被收进后续训练语料时，就形成生成内容经人类播出再回灌模型的大循环——不是抽象的合成数据讨论，而是日常广播链路上可被旁观者直接听见的闭环。[^oran-ge-tv-ai-script-corpus-loop]

When mass media such as TV use AI-written scripts, hosts read AI-flavored text aloud, and that broadcast content re-enters later training corpora, a generate→human-broadcast→retrain loop forms—not an abstract synthetic-data debate, but a closed path an onlooker can hear on the everyday broadcast chain.[^oran-ge-tv-ai-script-corpus-loop]
