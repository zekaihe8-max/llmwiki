# 哪些活已放心交给 AI

*Tasks confidently handed over to AI*

一线可把「AI 已自动化哪些活」收成可对照的清单问题：从年初到现在，哪些工作或任务被 AI 自动化了——特别是以前做不到、现在完全放心让 AI 来的。个案证据可把 App Store 发版也标成已放心交给 AI 的活。[^fkysly-tasks-handed-to-ai] 适用边界：开放调查与单人示例，不是评论区汇总、失败率或发版工具链规格。

Practitioners can frame “what AI has already automated” as a comparable inventory: from early year until now, which jobs or tasks AI has automated—especially ones that could not be done before and that people now fully trust AI to handle. Case evidence can mark App Store releases as work already fully trusted to AI.[^fkysly-tasks-handed-to-ai] Scope: an open survey plus one person’s example—not a reply tally, failure rates, or a release-toolchain spec.
