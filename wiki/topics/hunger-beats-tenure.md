# 冲劲胜过资历

*Hunger beats tenure*

当模型能替人写大量代码时，一个人能做成多大成果，不再那么取决于攒了多少年框架与代码库经验；入行不久、或公司里没人脉与话语权的工程师，若有「冲劲」并用周末拿出能跑的原型，可以跑赢代码更好、资历更老的人。传统小编程题因此更难提供面试信号——模型比人更会做那些题——招聘侧可转向简历与作品集里显品味、同理心与有原则思考的小原型。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]

When models can write a lot of the code, how much one person ships depends less on years of framework and codebase tenure; early-career engineers—or those without network and voice inside the firm—can outrun stronger, more senior coders if they bring hunger and weekend-shippable prototypes. Classic small coding puzzles then lose interview signal—models beat humans at them—so hiring can shift toward resumes and portfolios whose small prototypes show taste, empathy, and principled thinking.[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
