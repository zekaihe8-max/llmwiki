# AI 原生公司

*AI-native companies*

存量公司可以接入 AI 功能，但新公司可以从第一天起按 AI 原生方式构建。把已经成功的软件业务改造成 AI 原生，处在艰难的中间地带，少见到一旦发生就值得注意。[^paulg-ai-native-companies]

Incumbents can adopt AI features, but new companies can be built AI-native from day one. Remaking an already successful software business into an AI-native one sits in a hard middle and is rare enough to be noteworthy when it happens.[^paulg-ai-native-companies]
