# AI 聊天机器人安全的消费者保护诉讼

*Consumer-protection suits over AI chatbot safety*

州执法机关可以把前沿聊天机器人厂商（乃至具名 CEO）拖进消费者保护诉讼：核心主张不是抽象「AI 是否安全」，而是公司对安全性的对外说法是否误导、以及是否未能保护用户免受伤害。[^unusual-whales-texas-ag-openai-lawsuit]

State enforcers can pull a frontier chatbot maker—and a named CEO—into consumer-protection litigation: the core claim is not abstract “is AI safe,” but whether the company’s safety representations misled consumers and whether it failed to protect users from harm.[^unusual-whales-texas-ag-openai-lawsuit]
