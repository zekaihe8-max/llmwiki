# 智能体规模化工程

*Agent-scaled engineering*

一旦编码智能体产出团队的大部分乃至全部产出，稀缺工作转向打通速度：自动化评审与审批、跑长时多智能体循环、保持人团队精简、把产品对准下一版模型，并让领域专家拥有构建者。[^mihail-eric-bcherny-stanford][^shao-meng-bcherny-stanford]

Once coding agents produce most or all of a team's output, the scarce work shifts to unblocking velocity: automate review and approvals, run long multi-agent loops, keep the human team small, aim products at the next model release, and let domain experts own the builders.[^mihail-eric-bcherny-stanford][^shao-meng-bcherny-stanford]
