# 随手 AI 小游戏一次成功

*Casual AI mini-games succeeding on first try*

对轻量可玩小游戏一类任务，当前通用模型的能力地板已高到「随手」提示常能一次交出发可玩演示；作者据此把进步读成「随便一个模型都太强了」，而不是点名某一旗舰。[^oran-ge-pelican-bike-ai-game] 同类轻量发布也可点名具体工具链（如 Codex）并把重点放在可玩第一版与开源魔改，而不复述「一次就成 / 随便一个模型」话术。[^vista8-ai-fushengji]

For light playable mini-games, the floor of general models is high enough that casual prompts often ship a runnable demo on the first try; authors may read that as “any model is too strong,” without naming a flagship.[^oran-ge-pelican-bike-ai-game] Peer light ships can instead name a concrete toolchain (e.g. Codex) and stress a playable v1 plus open remix, without repeating the “one-shot / any model” framing.[^vista8-ai-fushengji]
