# 智能作为人类中心主义指标

*Intelligence as an anthropocentric benchmark*

「智能」不必当作人类最重要的品质，而可当作从人类中心主义出发定义的一个指标（benchmark）。该排序历史上把人类放在顶上；当 AI 眼看要超越它时，更稳的叙事是承认智能只是自造概念之一，人还有惯性、感性等更常主导决策的品质。[^oran-ge-intelligence-anthropocentric-benchmark]

“Intelligence” need not be treated as humanity’s most important quality; it can be read as a benchmark defined from anthropocentrism. That ranking historically put humans on top; when AI is about to surpass it, a steadier story is that intelligence is only one invented concept, and that inertia and emotion more often lead human decisions than reason.[^oran-ge-intelligence-anthropocentric-benchmark]
