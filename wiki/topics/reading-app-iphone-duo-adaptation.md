# 阅读应用适配 iPhone Duo

*Reading apps adapting to iPhone Duo*

双屏/折叠机型（如 iPhone Duo）上，阅读与预览类应用需要专门适配才能成为可用的阅读面；适配之外，记住上次阅读位置一类续读能力，会进一步降低长文中断后再开的成本。[^op7418-jilan-iphone-duo]

On dual-screen or foldable devices (e.g. iPhone Duo), reading and preview apps need dedicated adaptation to be a usable reading surface; beyond layout fit, resume features such as remembering the last reading position further cut the cost of reopening after a long-form interrupt.[^op7418-jilan-iphone-duo]
