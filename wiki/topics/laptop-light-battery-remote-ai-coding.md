# 远程 AI Coding 时笔记本只需轻与续航

*Laptops need only lightness and battery when AI coding is remote*

当 AI Coding 的重活放在家里（或他处）机器、外出只连回去做时，随身笔记本的取舍可跟着变：本地旗舰性能吸引力下降，核心需求收成轻与续航。[^fkysly-laptop-light-battery-remote-ai] 适用边界：这是个案购机与工作流体感，不是机型评测或销量数据；未规定连回家的协议、家用机规格，也未否定仍在本机跑模型或本地编译的场景。

When heavy AI coding sits on a home (or other remote) machine and travel work is “connect back,” travel-laptop tradeoffs can follow: flagship local performance loses appeal, and core needs shrink to lightness and battery life.[^fkysly-laptop-light-battery-remote-ai] Scope: one person’s purchase and workflow feel—not a product review or sales data; it does not specify the connect-home protocol or home-machine specs, and does not deny still running models or local builds on-device.
