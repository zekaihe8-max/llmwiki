# AI 任务时界增长

*AI task horizon growth*

衡量前沿进展的一个有用尺度，是顶尖 AI 在给定成功率下能完成多长一段人类工作。这一时界在短短几个「出书周期」里，已从分钟推到小时再推到周——正是从业者不断指向的那条指数曲线。[^emollick-ai-task-horizon]

A useful yardstick for frontier progress is how long a stretch of human work the best AI can finish at a stated success rate. That horizon has moved from minutes to hours to weeks over a short span of book cycles, which is the exponential practitioners keep pointing at.[^emollick-ai-task-horizon]
