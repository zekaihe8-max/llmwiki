# 把目标抬到更野心直到摸清 AI 前沿

*Raise ambition until you find the AI frontier*

用 AI 时的一条通用做法是：不断把正在做的事抬得更野心，直到撞墙或惊喜。撞墙时，你摸清了能力前沿的形状；更常见的是发现模型比原先以为的更能干。[^emollick-ai-more-ambitious] 卡住点也可被读成「不敢提要求」而非模型太弱；「更有野心」还可拆成速度、高度、深度、广度等方向，在同一业务场景里逐级抬高提问。[^shao-meng-felix-think-bigger-four-axes]

A general practice when using AI is to keep raising the ambition of what you are doing until you hit a wall or a surprise. Hitting a wall teaches the shape of the capability frontier; more often, you learn the model can do more than you thought.[^emollick-ai-more-ambitious] The stuck point can also be read as not daring to ask rather than a weak model; “more ambitious” can be split into axes such as speed, altitude, depth, and range, stepping the ask on one business scene.[^shao-meng-felix-think-bigger-four-axes]
