# 先驱离任与继任背锅梗

*Incumbent exit and successor blame-shift metaphor*

中文科技圈讨论里，大厂高管更替可与产品发布周期绑成讽刺叙事： incumbent 被读成「明知今年有几波烂发布，先跑」；继任则被读成接锅——「未来是你的」，先把「垃圾」发出去并算在新人头上。帖文常用苹果语境里「厨子」（Tim Cook 绰号）作梗，指向的是**背锅与时机**的 cynicism，而不是可核对的人事文件。[^dingyi-cook-exits-new-ceo-blame]

In Chinese tech discourse, executive turnover at big labs can be satirically tied to product cycles: the incumbent reads as “knowing several rough launches are coming this year, exit first”; the successor reads as inheriting blame—“the future is yours,” ship the “garbage” first and put it on the newcomer. Posts often use the Apple “Cook” (Tim Cook) nickname as meme shorthand for **blame and timing** cynicism, not a checkable personnel record.[^dingyi-cook-exits-new-ceo-blame]
