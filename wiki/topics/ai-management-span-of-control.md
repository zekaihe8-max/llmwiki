# AI 扩大高管管控跨度

*AI expands senior management span of control*

大组织采用 AI 后，一种可见变化是：能直接监控全司的高管，其角色与管理跨度扩大，从而深刻改变运营；相对整司改造，这条路径往往更易落地。[^emollick-ai-management-span]

At large organizations, a visible AI effect is that senior managers who can directly monitor the whole firm expand their role and span of control—vastly changing operations—and that path is often more straightforward to implement than firm redesign.[^emollick-ai-management-span]
