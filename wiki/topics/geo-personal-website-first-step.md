# GEO 第一步：个人网站与友情链接

*GEO’s first step — personal sites and friend links*

面向生成式引擎被看见时，实务起点可以不是先改搜索产品，而是先把自己的个人网站收拾好，让模型侧「认识」你；社区互荐则用交换链接延续 Web2.0 友情链接的形态，被戏称为 GEO 版。[^oran-ge-geo-personal-website]

When the goal is being seen by generative engines, a practical start can be tending your personal website so the model side “knows” you—not rewriting search products first; peer mutual recommendation then continues Web2.0 friend-link exchange, jokingly called a GEO edition.[^oran-ge-geo-personal-website]
