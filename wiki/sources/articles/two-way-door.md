# 双向门

*Two-Way Door*

帖文称，Vibecoder 应在 AGENTS.md 中写入：穿过双向门后展示结果，遇到单向门时停止。双向门包括可撤销的代码、分支、拉取请求、测试数据与开发基础设施；单向门包括生产数据、面向用户的部署、对共享分支强制推送、删除非自己制作的内容以及发送给他人的消息。帖文还说，若答案只是盖章就不应提问，能运行即运行，问题仅留给只有自己能做的品味与产品决策，每次只问一个并附带建议。[^two-way-doors-agents-md]

The post urges vibecoders to add a two-way-door rule to AGENTS.md: walk through reversible doors and show the result, stop at irreversible ones. Two-way doors include code, branches, pull requests, test data, and dev infrastructure; one-way doors include production data, user-facing deploys, force-pushing shared branches, deleting things you didn't make, and messages sent to others. It also says not to ask for rubber stamps, to run things instead of asking when possible, and to save questions for taste and product calls—one at a time, with a recommendation.[^two-way-doors-agents-md]
