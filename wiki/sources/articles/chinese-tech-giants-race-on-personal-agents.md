# 国内大厂卷 Personal Agent

*Chinese Tech Giants Race on Personal Agents*

帖文称，Grok Bot 的国内对手已到来，阿里、字节、腾讯、百度四家大厂都在往 Personal Agent 方向发展。作者总结称，个人 Agent 不再只是 AI 聊天助手，而是开始配备云电脑、云手机、长期记忆与 Skills，甚至能在后台长期处理航班理赔、淘宝价保等任务。阿里有千问 Go! 与千问办公两条路线，字节有扣子 3.0 与尚未开放的「小豆」，腾讯有 WorkBuddy 与把 Hermes Agent 做成云服务的 LightVela，百度则有面向普通用户场景的百度搭子 DuMate。作者认为字节和腾讯最有潜力，阿里整合能力最强但宣传较少，百度虽被自己了解最少却仍在牌桌上并混到市占率第二。[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]

The post claims that Grok Bot's domestic rivals have arrived, with Alibaba, ByteDance, Tencent, and Baidu all moving toward Personal Agents. The author summarizes that Personal Agents are no longer just AI chat assistants, but are being equipped with cloud computers, cloud phones, long-term memory, and Skills, and can even handle tasks like flight compensation and Taobao price protection in the background. Alibaba has Qianwen Go! and Qianwen Office; ByteDance has Coze 3.0 and the unreleased 'Xiao Dou'; Tencent has WorkBuddy and LightVela, which turns Hermes Agent into a cloud service; Baidu has DuMate focused on everyday user scenarios. The author sees ByteDance and Tencent as most promising, Alibaba as strongest in integration but weak in promotion, and Baidu as still competitive with second-place market share despite being least familiar to the author.[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 阿里千问 Go! 定位 Personal Agent，依托淘宝、高德、大麦、夸克网盘生态，部分能力（如航班理赔、淘宝价保）仍在邀测。 / Alibaba's Qianwen Go! is positioned as a Personal Agent, relying on the Taobao, Amap, Damai, and Quark Netdisk ecosystem; some capabilities such as flight compensation and Taobao price protection remain in invitation-only testing.[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]
- 字节扣子 3.0 支持云电脑、长期记忆、多 Agent 协作，并可接入 Hermes、Claude Code、Codex、OpenClaw；其 Personal Agent 项目「Spell」可能以「小豆」命名，尚未正式开放。 / ByteDance's Coze 3.0 supports cloud computers, long-term memory, and multi-agent collaboration, and can integrate Hermes, Claude Code, Codex, and OpenClaw; its Personal Agent project 'Spell' may launch as 'Xiao Dou' but is not yet officially open.[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]
- 腾讯 LightVela 将 Hermes Agent 做成云端服务，支持长期记忆与 Skills，可通过微信、QQ 远程安排任务；WorkBuddy 则侧重办公场景并支持云端长期执行任务。 / Tencent's LightVela turns Hermes Agent into a cloud service supporting long-term memory and Skills, allowing remote task arrangement via WeChat and QQ; WorkBuddy focuses on office scenarios and supports cloud-based long-term task execution.[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]
- 百度搭子 DuMate 提供自媒体运营、数字人生成、求职、法律诉讼等工具套件，主打让普通用户无需复杂配置即可使用工作流。 / Baidu's DuMate offers toolkits for self-media operations, digital human generation, job hunting, and legal litigation, aiming to let ordinary users use workflows without complex configuration.[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]
- 作者表示阿里、字节、腾讯的产品都有使用过，最看好字节（豆包生态）和腾讯（微信入口），认为阿里整合最好但宣传少，百度市占率第二但自己了解最少。 / The author says they have used products from Alibaba, ByteDance, and Tencent, and is most optimistic about ByteDance (Doubao ecosystem) and Tencent (WeChat entry), considering Alibaba strongest in integration but weak in promotion, and Baidu second in market share though least familiar to the author.[^2f5a8921a0f5a567]
