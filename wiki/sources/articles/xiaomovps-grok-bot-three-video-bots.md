# 小墨推荐三个实用 Grok Bot 视频 Bot

*Xiaomo recommends three practical Grok Bot video bots*

[@xiaomovps](https://x.com/xiaomovps)（小墨同学）称许多人只知 Grok Bot 做 X 内容整理，其实还可生成视频与图片，并称背后路由到 Opus 5.5，生成质量一般不错。他整理三个值得尝试的 Bot：Grok Bot Video Studio 支持文生视频、多镜头故事与产品宣传片，可自动规划分镜、生成画面、加字幕与剪辑（<https://x.ai/bot/wVv54dUKjA2MYaq1XMCzN>）；Grok Studio 可把文章、PDF 或网页链接做成带 AI 主持人、旁白与引用来源的讲解视频，适合把技术文章转成视频（<https://x.ai/bot/QXZCVsfDe4hNuHRLxkfRb>）；Video Editor 面向真人口播，上传素材后可自动挑片段、加字幕、B-roll 与背景音乐，底层用 FFmpeg、Whisper、Remotion 等（<https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/bots/video-editor>）。他个人最感兴趣第二与第三个——一个把文章变成视频，一个把拍好的视频剪出来——并称 Grok Bot 生态越来越丰富，许多以前要自写 Skill、搭工作流的功能，直接导入别人做好的 Bot 就能尝试。[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-three-video-bots] 原帖见 <https://x.com/xiaomovps/status/2115314460340748454>。

[@xiaomovps](https://x.com/xiaomovps) (Xiaomo) says many people only know Grok Bot for X content collation, yet it can also generate video and images, and he attributes routing to Opus 5.5 with generally solid quality. He lists three bots worth trying: Grok Bot Video Studio for text-to-video, multi-shot stories, and product promos, with auto storyboarding, frames, captions, and editing (<https://x.ai/bot/wVv54dUKjA2MYaq1XMCzN>); Grok Studio, which turns an article, PDF, or URL into an explainer with an AI host, narration, and citations—suited to converting tech posts to video (<https://x.ai/bot/QXZCVsfDe4hNuHRLxkfRb>); and Video Editor for talking-head footage—auto pick clips, captions, B-roll, and music, on FFmpeg, Whisper, Remotion, and peers (<https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/bots/video-editor>). He is most interested in the second and third—article→video and editing filmed clips—and says the Grok Bot ecosystem is getting richer, so many jobs that once needed self-written Skills or workflows can be tried by importing someone else’s ready bot.[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-three-video-bots] Post: <https://x.com/xiaomovps/status/2115314460340748454>.
