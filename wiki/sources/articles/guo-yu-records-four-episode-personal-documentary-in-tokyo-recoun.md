# 郭宇在东京录制四集个人纪录片，讲述从留守儿童到AI从业者的人生

*Guo Yu records four-episode personal documentary in Tokyo, recounting life from left-behind child to AI practitioner*

帖文称，郭宇（@turingou）在东京银座单向街书店的安排下，于自家录制了一部很长的个人纪录片，目前先剪出四集，后续还会继续更新。四集分别讲述他在江西矿山的童年与留守经历、深圳求学及自学编程进入支付宝、在字节跳动工作六年半后28岁退休并来日本、以及退休后与AI合作做产品和对数字孪生、永生的思考。[^turingou-guo-yu-tokyo-documentary]

The post says Guo Yu (@turingou) recorded a long personal documentary at his Tokyo home with Ginza One-Way Street Bookstore, releasing four episodes first with more updates to come. The episodes cover his childhood as a left-behind child in a Jiangxi mining area, moving to Shenzhen and self-teaching coding to join Alipay, working at ByteDance for six and a half years before retiring at 28 and moving to Japan, and after retirement working with AI daily while reflecting on digital twins, immortality, and changing human relationships.[^turingou-guo-yu-tokyo-documentary]
