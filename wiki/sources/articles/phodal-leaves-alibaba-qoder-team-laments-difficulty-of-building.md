# Phodal 离开阿里 Qoder 团队，感慨大厂做产品难

*Phodal Leaves Alibaba Qoder Team, Laments Difficulty of Building Products in Big Tech*

帖文称，10 月 28 日，知名开源开发者黄峰达（Phodal）宣布离开阿里 Qoder 团队。他在告别帖中感慨：「在阿里要做好一个产品，可太难了。技术只是其中一部分，更多挑战往往在技术之外。」[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]

The post says that on October 28, well-known open-source developer Huang Fengda (Phodal) announced his departure from Alibaba's Qoder team. In his farewell post, he remarked: "It is very hard to build a good product at Alibaba. Technology is only part of it; more challenges often lie beyond technology."[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 黄峰达此前在 Thoughtworks 工作十多年，曾任中国区开源负责人，开发过 AutoDev、ArchGuard 等开源项目。[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- Huang Fengda previously worked at Thoughtworks for over a decade, served as China open-source lead, and developed open-source projects including AutoDev and ArchGuard.[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- 他今年 5 月前后加入 Qoder，参与 Canvas 交互体系和 Agent 插件生态建设，并开发开源工具 Better Harness 用于分析 AI 编程 Agent 工作流程。[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- He joined Qoder around May this year, participated in Canvas interaction system and Agent plugin ecosystem building, and developed the open-source tool Better Harness to analyze AI programming Agent workflows.[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- 9 月，他参与推出 Mobile Use 插件，让 AI 修改手机应用代码后，还能在安卓、鸿蒙和 iOS 运行环境中检查实际效果。[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- In September, he participated in launching the Mobile Use plugin, which allows AI to modify mobile app code and then check actual effects across Android, HarmonyOS, and iOS runtime environments.[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- Qoder 9 月披露年化经常性收入达 1 亿美元，但阿里云同时宣布逐步关停 QoderWork，将用户引导至 Qoder 桌面版和千问办公，且迁移用户需重新注册、积分权益不直接互通。[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- Qoder disclosed $100 million in annual recurring revenue in September, but Alibaba Cloud simultaneously announced it would gradually shut down QoderWork, guiding users to Qoder desktop and Qwen Office, with migrated users required to re-register and original points benefits not directly interoperable.[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- 8 月，曾牵头通义灵码项目的产品技术负责人陈鑫（神秀）也已离职。[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
- In August, Chen Xin (Shenxiu), product and technical lead who previously headed the Tongyi Lingma project, also left.[^phodal-leaves-ali-qoder]
