# 格雷格·布罗克曼倡议以困惑度为指标设定共享 AI 安全上限

*Greg Brockman Proposes Shared Perplexity-Based AI Safety Upper Limits*

「帖文称」格雷格·布罗克曼发文表示，他帮助创立 OpenAI 是为了让 AGI 安全且造福人类，并以此推动业界共享安全标准；帖文还引用了关于预训练进度与对齐监控进度不匹配的观点，并提出基于困惑度等指标设定共同限制、以可监测里程碑作为放宽条件的框架。[^gdb-perplexity-shared-safety-limits]

The post says Greg Brockman helped start OpenAI because he wants AGI to be safe and beneficial for people, and shares transparency as a foundation for industry-wide shared safety bars; it quotes a view that alignment and monitoring progress is unlikely to keep pace with pretraining acceleration, and proposes common limits based on perplexity and other measurements, gated by concrete milestones such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors.[^gdb-perplexity-shared-safety-limits]
