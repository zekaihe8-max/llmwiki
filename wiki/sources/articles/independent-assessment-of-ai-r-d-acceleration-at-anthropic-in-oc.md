# Anthropic AI 研发加速独立评估报告

*Independent Assessment of AI R&D Acceleration at Anthropic in October 2026*

METR 发布的帖文称，其工作人员于 2026 年 8 月 12 日至 10 月 29 日期间在 Anthropic 开展高准入评估，考察 AI 对 AI 研发的加速程度。报告指出，Anthropic 模型在明确任务上的能力改进速度可能约为 2024 年 3 月至 2025 年 10 月历史速率的两倍，且当前加速大部分可能归因于 Anthropic 广泛使用 AI 进行研发。[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]

METR staff conducted a high-access evaluation at Anthropic from August 12 to October 29, 2026, to assess the degree to which AI is accelerating AI R&D. The report states that Anthropic is likely improving model capabilities on well-specified tasks at around twice the historical rate from March 2024 to October 2025, with a majority of current acceleration likely attributable to Anthropic’s extensive use of AI for R&D.[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 评估于 2026 年 8 月 12 日至 10 月 29 日在 Anthropic 进行，METR 获得了前所未有的内部数据访问权限。 / The assessment was conducted at Anthropic from August 12 to October 29, 2026, with METR granted unprecedented access to internal data.[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]
- 自 2025 年 10 月以来，Anthropic 内部模型的 AECI 基准分数增长速度约为历史速率的 1.9 倍（95% 置信区间：1.5–2.8 倍）。 / Since October 2025, Anthropic internal model benchmark scores measured by AECI have grown at about 1.9 times the historical rate (95% CI: 1.5–2.8x).[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]
- 评估方有信心，观察到的加速并非由训练计算规模的加速造成。 / Evaluators are confident the acceleration was not due to an acceleration in the scaling of training compute.[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]
- METR 主观估计，当前（2026 年 10 月）约 80% 的加速可能归因于 AI，但不确定性较大。 / METR’s subjective estimate is that a majority, perhaps around 80%, of the current October 2026 acceleration is due to AI, with substantial uncertainty.[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]
- 评估未发现会在未来 12 个月内阻碍进一步能力进步的明显瓶颈，包括算法进步、训练计算、实验计算或数据限制。 / The assessment did not identify clear bottlenecks—such as limits on algorithmic progress, training compute, experiment compute, or data—that would prevent further capabilities progress in the next 12 months.[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]
- Anthropic 行使了编辑权，删除了评估中大部分最具信息量的证据，公众需更多依赖 METR 的判断。 / Anthropic exercised redaction rights, removing a majority of the most informative evidence, so the public must rely more on METR’s judgment than ideal.[^metr-2026-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessmen]
