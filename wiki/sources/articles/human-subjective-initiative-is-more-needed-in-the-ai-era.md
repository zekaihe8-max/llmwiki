# AI 时代更需要人的主观能动性

*Human Subjective Initiative Is More Needed in the AI Era*

「帖文称」AI 时代需要充分发挥人的主观能动性。过去互联网白领工作分工过细，导致大家只擅长单点问题，缺乏全局视角；而 AI 时代强调“端到端交付”，要求人既懂业务又懂技术，具备独立判断、决策和推动落地的能力。这也是当下 AI 组织转型的阵痛：许多人习惯了在专业领域做到极致，却缺乏打破边界、独立面对完整问题的训练。[^barret-ai-subjective-initiative]

The post says the AI era calls for full play of human subjective initiative. In the past, internet white-collar work was overly divided, so people excelled at isolated point problems but lacked a holistic view; now AI emphasizes “end-to-end delivery,” requiring people to understand both business and technology and to independently judge, decide, and drive implementation. This is a pain point of current AI organizational transformation: many are trained to excel within a specialty but lack the training to break boundaries and face complete problems independently.[^barret-ai-subjective-initiative]
