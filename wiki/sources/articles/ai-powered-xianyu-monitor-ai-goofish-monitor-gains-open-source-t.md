# 闲鱼 AI 监控工具 ai-goofish-monitor 开源走红

*AI-powered Xianyu monitor ai-goofish-monitor gains open-source traction*

帖文称，开源工具 ai-goofish-monitor 已让闲鱼蹲好物变成可自动化的任务，仓库星标约 1.7 万且末段仍在明显加速。该工具支持用自然语言描述需求、由 AI 生成筛选标准，并按价格、发布时间、地区等条件监控商品；还能让支持看图的模型分析商品图和详情，筛出符合要求的再通知用户。作者同时提醒，AI 推荐不等于验货，成色、真假、卖家信用最后仍需自己核实，使用也要遵守平台规则，避免高频抓取。[^ai-goofish-monitor]

The post says open-source tool ai-goofish-monitor has turned “sniping good deals on Xianyu” into an automatable task, with the repo reportedly at about 17k stars and still accelerating. It supports describing needs in natural language for AI-generated filtering criteria, monitoring by price, publish time, region, and more, and using vision-capable models to analyze product images and detail pages before notifying users. The author also cautions that AI recommendation is not inspection—condition, authenticity, and seller credit still require self-verification, and usage should comply with platform rules while avoiding high-frequency scraping.[^ai-goofish-monitor]
