# 鸣迹：观鸟手账

*Mingji: Bird Watching Handbook*

「帖文称」「鸣迹：观鸟手账」是由开发者云飞 陈发布的免费 iPhone 应用，主打听声和拍照识别鸟类，并将识别结果贴进个人观鸟本。帖文称识别默认在手机本地完成，记录仅存在用户手机及其 iCloud 中，开发者看不到；应用还支持从 eBird、iNaturalist 和中国观鸟记录中心导入记录。[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]

The post claims that “Mingji: Bird Watching Handbook” is a free iPhone app by developer Yunfei Chen that identifies birds by sound and photo and lets users stick sightings into a personal notebook. The post claims identification is performed locally on the device by default, records reside on the user’s phone and personal iCloud and are invisible to the developer, and the app supports importing records from eBird, iNaturalist, and the China Bird Record Center.[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 应用免费、专为 iPhone 设计，由开发者“云飞 陈”提供，类别为参考资料，年龄分级 4+，体积最大 1 GB。[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- The app is free, designed for iPhone, provided by developer “Yunfei Chen”, categorized as Reference, rated 4+, and up to 1 GB in size.[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- 听声识别使用 BirdNET 在手机本地完成，无需联网；拍照识别使用 BioCLIP 2，本地认不出时可选择云端识别（默认关闭，每天 10 次）。[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- Sound identification uses BirdNET locally on the phone without internet; photo identification uses BioCLIP 2, with optional cloud recognition when local recognition fails (default off, 10 times per day).[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- 图鉴收录 IOC 世界鸟类名录 15.2 中有中文名的全部现生鸟类，共 11,041 种；中国有分布的近 1,600 种附有辨识要点、叫声和国家保护等级。[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- The field guide includes all 11,041 extant bird species with Chinese names from IOC World Bird List v15.2; nearly 1,600 species distributed in China have identification keys, calls, and national protection levels.[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- 无账号、不用登录、没有广告和追踪；记录存在手机里，默认同步到用户自己的 iCloud（开发者看不到），可随时导出表格或完整备份。[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- No account or login, no ads or tracking; records are stored on the phone and synced by default to the user’s own iCloud (invisible to the developer), and can be exported as a spreadsheet or full backup at any time.[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- 支持从 eBird、iNaturalist、中国观鸟记录中心的导出文件、表格或粘贴鸟名导入记录；附近观鸟点数据来自 eBird 公开数据（经 GBIF）。[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
- Supports importing records from eBird, iNaturalist, and China Bird Record Center export files, spreadsheets, or pasted bird names; nearby birding spot data comes from eBird public data via GBIF.[^mingji-bird-watching-handbook]
