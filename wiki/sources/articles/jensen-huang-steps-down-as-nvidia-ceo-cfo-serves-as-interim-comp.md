# 黄仁勋辞去英伟达 CEO，CFO 临时接任，公司设首任董事长

*Jensen Huang Steps Down as NVIDIA CEO, CFO Serves as Interim, Company Appoints First Chairman*

帖文称，英伟达创始人黄仁勋于美东时间 11 月 12 日开盘前辞去总裁、CEO 和董事职务，立即生效，结束了 33 年的 CEO 任期；CFO Colette Kress 兼任临时 CEO，Harvey C. Jones 被任命为英伟达第一任董事长。消息公布后英伟达股价大幅下跌，市值一度跌破 4 万亿美元，且公司正面临独立调查、国会质询及集体诉讼等多重压力。[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]

The post says NVIDIA founder Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately before the November 12 market open, ending a 33-year CEO tenure; CFO Colette Kress serves as interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones is appointed the company's first chairman. Following the announcement, NVIDIA shares fell sharply, with market cap briefly dropping below $4 trillion, while the company faces an independent investigation, congressional inquiries and a shareholder class action.[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 黄仁勋 1993 年创办英伟达，当了 33 年 CEO，11 月 12 日开盘前辞去总裁、CEO 和董事职务，立即生效。[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- Jensen Huang founded NVIDIA in 1993 and served as CEO for 33 years; he resigned as president, CEO and board member effective immediately before the November 12 market open.[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- CFO Colette Kress 兼任临时 CEO 并继续负责财务，Harvey C. Jones 被任命为英伟达第一任董事长。[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- CFO Colette Kress serves as interim CEO while retaining her CFO duties; Harvey C. Jones is appointed NVIDIA's first chairman.[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- 英伟达周三收盘 173.73 美元，周四开盘一度跌约 13%，市值跌破 4 万亿美元并降至第三；Decrypt 报道盘中最低曾到 148.80 美元。[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- NVIDIA closed at $173.73 on Wednesday; Thursday's open fell about 13%, with market cap below $4 trillion and ranking third; Decrypt reported an intraday low of $148.80.[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- 董事会于 11 月 10 日宣布独立调查，聘请 Joon H. Kim 及其所在的 Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz 律师事务所；美国国会共和党籍委员会主席要求黄仁勋 11 月 18 日出席闭门简报。[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- The board announced an independent investigation on November 10, hiring Joon H. Kim and Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz; three Republican committee chairs in the U.S. Congress asked Huang to attend a closed briefing on November 18.[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- 第三季度财报照原计划 11 月 17 日发布，Colette Kress 将主持其临时 CEO 任期内的第一份财报电话会。[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
- Q3 earnings will be released as scheduled on November 17, with Kress hosting her first earnings call as interim CEO.[^nvidia-huang-ceo-resignation-interim-kress]
