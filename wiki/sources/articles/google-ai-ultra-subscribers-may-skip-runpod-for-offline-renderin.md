# 订阅了 Google AI Ultra 的人，离线渲染甚至不必再找 RunPod

*Google AI Ultra Subscribers May Skip RunPod for Offline Rendering*

帖文称，如果已经订阅 Google AI Ultra Plan，做离线渲染任务时甚至不需要花钱付给 RunPod 来跑 GPU；只要任务不需要实时地为用户提供服务，Colab 的额度就“多到用不完”。该帖由郭宇（@turingou）发布，并附有一张图片。[^google-ai-ultra-offline-render-skip-runpod]

The post says that subscribers to Google AI Ultra Plan may not need to pay RunPod for GPUs when doing offline rendering; if the task does not require real-time service for users, Colab quota is more than enough. The post was published by Guo Yu (@turingou) with an image attached.[^google-ai-ultra-offline-render-skip-runpod]
