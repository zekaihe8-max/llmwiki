# 软件如水电般随处可用，按需现场制作

*Software as Ubiquitous as Utilities, Made On Demand*

「帖文称」当软件开发的成本和时间趋近于零，软件就会变得像水电一样随处可用；每件商品都可能自带软件，每次协作都可能产生一个软件，未来人们可能不再适应软件，而是按需现场制作所需软件。[^a1ea66ede5a3cb12]

The post claims that when software development cost and time approach zero, software will become as ubiquitous as water and electricity; every product may come with software, and every collaboration may generate software, so people may no longer adapt to software but make what they need on the spot.[^a1ea66ede5a3cb12]
