# 歸藏谈谷歌 Genie 4 世界模型

*歸藏 on Google’s Genie 4 world model*

[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418)（歸藏）称谷歌发布 Genie 4 世界模型，并把它框成「世界模型领域最高的山」。他指出这次可实时生成周围原声的音频，感觉画质与物理效果大幅提升，并终于实装了在生成过程中通过提示词生成事件的能力；演示效果很好，希望 Ultra 会员早日能体验，但现在还不行。帖内引用 [@sundarpichai](https://x.com/sundarpichai) 相关帖。[^op7418-genie-4-world-model] 原帖见 <https://x.com/op7418/status/2115128743408828720>。

[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418) (歸藏) says Google released Genie 4, framing it as “the highest mountain in the world-model field.” He notes real-time generation of ambient surround audio, a felt large jump in image quality and physics, and finally shipping the ability to spawn events via prompts during generation; the demo looks excellent, and he hopes Ultra members can try it soon—but not yet. The post quotes [@sundarpichai](https://x.com/sundarpichai).[^op7418-genie-4-world-model] Post: <https://x.com/op7418/status/2115128743408828720>.
