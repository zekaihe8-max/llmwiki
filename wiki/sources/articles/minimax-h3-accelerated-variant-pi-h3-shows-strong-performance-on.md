# MiniMax H3 推理加速版 pi-H3 在 HuggingFace Demo 中表现亮眼

*MiniMax H3 Accelerated Variant pi-H3 Shows Strong Performance on HuggingFace Demo*

帖文称 MiniMax H3 的加速变体 pi-H3 可能是目前最快的 H3 推理实现之一。在 HuggingFace 的在线 Demo 中，生成 15 秒视频仅耗时约 1 分钟，整体效果尚可，仅少量帧存在逻辑问题。模型权重托管于 HuggingFace 的 Lakonik/pi-H3 仓库，并提供在线体验 Space。[^gorden-sun-pi-h3-minimax-h3-speed]

The post claims pi-H3, an accelerated variant of MiniMax H3, may be one of the fastest H3 inference implementations currently. On the HuggingFace Demo, generating a 15-second video takes about 1 minute, with decent overall quality and only minor logical artifacts in a few frames. Model weights are hosted at HuggingFace's Lakonik/pi-H3 repository with an online Space for testing.[^gorden-sun-pi-h3-minimax-h3-speed]
