# Mirage：22MB 的端侧视觉模型

*Mirage: 22MB Edge-Side Visual AI Model*

帖文称，Mirage 是一款仅 5130 万参数的超轻量多功能视觉 AI 模型，通过专门的 2 比特量化技术将体积压缩到约 22MB；在三星 S26 手机 NPU 上，单次全流程分析仅需 74 毫秒。该模型号称能瞬间看懂画面场景、圈出各类物品并生成精细轮廓、自动读取招牌或标签文字、理清物体空间关系，并按预设选项输出结构化数据，相关模型链接指向 Hugging Face 上的 Cactus-Compute 仓库。[^mirage-22mb-edge-vision-model]

The post says Mirage is an ultra-lightweight multifunctional vision AI model with only 51.3 million parameters, compressed to about 22 MB via specialized 2-bit quantization; a single full-process analysis takes just 74 ms on a Samsung S26 phone NPU. It is claimed to understand scenes instantly, detect objects and generate fine contours, read signage or label text automatically, clarify spatial relationships among objects, and output structured data based on preset options, with the model link pointing to a Cactus-Compute repository on Hugging Face.[^mirage-22mb-edge-vision-model]
