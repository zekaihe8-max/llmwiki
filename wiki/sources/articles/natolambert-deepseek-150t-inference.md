# Nathan Lambert 谈 DeepSeek 单集群日推 150T

*Nathan Lambert on DeepSeek’s 150T tokens/day on one cluster*

[@natolambert](https://x.com/natolambert) 称 DeepSeek 在一个集群上日推约 150T tokens，超过 OpenRouter、Fireworks、Together 与 BaseTen 上开放模型推理之和，并附上对各家推理量报告的重建；线程中 [@teortaxesTex](https://x.com/teortaxesTex) 回复称数字「right from the horse's mouth」，并提醒这是「a single cluster」，不知他们共有多少集群，且链到 DeepSeek DeepInfer 文档。[^natolambert-deepseek-150t-inference] 原帖见 <https://x.com/natolambert/status/2113685620941885732>。

[@natolambert](https://x.com/natolambert) says DeepSeek is serving about 150T tokens a day on one cluster—more than open-model inference on OpenRouter, Fireworks, Together, and BaseTen combined—and shares a reconstruction of inference-volume reports across those companies; in-thread [@teortaxesTex](https://x.com/teortaxesTex) replies that it is “right from the horse's mouth,” notes that is “a single cluster” (who knows how many they have), and links DeepSeek DeepInfer docs.[^natolambert-deepseek-150t-inference] Post: <https://x.com/natolambert/status/2113685620941885732>.
