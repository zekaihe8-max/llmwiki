# 5 个 20 美元以内的 AI 会员口粮盘点

*Five AI Membership Plans Under $20*

帖文称，不必硬砸 200 美元档的 AI 会员，下面 5 个套餐月费都在 20 美元以内，给出的额度和福利却远超票价。作者按主力编码、半夜后台杂活和常驻挂机三类负载，建议挑一两个搭配使用。[^five-ai-plans-under-20-dollars]

The post argues there is no need to splurge on a $200-tier AI membership: these five plans all cost under $20 a month yet, the author claims, offer quotas and perks that far exceed the price. It recommends picking one or two to pair based on three workloads: main coding, overnight background chores and an always-on host.[^five-ai-plans-under-20-dollars]
