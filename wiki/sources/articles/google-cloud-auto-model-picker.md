# Google Cloud 模型选择器 Auto

*Google Cloud model picker Auto*

[@googlecloud](https://x.com/googlecloud) 称：在模型选择器中选择 Auto 后，Gemini 会为每项任务选用最合适的模型，在 Gemini 与 Claude 之间编排，并预告以后接入更多领先的私有与开源模型，以获得更优质量并降低成本。[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker] 帖文见 <https://x.com/googlecloud/status/2114431803394048151>。

[@googlecloud](https://x.com/googlecloud) says that by selecting Auto in the model picker, Gemini runs each job on the best-fit model, orchestrating across Gemini and Claude models, and other leading private and open models in the future, to deliver optimal quality and lower cost.[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker] The post is at <https://x.com/googlecloud/status/2114431803394048151>.

## 摘要 / Summary

- Auto 模式由 Gemini 按任务选最合适的模型，而不是用户手选单一模型。[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker]
- Auto mode has Gemini pick the best-fit model per job instead of the user choosing one model by hand.[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker]
- 当前编排范围包括 Gemini 与 Claude；帖文称未来还会纳入其他领先的私有与开源模型。[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker]
- Current orchestration spans Gemini and Claude; the post says other leading private and open models will follow.[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker]
- 目标表述为最优质量与更低成本。[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker]
- The stated goals are optimal quality and lower cost.[^google-cloud-auto-model-picker]
