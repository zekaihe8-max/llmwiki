# Anthropic 分析约 20.7 万次科研会话：数据分析与写作最受欢迎，构思与采集用得最少

*Anthropic Analyzes ~207,000 Research Sessions: Data Analysis and Writing Most Popular, Ideation and Collection Least Used*

帖文称，Anthropic 于 11 月 4 日发布研究报告，统计了 2026 年 8 月 2 日至 9 月 30 日约 20.7 万次与科研相关的 Claude 会话，样本来自个人订阅用户的聊天与 Cowork 以及 Claude Code。报告显示，数据分析是最大用途，占 Claude 科研会话约三分之一、Claude Code 近一半；而科学家自认为最重要的提出想法和收集数据，AI 使用率反而最低。[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]

The post says Anthropic released a research report on November 4 analyzing about 207,000 Claude research-related sessions from August 2 to September 30, 2026, with samples from personal subscribers' chats and Cowork plus Claude Code. The report finds data analysis is the largest use case, accounting for about one-third of Claude research sessions and nearly half of Claude Code sessions, while the two activities scientists consider most important—proposing ideas and collecting data—have the lowest AI usage.[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 数据分析占 Claude 科研会话 33.7%，在 Claude Code 上占 48.1%，远高于其在科研时间中的占比约 28%。 / Data analysis accounts for 33.7% of Claude research sessions and 48.1% on Claude Code, far above its roughly 28% share of research time.[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]
- 提出想法和假设仅占 Claude 会话 4.2%、Claude Code 2.1%，低于科研时间占比 7.1%；528 位受访科学家将其评为最重要工作（35.6 分）。 / Proposing ideas and hypotheses accounts for only 4.2% of Claude sessions and 2.1% on Claude Code, below the 7.1% of research time; 528 surveyed scientists rated it the most important work (35.6 points).[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]
- 若 AI 包揽全部不用动手的步骤，统计、计算机等领域科研速度理论上可达 15 到 16 倍，但生物、化学、材料等实验学科仅 3 到 4 倍，因约 40% 科研时间花在动手步骤。 / If AI handles all non-hands-on steps, statistics, computer science and similar fields could theoretically speed up 15–16×, but experimental fields like biology, chemistry and materials only 3–4×, because about 40% of research time is spent on hands-on steps.[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]
- Claude Science 会话中 73% 由 AI 高度自主完成，普通 Claude 仅 24%；生物与生物医学任务在 Claude Science 上平均耗时 10.3 小时，普通 Claude 上 4.9 小时。 / 73% of Claude Science sessions are highly autonomous AI completion, versus 24% on regular Claude; biology and biomedical tasks average 10.3 hours on Claude Science versus 4.9 hours on regular Claude.[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]
- 约 38% 受访科学家认为 AI 数据分析已达本领域顶尖研究者水平；约 40% 预计 2030 年前 AI 能独立完成完整研究项目，2035 年前比例约 64%。 / About 38% of surveyed scientists believe AI data analysis has reached top-researcher level in their field; about 40% expect AI to independently complete full research projects before 2030, rising to about 64% before 2035.[^anthropic-claude-research-sessions]
