# Google Flow 保姆级教程｜从零做出第一条有对白的 AI 短片

*Google Flow Beginner Tutorial: Making Your First AI Short Film with Dialogue from Scratch*

帖文称，Google 套餐自带 Flow 积分，蓝哥AI 发布了一份从空项目到导出 MP4 的 Google Flow 保姆级教程，示例片名为《雨夜霓虹修表匠》，全程在电脑网页版 Flow 中完成，包含角色卡建立、两张参考图、三个带声音镜头及场景剪辑。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]

The post says Google subscriptions come with Flow credits, and Blue Brother AI published a beginner tutorial taking a Google Flow project from empty to exported MP4, with the example film "Rainy Night Neon Watchmaker," covering character card creation, two reference images, three voiced shots, and Scene timeline editing—all on the desktop web version.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 教程示例片《雨夜霓虹修表匠》共三个主镜头，每段约 8 秒，成片时长约 24 秒，分辨率 1280 × 720。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- The tutorial's example film "Rainy Night Neon Watchmaker" has three main shots of about 8 seconds each, totaling about 24 seconds at 1280 × 720 resolution.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- 角色卡包含人像、全身像和声线，示例中声线可选 Algenib、Enceladus 或 Umbriel，第三镜用 Omni Flash 1.2 的素材模式引用指定声音生成对白。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- The character card includes portrait, full-body, and voice settings; example voice options are Algenib, Enceladus, or Umbriel, and the third shot uses Omni Flash 1.2 Ingredients mode to reference a selected voice for dialogue.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- 生成模型包括 Nano Banana 2.1（图片）、Veo 3.1 Lite（前两镜视频）和 Omni Flash 1.2（第三镜带对白视频），比例统一 16:9。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- Generation models include Nano Banana 2.1 for images, Veo 3.1 Lite for the first two video shots, and Omni Flash 1.2 for the third dialogue shot, all at 16:9 aspect ratio.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- Google Flow 面向受支持地区 18 岁及以上用户，符合条件账号每日可用 50 积分，Pro 每月 1,000 积分，Ultra 5x 每月 10,000 积分；示例在 Ultra 5x 上完成，余额从 10,000 降至 9,927，净消耗 73 分。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- Google Flow is for users 18+ in supported regions; eligible accounts get 50 daily credits, Pro gets 1,000 monthly, and Ultra 5x gets 10,000 monthly; the example ran on Ultra 5x, with balance dropping from 10,000 to 9,927, a net spend of 73 credits.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- 导出需在「场景」页面建时间线，把三段视频按顺序拼接并可修剪片段，最后点右上角下载图标导出整段场景为 MP4。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- Export requires building a timeline in the Scene page, stitching the three videos in order with optional trimming, then clicking the top-right download icon to export the whole scene as MP4.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- 文章提示 Veo 3.1 Fast 与 Quality 两档将在 2026 年 12 月 5 日从 Flow 下线，且部分地区声音功能有限制。[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
- The article notes Veo 3.1 Fast and Quality will be removed from Flow on December 5, 2026, and voice features are limited in some regions.[^google-flow-ai-short-film-tutorial]
