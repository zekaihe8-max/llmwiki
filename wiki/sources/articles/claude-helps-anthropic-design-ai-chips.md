# Claude 帮 Anthropic 造芯

*Claude Helps Anthropic Design AI Chips*

帖文称，Anthropic 在冲刺 IPO 期间向投资者展示 Claude 已用于其 15 人自研芯片团队的实际工程设计，部分工作耗时低于对英伟达、AMD 等传统厂商流程的估算；公司还暗示未来可能向其他半导体企业出售芯片设计工具，切入百亿美元级市场，Synopsys 与 Cadence 股价曾因此盘中下跌约 4%。OpenAI 亦在该领域布局，曾用自家模型辅助 Jalapeño 芯片研发并与 Synopsys 签订多年协议。[^anthropic-claude-chip-design]

The post claims Anthropic is showing IPO investors that Claude is already used in actual engineering design by its 15-person in-house chip team, with some tasks taking less time than estimated for traditional vendors such as NVIDIA and AMD; the company also hints it may sell chip design tools to other semiconductor firms, entering a tens-of-billions market, while Synopsys and Cadence shares once fell about 4% intraday. OpenAI is also active in this area, having used its own model to assist Jalapeño chip development and signed a multi-year agreement with Synopsys.[^anthropic-claude-chip-design]
