# 腾讯混元 Hy4 接入 WorkBuddy 后推理集群扩容 10 倍

*Tencent Hunyuan Hy4 inference cluster expanded 10x after WorkBuddy integration*

帖文称，腾讯混元与 WorkBuddy 联合项目团队宣布 Hy4 正式版自 11 月 4 日接入 WorkBuddy 后，上线首日大量用户触及使用额度并出现任务排队；团队已紧急将 Hy4 推理集群扩容 10 倍并提高免费体验额度，但受高端算力总量和高峰时段集中请求限制，部分时段仍可能排队或限额，建议用户切换至 Hy3 或在晚间错峰使用，Hy3 免费体验期延长至 11 月 15 日 23:59；8 月 Hy4 Preview 首发时 WorkBuddy 亦曾因调用激增紧急扩容并出现类似问题。[^hy4-workbuddy-inference-expansion]

The post states that Tencent Hunyuan and WorkBuddy's joint project team announced that after Hy4 official version integrated with WorkBuddy on November 4, many users hit usage limits and task queuing on launch day; the team urgently expanded Hy4's inference cluster 10x and increased free experience quota, but due to limited high-end compute capacity and concentrated peak-hour requests, some periods may still queue or limit usage, suggesting users switch to Hy3 or use off-peak evenings; Hy3's free trial on WorkBuddy is extended to November 15 23:59; during Hy4 Preview launch in August, WorkBuddy also emergency expanded due to call surge and experienced similar issues.[^hy4-workbuddy-inference-expansion]
