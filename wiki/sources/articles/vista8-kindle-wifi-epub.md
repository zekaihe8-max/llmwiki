# 向阳乔木谈 WiFi 传 epub 到越狱 Kindle

*vista8 on WiFi-sending epub to a jailbroken Kindle*

[@vista8](https://x.com/vista8)（向阳乔木）称给 Qiaomu Reader Obsidian 插件加了功能：右键通过 WiFi 发送 epub 给越狱后的 Kindle；再加上自用 ebook MCP，可实现一句话找书传 Kindle；并称亚马逊退出中国后，「泡面伴侣」焕发新生机。[^vista8-kindle-wifi-epub] 原帖见 <https://x.com/vista8/status/2113936883394937223>。

[@vista8](https://x.com/vista8) (向阳乔木) says he added a Qiaomu Reader Obsidian plugin feature: right-click to send an epub over WiFi to a jailbroken Kindle; with a personal ebook MCP, one sentence finds a book and sends it to the Kindle; and after Amazon left China, the “instant-noodle companion” (泡面伴侣) Kindle finds new life.[^vista8-kindle-wifi-epub] Post: <https://x.com/vista8/status/2113936883394937223>.
