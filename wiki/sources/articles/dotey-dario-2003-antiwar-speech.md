- 2003-03-10，帕萨迪纳市议会特别会议；Dario 自称加州理工大二学生，宣读上周四已有约 400、当天近 600 名师生签名的反战请愿书。[^dotey-dario-2003-antiwar-speech]
- On 10 March 2003 at a Pasadena City Council special meeting, Dario identified as a Caltech sophomore and read an anti-war petition that had about 400 Caltech signatures the prior Thursday and nearly 600 that day.[^dotey-dario-2003-antiwar-speech]
- 请愿书一句：「我们创造推动这个国家前进的技术，看到它被不加区分地用于战争，深感痛心。」收束请勿扼杀和平而激动人心的科学。[^dotey-dario-2003-antiwar-speech]
- One petition line: creating the technology that advances the country and feeling deep sorrow seeing it used indiscriminately for war; he closed asking not to kill peaceful, exciting science.[^dotey-dario-2003-antiwar-speech]
- 10 天后伊拉克战争爆发；18 年后创办 Anthropic。对照：20 岁忧技术被拿去打仗，现在整天忧 AI 被利用。[^dotey-dario-2003-antiwar-speech]
- The Iraq War began 10 days later; 18 years later he co-founded Anthropic. Contrast: at 20, worry that tech would be used for war; now, day-to-day worry that AI will be misused.[^dotey-dario-2003-antiwar-speech]
