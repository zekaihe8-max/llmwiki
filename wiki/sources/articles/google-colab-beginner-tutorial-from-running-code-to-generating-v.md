## 摘要 / Summary

- Colab是Google官方网页版云端运行环境，笔记本保存在Google Drive，但云端电脑是临时分配，/content目录下的文件关机后会清空[^0xlangeai-google-colab-tutorial-2126239508475867]
- Colab is Google's official web-based cloud runtime; notebooks are saved to Google Drive, but cloud machines are temporary and /content files are cleared after shutdown[^0xlangeai-google-colab-tutorial-2126239508475867]
- 免费版提供免费CPU和动态基础GPU（如T4），付费路径包括Colab Pro/Pro+及按量算力包，Google AI Pro/Ultra订阅自动包含每月200至2000分不等的Colab算力分[^0xlangeai-google-colab-tutorial-2126239508475867]
- Free tier offers free CPU and dynamic basic GPU such as T4; paid paths include Colab Pro/Pro+ and pay-as-you-go compute packs, while Google AI Pro/Ultra subscriptions automatically include 200 to 2000 monthly Colab compute units[^0xlangeai-google-colab-tutorial-2126239508475867]
