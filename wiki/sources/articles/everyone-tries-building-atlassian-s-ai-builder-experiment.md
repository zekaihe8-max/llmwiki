# 全员试法

*Everyone Tries Building: Atlassian's AI Builder Experiment*

Lenny Rachitsky 在 X 上发帖称，Atlassian 曾尝试组建只有“AI Builder”、没有产品经理或设计师的团队，初期效率显著提升，但约一个月后所有人都回到了原岗位；一位产品经理解释称，团队瓶颈不在于产出，而在于方向。该帖还引用了与 Atlassian AI 与产品工艺负责人 Sherif Mansour 的对话，涉及产品角色不应合并、更多招聘初级人才、三类产品经理原型、五大产品经理迷思以及设计价值日益凸显等议题。[^ccdd36c605cceec6]

Lenny Rachitsky posted on X that Atlassian experimented with teams composed solely of “AI builders,” with no product manager or designer roles; initial speed was impressive, but within a month everyone reverted to their previous jobs. A PM explained that throughput was not the problem—direction was. The post also references a conversation with Sherif Mansour, Head of AI and Product Craft at Atlassian, covering why product roles are not merging, increased junior hiring, three PM archetypes, five PM myths, and why design matters more than ever.[^ccdd36c605cceec6]
