# No AI Slop Manifesto：人类判断不可被 AI 取代

*No AI Slop Manifesto: Human Judgment Cannot Be Replaced by AI*

「帖文称」@dexhorthy 在 @aiDotEngineer 旧金山代码峰会上与多位演讲者和参会者共同发布了《No AI Slop Manifesto》。该宣言已登上 Hacker News 首页，获得 300 多个签名，签署者来自 Baseten、SpaceX、OpenAI、PostHog、Shopify、Glean、ZoomInfo 等公司，以及 swyx、Kent C. Dodds 等知名开发者。帖文引用的观点包括：AI 不能取代人类判断，只能放大判断（或缺陷）；出品方需对所交付内容的意图、品味和后果负责；无论模型多强，审查计划、设计或结果始终有价值，否则所有人都会得到相同输出。可在 slopmanifesto dot com 阅读并签名。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]

The post says @dexhorthy published the 'No AI Slop Manifesto' at the @aiDotEngineer Code Summit in SF, written with many speakers and attendees. It reached Hacker News' front page and has 300+ signatures from people at Baseten, SpaceX, OpenAI, PostHog, Shopify, Glean, ZoomInfo, and builders such as swyx, Kent C. Dodds, and others. Quoted points include: AI cannot replace human judgment, only amplify it; shippers own the intent, taste, and consequences of what they ship; and there is always alpha in reviewing a plan, design, or result. Readers can read and sign at slopmanifesto dot com.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
