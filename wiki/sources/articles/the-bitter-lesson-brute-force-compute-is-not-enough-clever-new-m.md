# 《苦涩的教训》常被误读为“堆算力就够了”，Sutton 称仍需巧妙新方法

*The Bitter Lesson: Brute-force compute is not enough; clever new methods are still needed*

帖文称，许多大语言模型信徒把《苦涩的教训》当作圣经，认为无脑堆算力、堆数据就足以实现 AGI，无需新算法。文章引用 Richard Sutton 的推文指出，光靠暴力计算不足以实现真正智能，需要巧妙的新方法，AI 科学远未终结；Prashant Rai 进一步澄清，该教训真正反对的是把人类小聪明硬编码进系统，而非反对算法与架构层面的精巧创新。[^dotey-bitter-lesson-not-just-compute]

The post says many LLM believers treat The Bitter Lesson as a scripture for compute-and-data-only scaling to AGI. It cites Richard Sutton's tweet that brute-force computation is not enough for real intelligence, clever new methods are needed, and AI science is far from finished; Prashant Rai further clarifies that the lesson targets hardcoding human ingenuity into systems, not algorithmic or architectural innovation.[^dotey-bitter-lesson-not-just-compute]
