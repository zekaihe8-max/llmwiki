# GR00T 2：可在机器人板载计算上运行的开放 System One 模型

*GR00T 2: Open System One Robotics Model Running Onboard Robot Compute*

「帖文称」GR00T 2 是团队最先进、开放的机器人 System One 模型，可完全在机器人板载计算上运行，无需连线，用户拥有全部权重。它支持 20 步以上、5 分钟以上的长时程任务，例如化学实验或模型飞机组装；通过 X-Adapt 从人类可穿戴设备和仿真等简易数据源零样本迁移至多种真实机器人；并与前沿 Agent 原生集成，实现物理推理、错误恢复与安全监控。帖文还称将开放模型权重、X-Adapt 配方、Agent 框架、仿真引擎、标注引擎、硬件设计及 10 万小时训练数据，其中 X-Adapt 配方将于 12 月发布。[^gr00t-2]

The post says GR00T 2 is the team's most advanced, open System One model for robotics, running entirely on onboard robot compute without tethering, with all weights owned by the user. It supports long-horizon tasks of 20+ steps and 5+ minutes such as chemistry experiments or model airplane assembly; X-Adapt enables zero-shot transfer from easy data sources like human wearables and simulation to diverse real robots; and Unified Cognition natively integrates frontier agents for physical reasoning, error recovery, and safety monitoring. The post also announces release of model weights, X-Adapt recipes (December), agentic framework, simulation engine, annotation engine, hardware design, and 100K hours of training data.[^gr00t-2]
