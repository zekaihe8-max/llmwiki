# 本地部署 Minimax H3 制作数字人，视频成本降至每分钟不到5元

*Local Minimax H3 Deployment for Digital Humans Cuts Video Cost to Under 5 Yuan per Minute*

「帖文称」@Pluvio9yte 本地部署 Minimax H3 制作数字人，将成本从云平台 Seedacne 的每分钟几十至上百元压至每秒0.8元、一分钟成片视频不到5元（租赁40系）；声线由 Index TTS2 复刻并经 Fish Audio 调整情绪固化参数后重新复刻，人物由 Midjourney 融合多位美女网红生图，视频由本地 Minimax H3 制作，作者称下周公开全部流程。[^pluvio9yte-minimax-h3-digital-human]

The post claims @Pluvio9yte locally deployed Minimax H3 to create a digital human, cutting cost from cloud platform Seedacne's dozens to hundreds of yuan per minute to 0.8 yuan per second and under 5 yuan per minute of finished video (renting 40-series GPU); voice is cloned via Index TTS2 with Fish Audio emotion parameters fixed, the character is a Midjourney fusion of multiple beauty influencer images, and video is produced by local Minimax H3, with the full process to be published next week.[^pluvio9yte-minimax-h3-digital-human]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 本地部署 Minimax H3 制作数字人，成本压到每秒0.8元，一分钟成片视频不到5元（租赁40系）。[^pluvio9yte-minimax-h3-digital-human]
- Locally deployed Minimax H3 for digital humans, cost reduced to 0.8 yuan per second, under 5 yuan per minute of finished video (renting 40-series GPU).[^pluvio9yte-minimax-h3-digital-human]
