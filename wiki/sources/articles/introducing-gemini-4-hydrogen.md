# 推出 Gemini 4 Hydrogen：面向日常代理工作流的高效模型

*Introducing Gemini 4 Hydrogen*

帖文称，Google 于 2026 年 11 月 12 日推出 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，将其定位为面向日常编码和代理工作流的“日常驱动”模型，与主打深度长时推理的 Gemini 4 Carbon 和 Gemini 4 Argon 形成互补。Hydrogen 面向 Google AI Studio、Gemini API、Gemini 企业版、Gemini 应用、Google Search 以及 Google Antigravity 中的付费 AI Pro 和 Ultra 订阅者开放，并在默认高思考级别的人工智能分析指数上超过 Argon（51 对 50），最高 xhigh 设置可达 54。其 100 万令牌上下文窗口定价固定，输入每百万令牌 1.50 美元、输出每百万令牌 7.50 美元，并提供低、中、高（默认）及新增 xhigh 四种思考级别。[^gemini-4-hydrogen]

Google announced Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, 2026, positioning it as a “daily driver” model for everyday coding and agentic workflows, complementing Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon which focus on deep, long-horizon reasoning. Hydrogen is available across Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Google Search, and paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity. It surpasses Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default high thinking level (51 vs. 50), reaching 54 at xhigh, with flat pricing of $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across its 1 million-token context window, and four thinking levels: low, medium, high (default), and xhigh.[^gemini-4-hydrogen]
