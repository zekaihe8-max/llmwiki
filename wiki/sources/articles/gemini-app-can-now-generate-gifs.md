# Gemini App 也能生成 GIF 了

*Gemini App Can Now Generate GIFs*

帖文称，Gemini APP 现已可以生成动图 GIF，使用的方案是生成视频然后转成 GIF 图，效果会比 GPT 好非常非常多，动作会很连贯不跳帧。Gemini 官方账号也宣布，借助最新视频模型 Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash，用户可以创建任意想象中的 GIF，实现每一帧的流畅动作与一致画质。[^gemini-app-gif-generation]

A post claims Gemini App can now generate animated GIFs by generating video first and then converting it to GIF, with results said to be far better than GPT and motion that is coherent without frame skipping. The official Gemini account also announced the feature, powered by the latest video model Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, for smooth motion and consistent quality across every frame.[^gemini-app-gif-generation]
