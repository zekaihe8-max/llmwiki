# 他把斯坦福新书做成 Prompt，先给自己写出一本《个人意义设计手册》

*He turned Stanford's new book into a Prompt, first writing a "Personal Meaning Design Manual" for himself*

「帖文称」数字生命卡兹克（@Khazix0918）把斯坦福《人生设计课》作者比尔·博内特与戴夫·伊万斯的新书《人生意义设计课》做成了一个可与 AI 对话的 Prompt，并以自身为例演示了如何生成《个人意义设计手册》。[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]

The post says Digital Life Kha'Zix (@Khazix0918) turned the new book by Bill Burnett and Dave Evans, authors of Stanford's "Designing Your Life", into a conversational AI Prompt, and demonstrated with himself how to generate a "Personal Meaning Design Manual."[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称，作者把斯坦福《人生意义设计课》中的互动思考与方法做成了一个名为“人生意义设计师”的 AI Prompt。[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- The post says the author turned the interactive thinking and methods in Stanford's "Designing Meaning in Life" into an AI Prompt called "Life Meaning Designer."[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- 该 Prompt 要求 AI 分四个阶段、问六到九个问题，与用户对话约 20 至 30 分钟，最后输出 5000 至 7000 字的《个人意义设计手册》。[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- The Prompt requires the AI to go through four stages, ask six to nine questions, converse with the user for about 20 to 30 minutes, and finally output a 5,000 to 7,000-word "Personal Meaning Design Manual."[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- 文中列出意义的四个来源：惊奇、连贯一致、心流、一起变好的关系。[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- The article lists four sources of meaning: surprise, coherence, flow, and relationships that grow together.[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- 作者以自身对话为例，展示了手册中对思维误区、罗盘价值观、焦点问题和精心营造的意义时刻的提炼。[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- The author used his own conversation as an example to show the manual's extraction of cognitive biases, compass values, focal questions, and carefully designed meaning moments.[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- 帖文推荐使用 ChatGPT 网页版 GPT-6.2（思考强度调至 Extra High），也提到 Gemini 或 Claude 可用。[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
- The post recommends using ChatGPT web version GPT-6.2 with thinking intensity set to Extra High, and also mentions Gemini or Claude as usable alternatives.[^fad31c4e7e2c4c48]
