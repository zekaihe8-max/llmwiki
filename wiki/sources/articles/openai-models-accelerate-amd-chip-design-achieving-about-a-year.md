# OpenAI 模型助力 AMD 芯片设计，五个月实现约一年进度

*OpenAI models accelerate AMD chip design, achieving about a year of progress in five months*

「帖文称」Greg Brockman 发文表示，OpenAI 模型在不到五个月内为 AMD 芯片设计带来了大约一年的额外进展，部分成果已用于最近流片的设计，被描述为芯片设计前沿的前所未有结果；与 AMD 合作期间，模型在不到五个月内识别出旗舰 GPU IP 最高 14% 的 PPA 改进，且这些改进建立在 AMD 工程师已花费多年优化的设计之上；同一方法还帮助改进了 OpenAI 首款定制推理芯片 Jalapeño，相关收益已得到验证。[^gdb-openai-amd-chip-design-progress]

Greg Brockman's post states that OpenAI models brought roughly an additional year of progress to AMD chip design in less than five months, with some results included in a recently taped-out design described as unprecedented at the frontier of chip design; working with AMD, the models identified up to 14% PPA improvement on flagship GPU IP in under five months, building on designs AMD engineers had already spent years optimizing; the same approach also helped improve Jalapeño, OpenAI's first custom inference chip, with gains verified.[^gdb-openai-amd-chip-design-progress]
