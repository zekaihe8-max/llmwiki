# 让 AI 写界面时守住你的设计规范

*Keep Your Design Specs Intact When Letting AI Write Interfaces*

「帖文称」StyleSeed 能把字体层级、色板、间距、组件约束等设计决定保存为项目规范文件，让 AI 助手在生成界面前先读规则，渲染后再回头检查打分；已有选择会被保留，换会话或换人也不会被推翻，适合已有明确视觉标准的团队和个人项目。[^styleseed]

StyleSeed helps teams and individuals lock design decisions into project-level rules that AI coding assistants read before generating interfaces, then verify rendered results against those specs. Existing choices persist across sessions and contributors, aiming to keep multiple pages consistent without relying on individual memory.[^styleseed]
