# 宝玉解读：Google 开放 Gemini 4 Carbon，普通用户需订 Ultra 月费 99.99 美元起

*Bao Yu interprets: Google opens Gemini 4 Carbon; ordinary users need Google AI Ultra from $99.99/month*

帖文称，Google 于 11 月 10 日正式开放 Gemini 4 Carbon 给付费开发者、企业和 Google AI Ultra 订阅用户，普通用户需订 Ultra 才能在 Gemini App 等使用，月费 99.99 美元起；API 模型名 gemini-4-carbon，无免费层，11 月 30 日前引入价后 12 月 1 日翻倍。文中还介绍了 Antigravity 预览的 /swarm 命令，并对比了 Carbon 与 Argon、Claude Fable 5.2、GPT-6 Astra 在价格与评测上的表现。[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]

The post says Google officially opened Gemini 4 Carbon on November 10 to paying developers, enterprises, and Google AI Ultra subscribers; ordinary users need Ultra to use it in Gemini App etc., starting at $99.99/month. The API model name is gemini-4-carbon, with no free tier, introductory pricing until Nov 30 then doubling on Dec 1. It also introduces Antigravity's /swarm command and compares Carbon with Argon, Claude Fable 5.2, and GPT-6 Astra on price and benchmarks.[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 普通用户需订阅 Google AI Ultra，月费 99.99 美元（Pro 用量 5 倍）或 199.99 美元（20 倍）才能使用 Carbon；Pro 用户不可用，最强仍是 Argon。[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- Ordinary users must subscribe to Google AI Ultra at $99.99/month (5x Pro usage) or $199.99/month (20x) to use Carbon; Pro users cannot use it, strongest remains Argon.[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- 开发者付费 API 调用 gemini-4-carbon，无免费层；11 月 30 日前引入价每百万 Token 输入 2 美元、输出 10 美元，12 月 1 日起翻倍至 4 美元和 20 美元。[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- Developer paid API calls gemini-4-carbon, no free tier; introductory price before Nov 30 is $2 input / $10 output per million tokens, doubling to $4 and $20 from Dec 1.[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- 官方诚实度测试中 Carbon 通过率 92%，Argon 70%；碰到缺失实验数据时 98% 不会自行编造。[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- In official honesty tests Carbon passed 92% vs Argon 70%; with missing experimental data it does not fabricate 98% of the time.[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- Antigravity 预览 /swarm 命令，把任务拆分给命名 agent 并配验证员，所有 agent 共享留言板；Pro 和 Ultra 用户均可用，但 Pro 选不到 Carbon，每个 agent 单独消耗额度。[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- Antigravity previews /swarm command, splitting tasks to named agents with validators, all agents share a message board; Pro and Ultra users can use it, but Pro cannot select Carbon; each agent consumes quota separately.[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- 综合评测方面，Artificial Analysis 综合榜两周前 Carbon 排第一，昨日 Claude Fable 5.2 发布后榜首易主；Zapier AutomationBench 上 Carbon High 档 53.9%，单任务 2.12 美元，优于 Fable 5.2 Max 档 51.5% 且单任务 4.64 美元。[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
- Artificial Analysis comprehensive ranking had Carbon first two weeks ago, but Claude Fable 5.2 took the lead after release; on Zapier AutomationBench Carbon High tier 53.9% at $2.12 per task, better than Fable 5.2 Max tier 51.5% at $4.64 per task.[^gemini-4-carbon-dotey]
