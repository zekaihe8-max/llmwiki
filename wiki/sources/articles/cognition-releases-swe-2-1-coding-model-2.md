# Cognition 发布 SWE-2.1 编程模型

*Cognition releases SWE-2.1 coding model*

「帖文称」Cognition 发布其最新编程模型 SWE-2.1，该模型在 Kimi K3 上通过纯强化学习训练，在多项基准测试中较 SWE-2 取得显著提升。SWE-2.1 将在未来两周内免费向 Devin Pro、Max 和 Teams 用户开放。[^cognition-swe-2-1]

According to the post, Cognition has introduced SWE-2.1, its latest coding model trained with pure reinforcement learning on Kimi K3. The post claims large gains over SWE-2 across a wide range of benchmarks, and SWE-2.1 is free for all Devin Pro, Max, and Teams users for the next two weeks.[^cognition-swe-2-1]
