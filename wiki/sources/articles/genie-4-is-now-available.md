# Genie 4 现已推出

*Genie 4 is now available*

「帖文称」Google 正面向符合条件的 Google AI Ultra 订阅者推出新的 Project Genie 体验，首次开放 Genie 4 世界模型，并配套 Genie 3 升级、Project Genie 创作智能体与社区画廊，用户可在浏览器中通过自然语言生成、编辑并分享交互式数字世界。[^a261d2eee241a886]

Google says it is rolling out a new Project Genie experience to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, opening first-time access to the Genie 4 world model alongside Genie 3 upgrades, the Project Genie creation agent, and a community gallery for generating, editing, and sharing interactive digital worlds in the browser.[^a261d2eee241a886]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Genie 4 允许用户在导航世界时实时输入提示词，改变天气、添加角色或重定向动作，并配备随场景变化的原生音频。[^a261d2eee241a886]
- Genie 4 lets users prompt mid-session to change weather, add characters, or redirect action while navigating, with real-time native audio that adapts to the scene.[^a261d2eee241a886]
- Genie 3 更新后支持每次生成最长 3 分钟（此前为 60 秒）、世界链接、360 度全景图基准以及实时提示词事件。[^a261d2eee241a886]
- Updated Genie 3 supports up to 3 minutes per generation (up from 60 seconds), world chaining, 360-degree panorama grounding, and real-time promptable events.[^a261d2eee241a886]
- 新的 Project Genie 智能体可通过自然语言对话协调 Google 多模态模型，创建世界初始视觉风格并搭配自定义背景音乐。[^a261d2eee241a886]
- The new Project Genie agent orchestrates Google's multimodal models through natural-language conversation to create a world's signature visuals and custom background scores.[^a261d2eee241a886]
- 所有 Project Genie 生成的视频和音频均包含 SynthID 水印，且所有模型输入输出都经过安全审查。[^a261d2eee241a886]
- All video and audio generated in Project Genie includes SynthID watermarking, and all model inputs and outputs go through safety screenings.[^a261d2eee241a886]
- 符合条件的 Google AI Ultra 订阅者可立即试用，Google AI Pro 会员可在同一页面加入候补名单。[^a261d2eee241a886]
- Eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers can try it today, while Google AI Pro members can join the waitlist on the same page.[^a261d2eee241a886]
