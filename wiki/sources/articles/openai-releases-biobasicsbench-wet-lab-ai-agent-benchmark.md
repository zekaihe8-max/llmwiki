# OpenAI 发布 BioBasicsBench 湿实验 AI 智能体评测基准

*OpenAI Releases BioBasicsBench Wet-Lab AI Agent Benchmark*

帖文称，OpenAI 与酶设计生物技术公司 Ligo 联合开发 BioBasicsBench，这是衡量 AI 智能体处理湿实验室日常分析的新方法。基准汲取 100 项来自真实实验的任务，考察模型能否解读原始实验数据、识别错误，并判断证据是否不足以支持结论。OpenAI 表示，BioBasicsBench 可显示模型需要改进的地方，并帮助追踪进步对科学进展、生物安全与生物安保的意义。[^openai-biobasicsbench]

The post says OpenAI developed BioBasicsBench with Ligo, a biotech company focused on enzyme design, as a new way to measure how AI agents handle routine wet-lab science analyses. It draws on 100 tasks from real experiments to test whether agents can interpret raw lab data, identify errors, and recognize when evidence does not support a conclusion. OpenAI says BioBasicsBench shows where models need improvement and helps track what gains mean for scientific progress, biosafety, and biosecurity.[^openai-biobasicsbench]
