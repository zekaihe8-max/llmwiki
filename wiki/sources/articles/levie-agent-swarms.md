*Levie · Agent Swarms*

「帖文称」AI 的演进大致经历了从聊天到智能体、再到智能体蜂群的三个阶段；蜂群具有目标导向，会寻找任何路径完成项目，且在自包含的数字环境中创造价值的任务上，可能比任何其他方式更快完成工作。Levie 还提到，蜂群将在游戏开发、编码、交易、生命科学、IT 自动化、网络安全、数学突破、动画等领域发挥重要作用，并催生构建 Agent 运行工具链、应用 AI 产品与基础设施的机会，以及全新的 FDE 工作领域。[^levie-agent-swarms]

The post says AI has roughly progressed from chat to agents to agent swarms, with swarms being goal-seeking and able to find any route to project completion, potentially completing value-creating tasks in self-contained digital environments faster than any other approach. Levie also highlights opportunities in game development, coding, trading, life sciences, IT automation, cyber, math, animation, and beyond, plus new harnesses, applied AI products, infrastructure, and an entirely new field of FDE work.[^levie-agent-swarms]
