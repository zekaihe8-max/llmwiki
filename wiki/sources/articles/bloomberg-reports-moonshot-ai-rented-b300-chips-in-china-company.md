# 月之暗面被曝国内租用 B300，公司随即否认

*Bloomberg reports Moonshot AI rented B300 chips in China, company denies*

帖文称彭博找到两份提交给监管数据库的融资合同，显示 32 台搭载英伟达 B300 芯片的美超微服务器位于甘肃庆阳，总估值 4.32 亿元，合同签署于 8 月，服务器由凌美芯（北京）科技持有并向北京中关村银行抵押贷款，抵押物还包括一份涉及 3.456 亿元预期收入的月之暗面算力服务合同。彭博求证后相关文件不再出现月之暗面名字；月之暗面否认参与或授权相关融资担保，称与凌美芯无业务关系，英伟达和美超微也称涉及企业并非其客户。同一帖文还提到月之暗面正在欧洲洽谈租用至少 8000 块 Blackwell GPU。[^moonshot-ai-b300-domestic-rental-denial]

The post says Bloomberg found two financing contracts filed with a regulatory database showing 32 Supermicro servers equipped with NVIDIA B300 chips located in Qingyang, Gansu, valued at 432 million yuan total, signed in August, held by Lingmeixin (Beijing) Technology and mortgaged to Beijing Zhongguancun Bank; collateral also included a computing service contract with Moonshot AI involving 345.6 million yuan in expected revenue. After Bloomberg sought confirmation, Moonshot AI's name disappeared from the database; Moonshot AI denies participating in or authorizing related financing and guarantees, says it has no business relationship with Lingmeixin, and NVIDIA and Supermicro say the companies involved are not their customers. The same post also mentions Moonshot AI is negotiating in Europe to rent at least 8,000 Blackwell GPUs.[^moonshot-ai-b300-domestic-rental-denial]
