# 电话申请美国公司 EIN 的坑

*Pitfalls of Applying for US Company EIN by Phone*

帖文称，电话申请美国公司 EIN 需注意几个坑：若使用 Muse、Grok Bot 等非顶级模型，应先确认其提供的电话号码等信息是否正确；建议全程录音并制作会议纪要，防止听错或听漏；EIN 国际申请专线不提供中文服务，英文口语和听力不佳者可找中文代办，但全程由国税局雇员以纯美国口音服务。结束前应让对方复述 EIN，并确认 CP 575 信件的邮寄地址；作者曾遇到地址填错、确认后纠正的情况。[^f250f55d9d573a6f]

The post claims that when applying for a US company EIN by phone, one should note several pitfalls: if using non-top-tier models such as Muse or Grok Bot, verify the phone numbers and other information they provide are correct; it is recommended to record the entire call and create meeting minutes to prevent mishearing or missing content; the EIN international application hotline does not provide Chinese service, so those with poor English speaking and listening can hire a Chinese agent, but the entire call is handled by IRS employees with pure American accents. Before ending, ask the other party to repeat the EIN and confirm the mailing address for the CP 575 letter; the author once encountered an incorrect address that was corrected after confirmation.[^f250f55d9d573a6f]
