# Gemini 4 Carbon 现已推出

*Gemini 4 Carbon is now available*

「帖文称」，Google 于 2026 年 11 月 10 日宣布其最强前沿模型 Gemini 4 Carbon 现已面向开发者、企业、科学家和消费者开放。帖文称 Carbon 在网络安全能力和长周期智能体工作流方面处于前沿，适用于复杂多步编程与调试、开放式科学研究等考验模型智能极限的任务；Gemini 4 Argon 则仍是大多数软件工程和知识工作的理想选择。[^gemini-4-carbon-now-available]

Google announced on November 10, 2026 that its strongest frontier model, Gemini 4 Carbon, is now available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers. The post says Carbon delivers frontier performance in cybersecurity capabilities and long-horizon agentic workflows, suited for tasks that test the limits of model intelligence such as complex multi-step coding and debugging and open-ended scientific research, while Gemini 4 Argon remains the ideal choice for most software engineering and knowledge work.[^gemini-4-carbon-now-available]
