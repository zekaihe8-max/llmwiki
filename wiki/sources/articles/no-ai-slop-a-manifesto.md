# No AI Slop：反 AI 滥制内容宣言

*No AI Slop: A Manifesto*

「帖文称」，由 AIE Code Summit 2026 的演讲者与与会者共同撰写的《No AI Slop：反 AI 滥制内容宣言》，以 2001 年敏捷宣言为范式，提出“结果重于产出”“理解重于速度”等价值观，并定义 slop 为“生产投入低于消费者理解成本”的内容，呼吁在 AI 辅助编写代码、文档与消息时保留人类判断与审核责任。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]

Written by speakers and attendees of AIE Code Summit 2026, the "No AI Slop: A Manifesto" takes the 2001 Agile Manifesto as a model, proposing values such as outcomes over output and understanding over speed, defining slop as output produced with less effort than the consumer must spend understanding it, and calling for human judgment and review in AI-assisted writing of code, docs, and messages.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 宣言由 AIE Code Summit 2026 的演讲者和与会者撰写，灵感来源于 2001 年在 Utah Snowbird 产生的敏捷宣言。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- The manifesto was written by speakers and attendees of AIE Code Summit 2026, inspired by the 2001 Agile Manifesto created at Snowbird, Utah.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 文中列出五条价值观：结果重于产出、理解重于速度、自身思考重于模型初稿、更好工作重于更轻松工作、改进系统重于修补产出。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- It lists five values: outcomes over output, understanding over speed, our own thinking over a model's first draft, better work over easier work, and improving the system over patching the output.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- Slop 被定义为生产投入少于要求消费者理解成本的内容，且 AI 输出未经责任把关即传递即构成 slop。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- Slop is defined as putting less effort into producing something than asking the consumer to spend understanding it, and passing AI output without taking responsibility for quality or reader experience counts as slop.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 宣言主张在代码、文档与消息中保留人类判断，代理撰写内容发出前应注明并检查关键部分，错误出现两次则应通过系统与护栏加以改进。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- It asserts that human judgment should be retained in code, docs, and messages; agent-written content should be disclosed and key parts checked before sending; when a mistake appears twice, the system and guardrails should be improved.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 文中提到“一键提示与 vibe coding 是高杠杆工具”，但 slop 在破坏有用产品、可维护系统或节省时间与精力的结果时才是坏的。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- It notes that one-shot prompts and vibe coding are high-leverage tools, but slop is bad only when it undermines outcomes such as a useful product, maintainable system, or saved time and energy.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 宣言已有 352 位签署者，包括 Dex Horthy、Philip Kiely、Kent C. Dodds 等，并接受通过 Gist 评论签名。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- The manifesto has 352 signatories including Dex Horthy, Philip Kiely, and Kent C. Dodds, and accepts signatures via Gist comments.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
