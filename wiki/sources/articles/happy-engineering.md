*Happy Engineering*

Happy Engineering 是一个面向 AI 编程的开源工具仓库，主打“让制作事物变得有趣”，支持 Claude、Codex、Grok、Gemini 等多种模型，并强调复用现有订阅、本地运行、端到端加密移动端同步与原生多人协作。[^happy-engineering]

Happy Engineering is an open-source repository for AI programming tools, aiming to make building things fun.
