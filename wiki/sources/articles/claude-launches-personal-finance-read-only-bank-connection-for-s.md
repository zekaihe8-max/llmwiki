# Claude 推出个人理财功能，连银行账户只读查收支

*Claude launches Personal Finance, read-only bank connection for spending queries*

「帖文称」Anthropic 于 11 月 5 日推出 Claude Personal Finance 测试版，美国 Pro、Max、Ultra 个人订阅用户可通过 Plaid 连接银行卡与信用卡，以自然语言查询余额和交易；功能仅限读取，不能转账、管理资金或投资，免费版、团队版与企业版暂不提供，且仅支持美国银行。[^4cc325534e4981d3]

The post says Anthropic launched Claude Personal Finance in beta on November 5, allowing US Pro, Max, and Ultra individual subscribers to connect bank cards and credit cards via Plaid and query balances and transactions in natural language; the feature is read-only and cannot transfer, manage, or invest money, and free, Team, and Enterprise plans are not included, with support limited to US banks.[^4cc325534e4981d3]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Claude 个人理财处于测试版，仅面向美国 Pro、Max、Ultra 个人订阅用户分批推出，免费版、团队版和企业版暂不提供，官方未提额外收费。 / Claude Personal Finance is in beta, rolling out in batches to US Pro, Max, and Ultra individual subscribers only; free, Team, and Enterprise plans are not included, and no extra charge is mentioned.[^4cc325534e4981d3]
- 功能通过 Plaid 连接银行卡与信用卡，权限为只读，Claude 不能转账、管理资金或进行投资，也看不到投资持仓、利率和合同条款。 / The feature connects bank and credit card accounts via Plaid with read-only access; Claude cannot transfer money, manage funds, or invest, and cannot see investment holdings, interest rates, or contract terms.[^4cc325534e4981d3]
- Claude 能查看最多 24 个月交易记录和最多 16 家银行账户，理财页面对话一律不用来训练模型，但解绑后已同步数据的删除时限未说明。 / Claude can view up to 24 months of transaction history and up to 16 bank accounts; finance-page conversations are not used for model training, but the retention period for synced data after unbinding is not stated.[^4cc325534e4981d3]
- 与 ChatGPT Finances 对比，Claude 范围更窄，不支持投资持仓读取，也不会将理财对话存入记忆；ChatGPT 曾支持投资组合和净资产模块。 / Compared with ChatGPT Finances, Claude has a narrower scope, does not read investment holdings, and does not store finance conversations in memory; ChatGPT previously supported portfolio and net worth modules.[^4cc325534e4981d3]
- 两家公司均提醒 AI 可能算错，重要数字需回银行 App 核对，余额可能延迟一两天，同一账户重复登录可能被重复计算。 / Both companies warn that AI may make calculation errors, important figures should be verified in the banking app, balances may be delayed by a day or two, and logging into the same account twice may cause double counting.[^4cc325534e4981d3]
