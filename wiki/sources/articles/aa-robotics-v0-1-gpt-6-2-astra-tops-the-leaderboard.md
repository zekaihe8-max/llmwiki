# AA-Robotics v0.1：GPT-6.2 Astra 暂居榜首

*AA-Robotics v0.1: GPT-6.2 Astra Tops the Leaderboard*

「帖文称」GPT-6.2 Astra 成为 AA-Robotics v0.1 新领导者，得分 79，比 GPT-6 Astra 提升 10 分，领先 GPT-6.2 Sol（78）、Claude Fable 5.2（75）和 Gemini 4 Hydrogen（58）。该模型通过 Codex 控制真实机械臂，在八项物理任务中通过六项，部分完成液体倒出，仅在测量盒子任务中失败。[^aa-robotics-gpt-6-2-astra-leader]

The post from Artificial Analysis claims GPT-6.2 Astra is the new leader in AA-Robotics v0.1 with a score of 79, up 10 points from GPT-6 Astra and ahead of GPT-6.2 Sol (78), Claude Fable 5.2 (75), and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (58). It says the model controlled a real robot arm through Codex, passing six of eight physical tasks, partly completing liquid pouring, and failing only to measure the box.[^aa-robotics-gpt-6-2-astra-leader]
