# Agent 交活必须带截图

*Agent Deliveries Must Include Screenshots*

帖文称，宝玉曾因团队员工提交 PR 不自测、审查问题多，而强制要求附带功能修改截屏，以此倒逼测试；他现在将同一方法用于 AI Agent，要求任务完成后由 Agent 自行测试验证并生成效果截图供其查看，Agent 在截图过程中常能自行发现并顺手修复问题。[^agent-delivery-must-screenshot]

The post says Bao Yu once forced team members to attach screenshots of function changes when submitting PRs because they avoided self-testing and reviews found many issues; he now applies the same method to AI agents, requiring agents to self-test and verify after task completion and generate effect screenshots for his review, during which agents often discover and fix problems on their own.[^agent-delivery-must-screenshot]
