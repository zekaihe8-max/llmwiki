# Artificial Analysis · GPT-6.2 更短、更先给答案，也更少承认不确定

*Artificial Analysis · GPT-6.2 Shorter, Answer-First, Less Likely to Admit Uncertainty*

帖文称，Artificial Analysis 发布的 GPT-6.2 Astra 与 GPT-6.2 Sol 性格较前版本明显变化：回复更短、结构更少、更倾向于先给答案，且不太可能承认不确定。Astra 回复字数从 352 降至 210，Sol 从 337 降至 223；不确定性承认率 Astra 从 96 降至 24，Sol 从 98 降至 51。[^3ca460a6c1631242]

The post says Artificial Analysis's GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol personalities changed noticeably from prior releases: replies are shorter, less structured, more answer-first, and less likely to admit uncertainty. Astra response length dropped from 352 to 210 words, Sol from 337 to 223; uncertainty admission dropped from 96 to 24 for Astra and 98 to 51 for Sol.[^3ca460a6c1631242]
