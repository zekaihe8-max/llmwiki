# 从需求梳理到交付的完整标志设计流程

*Complete Logo Design Process from Requirement Sorting to Delivery*

「帖文称」完整走完从设计简报到成品 SVG 的标志设计流程：先问清用途和受众，把关键词映射成图形线索，再决定字标、字母组合、图形或抽象符号；构图讲究几何比例，并修正弧度溢出、线条粗细补偿等视觉错觉；做好后过硬测试，包括缩到十六像素是否还认得出、单色是否成立、反白是否清楚。文中还附带一千四百多个按类型、技法和行业分类的真实标志，可检索浏览，用来研究构造和避开雷同，不用于照抄。[^logo-design-skill]

The post describes a complete logo design workflow from brief to finished SVG: clarify usage and audience first, map keywords to graphic cues, then choose wordmark, lettermark, graphic, or abstract symbol; composition stresses geometric proportions and corrections for visual illusions such as arc overflow and stroke-weight compensation; final tests include recognizability at 16 pixels, monochrome validity, and reverse-white clarity. It also attaches over 1,400 real logos classified by type, technique, and industry for research and reference, not copying.[^logo-design-skill]
