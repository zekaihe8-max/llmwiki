# AI Coding 套餐已卷至 39 元/月，六款低价订阅对比

*AI Coding Plans Drop to 39 Yuan/Month, Six Low-Cost Subscriptions Compared*

「帖文称」AI Coding 套餐已经卷到 39 元/月，国内外多家厂商推出低价订阅，作者结合官方价格和社区反馈筛选了 6 款性价比较高的套餐，涵盖阿里百炼、火山方舟、MiniMax、OpenCode、Claude Pro 和 ChatGPT Plus，并建议预算有限用户优先考虑阿里百炼和 MiniMax，复杂项目开发倾向 Claude Pro 和 ChatGPT Plus。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]

The post claims AI Coding subscription plans have dropped to 39 yuan per month, with domestic and foreign vendors launching low-cost subscriptions. Based on official prices and community feedback, the author selected six high-value plans covering Alibaba Bailian, Volcano Ark, MiniMax, OpenCode, Claude Pro, and ChatGPT Plus, recommending Alibaba Bailian and MiniMax for limited budgets and Claude Pro and ChatGPT Plus for complex project development.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 阿里百炼 Lite 月费 39 元，提供 20000 Credits，支持 Qwen、DeepSeek 等模型，取消了独立周额度限制。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- Alibaba Bailian Lite costs 39 yuan/month, provides 20,000 Credits, supports Qwen and DeepSeek models, and removes independent weekly quota limits.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- 火山方舟 Agent Small 月费 40 元，支持豆包、DeepSeek、GLM、Kimi 等模型，提供 20000 AFP，但有每周和 5 小时额度限制。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- Volcano Ark Agent Small costs 40 yuan/month, supports Doubao, DeepSeek, GLM, and Kimi, provides 20,000 AFP, but has weekly and 5-hour quota limits.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- MiniMax Plus 月费 49 元，支持 M3、M2.7，官方给出的 M3 月度参考用量约 6 亿 Token，支持 3～4 个 Agent 并发。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- MiniMax Plus costs 49 yuan/month, supports M3 and M2.7, with official reference monthly usage of about 600 million tokens for M3, and supports 3-4 Agent concurrency.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- OpenCode Go 月费 10 美元，支持 Kimi、GLM、MiniMax、DeepSeek、Qwen 等模型，不同模型有独立额度。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- OpenCode Go costs 10 USD/month, supports Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, DeepSeek, and Qwen, with independent quotas for different models.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- Claude Pro 月费 20 美元，包含 Claude Code，可使用 Opus、Sonnet 等模型，适合复杂编程任务。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- Claude Pro costs 20 USD/month, includes Claude Code, supports Opus and Sonnet models, and is suited for complex programming tasks.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- ChatGPT Plus 月费 20 美元，同时包含 ChatGPT 和 Codex，支持 CLI、桌面 App 和云端编程。[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
- ChatGPT Plus costs 20 USD/month, includes both ChatGPT and Codex, and supports CLI, desktop app, and cloud programming.[^ai-coding-plans-39-yuan]
