# 去 AI 味 Skill 合集：从文案到 Logo 与字体设计

*AI-Flavor Removal Skill Collection: From Copywriting to Logo and Font Design*

「帖文称」Orange AI 最近整理了一批去 AI 味 Skill，从文章到排版设计再到 Logo 设计、字体设计，主张全方位去除 AI 味儿，让 DeepSeek Flash 等模型也能有一些高级感，并列出 ste80-humanizer、simpleenglish、ui-design、styleseed、logo-design-skill 与 vane-cinematic-poster 六项资源供自取。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]

The post says Orange AI recently compiled a batch of AI-flavor removal skills spanning articles, layout design, logo design, and font design, aiming to strip AI flavor comprehensively and give models like DeepSeek Flash a more premium feel; it lists six resources—ste80-humanizer, simpleenglish, ui-design, styleseed, logo-design-skill, and vane-cinematic-poster—for self-service use.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文列出 ste80-humanizer，专治“不是…而是…”、三连排比和满屏破折号，采用两遍改写，先清习惯再重搭句子。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- The post lists ste80-humanizer, which targets “not...but...”, triple parallelism, and screens full of em dashes, using two-pass rewriting to first clear habits then rebuild sentences.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- simpleenglish 采用飞机维修手册使用四十年的受控英语，主张短句、单义、主动语态。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- simpleenglish uses controlled English from aircraft maintenance manuals used for forty years, advocating short sentences, single meaning, and active voice.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- ui-design 要求先定配色、字阶、间距，再写组件，目标是让界面一眼看不出是 AI 制作。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- ui-design requires defining color, type scale, and spacing before writing components, aiming to make interfaces unrecognizable as AI-made at a glance.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- styleseed 把设计决定编译成规则文件，渲染完再打分，避免同一提示每次生成不同面貌。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- styleseed compiles design decisions into rule files and scores after rendering, preventing the same prompt from producing different looks each time.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- logo-design-skill 附 1400+ 真标志库，但只准研究不准抄，涵盖简报、图形策略到 16px、单色、反白测试。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- logo-design-skill includes a 1400+ real logo library for research only, not copying, covering briefs, graphic strategy, and 16px, monochrome, and reverse-white tests.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- vane-cinematic-poster 提供 36 种按影片情绪挑的构图，中文字体按题材选宋体、黑体、手写或书法，不默认书法。[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
- vane-cinematic-poster offers 36 compositions chosen by film mood and selects Chinese fonts by genre—Song, Hei, handwriting, or calligraphy—without defaulting to calligraphy.[^oran-ge-ai-flavor-removal-skills]
