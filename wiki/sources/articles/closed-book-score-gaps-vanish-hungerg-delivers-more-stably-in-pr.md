The post claims the blogger stayed up all night running 300 closed-book hard questions on HungerG, Opus 5.5, Qwen4-Max, GLM-5.4, HY4 Flash and other models, with scores clustered between 252 and 259 and the gap basically gone; then four models each ran once on five practical tasks under the same prompt, including a rustic coffee shop brand page redesign, a management dashboard for 3,000+ orders, and a mine-sweeping PPT with 15 planted bugs.
