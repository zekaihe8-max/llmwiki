# PM 回归：Gemini 产品负责人谈 AI 时代，为什么产品判断更值钱

*PMs Are Back: Gemini Product Lead on Why Product Judgment Matters More in the AI Era*

帖文称，Lenny’s Podcast 与 Google 前沿模型产品负责人 Tulsee Doshi 对谈：AI 降低了实现成本，却未降低选错方向的代价，因此“能不能做出来”不再最难，做什么、什么才算好、哪些能力值得押注更重要。帖文还介绍了她对模型评测、产品经理招聘以及 60/20/20 资源分配的看法。[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]

The post cites a Lenny’s Podcast conversation with Tulsee Doshi, Google’s frontier AI model product lead, saying AI has lowered implementation cost but not the cost of choosing the wrong direction, so product judgment—what to build, what counts as good, and which capabilities to bet on—matters more. It also summarizes her views on model evaluation, PM hiring, and 60/20/20 resource allocation.[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称，模型开发不像传统软件按规格实现即得确定结果，训练需要目标与评测，但也可能出现预期之外的能力。 / The post says model development differs from traditional software where specs yield deterministic results; training needs goals and evaluations, but can also produce unexpected capabilities.[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]
- 产品负责人既要检查指标，也要亲自使用模型，发现其在哪些任务上表现突出，再判断这些能力能否形成有价值的体验；只看排行榜可能错过值得产品化的变化。 / Product leads should check metrics and personally use models to find tasks where they excel, then judge whether those capabilities can form valuable experiences; only looking at leaderboards may miss productizable changes.[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]
- 她招聘时重视持续使用模型、有自己的质量标准、能动手做评测、并愿意随能力变化调整路线的人；评测可先从 5—20 条关心的提示词开始，亲自判断结果并明确“好”的标准。 / In hiring she values people who continuously use models, have their own quality standards, can run hands-on evaluations, and adjust direction as capabilities change; evaluations can start with 5–20 prompts you care about, personally judge results, and define what “good” means.[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]
- 模型升级后，系统提示、工具和技能也要重新测试，以前为弥补短板加上的规则可能已多余，甚至限制新能力。 / After model upgrades, system prompts, tools, and skills should be retested; rules previously added to compensate for weaknesses may become redundant or even limit new capabilities.[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]
- 她提出 60/20/20 投入方式：约 60% 做当前稳定实现的能力，20% 押注近期可能补齐的能力，20% 探索已出现苗头但尚未成熟的体验，重点是避免只围绕今天局限设计，或把全部价值押在未来模型一定进步上。 / She proposes a 60/20/20 allocation: about 60% for currently stable capabilities, 20% for near-term capabilities that may be filled, and 20% for exploring emerging but immature experiences; the point is to avoid designing only around today’s limits or betting all value on future model improvement.[^shao-meng-pms-are-back]
