# 转译：面试聊得挺好为什么还是没 Offer

*Translation: Why You Interviewed Well but Still Didn't Get the Offer*

帖文称，从面试官角度分享了多种常见原因，解释为什么候选人自觉面试发挥不错，却仍未拿到 Offer，包括核心技能回答不够扎实、存在内定或更偏爱的人选、与特定岗位不匹配、未明说的隐性门槛、竞争对手更匹配，以及面试实际未如候选人所感的顺利。[^interview-no-offer-interviewer-perspective]

The post shares, from an interviewer's perspective, several common reasons why candidates who feel they interviewed well still do not receive offers, including weak answers on core mandatory skills, pre-selected or internal candidates, poor fit for the specific role, unstated hidden thresholds or preferences, stronger competing candidates, and interviews that may not have gone as well as the candidate believed.[^interview-no-offer-interviewer-perspective]
