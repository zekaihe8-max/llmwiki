# Dario Amodei 否认主张将人类自由意志交给 AI

*Dario Amodei Denies Advocating Surrender of Human Free Will to AI*

「帖文称」Dario Amodei 否认自己主张“向 AI 系统交出人类自由意志”，称该说法荒谬，并强调自己一贯主张 AI 必须由合法民主程序治理，绝不能为了效率或安全牺牲合法性与自决权；他在具体任务自动化问题上持混合看法，并反对将治理权交给 AI 本身。[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]

The post says Dario Amodei denies advocating that human free will be “surrendered” to AI systems, calling the claim absurd, and stresses he has consistently argued AI must be governed by legitimate democratic processes without sacrificing legitimacy or self-determination for efficiency or security; he holds mixed views on automating specific tasks and opposes turning governance over to AI itself.[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 他表示在 CBS 采访、《AI 指数上的政策》、《慈爱的机器》、《技术的青春期》和《我们必须调节前沿》中都主张 AI 民主治理。[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- He says democratic governance of AI is advocated in his CBS interview, “Policy on the AI Exponential,” “Machines of Loving Grace,” “The Adolescence of Technology,” and “We Must Pace the Frontier.”[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- 他反对递归自我改进让 AI 完全自主构建下一代前沿模型，称 Anthropic 不期望 Claude 在没有有意义的人类监督或理解的情况下完全自主构建连续前沿模型是安全的。[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- He opposes letting recursive self-improvement build next-generation frontier models fully autonomously, stating Anthropic does not expect it to be safe for Claude to build successive frontier models without meaningful human supervision or understanding.[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- 他承认 AI 自动化驾驶、设计抗癌药物或编写软件等具体任务具有经济价值并可拯救生命，但也可能造成严重工作岗位中断，且在许多情况下人类更希望亲自参与。[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- He acknowledges AI automation of specific tasks such as driving, designing cancer drugs, or writing software is economically valuable and can save lives, but may cause serious job disruption, and in many cases humans prefer to do activities themselves.[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- 他批评部分 AI 首席执行官进行“氛围感”的商业和政治定位，重复“人类控制与主权”或“AI 应仅是工具”等口号，但产品并未实质不同。[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- He criticizes some AI CEOs for “vibes-based” commercial and political positioning, repeating slogans like “human control and agency” or “AI should be just a tool” without materially different products.[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- 他提到《慈爱的机器》中有关于利用 AI 使民主重获活力的扩展章节，主张 AI 应作为公民决策的辅助，而非替代人类做出政治和治理决策。[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
- He notes “Machines of Loving Grace” contains an extended section on using AI to revitalize democracy, proposing AI as an aid to citizen decision-making rather than a replacement for human political and governance decisions.[^dario-amodei-denies-surrendering-human-free-will]
