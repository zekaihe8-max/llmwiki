# Google DeepMind 以 Gemini 构建 PlanAssist，助力英国地方规划审批

*Google DeepMind builds PlanAssist with Gemini to speed up UK planning approvals*

该帖文称，Google DeepMind 开发了 PlanAssist 以帮助英国政府规划官员加速全国建筑与翻修工作；该工具基于 Gemini 构建，据称可将规划官员评估住户申请所花时间削减多达 58%，并现正向 12 个更多议会开放，以协助其更快做出决策。[^google-deepmind-planassist-uk-planning]

The post states that Google DeepMind developed PlanAssist to help UK government planning officers accelerate building and renovation work nationwide; the tool is built with Gemini and is claimed to cut the time planning officers spend assessing householder applications by up to 58%, and is now being made available to 12 more councils to help them make faster decisions.[^google-deepmind-planassist-uk-planning]
