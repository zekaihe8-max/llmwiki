- AI MediaKit 支持画质增强，Seedance 先出 480p 再超分到 1080p，帖文称成本为 2.73 元，低于 SD 直出 1080p 的 12.39 元，降低 78%。[^eianlu-niche-models]

- Seed Audio 1.0 generates dialogue, sound effects, and ambient sound in one segment from a single prompt, with multiple characters having distinct voices and emotions.[^eianlu-niche-models]
