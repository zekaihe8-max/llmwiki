# 美团开源 LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6，支持超 10 分钟音频驱动数字人视频生成

*Meituan Open-Sources LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6, Supporting Audio-Driven Digital Human Video Generation Over 10 Minutes*

「帖文称」美团开源 LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6，可根据音频、文本、图像输入生成单人及多人的说话视频；新版本改善唇形与音频同步自然度，减少嘴部僵硬或画面闪烁，并通过优化长视频续写机制稳定生成超过 10 分钟的长时视频并保持人物外观一致。帖文还称该模型仅次于 OmniHuman 2.0，采用 MIT 开源协议，开发者和内容创作者可免费下载权重进行本地部署或商业应用，模型权重在 Hugging Face 上提供。[^gorden-sun-longcat-video-avatar-1-6]

The post states that Meituan has open-sourced LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6, which generates single- and multi-person talking videos from audio, text, and image inputs; the new version improves lip-sync naturalness with audio, reduces stiff mouth movements or screen flickering, and uses an optimized long-video continuation mechanism to stably generate videos over 10 minutes while maintaining character appearance consistency. The post also claims the model is second only to OmniHuman 2.0, is released under the MIT license, and allows developers and content creators to freely download weights for local deployment or commercial use, with weights available on Hugging Face.[^gorden-sun-longcat-video-avatar-1-6]
