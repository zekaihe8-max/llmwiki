# Ethan Mollick 谈 AI 任务地平线增长

*Ethan Mollick on AI task horizon growth*

[@emollick](https://x.com/emollick) 标记了他写书期间 AI「可靠人类工作」地平线走了多远：2024 年第一本 AI 书出版时，约四分钟、成功率 50%；2026 年早些时候交上 Co-Existence 初稿时约十七小时；该书在帖文两天前出版时约十七周。[^emollick-ai-task-horizon] 原文见 <https://x.com/emollick/status/2113323645284344215>，并外链其 Substack 笔记 [30 Months On: The Exponential in Charts](https://open.substack.com/pub/oneusefulthing/p/30-months-on-the-exponential-in-charts)。

[@emollick](https://x.com/emollick) marks how far AI's reliable human-work horizon moved across the writing of his books: about four minutes at 50% success when his first AI book published in 2024, seventeen hours when he turned in the Co-Existence draft earlier in 2026, and seventeen weeks when that book published two days before the post.[^emollick-ai-task-horizon] The post is at <https://x.com/emollick/status/2113323645284344215>, and links out to his Substack note [30 Months On: The Exponential in Charts](https://open.substack.com/pub/oneusefulthing/p/30-months-on-the-exponential-in-charts).
