# Berryxia.AI 宣布开源「豆仓」录音豆转录项目

*Berryxia.AI Announces Open-Sourcing of 'Dou Cang' Recording Bean Transcription Project*

「帖文称」Berryxia.AI 将于明天开源「豆仓」录音豆的整个项目，用户可用自己的 Anker 录音豆连接 Mac 并接入本地大模型或 API 模型进行转录，同时支持录音豆与 Mac 本地/系统音频录制，并可下载视频号、抖音、小红书、YouTube 及播客音频入知识库；iOS 刘海屏设备也支持录音、实时转录和语音输入，且不受会员限制。[^berryxia-dou-cang-open-source]

According to the post, Berryxia.AI will open-source the entire 'Dou Cang' recording bean project tomorrow, allowing users to connect their own Anker recording bean to Mac and access local or API models for transcription, while supporting recording bean and Mac local/system audio recording and downloading Video Account, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, YouTube, and podcast audio into a knowledge base; iOS notch-screen devices also support recording, real-time transcription, and voice input, without membership restrictions.[^berryxia-dou-cang-open-source]
