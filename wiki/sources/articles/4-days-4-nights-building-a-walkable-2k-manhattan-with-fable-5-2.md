# 肝了 4 天 4 夜，Fable5.2+Blender 造出一座 2K 的曼哈顿｜地图、地标、镜头、渲染、配乐剪辑保姆级教程

*4 Days 4 Nights: Building a Walkable 2K Manhattan with Fable 5.2 and Blender*

「帖文称」肝了 4 天 4 夜，作者借助 AI 编程助手与开源工具，把曼哈顿中城搭成了一座能在浏览器里走路、开车、切换日夜的 2K 城市，并拍摄了一支 60 秒、每秒 60 帧的短片。全程未手写代码，两万多栋楼与四十五座地标均由程序生成或 AI 建模，最终在自有 RTX 4090 服务器上用 Blender 光线追踪渲染完成。[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]

The post claims the author spent four days and nights building a browser-walkable 2K Midtown Manhattan using AI coding assistants and open-source tools, then shot a 60-second, 60-fps short film. No code was hand-written; over twenty thousand buildings and forty-five landmarks were procedurally generated or AI-modeled, and the final film was rendered with Blender ray tracing on a self-hosted RTX 4090 server.[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 成片时长 60 秒，分辨率 2K（2560×1440），帧率 60 帧，包含 20,077 栋楼、14,730 段道路和 32,407 棵树，另有 45 座地标。[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- The 60-second film is 2K (2560×1440) at 60 fps, containing 20,077 buildings, 14,730 road segments and 32,407 trees, plus 45 landmarks.[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- 九样工具包括 Claude Code、Codex、OpenStreetMap、Three.js、Tripo、Blender、RTX 4090 服务器、CosyVoice 和 ffmpeg，大部分免费。[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- Nine tools are used—Claude Code, Codex, OpenStreetMap, Three.js, Tripo, Blender, an RTX 4090 server, CosyVoice and ffmpeg—most of them free.[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- 渲染共 3,642 帧，约 3,450 帧需要渲染，每帧 16 至 21 秒，单帧占用十几 GB 显存，24 GB 显存足够。[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- Rendering covers 3,642 frames, about 3,450 of them rendered, at 16–21 seconds per frame, with over 10 GB VRAM per frame; 24 GB VRAM is sufficient.[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- 旁白共七句英文，使用开源模型 CosyVoice 在 RTX 4090 服务器上合成，字幕为中英双语。[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- The seven-sentence English narration is synthesized with the open-source CosyVoice model on the RTX 4090 server, with Chinese-English bilingual subtitles.[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
- 作者强调全程没手写过代码，AI 写全部代码，人类只负责提要求、看画面、挑毛病。[^servasyy-ai-2k-manhattan-blender]
