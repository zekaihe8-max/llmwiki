# DSH 公开 7 套插件开发 Prompt

*DSH Releases 7 Plugin Development Prompts*

帖文称，DeepSeek Harness（DSH）官方已将演示视频中使用的 7 套插件开发 Prompt 全部公开，提供中英文版本。案例包括 Transformer 3D 可视化、视频编辑工作台、论文阅读器，此外还有 3D 地标展示、像素风界面、海报设计工作台和 Token 会话统计。用户只需复制对应提示词，在 DeepSeek Harness 的 Creator Mode 中即可尝试开发插件。作者指出，官方演示可能经过多轮对话调整，复制一次不一定能完全复现；未来使用 Agent 时，可能不需要到处找现成插件，而是直接让 DSH 按需开发并迭代。[^4777d5b2e306a8a8]

The post says DeepSeek Harness (DSH) has officially released all 7 plugin development prompts used in its demo videos, in both Chinese and English. Cases include Transformer 3D visualization, video editing workbench, and paper reader, plus 3D landmark display, pixel-style UI, poster design workbench, and token conversation statistics. Users can copy the corresponding prompts and try developing plugins in DeepSeek Harness's Creator Mode. The author notes that official demos may have undergone multi-turn dialogue adjustments and may not be fully replicated in one attempt; in the future, using Agent may not require searching for existing plugins, but rather asking DSH to build as needed and iterate.[^4777d5b2e306a8a8]
