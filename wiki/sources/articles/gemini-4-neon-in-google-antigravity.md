# Google Antigravity 推出 Gemini 4 Neon 模型

*Gemini 4 Neon in Google Antigravity*

「帖文称」Google Antigravity 于 2026 年 11 月 18 日宣布向 Google AI Pro 和 Google AI Ultra 付费订阅者推出 Gemini 4 Neon，将其定位为介于 Gemini 4 Carbon 与 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 之间的前沿模型，并可通过 Orchestrator 让 Neon 担任规划器、Hydrogen 担任执行器。[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]

The post says Google Antigravity announced on November 18, 2026 that Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out to Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra paid subscribers, positioning it as a frontier model between Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and enabling Orchestrator to pair Neon as planner with Hydrogen as executor.[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Neon 在 Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 总体排名第 3，得分 61；在 Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index 得分 58.6 并排名第 1，在 Zapier AutomationBench 得分 56.3% 并排名第 1。[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Neon ranks #3 overall with a score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, #1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6), and #1 on Zapier's AutomationBench (56.3%).[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Neon 提供 Low 到 Extra High 四种思考等级，默认 High 等级下每任务成本比 Carbon 低 32%。[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Neon offers four thinking levels from Low to Extra High; at the default High level it shows 32% lower cost per task than Carbon.[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Neon 适用于仓库级编辑、调试、重构、安全代码生成、基于企业的分析和 STEM 推理等场景。[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Neon is engineered for repo-level edits, debugging, refactoring, secure code generation, grounded enterprise analysis, and STEM reasoning.[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Orchestrator 支持将 Gemini 4 Neon 作为规划器与 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 作为执行器在单个会话中配对，Hydrogen 遇到阻力时可交回 Neon 检查状态并修复计划。[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Orchestrator supports pairing Gemini 4 Neon as planner with Gemini 4 Hydrogen as executor in a single session, handing back to Neon to inspect state and repair plans when Hydrogen hits friction.[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Gemini 4 Neon 在 Antigravity 桌面应用、CLI 和 IDE 扩展中推出，企业可通过 Gemini for business 访问。[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
- Gemini 4 Neon rolls out across the Antigravity desktop app, CLI, and IDE extensions, with enterprise access via Gemini for business.[^antigravity-gemini-4-neon]
