# Claude 解出细菌穹窿体结构

*Claude Solves Bacterial Vault Structure*

「帖文称」Anthropic 生物研究团队使用 Claude 在细菌和噬菌体中发现了穹窿体同类蛋白，并解出一种名为 L05 的噬菌体穹窿体的 3D 结构，将冷冻电镜分析时间从几周缩短至约 34 小时出初模、约一周出最终结果。[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]

According to the post, Anthropic's biology research team used Claude to find vault-like proteins in bacteria and phages, and solved the 3D structure of a phage vault called L05, reducing cryo-EM analysis from weeks to about 34 hours for an initial model and about a week for final results.[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 穹窿体是人体细胞中巨大的空心蛋白“桶”，1986 年被发现，功能至今不明。 / Vault particles are large hollow protein “barrels” in human cells, discovered in 1986, with function still unknown.[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]
- Claude 在约 150 亿条预测蛋白序列中搜索，发现穹窿体同类分布于 29 个细菌门和 4 个古菌门。 / Claude searched about 15 billion predicted protein sequences and found vault homologs across 29 bacterial phyla and 4 archaeal phyla.[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]
- 团队解出噬菌体蛋白 L05 的结构，由 96 个相同蛋白拼成，比人穹窿体（78 个）更矮更胖，内部空间大约大 25%。 / The team solved the structure of phage protein L05, assembled from 96 identical proteins, shorter and wider than human vaults (78 proteins), with about 25% more internal space.[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]
- 冷冻电镜分析由 Claude 在多 agent 系统中自行安装 RELION 并运行，34 小时出第一版模型，约一周出最终结果。 / Cryo-EM analysis was performed by Claude installing and running RELION in a multi-agent system, producing an initial model in 34 hours and final results in about a week.[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]
- 实验分离时间从约 3 天缩短到约 90 分钟，15 种候选蛋白中有 7 种拼成穹窿体颗粒，冷冻电镜确认 4 种。 / Particle separation time was reduced from about 3 days to about 90 minutes; 7 of 15 candidate proteins assembled into vault-like particles, with 4 confirmed by cryo-EM.[^claude-solves-bacterial-vault-structure]
