# OpenAI 面向全球科学家扩容免费前沿模型访问

*OpenAI expands free frontier model access for up to 200,000 scientists*

「帖文称」OpenAI 将面向全球最多 200,000 名科学家扩容免费访问其前沿模型，并提供培训与支持，申请于 11 月 23 日开放；默认情况下，通过该计划共享的数据不会用于训练模型。此前 OpenAI 表示，将从 10,000 名研究人员开始，到 2027 年扩大至 100,000 名，面向科学家、数学家和工程师。[^openai-expand-free-access-scientists-200k]

The post says OpenAI is expanding free access to its frontier models for up to 200,000 scientists worldwide, with training and support; applications open Nov. 23, and by default data shared through the program is not used to train models. Previously, OpenAI said it would start with 10,000 researchers and expand to 100,000 through 2027, targeting scientists, mathematicians, and engineers.[^openai-expand-free-access-scientists-200k]
