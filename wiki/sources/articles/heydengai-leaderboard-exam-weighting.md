# 小邓同学谈 AI 排行榜像考试：换考卷排名就变

*Heydengai on AI leaderboards as exams—change the test, change the rankings*

[@heydengai](https://x.com/heydengai)（小邓同学）称 AI 模型排行榜**越来越像考试**：昨天考编程、开源模型还能和闭源掰掰手腕；今天科学题加权，差距从 **3 分**拉到 **12 分**——**模型没换，考卷换了，排名就变了**。他据此提问：排行榜究竟是在**衡量**模型能力，还是在**定义**什么叫能力？[^heydengai-leaderboard-exam-weighting] 原帖 <https://x.com/heydengai/status/2115754265155100896>。

[@heydengai](https://x.com/heydengai) (Heydengai / 小邓同学) says AI model leaderboards **look more like exams**: yesterday’s coding emphasis let open models “arm-wrestle” closed ones; today’s science weighting widened a gap from **3** to **12** points—**the models did not change, the test paper did, and the standings moved**. He asks whether leaderboards **measure** model capability or **define** what capability means.[^heydengai-leaderboard-exam-weighting] Post: <https://x.com/heydengai/status/2115754265155100896>.
