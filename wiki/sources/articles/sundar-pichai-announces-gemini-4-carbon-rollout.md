# Sundar Pichai 宣布 Gemini 4 Carbon 开始推出

*Sundar Pichai Announces Gemini 4 Carbon Rollout*

帖文称，Sundar Pichai 宣布 Gemini 4 Carbon 今日起推出，该模型专为持续的长时域任务设计，能在复杂软件工程、网络安全和科学发现中进行深度推理。该模型将通过 Gemini API 在 Google AI Studio 及面向企业的 Gemini 全球提供，并面向 Ultra 订阅用户在 antigravity、Gemini 应用及搜索 AI 模式中推出。[^sundar-gemini-4-carbon]

The post says Sundar Pichai announced Gemini 4 Carbon is rolling out today, built for sustained, long-horizon tasks and deep reasoning through complex software engineering, cybersecurity, and scientific discovery. It will be available globally via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for business, and for Ultra subscribers in antigravity, the Gemini App, and AI Mode in Search.[^sundar-gemini-4-carbon]
