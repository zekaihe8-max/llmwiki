# 软件工厂：智能体上层的自动化基建

*Software Factory: Automation Infrastructure Above Agents*

Matt Pocock 在 X 上发帖为“软件工厂”这一术语辩护。帖文称，该词虽听起来花哨、常被用来炒作 VC 概念，但也指向真实变化：早期顶尖团队让智能体直接开发产品、修复 Bug，而现在他们正在构建帮助智能体为其开发产品的自动化，即“向上一层”。作者称，不再直接开产品，而是开造产品的工厂；不再是产品工程师，而是工厂工程师。[^d91fc180e0f82905]

Matt Pocock defends the term “software factory” on X. The post says the term sounds fancy and is often used to hype VC narratives, but also points to a real shift: top teams previously had agents work directly on products, and are now building automations that help agents work on products for them—moving up a layer. The author says they are no longer building the product, but the factory that builds the product; no longer product engineers, but factory engineers.[^d91fc180e0f82905]
