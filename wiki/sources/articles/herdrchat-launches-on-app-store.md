# HerdrChat 上架 App Store

*HerdrChat Launches on App Store*

帖文称，HerdrChat 已上架 App Store，支持 iPhone 和 iPad 且免费，可通过 SSH 直接连接自己的电脑，在手机上查看 Claude Code、Codex、Grok、Antigravity 等多个 Agent 的运行状态与历史会话，并可接管任务、查看子 Agent 与执行记录、发送图片或打开完整终端；作者提醒，HerdrChat 本身不在手机端运行 Agent，电脑或 VPS 需保持在线。[^0f33980c7a61b167]

The post says HerdrChat has launched on the App Store, supports iPhone and iPad and is free, and can connect directly to one's own computer via SSH to view running status and history of multiple agents such as Claude Code, Codex, Grok, and Antigravity on a phone, and to take over tasks, view sub-agents and execution records, send images, or open a full terminal; the author notes that HerdrChat does not run agents on the phone itself, so the computer or VPS must stay online.[^0f33980c7a61b167]
