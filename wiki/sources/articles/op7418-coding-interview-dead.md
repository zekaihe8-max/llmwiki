# 歸藏转述：编码面试已死

*歸藏 relays that the coding interview is dead*

[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418)（歸藏）转述：Anthropic 工程负责人称传统编码面试已经死了——面试过程中不再让候选人写代码（算法题或用代码实现小功能）。他持乐观看法：借助 AI，初级工程师很有希望快速超过资历更老的工程师，并给出五条建议——追求商业影响力而非只埋头需求列表；要有大胆想法；用一个周末做出来；专注一个非常好的想法、不要把多个想法塞进同一原型；善用新人的新鲜视角与用户共情。帖内引用 [@felixrieseberg](https://x.com/felixrieseberg) 英文原帖：其团队本月起去掉编程面试，因已不再提供信号，并链向博客 <https://felixrieseberg.com/hunger-beats-tenure-advice-for-junior-engineers/>。[^op7418-coding-interview-dead] 原帖 <https://x.com/op7418/status/2115281467236585804>。

[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418) (歸藏) relays that an Anthropic engineering lead says the traditional coding interview is dead—candidates are no longer asked to write code in the loop (algorithm puzzles or small feature implementations). He is optimistic: with AI, juniors have a real shot at quickly surpassing more senior engineers, and offers five tips—chase business impact instead of only ticket lists; hold bold ideas; ship it over a weekend; focus on one very good idea per prototype; use the newcomer’s fresh eyes and user empathy. The post quotes [@felixrieseberg](https://x.com/felixrieseberg)’s English original: his team dropped the coding interview this month because it no longer provides signal, linking <https://felixrieseberg.com/hunger-beats-tenure-advice-for-junior-engineers/>.[^op7418-coding-interview-dead] Post: <https://x.com/op7418/status/2115281467236585804>.
