# AI 视频的门槛变成预算内做完整成片

*AI video threshold becomes completing a full film within budget*

「帖文称」AI 视频的门槛已经不是能不能把画面做的好看，而是能不能在自己的预算内做出一条完整成片；至少在科技宣传和动效领域，用 Claude 直接写代码控制镜头，确实比 SD 这类视频模型更实用，改需求改画面只需要改几行参数。被引用的 Hailey 亦表示，AI 视频最烧钱的不是模型贵，而是生成的东西根本没法用，她折腾了两三个月，从治愈动画、女团 MV 到 3D 短片、产品宣传，摸清了不同模型的优势和坑，主张不看排行榜，只看能否成片、好不好改、最终花多少钱。[^ai-video-budget-complete-film]

The post claims the AI video threshold is no longer whether visuals look good, but whether a complete film can be made within one’s budget; in tech promotion and motion graphics, using Claude to write code that controls the camera is more practical than SD-style video models, since changing requirements and visuals only requires editing a few parameters. The cited Hailey also says the biggest cost in AI video is not expensive models but unusable generated output; after two to three months spanning healing animation, girl group MVs, 3D shorts, and product promos, she learned model strengths and pitfalls and advises ignoring rankings in favor of whether a model can produce a finished film, how easy it is to revise, and the final cost.[^ai-video-budget-complete-film]
