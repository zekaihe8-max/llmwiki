## 摘要 / Summary

- 多人协作：可将 Bot 分享给团队，带入 Slack、Microsoft Teams 和视频通话；同一 Team Bot 可由团队共用，或仅分享给特定人员或群组，并在多人对话中跟上讨论。[^79f21d3568602f48]
- Multi-person collaboration: Bot can be shared with teams and brought into Slack, Microsoft Teams, and video calls; the same Team Bot can be shared by the team or only with specific people or groups, and can follow multi-person conversations.[^79f21d3568602f48]
- 主动接手：Bot 能识别待办并主动承担，跟进未完成事项，预估团队下一步需求，并在需要判断时把相关人员拉进来。[^79f21d3568602f48]
- Proactive takeover: Bot can identify to-dos and proactively take them on, follow up unfinished items, estimate the team's next steps, and pull in relevant people when judgment is needed.[^79f21d3568602f48]
- 能力提升：浏览网页更稳定，写文档和做演示更快、更可靠，并减少完成任务所需资源。[^79f21d3568602f48]
- Capability improvement: web browsing is more stable, document writing and presentations are faster and more reliable, and fewer resources are needed to complete tasks.[^79f21d3568602f48]
- 会议与集成：Bot 可加入 Google Meet、Zoom 或 Microsoft Teams，会议中回答问题，结束后发送摘要和待办；与 Gmail、Google Calendar、GitHub 等工具的连接有所加强。[^79f21d3568602f48]
- Meetings and integrations: Bot can join Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams, answer questions during meetings, and send summaries and to-dos afterward; connections with Gmail, Google Calendar, GitHub, and other tools are strengthened.[^79f21d3568602f48]
- 计划与权限：Team Bot 面向 Team 和 Enterprise 套餐；会议功能面向 Team、Enterprise 和 Ultra；企业套餐需由管理员打开。共享空间需遵守各人权限，并检查信息是否适合发到公开频道。[^79f21d3568602f48]
