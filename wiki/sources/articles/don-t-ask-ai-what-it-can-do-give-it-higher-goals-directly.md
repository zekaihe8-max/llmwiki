# 别问 AI 会什么，直接给它更高的目标

*Don't ask AI what it can do; give it higher goals directly*

「帖文称」用 AI 很忌讳先问它会什么，再让它做自己以为能做的事，因为 AI 并不清楚自己的能力，尤其是在不知道能用什么工具和 Skill 的情况下；应直接给 AI 目标，要求高一点，并提供方向、上下文和工具支持，这类似厉害的领导带员工，且模型升级后以前不能做的任务可以再试试。[^c72fbc166dddd986]

The post says that using AI, it is taboo to first ask what it can do and then have it do what it thinks it can, because AI does not clearly know its own capabilities, especially when it does not know what tools and Skills are available; instead, give AI goals directly, set higher requirements, and provide direction, context, and tool support, similar to how strong leaders manage employees, and tasks previously impossible can be retried after model upgrades.[^c72fbc166dddd986]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 用 AI 忌讳先问它会什么，再让它做自己以为能做的事。[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- Using AI, it is inadvisable to first ask what it can do, then have it do what it thinks it can do.[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- AI 并不清楚自己的能力，尤其是在不知道可用工具和 Skill 的情况下。[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- AI does not clearly know its own capabilities, especially when it does not know what tools and Skills are available.[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- 应直接给 AI 目标，要求更高，并提供方向、上下文和工具支持。[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- AI should be given goals directly, with higher requirements, along with direction, context, and tool support.[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- 这类似厉害的领导不问员工会什么，只告诉目标并给方向与支持，挖掘潜力。[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- This is like strong leaders who do not ask employees what they can do, but only tell them the goal and provide direction and support to unlock potential.[^c72fbc166dddd986]
- 每次模型升级后，以前不能做的任务可以再试试，新模型可能就能做了。[^c72fbc166dddd986]
