# Grok Bot 周额度重置出现时间差，新老订阅用户分批恢复

*Time lag in Grok Bot weekly quota reset: old and new subscribers restored in batches*

帖文称，Grok Bot 官方此次重置周额度存在时间差：昨日先给老订阅用户重置，新订阅用户大约晚半天才陆续重置。答疑群里不少刚订阅的朋友一度慌张，担心自己不在重置范围内。作者认为，官方把 X、Grok、Cursor 整合在一起显然是要重点推动新订阅，因此新订阅用户不可能被落下，重置只是早晚问题；随后官方账号发布“已重置所有 Grok Bot 用户周限额”，印证了这一判断。[^grok-bot-quota-reset-time-lag]

The post reports that Grok Bot's weekly quota reset had a time lag: old subscribers were reset first, while new subscribers were restored about half a day later. Some newly subscribed users in the Q&A group panicked, fearing they were excluded. The author argued that since the platform is integrating X, Grok, and Cursor to push new subscriptions, new subscribers would not be left out and the reset was only a matter of timing; the official account later announced that weekly limits for all Grok Bot users had been reset.[^grok-bot-quota-reset-time-lag]
