# 反重力全员重置额度，付费 Pro 也能用 Carbon 了

*Antigravity Resets Quotas for All, Paid Pro Users Now Get Carbon*

「帖文称」谷歌 Antigravity 今早重置全员额度，各档位模型集体提升一档；原本仅 Ultra 才可用的顶配模型，现已向月付 19.99 美元的付费 Pro 用户开放 Carbon，免费版、Plus 和普通试用 Pro 用户默认切换到 Hydrogen，且 Argon 与 3.8 Flash 将于 11 月 30 日下线。[^antigravity-quota-reset-carbon-pro]

The post says Google Antigravity reset quotas for all users this morning and upgraded models across all tiers; top models previously limited to Ultra are now available to paid Pro users at $19.99/month via Carbon, while free, Plus, and ordinary trial Pro users are switched to Hydrogen by default, and Argon and 3.8 Flash will be deprecated on November 30.[^antigravity-quota-reset-carbon-pro]
