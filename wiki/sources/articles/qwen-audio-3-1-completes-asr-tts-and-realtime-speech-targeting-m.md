# Qwen-Audio-3.1 补齐听写、配音与实时对话，主攻多语言和中文方言

*Qwen-Audio-3.1 Completes ASR, TTS, and Realtime Speech, Targeting Multilingual and Chinese Dialects*

「帖文称」阿里于今日中午为 Qwen-Audio-3.1 推出大升级，把听写、配音、实时对话三套语音能力全部补齐，专门针对多语言和中文方言薄弱环节进行攻坚。[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]

The post claims Alibaba released a major upgrade to Qwen-Audio-3.1 at noon today, completing all three speech capabilities—transcription, text-to-speech, and real-time conversation—with a focus on multilingual support and Chinese dialects.[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 听写（ASR）支持 59 种语言和 8 种中文方言，具备润色与多人分角色转写功能。[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- ASR supports 59 languages and 8 Chinese dialects, with text polishing and multi-speaker diarization.[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- 配音（TTS）支持 65 种语言和 6 种中文方言，可调节语气语速，并在带杂音录音中克隆声音。[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- TTS supports 65 languages and 6 Chinese dialects, allows tone and speed adjustment, and can clone voices from noisy recordings.[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- 实时对话支持 53 种语言和 6 种中文方言，支持边听边说、随时打断，并可接知识库查资料。[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- Realtime speech supports 53 languages and 6 Chinese dialects, supports listen-and-speak with interruptions, and can connect to a knowledge base.[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- QwenCloud Flash 版定价：听写输入每百万 token 0.15 美元，配音和实时对话音文输出每百万 token 1.87 美元。[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
- QwenCloud Flash pricing: ASR input costs $0.15 per million tokens; TTS and realtime audio-text output cost $1.87 per million tokens.[^qwen-audio-3-1-dialect-upgrade]
