# 新号想挤进早鸟，群里在用 4.99 刀打法

*New users try to squeeze into early-bird pricing; groups use the $4.99 trick*

帖文称，谷歌 AI 黑五将 Pro 年付从 199 美元降至 99 美元，但 11 月 26 日前仅面向老用户开放入口；新号可先花 4.99 美元开通月付，再重进订阅页切换至 99.99 美元年付并按比例退回首月费用，群里有人晒退款约 4.82 美元，但该打法并非百分百生效，结账前需确认实付金额并关闭自动续费，或等至 27 日黑五当天看新号优惠。[^google-ai-black-friday-4-99-early-bird-trick]

The post claims Google AI Black Friday pricing cuts the Pro annual plan from $199 to $99, but the early-bird entry before November 26 is only for existing users; new accounts can first pay $4.99 for a monthly subscription, reload the subscription page to reveal a $99.99 annual option, upgrade, and receive a proportional refund of the initial fee, with one user reportedly refunded about $4.82, though the trick is not guaranteed, so buyers should verify the final charge and cancel auto-renewal or wait for new-user deals on November 27.[^google-ai-black-friday-4-99-early-bird-trick]
