# 别忘了 Gemini：Google AI 会员自带的三大实用功能

*Don't Forget Gemini: Three Practical Features Included with Google AI Membership*

「帖文称」很多人开通了谷歌 AI 会员却只关注 AGY，把自带的 Gemini 闲置起来；作者介绍了 Gemini 的深度研究、画布协作和 Google 生态原生联动三个功能，并指出 AGY 与 Gemini 额度分开，建议会员把两者结合使用以值回票价。[^69fac1df1c0733c8]

The post claims many people subscribe to Google AI but only use AGY while leaving Gemini idle; the author introduces Gemini's Deep Research, Canvas collaboration, and native Google ecosystem integration, and notes that AGY and Gemini quotas are separate, suggesting members combine both to get their money's worth.[^69fac1df1c0733c8]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称 AGY 适合写代码和搞开发，而 Gemini 更适合日常查资料、写东西和看长视频。[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- The post claims AGY suits coding and development, while Gemini is more convenient for daily information lookup, writing, and watching long videos.[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- Gemini Deep Research 会先列出研究计划供确认，确认后自动翻阅几十个网页和官方文档，最后输出带出处链接的结构化报告，并可导出到 Google Docs。[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- Gemini Deep Research first presents a research plan for confirmation, then automatically browses dozens of webpages and official documents, finally outputting a structured report with source links that can be exported to Google Docs.[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- Canvas 提供左右分屏界面，支持划选右侧文字或代码仅修改该片段，并可实时预览网页原型效果。[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- Canvas provides a split-screen interface where users can select specific text or code on the right to edit only that segment, and supports real-time preview of web prototypes.[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- Gemini 打通 YouTube、Gmail、Google Drive、Docs 和日历，可拆解长视频重点、翻找历史邮件、总结云端资料，并直接存成文档和日程。[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- Gemini integrates with YouTube, Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, and Calendar, enabling long-video key-point extraction, historical email search, cloud file summarization, and direct saving to documents and calendar.[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- 帖文称 AGY 与 Gemini 的额度是分开的，AGY 额度用完后仍可使用 Gemini 额度。[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
- The post claims AGY and Gemini have separate quotas, so Gemini quota remains available after AGY quota is exhausted.[^69fac1df1c0733c8]
