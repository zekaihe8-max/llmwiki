# 帖文称 Meta 首席 AI 官发文：超级智能正在到来，瓶颈不再是智力

*Post Says Meta's Chief AI Officer: Superintelligence Is Arriving, Intelligence No Longer the Bottleneck*

帖文称 Meta 首席 AI 官 Alexandr Wang 发表长文《Superintelligence is Arriving》，认为 AI 现在缺的不是智力，而是各行业的专门知识、背景信息和实际操作经验；文中以 AIME 满分和黎曼猜想为例，主张 AI 研究本身会被自动化，并反对把社会交给不断自我升级的超级智能。[^3c73e56c93e6e632]

The post says Meta's chief AI officer Alexandr Wang published "Superintelligence is Arriving," arguing AI now lacks not intelligence but industry-specific knowledge, background, and operational experience; it uses AIME perfect scores and the Riemann conjecture as examples, argues AI research itself will be automated, and opposes handing society to a continuously self-upgrading superintelligence.[^3c73e56c93e6e632]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称 OpenAI o3 模型在 2024 年 12 月的 AIME 上几乎拿满分，模型已足够聪明，缺的是各行业专门知识与实际操作经验。[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- The post says OpenAI's o3 model nearly scored perfectly on AIME in December 2024; models are now smart enough, lacking industry-specific knowledge and practical experience.[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- 帖文称相较于黎曼猜想，研制原子弹只算一道家庭作业题，超越人类的障碍已不是智力，而是领域专门知识、工程搭建和实验。[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- The post says compared with the Riemann conjecture, building the atomic bomb is like a homework problem; the remaining obstacles to surpassing humans are not intelligence but domain expertise, engineering setup, and experiments.[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- 帖文称 AI 研究比证明黎曼猜想容易，模型可能聪明到能完成全部 AI 研究并自我改进，生物、武器、经济等研究也会被大幅加速，快到人类看不懂、监督不过来。[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- The post says AI research is easier than proving the Riemann conjecture; models may become smart enough to conduct all AI research and self-improve, while research in biology, weapons, and economics could accelerate beyond human comprehension or supervision.[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- 帖文称人类很难控制不受约束、不断自我升级的 AI，也很难控制每个领域都比人类强 1000 倍的超级智能，社会不应接受这种移交。[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- The post says humans can hardly control an unconstrained, self-upgrading AI, nor a superintelligence 1,000 times more capable in every field, and society should not accept this handover.[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- 帖文称过去两年补齐专门知识后，写代码几乎被解决，模型网络攻防能力很强，还能自行连续完成多步任务，成为通用助手。[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
- The post says after filling domain knowledge over the past two years, coding is nearly solved, models have strong network offense and defense, and can autonomously complete multi-step tasks as general assistants.[^3c73e56c93e6e632]
