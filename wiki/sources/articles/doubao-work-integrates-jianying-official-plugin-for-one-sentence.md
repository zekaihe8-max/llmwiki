# 豆包工作接入剪映官方插件，支持一句话 AI 自动剪辑

*Doubao Work integrates Jianying official plugin for one-sentence AI video editing*

帖文称，豆包工作已接入剪映官方插件，用户上传视频素材并用一句话描述剪辑需求，即可调用剪映自动剪辑；生成初稿后，可以通过聊天反复修改，或直接打开剪映的多轨草稿进行精修。插件还接入了剪映 Hub，可把文稿、代码、脚本或商品图片转化成科普动画、知识短视频、短漫剧和营销短片，将 AI 生成素材与视频剪辑结合。目前插件仅在豆包工作电脑端开放，用户需使用本地电脑模式，在插件栏搜索「剪映」并安装剪映官网最新版客户端；首次接入并登录的用户，还有机会获得 200 剪映积分体验优惠。[^doubao-work-jianying-plugin]

The post claims Doubao Work has integrated the Jianying official plugin: users upload video footage and describe editing needs in one sentence to trigger automatic Jianying editing, then iterate via chat or refine multi-track drafts in Jianying. The plugin also connects to Jianying Hub to turn documents, code, scripts, or product images into science animations, knowledge shorts, short dramas, and marketing videos. It is currently available on the Doubao Work desktop client in local computer mode, requiring the latest Jianying client from the official website; first-time users who log in may receive 200 Jianying credits.[^doubao-work-jianying-plugin]
