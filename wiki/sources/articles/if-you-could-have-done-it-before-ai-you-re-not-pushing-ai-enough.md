# 如果 AI 前你也能做，就说明没用够 AI

*If you could have done it before AI, you're not pushing AI enough*

帖文称，AI 使用的试金石在于：如果你在没有 AI 的情况下也能完成当前工作，那就说明没有把 AI 用够。艾伦·利维（Aaron Levie）指出，早期 AI 主要是让已有产出更快，而现在应当在极短时间内产出远超以往能力的结果，包括功能更好的原型、更强的工程架构、更全面的研究以及完全不同的业务流程。[^c44ee44b0efe9208]

The post proposes a litmus test for AI use: if you could have done what you're doing before AI, you're likely not pushing it enough. Aaron Levie argues that AI should now enable outputs that dramatically exceed prior capabilities in a fraction of the time—better product prototypes, stronger engineering architecture, exhaustive research, and business processes that learn from failures in ways humans cannot.[^c44ee44b0efe9208]

- 帖文称，直到大约六个月前，AI 最好的用法只是让你已会做的事情更快完成。 / The post says that until about six months ago, the best AI could do was produce what you already would, just faster.[^c44ee44b0efe9208]
- 现在 AI 产出应当在极短时间内显著超越以往能力。 / Now AI output should dramatically exceed what you could do before, in a fraction of the time.[^c44ee44b0efe9208]
- 产品原型应当功能完整、可交互，并具有比以往更有意义的特性。 / The product prototype should be fully functional and interactive, with meaningfully better features than before.[^c44ee44b0efe9208]
- 研究应当覆盖主题上的每一个事实和每一个分析维度。 / Research should consume every single fact on the subject across every dimension of analysis.[^c44ee44b0efe9208]
