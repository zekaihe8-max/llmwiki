# Gemini 4 Neon「鹈鹕骑自行车」盲测：单 Agent 对比 Swarm

*Gemini 4 Neon Pelican Riding a Bicycle Blind Test: Single Agent vs Swarm*

帖文称，作者用 Google Gemini 4 Neon 跑了一组「鹈鹕骑自行车」盲测，对比单 Agent 独立作画并自检 3 轮，与开启 5 个专精角色的 Swarm 流水线协作。Swarm 通过共享任务板、留言板、锚点契约、自动合成和独立视觉 QA 返工，拉开细节差距。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]

The post says the author used Google Gemini 4 Neon for a ‘pelican riding a bicycle’ blind test, comparing a single agent drawing independently with three self-check rounds against a five-role Swarm pipeline. The Swarm uses a shared task board, message board, anchor contracts, automatic compositing, and independent visual QA rework to expose detail differences.[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称，测试使用 Google Gemini 4 Neon，主题是「鹈鹕骑自行车」的盲测。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- The post says the test used Google Gemini 4 Neon with a blind-test prompt of a pelican riding a bicycle.[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- 单 Agent 方案是独立作画并自检 3 轮；Swarm 方案是 5 人流水线协作并包含 QA 返工。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- The single-agent setup drew independently and self-checked for three rounds, while the Swarm setup used a five-person pipeline with QA rework.[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- Swarm 的 5 个角色分别是美术指导、自行车工程师、鹈鹕画师、合成工程师和独立视觉 QA。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- The Swarm had five roles: art director, bicycle engineer, pelican painter, compositing engineer, and independent visual QA.[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- 美术指导先锁定轮心、座垫、双脚踏、车把等 8 个锚点，防止合成时脚悬空。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- The art director first locked eight anchors such as wheel centers, saddle, pedals, and handlebars to prevent floating feet during compositing.[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- 独立视觉 QA 将成图放大 2 倍找茬，第一版揪出大腿接缝缺口、握把糊在一起、踏板对比度低、轮胎缺阴影、云朵太对称等 10 个毛病。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- The independent visual QA enlarged the render 2x and found 10 issues in the first version, including a thigh seam gap, blurry grip area, low pedal contrast, missing tire shadow, and overly symmetrical clouds.[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
- 帖文称，双车同步蹬起来的对比成片也由 Agent 逐帧生成，全程没有使用剪辑软件。[^gemini-4-neon-pelican-bicycle-swarm-blind-test]
