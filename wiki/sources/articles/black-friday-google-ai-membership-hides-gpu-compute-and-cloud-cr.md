# 黑五 Google AI 会员藏显卡算力与云抵扣金，别只拿来聊天

*Black Friday Google AI membership hides GPU compute and cloud credits—don't just use it for chat*

帖文称，黑五开通 Google AI Pro 或 Ultra 会员后，除了网页聊天，还能在 Colab 查看并使用每月发放的显卡计算单元（Pro 200 个、Ultra 2000 个），并通过谷歌开发者计划或 AI Studio 领取每月 10 至 100 美元的云抵扣金；文中还建议用抵扣金开设标准网络的 200G 流量云主机，并提醒 Colab 单元 90 天未用会过期、云抵扣金需手动绑定后 12 个月内使用。[^f7ac2e98981020b2]

The post claims that after subscribing to Google AI Pro or Ultra on Black Friday, users can access monthly GPU compute units in Colab (200 for Pro, 2000 for Ultra) and claim $10–$100 in monthly Google Cloud credits via the developer program or AI Studio; it also suggests using credits for a Standard-network cloud host with 200G egress, and warns that Colab units expire after 90 days of non-use while cloud credits must be manually bound and used within 12 months.[^f7ac2e98981020b2]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Google AI Pro 每月自动发放 200 个 Colab 计算单元，Ultra 发放 2000 个，可用于借用 A100 显卡运行开源模型。[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- Google AI Pro automatically grants 200 Colab compute units monthly, Ultra grants 2000, usable for renting A100 GPUs to run open-source models.[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- Pro 会员每月送 10 美元谷歌云抵扣金，Ultra 按档位送 40 或 100 美元，需在开发者计划或 AI Studio 绑定结算账号并勾选自动领取。[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- Pro members receive $10 in Google Cloud credits monthly, Ultra receives $40 or $100 by tier, requiring billing account binding in the developer program or AI Studio and opting into auto-claim.[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- 云抵扣金可用于支付 Gemini 接口费或谷歌云机器费，建议用于开设标准网络的 200G 出站流量云主机，机器费可被抵扣金覆盖。[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- Cloud credits can pay for Gemini API fees or Google Cloud machine fees; recommended for a Standard-network cloud host with 200G monthly egress, with machine fees covered by credits.[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- Colab 计算单元自动发放但 90 天不用会过期；云抵扣金需手动绑定账号才发放，到账后 12 个月内可累积使用。[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- Colab compute units are auto-granted but expire after 90 days of non-use; cloud credits require manual account binding before issuance and can be accumulated for 12 months after arrival.[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- 帖文还提示，香港区年付 Pro 可能因不在 Antigravity 支持区而无法在谷歌编程工具中使用 Gemini 4 Neon，改归属地至美国后虽能登录但仍锁定免费额度。[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
- The post also warns that Hong Kong annual Pro may fail to use Gemini 4 Neon in Google coding tools due to Antigravity support restrictions; switching billing to the US enables login but locks the account to free-tier quotas.[^f7ac2e98981020b2]
