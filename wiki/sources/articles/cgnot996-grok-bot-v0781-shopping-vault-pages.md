[@cgnot996](https://x.com/cgnot996) says Grok [@bot](https://x.com/bot) shipped **v0.78.1**, merging **0.75 through 0.78** with shopping cards, **Pages**, **Vault**, and related features, plus fixes for cross-app copy formatting, long-conversation zoom/scroll jitter, and connected-app status display; more detail on the learning site [bot.fmcat.top](https://bot.fmcat.top).[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages] Post: <https://x.com/cgnot996/status/2115752949276123606>.

## 摘要 / Summary

- **购物卡**：商品结果带图、评分与价格，支持横滑查看；**下单前必须你确认**。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Shopping cards**: product results show images, scores, and prices with horizontal browse; **you must confirm before placing an order**.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **物流**：买完自动跟进快递，发货与派送会通知。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Logistics**: after purchase, automatic courier tracking with notifications on ship and delivery.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **订阅管家**：帮开免费试用，计费前取消或提醒；盯续费价与闲置订阅。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Subscription manager**: help start free trials, cancel or remind before billing; watch renewal prices and idle subscriptions.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Pages**：新增与 Bot 共同写备忘、看板并持续更新。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Pages**: co-author memos and boards with the bot with ongoing updates.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **视频生成**：支持按文字描述或参考图直接生成视频。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Video generation**: generate video from a text description or a reference image.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Vault**：存账号卡片；可连 **1Password** 逐条授权，且 **Bot 看不见密码**。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Vault**: store account cards; connect **1Password** with per-item authorization, and the **bot cannot see passwords**.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **审批**：动作审批卡窗口开到 **30 分钟**，日常安全操作更少打扰。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
- **Approvals**: action-approval cards last **30 minutes** for less friction on routine safe operations.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-v0781-shopping-vault-pages]
