# METR 独立评估：Anthropic 模型能力进步速度约翻倍，主因可能是 AI 参与研发

*METR Independent Assessment: Anthropic Model Capability Progress Speed Roughly Doubles, Main Cause Possibly AI Participation in R&D*

宝玉（@dotey）在 X 上转发 METR 独立评估报告，称截至 2026 年 10 月，Anthropic 在目标明确任务上的模型能力提升速度约为 2024 年 3 月至 2025 年 10 月的 2 倍，METR 猜测大部分加速来自 AI 参与研发，但公开版报告中大部分证据已被 Anthropic 涂黑。[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]

宝玉 (@dotey) on X shared METR's independent assessment report, stating that as of Oct 2026, Anthropic's model capability improvement speed on well-defined tasks is about 2x that of Mar 2024–Oct 2025; METR speculates most acceleration comes from AI participation in R&D, but most evidence in the public report has been redacted by Anthropic.[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]

## 摘要 / Summary

- METR 11 月 5 日发布报告，判断截至 2026 年 10 月，Anthropic 在“目标明确的任务”上模型能力提升速度约为 2024 年 3 月至 2025 年 10 月的 2 倍，不确定区间为 1.3 到 3 倍。 / METR released a report on Nov 5 estimating that as of Oct 2026, Anthropic's model capability improvement speed on “well-defined tasks” is about 2x that of Mar 2024–Oct 2025, with an uncertainty range of 1.3 to 3x.[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]
- METR 认为加速大部分可能来自 Anthropic 大量使用 AI 进行研发，猜测约占 8 成，但团队内部有分歧且对 Anthropic 员工内部缺乏共识。 / METR believes most acceleration likely comes from Anthropic extensively using AI for R&D, estimating about 80%, but the team has internal disagreements and lacks consensus among Anthropic staff.[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]
- 公开报告中最具体的证据是 AECI 分数：2025 年 10 月以来 Anthropic 模型 AECI 增速是此前的 1.9 倍（95% 置信区间 1.5 到 2.8 倍）。 / The most concrete evidence in the public report is the AECI score: since Oct 2025, Anthropic model AECI growth speed is 1.9x the previous period (95% CI 1.5 to 2.8x).[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]
- Anthropic 单方面删除了公开报告中约 100 页完整报告里的大部分非公开证据，包括训练各环节加速估算、算法进步估算及对未来模型的预测。 / Anthropic unilaterally deleted most non-public evidence from the ~100-page full report in the public version, including acceleration estimates across training stages, algorithmic progress estimates, and predictions for future models.[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]
- METR 评估从 8 月中进行到 10 月底，投入相当于 2.5 个全职人员，未收取 Anthropic 费用，且需主动申请数据、Anthropic 可批可拒。 / METR's evaluation ran from mid-August to end of October, with effort equivalent to 2.5 full-time staff, no fees charged by Anthropic, and data had to be actively requested with Anthropic able to approve or reject.[^metr-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment]
