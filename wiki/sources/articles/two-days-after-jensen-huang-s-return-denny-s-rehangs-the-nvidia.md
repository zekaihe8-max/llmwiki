# 黄仁勋复职两天后，Denny's 把「英伟达诞生卡座」的铭牌挂了回去

*Two days after Jensen Huang's return, Denny's rehangs the "NVIDIA founding booth" plaque*

帖文称，Denny's 于 11 月 25 日在官方 Instagram 发帖表示，圣何塞门店纪念英伟达诞生的卡座铭牌已“挂回了它该在的地方”，并留言“Jensen，你的卡座在等你，咖啡管够，我们 24 小时营业”。文章还提到，铭牌在黄仁勋辞职当晚被摘下，随后 Denny's 承诺重新挂上，但未说明摘牌原因或决策者。[^dotey-2125748227790327906]

The post says Denny's posted on its official Instagram on November 25 that the plaque commemorating NVIDIA's founding at its San Jose store had been "hung back where it belongs," with a message: "Jensen, your booth is waiting for you, coffee on the house, we're open 24 hours." The article also notes the plaque was removed the night Huang resigned, and Denny's later promised to rehang it without explaining why it was removed or who ordered it.[^dotey-2125748227790327906]
