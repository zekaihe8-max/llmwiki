# Orange AI 谈智能只是人类中心主义的 benchmark

*Orange AI on intelligence as an anthropocentric benchmark*

[@oran_ge](https://x.com/oran_ge)（Orange AI）读《菌落万象》后感：作者称「智能」只是从人类中心主义出发定义的一个指标（benchmark）；在古希腊以来的排序里人类居顶，其下是黑猩猩等动物、植物、菌落。该指标眼看要被 AI 超越，但智能本是人类发明的概念；脱离人类中心主义，或可说小麦大米、猫狗驯化了人类，细菌真菌病毒默默支撑生态。并引 Ilya 2023 年言：若把智力当作人类最重要品质，接下来日子会过得很惨；人的品质很多，智能只是自定的其中一个 benchmark。人类做决策的顺序是惯性＞感性＞理性，理性对应智能、排第三。[^oran-ge-intelligence-anthropocentric-benchmark] 帖文见 <https://x.com/oran_ge/status/2113944865830846746>。

[@oran_ge](https://x.com/oran_ge) (Orange AI), after reading 《菌落万象》, relays that “intelligence” is only a benchmark defined from anthropocentrism; in a ranking that goes back to ancient Greece, humans sit at the top, then chimpanzees and other animals, plants, and colonies. That metric is about to be surpassed by AI, yet intelligence is a human-invented concept; without anthropocentrism, one might say wheat and rice or cats and dogs domesticated humans, while bacteria, fungi, and viruses quietly sustain ecology. He cites Ilya in 2023: if you treat intellect as humanity’s most important quality, the days ahead will be miserable; humans have many qualities, and intelligence is only one self-defined benchmark. Human decision order is inertia > emotion > reason, with reason (intelligence) third.[^oran-ge-intelligence-anthropocentric-benchmark] The post is at <https://x.com/oran_ge/status/2113944865830846746>.
