# Lauren 谈 Grok Bot 自动化：先分清目标与过程，再把判断和反馈教给 Bot

*Lauren on Grok Bot Automation: Separate Goals from Processes, Then Teach Judgment and Feedback to the Bot*

帖文称，Lauren 认为很多人还没用上 Grok Bot 的完整能力，难点往往不是工具本身，是不太会把一件事拆成可以自动化的目标和过程。她建议先把目标告诉 Bot，再把做法、判断、数据冲突处理和完成标准讲清楚，并通过 routines 设置自动触发，让 Bot 从反馈中自我改进。[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]

The post says Lauren believes many people have not yet used Grok Bot to its full potential; the difficulty is often not the tool itself but not knowing how to break a task into automatable goals and processes. She advises telling the Bot the goal first, then explaining the steps, judgments, conflict resolution, and completion criteria, setting automatic triggers via routines, and letting the Bot improve from feedback.[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 目标是终点，过程是怎么走到那里；填表、发邮件只是步骤，真正的目标可能是促成销售、做出新功能或把库存订到刚好够用。[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- The goal is the endpoint and the process is how to get there; filling forms and sending emails are just steps, while the real goal may be closing a sale, launching a feature, or ordering just enough inventory.[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- 在 Grok Bot 新建 Bot 后，先把目标告诉它；目标说不清时，可让 Bot 先提问、一起访谈，直到有满意表述。[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- After creating a Bot in Grok Bot, tell it the goal first; if the goal is unclear, let the Bot ask questions and interview together until a satisfactory statement is reached.[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- 需要把常用步骤、留意事项、使用的应用和工具、检查方式、信息来源和反馈来源讲给 Bot，并说明数据冲突以哪份为准、什么时候算做完。[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- You need to tell the Bot the usual steps, things to watch for, apps and tools used, how to check correctness, information sources and feedback sources, and specify which data prevails in conflicts and what counts as done.[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- Bot 开始接手后，要给它获取反馈的途径（如邮件、社交平台或评价网站），让它对照记录主动提出改进建议。[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- After the Bot starts handling tasks, give it access to feedback channels such as email, social platforms, or review sites so it can compare records and proactively suggest process improvements.[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- 在 Grok Bot 里用 routines 设置何时开始干活，如邮件到达、Slack 或 Teams 消息，或按周、隔天等固定节奏；没有 routines，Bot 只有被问起才会做。[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
- In Grok Bot, use routines to set when to start, such as email arrival, Slack or Teams messages, or fixed schedules like weekly or every other day; without routines, the Bot only acts when asked.[^grok-bot-automation-goals-processes]
