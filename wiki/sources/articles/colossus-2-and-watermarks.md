# Colossus 2 与水印

*Colossus 2 and Watermarks*

帖文称，马斯克披露孟菲斯超级计算集群 Colossus 2 计划在年底前部署约 120 万块英伟达 Blackwell GPU；除现有 Macrohard、Macroharder、Minihard 外，Gun Range 与 Spam 两座新设施亦将投运，五座设施合计约 119 万块 GPU，其中推理设施 Spam 的网络配置更简单、光纤用量不到训练设施一半。[^colossus-2-blackwell-deployment]

The post says Musk disclosed that the Memphis supercomputer cluster Colossus 2 plans to deploy about 1.2 million NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs by year-end; besides existing Macrohard, Macroharder and Minihard, the new Gun Range and Spam facilities will also come online, with five facilities totaling about 1.19 million GPUs, and the inference facility Spam using a simpler network configuration with less than half the fiber of training facilities.[^colossus-2-blackwell-deployment]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称 Colossus 2 年底前约部署 120 万块英伟达 Blackwell GPU。 / The post says Colossus 2 will deploy about 1.2 million NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs by end of year.[^colossus-2-blackwell-deployment]
- Gun Range 预计 11 月底上线 22 万块 GB300 用于模型训练；Spam 计划 12 月部署约 20 万块 GB300，专门用于 AI 推理。 / Gun Range is expected to go online late November with 220k GB300 for model training; Spam plans to deploy about 200k GB300 in December for AI inference.[^colossus-2-blackwell-deployment]
- 现有设施中，Macrohard 配有 11 万块 GB200 和 22 万块 GB300，Macroharder、Minihard 各有 22 万块 GB300。 / Existing facilities: Macrohard has 110k GB200 and 220k GB300; Macroharder and Minihard each have 220k GB300.[^colossus-2-blackwell-deployment]
- 五座设施年底合计约 119 万块 GPU，加上计划新增的 42 万块。 / Five facilities total about 1.19 million GPUs by year-end, including about 420k newly added.[^colossus-2-blackwell-deployment]
