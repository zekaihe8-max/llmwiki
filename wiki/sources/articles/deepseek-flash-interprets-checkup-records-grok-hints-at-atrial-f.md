# DeepSeek Flash 解读体检单，Grok 提示房颤，Apple Watch 心电图显示窦性心律

*DeepSeek Flash interprets checkup records, Grok hints at atrial fibrillation, Apple Watch ECG shows normal sinus rhythm*
