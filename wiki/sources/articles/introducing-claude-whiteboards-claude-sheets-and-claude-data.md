# Claude 白板、表格与数据功能说明

*Introducing Claude Whiteboards, Claude Sheets, and Claude Data*

「帖文称」Claude 于 2026 年 11 月 10 日推出 Whiteboards 与 Sheets 的公测版，并将此前公测的 Claude Dashboards 正式发布为 Claude Data，三项功能面向所有付费计划用户，可在网页、桌面和移动端使用。[^claude-whiteboards-sheets-data]

Claude launched Whiteboards and Sheets in beta on November 10, 2026, and released Claude Dashboards generally as Claude Data; all three are available to paid plan users on web, desktop, and mobile.[^claude-whiteboards-sheets-data]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Whiteboards 是共享画板，团队可与 Claude 实时头脑风暴，Claude 可将讨论转化为带时间线、负责人和日期的看板，并可导出为文档或幻灯片，或导入 Lucid、Miro、Vani 等工具继续协作。[^claude-whiteboards-sheets-data]
- Whiteboards is a shared canvas where teams can brainstorm with Claude in real time; Claude can turn discussions into boards with timelines, owners, and dates, and export to docs or decks or import into Lucid, Miro, or Vani.[^claude-whiteboards-sheets-data]
- Sheets 可从提示生成带标签页、公式、格式和下拉菜单的电子表格，支持单元格编辑与变更追踪，并可导出为 .xlsx 或 CSV，或在 Airtable、Asana、monday.com、Smartsheet 中继续使用。[^claude-whiteboards-sheets-data]
- Sheets can generate spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting, and drop-downs from prompts, supports cell editing and change tracking, and can export to .xlsx or CSV, or continue using in Airtable, Asana, monday.com, or Smartsheet.[^claude-whiteboards-sheets-data]
