# 让 Grok Bot 造一个设计师 Bot

*Let Grok Bot Create a Designer Bot*

帖文称，将“100 个提升审美的设计网站”文章与一段结构化提示词丢给 Grok bot，即可创建名为「设计师」的新 Bot；提示词要求它按 5 轮整理 100 个设计网站，每轮开头标「第 X 轮 Y/Z」，用户回复「继续」后再发下一轮，最终输出风格表、精选 10 种风格、5 个混合方向及英文 AI 生图提示词，供以后做海报、品牌、网页、UI、社媒、电商图时参考。[^5856b210d0403757]

The post says feeding the “100 design websites” article and a structured prompt to Grok bot creates a new Bot named “Designer”; the prompt asks it to organize 100 design websites in 5 rounds, each starting with “Round X Y/Z” and continuing only after the user replies “Continue”, ultimately producing style sheets, 10 selected styles, 5 hybrid directions, and English AI image-generation prompts for future poster, branding, webpage, UI, social media, and e-commerce image work.[^5856b210d0403757]
