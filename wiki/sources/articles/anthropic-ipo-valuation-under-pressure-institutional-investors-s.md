# Anthropic IPO 估值承压：机构投资者建议下调至 1 万亿美元

*Anthropic IPO Valuation Under Pressure: Institutional Investors Suggest Cut to $1T*

帖文引用《华尔街日报》报道，称三名计划参与 Anthropic IPO 的大型机构投资者认为此前预期的 1.5 万亿美元估值仍然偏高，其中一人建议降至约 1 万亿美元。知情人士透露，截至 10 月底公司年化收入运行率已超过 800 亿美元，较 7 月底的 650 亿美元大幅增长。投资者对上市方案意见不一：有人想先看 10 月财报再决定、有人建议将募资规模从 1000 亿美元降至 800 亿美元、也有人提议若市场持续低迷则推迟上市。最终决定权仍在 Anthropic 及其承销商手中。[^anthropic-ipo-valuation-pressure-2025]

The post cites WSJ reporting that three large institutional investors planning to participate in Anthropic's IPO view the previously expected $1.5T valuation as too high, with one suggesting a cut to ~$1T. Insiders say ARR exceeded $80B as of end-October, up from $65B in July. Investors differ on approach: some want to see October earnings first, some propose reducing the raise from $100B to $80B, others suggest delaying the IPO if markets stay weak. Final decisions rest with Anthropic and its underwriters.[^anthropic-ipo-valuation-pressure-2025]
