# 字节发布 Seedream 5.5 图像模型：支持像素级不变编辑与透明底输出

*ByteDance Releases Seedream 5.5 Image Model with Pixel-Perfect Editing and Transparent Background Output*

帖文称，字节跳动 Seed 团队于 11 月 9 日发布图像模型 Seedream 5.5，主打同一张图连续编辑 100 轮不走样、像素级保留未改区域，并可直接输出背景透明的图像，羽毛等难抠边缘也能保留。帖文还称该模型严重文字错误率从上一代 2.8% 降至 0.4%，在与 OpenAI GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst、Google Nano Banana 2.1 的对比中文字错误率最低，文本生图真实感领先，但对 Nano Banana 2.1 的图像修改真实感仍落后约 6%。模型正在即梦、豆包、豆包工作上线，开发者可在火山方舟以 Doubao-Seedream-5.5 名称试用，2K 方图编辑单张成本约 0.28 元，不到 Seedream 5.0 Pro 一半。[^bb1ec4428e4b162e]

The post says ByteDance's Seed team released image model Seedream 5.5 on November 9, highlighting continuous editing of the same image for 100 rounds without degradation, pixel-level preservation of unchanged areas, and direct output of transparent-background images with hard-to-cut edges like feathers preserved. The post also claims serious text error rate dropped from 2.8% to 0.4% versus the previous generation, with lowest text error rate and leading realism in text-to-image generation compared to OpenAI GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst and Google Nano Banana 2.1, though image editing realism still lags Nano Banana 2.1 by about 6%. The model is rolling out to Jimeng, Doubao, and Doubao Workspace, and developers can try Doubao-Seedream-5.5 on Volcano Ark; editing a 2K square image costs about 0.28 yuan per image, less than half of Seedream 5.0 Pro.[^bb1ec4428e4b162e]
