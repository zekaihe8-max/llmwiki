# Ramp：企业用 AI 更多，花钱几乎不涨

*Ramp: Enterprises Use More AI, Spend Almost No More*

帖文称，Ramp 最新数据显示企业消耗的 AI Token 还在猛涨，但从 8 月起花在 Token 上的钱基本停了；7 月环比涨 42%，8 月仅涨 1%，9 月仅涨 0.2%。OpenAI 与 Anthropic 综合单价在 9 月下旬到 10 月下旬下降 27%，企业通过预算与模型路由控制成本，Anthropic IPO 估值预期已从最高 2 万亿美元下调至 1.2 万亿至 1.3 万亿美元区间。[^e113c4a55314cc00]

The post says Ramp's latest data shows enterprise AI token consumption keeps surging, but spending on tokens has basically stalled since August; up 42% month-over-month in July, only 1% in August, and 0.2% in September. The combined unit price for OpenAI and Anthropic fell 27% from late September to late October, with enterprises controlling costs through budgets and model routing, and Anthropic's IPO valuation expectations lowered from a high of $2 trillion to the $1.2–1.3 trillion range.[^e113c4a55314cc00]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称，从 8 月初到 10 月中，企业 Token 用量增加约 65%，支出仅增加 1.5%。 / The post says that from early August to mid-October, enterprise token usage rose about 65% while spending rose only 1.5%.[^e113c4a55314cc00]
- Ramp 统计的 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 综合单价，从每百万 Token 0.76 美元降至 0.56 美元，降幅 27%。 / Ramp's combined OpenAI and Anthropic unit price dropped from $0.76 to $0.56 per million tokens, a 27% decrease.[^e113c4a55314cc00]
- 最舍得花钱的 1% 企业贡献了 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 约 80% 的支出，该群体 8 月人均月支出从 9873 美元降至 8568 美元。 / The top 1% of spending enterprises account for about 80% of OpenAI and Anthropic expenses; this group's average monthly spend per person fell from $9,873 to $8,568 in August.[^e113c4a55314cc00]
