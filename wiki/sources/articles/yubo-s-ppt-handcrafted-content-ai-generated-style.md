# 玉伯 PPT：内容手搓、风格 AI 直出

*Yubo's PPT: Handcrafted Content, AI-Generated Style*

「帖文称」Frank Wang 玉伯（@lifesinger）在 X 上发布帖文，表示已提前两天准备好 GEO 大会的分享 PPT；内容全部由本人撰写，模版风格“偷懒”交给 AI 工具 YouMind 直出。他还提到，写文章仍可回归手搓，手搓的快乐很难被 AI 替代，尽管 AI 已快速降低内容创作门槛且直出质量往往不错。[^yubo-ppt-ai-style]

The post states that Frank Wang (Yubo, @lifesinger) posted on X that he had prepared the GEO conference sharing PPT two days in advance; all content was written by himself, while the template style was delegated to AI tool YouMind for direct output. He also mentioned that writing can still return to handcrafting, and the joy of handcrafting is hard for AI to replace, even though AI has rapidly lowered the content creation threshold and direct output quality is often good.[^yubo-ppt-ai-style]
