# Levie：Box AI 复杂工作评测显示 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 显著提升

*Levie: Box AI Complex Work Eval Shows Major Gemini 4 Hydrogen Gains*

帖文称，Box 与 Aaron Levie 表示已在 Box AI Complex Work Eval 中提前测试 Gemini 4 Hydrogen。该模型在金融、咨询、生命科学等行业的企业文档复杂任务上，比 Gemini 3.8 Flash 提高 5 个百分点的准确率，同时用时不足一半、Token 消耗约减少 12%。Hydrogen 即将在 Box AI Studio 推出，供任何客户构建自定义智能体。[^c73069c6c5d0b7a9]

Aaron Levie's post says Box tested Gemini 4 Hydrogen in early access with the Box AI Complex Work Eval. On complex enterprise-document tasks across finance, consulting, life sciences and other industries, Hydrogen improved accuracy by 5 percentage points over Gemini 3.8 Flash while taking less than half the time and using about 12% fewer tokens. It will roll out in Box AI Studio shortly so any customer can build custom agents.[^c73069c6c5d0b7a9]
