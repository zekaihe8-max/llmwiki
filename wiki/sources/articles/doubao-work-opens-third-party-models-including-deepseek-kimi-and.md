# 豆包工作对个人用户开放 DeepSeek、Kimi、GLM 等第三方模型

*Doubao Work Opens Third-Party Models Including DeepSeek, Kimi, and GLM to Individual Users*

「帖文称」豆包工作今日对个人用户开放了 DeepSeek、Kimi、GLM 等第三方模型，每个模型均标注了消耗倍率，其中 DeepSeek 4.1 Flash 低至 0.01x。发帖人认为，自建模型的公司接入他家模型，说明模型已是可替换的零件，Codex、Claude Cowork 等产品也已支持第三方模型，模型不再是护城河，Harness 才是。此外，发帖人不喜欢更新后的图标，希望官方增加换图标设置，并许愿尽快推出 Linux 版。[^doubao-work-third-party-models]

The post says Doubao Work today opened third-party models including DeepSeek, Kimi, and GLM to individual users, with each model labeled by a consumption multiplier; DeepSeek 4.1 Flash is as low as 0.01x. The author argues that a company building its own models integrating others' models shows models have become interchangeable parts, and products like Codex and Claude Cowork already support third-party models, so models are no longer the moat—Harness is. The author also dislikes the new icon, hopes for an icon-change setting, and wishes for a Linux version soon.[^doubao-work-third-party-models]
