# 从 App Store 订 Gemini，价格页面要看对

*Check Google One App pricing before subscribing to Gemini via App Store*

帖文称，Gemini 新用户若想通过 App Store 订阅，应先查看 Google One App 内的订阅价格；Gemini App 内没有三个月优惠价，默认 Pro 月费为 19.9 美元，而单独下载的 Google One App 可提供与官网一致的 4.99 美元优惠价，优惠期为三个月。[^gemini-app-store-subscription-price]

The post says new Gemini users planning to subscribe via the App Store should check subscription prices in the Google One App first; the Gemini App lacks the three-month promotional price and defaults to Pro at $19.9 per month, while the standalone Google One App offers the same $4.99 promotional price as the official website for three months.[^gemini-app-store-subscription-price]
