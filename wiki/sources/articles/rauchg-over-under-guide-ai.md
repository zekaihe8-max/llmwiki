# Rauch 谈过度与不足引导 AI

*Rauch on over- and under-guiding AI*

[@rauchg](https://x.com/rauchg)（Guillermo Rauch）指出：用 AI 时既会过度引导，也会引导不足；管好这颗「超能力」旋钮，是他招人时看的 #0 技能。[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai] 帖文见 <https://x.com/rauchg/status/2114085432749347086>。

[@rauchg](https://x.com/rauchg) (Guillermo Rauch) argues that working with AI means both over-guiding and under-guiding it, and that managing that “superpower” dial is the #0 skill he looks for when hiring.[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai] Post: <https://x.com/rauchg/status/2114085432749347086>.

## 摘要 / Summary

- 有时你会过度引导 AI——它其实比你更懂、也比你更聪明。[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai]
- There will be times where you over-guide the AI—it knows better than you and is more intelligent than you.[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai]
- 有时你会引导不足——它可能跑偏、带上错误偏见，或错过上下文。[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai]
- There will be times where you under-guide the AI—it can go off-track, have the wrong biases, or miss context.[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai]
- 你管好这颗超能力的好坏，是他招人时看的 #0 技能。[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai]
- How well you manage this superpower is the #0 skill he looks for.[^rauchg-over-under-guide-ai]
