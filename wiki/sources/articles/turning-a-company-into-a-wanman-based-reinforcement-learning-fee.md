# 把公司改造成基于 wanman 的强化学习反馈循环

*Turning a Company into a Wanman-Based Reinforcement Learning Feedback Loop*

帖文称，郭宇（guoyu.eth，@turingou）已把自己的公司改造成一套基于 wanman 的强化学习反馈循环组织：Agent 会派探索者寻找现实中的需求、建立关系与销售渠道、从灵感库中找点子，并实际做出产品；他本人唯一的动作是在 Agent 中批准——收到 Agent 的邮件后打开公司网站，用 Passkey 登录批准。帖文称批准后从服务端密钥、iOS/Android 应用创建、构建、测试与生产部署、CI/CD，到内部外部 TestFlight 提交和正式商店审核全部自动化，新 App 的研发时间从年初的一到两周、一个月前的一天缩短到 30 分钟以内，他相信明年能发布远不止 300 个应用和网站。[^wanman-rl-loop-company]

According to the post, Guo Yu (guoyu.eth, @turingou) has turned his company into an organization built as a wanman-based reinforcement learning feedback loop: agents dispatch explorers to find real-world needs, build relationships and sales channels, pick ideas from an inspiration library, and actually build products. His only action is to approve in the agent — he opens the company website after receiving an email from the agent and approves after logging in with a Passkey. The post says that once approved, everything from server-side keys, iOS/Android app creation, build, test and production deployment, CI/CD, to internal and external TestFlight submissions and official store review is automated; new-app development has gone from one to two weeks at the start of the year, to one day a month ago, to under 30 minutes, and he believes he can release far more than 300 apps and websites next year.[^wanman-rl-loop-company]
