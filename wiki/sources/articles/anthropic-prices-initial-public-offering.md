# Anthropic 定价首次公开募股

*Anthropic prices initial public offering*

「帖文称」Anthropic 已将其 Class A 普通股首次公开募股定价为每股 83.00 美元，发行股份总计 980,000,000 股，其中 956,351,354 股由 Anthropic 出售，23,648,646 股由某些销售股东出售；股票预计于 2026 年 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克全球精选市场以 "ANT" 交易，交割预计于 2026 年 11 月 20 日完成。[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]

Anthropic has priced its initial public offering of Class A common stock at $83.00 per share, comprising 980,000,000 shares mostly sold by Anthropic and the remainder by certain selling stockholders; shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT" on November 19, 2026, with closing expected on November 20, 2026.[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 首次公开募股定价为每股 Class A 普通股 83.00 美元。[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]
- The initial public offering is priced at $83.00 per share of Class A common stock.[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]
- 发行股份总计 980,000,000 股，其中 956,351,354 股由 Anthropic 出售，23,648,646 股由某些销售股东出售。[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]
- The offering consists of 980,000,000 shares, of which 956,351,354 are sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by certain selling stockholders.[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]
- 股票预计于 2026 年 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克全球精选市场以交易代码 "ANT" 开始交易，交割预计于 2026 年 11 月 20 日完成。[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]
- Shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol "ANT," with the offering expected to close on November 20, 2026.[^anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering]
