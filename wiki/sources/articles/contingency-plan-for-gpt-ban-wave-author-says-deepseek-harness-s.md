# GPT 封号潮预案：作者称 DeepSeek Harness 支持多模型切换与迁移

*Contingency plan for GPT ban wave: author says DeepSeek Harness supports multi-model switching and migration*

帖文称，GPT、Claude 接连出现封号风波，尤其是反代、共享账号和第三方接入用户，应提前做好迁移准备，甚至将国产模型视为最终归宿。作者个人非常看好 DeepSeek 的统治力，并推荐提前了解其开源的 DeepSeek Harness（DSH）。帖文称 DSH 底层接入 pi-ai，支持 30 多类模型 Provider，并可自行添加 OpenAI、Anthropic 兼容接口，列举了 DeepSeek、GLM、Kimi、MiniMax、Qwen、OpenCode Go、Command GOAT、Ollama、OpenRouter 等模型与套餐，并称最大优势是不绑定单一模型服务商，可在 DeepSeek、GLM、Kimi、Qwen 及各类 Coding Plan 之间切换，无需重新折腾整套 Agent。帖文还提醒，通过 API 接入海外模型不代表可直接使用 ChatGPT Plus、Claude Pro 订阅额度，并建议在 ChatGPT 网页端设置的数据管理中导出数据，以防账号被封。[^d6c34b86]

The post says GPT and Claude have faced consecutive account ban waves, especially for reverse-proxy, shared-account, and third-party access users, who should prepare migration in advance and may ultimately turn to domestic models. The author personally is optimistic about DeepSeek's dominance and recommends learning its open-source DeepSeek Harness (DSH). The post says DSH integrates pi-ai at the underlying layer, supports over 30 model Provider types, and allows adding OpenAI/Anthropic-compatible interfaces, listing DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, MiniMax, Qwen, OpenCode Go, Command GOAT, Ollama, OpenRouter and related plans. It claims the biggest advantage is not being bound to a single model vendor, allowing switching among DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Qwen and Coding Plans without reworking the whole Agent. The post also notes that API access to overseas models does not mean direct use of ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro subscription quotas, and reminds users to export data via ChatGPT web settings > data management in case of account bans.[^d6c34b86]
