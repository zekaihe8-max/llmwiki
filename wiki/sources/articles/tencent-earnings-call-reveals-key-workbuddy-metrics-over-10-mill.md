# 腾讯电话会披露 WorkBuddy 关键数据：日活超 1000 万，日耗 Token 超 100 万亿

*Tencent earnings call reveals key WorkBuddy metrics: over 10 million DAU, over 100 trillion daily tokens*

「帖文称」刚看完腾讯财报后电话会议，马化腾和刘炽平分享了 WorkBuddy 的观点与数据：按每日大语言模型 Token 消耗总量，WorkBuddy 已发展为国内规模最大的 AI 应用，日活超过 1000 万，日均 Token 消耗量超 100 万亿；它也是中国几乎所有开源模型最大的客户；并把 Flash 模型作为获客工具，免费用户低成本进入后再升级订阅并转向更强模型变现。[^tencent-workbuddy-earnings-call]

The post says that after Tencent's earnings call, Ma Huateng and Liu Zhiping shared views and data on WorkBuddy: measured by daily LLM token consumption, WorkBuddy has become China's largest AI application, with over 10 million daily active users and over 100 trillion daily tokens consumed; it is also the largest customer for nearly all Chinese open-source models; and it positions Flash models as an acquisition tool, letting free users enter at low cost before upgrading to subscriptions and stronger models for monetization.[^tencent-workbuddy-earnings-call]
