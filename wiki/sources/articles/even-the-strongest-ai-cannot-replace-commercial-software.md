# 再强的 AI，也替不掉商业化软件

*Even the strongest AI cannot replace commercial software*

帖文称，不管 AI 如何发展，商业化软件的模式依然成立，根本原因是人的精力有限。作者举例，一天能赚一万或十万美元的忙碌人士，不太可能晚上花一小时用 vibe coding 写出整理明天行程的工具，也不会自行研究 Herdr 新版本滑动手势的适配细节。帖文还强调，软件无论怎样变化，都需要人来维护，需要软件公司——包括 AI 原生公司——来维护。[^bb4044784208c49c]

The post states that no matter how AI develops, the commercial software model remains valid because human energy is limited. It gives the example that a busy person earning $10,000 or $100,000 a day is unlikely to spend one hour vibe-coding a tool to organize tomorrow's schedule, or to study the swipe-gesture adaptation details in a new Herdr version by themselves. The post also stresses that software, however it changes, still needs people to maintain it and software companies—including AI-native companies—to maintain it.[^bb4044784208c49c]
