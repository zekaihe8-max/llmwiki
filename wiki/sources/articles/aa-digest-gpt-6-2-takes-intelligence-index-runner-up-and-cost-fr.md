# AA 总评：GPT-6.2 夺情报指数亚军并占据成本前沿，编程评分持平或下滑

*AA Digest: GPT-6.2 Takes Intelligence Index Runner-Up and Cost Frontier, Coding Score Flat or Down*

「帖文称」OpenAI 于 2026 年 11 月 17 日发布 GPT-6.2 Astra 与 GPT-6.2 Sol，人工智能分析（Artificial Analysis）指出 GPT-6.2 Astra 以比 GPT-6 Astra 低 20% 的定价登上情报指数亚军，并占据大部分成本与输出 Token 帕累托前沿；在科学、知识、3D 与机器人基准中表现强劲，但编程 Agent 指数未见提升，Sol 甚至较前代下滑。[^df08b908c867800c]

The post says OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on November 17, 2026; Artificial Analysis states that GPT-6.2 Astra, priced 20% below GPT-6 Astra, ranks second on the Intelligence Index and occupies most of the cost and output-token Pareto frontiers, while performing strongly in science, knowledge, 3D, and robotics benchmarks, though the Coding Agent Index shows no improvement and Sol declines versus its predecessor.[^df08b908c867800c]

## 摘要 / Summary

- GPT-6.2 Astra 定价为每百万输入/输出 Token $8/$40，比 GPT-6 Astra 低 20%，缓存输入降至 $0.40；GPT-6.2 Sol 定价维持 $2/$10 不变。[^df08b908c867800c]
- GPT-6.2 Astra is priced at $8/$40 per million input/output tokens, 20% below GPT-6 Astra, with cached input down to $0.40; GPT-6.2 Sol pricing is unchanged at $2/$10.[^df08b908c867800c]
- 情报指数上，OpenAI 仅落后于 Claude Fable 5.2；Astra 比 GPT-6 Astra 提升 5 分，超越 Gemini 4 Carbon（60）与 Claude Opus 5.5（59）；Sol 得 57 分，约以 GPT-6 Astra 15% 的成本持平。[^df08b908c867800c]
- On the Intelligence Index, OpenAI trails only Claude Fable 5.2; Astra gains 5 points over GPT-6 Astra, overtaking Gemini 4 Carbon (60) and Claude Opus 5.5 (59); Sol scores 57, on par with GPT-6 Astra at roughly 15% of the cost.[^df08b908c867800c]
- 科学与知识方面，Astra 领先 Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1，在 CritPt v2 难题集中仅次于 Claude Fable 5.2，并在 AA-Omniscience v1.1 与 HLE-Diamond 中同样仅落后于 Claude Fable 5.2。[^df08b908c867800c]
- In science and knowledge, Astra leads Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, ranks second only to Claude Fable 5.2 in CritPt v2 hard subset, and trails only Claude Fable 5.2 in AA-Omniscience v1.1 and HLE-Diamond.[^df08b908c867800c]
- 3D 与机器人方面，Astra 在 AA-3D 得 1318 分（Claude Fable 5.2 1271，Sol 1259），并在 AA-Robotics v0.1 中以 79/78 分领先 Claude Fable 5.2 的 75 分。[^df08b908c867800c]
- In 3D and robotics, Astra scores 1318 on AA-3D (Claude Fable 5.2 at 1271, Sol at 1259), and leads AA-Robotics v0.1 with 79 and 78 points versus Claude Fable 5.2 at 75.[^df08b908c867800c]
- 编程 Agent 指数方面，Astra 与 GPT-6 Astra 持平，Sol 比 GPT-6.1 Sol 下降 3 分，降幅主要由 SWE-Atlas-QnA-AA 中 14 分的跌落驱动。[^df08b908c867800c]
- On the Coding Agent Index, Astra is flat versus GPT-6 Astra, while Sol drops 3 points versus GPT-6.1 Sol, driven by a 14-point decline in SWE-Atlas-QnA-AA.[^df08b908c867800c]
