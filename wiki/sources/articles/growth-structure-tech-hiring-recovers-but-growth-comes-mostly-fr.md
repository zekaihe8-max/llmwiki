# 增长结构：科技招聘回暖，但增长主要来自硬件与基础设施岗位

*Growth Structure: Tech Hiring Recovers, but Growth Comes Mostly from Hardware and Infrastructure Roles*

「帖文称」科技岗位市场处于近年最佳状态，科技公司空缺超过 28.1 万个，较一年前增长 17%，较 2023 年 3 月低点增长 73%。然而，产品、工程和设计岗位空缺仅增长 2%，设计岗位下降 28%、用户体验研究岗位下降 45%。增长主要来自硬件工程岗位翻倍、业务运营新增约 4200 个空缺以及供应链岗位增长 63%；数据中心技术员、安装工和现场服务工程师等实体岗位增速最快。AI 热潮创造的岗位多集中在芯片、数据中心和硬件等物理基础设施的建设与运营，而非软件开发。[^lenny-tech-job-market-growth-structure]

The post says the tech job market is in its best shape in years, with over 281,000 open roles at tech companies, up 17% year over year and 73% from the March 2023 low. However, open product, engineering, and design roles grew only 2%, with design down 28% and UX research down 45%. Growth mainly comes from hardware engineering openings doubling, business operations adding about 4,200 openings, and supply chain roles up 63%; physical roles such as data center technicians, installers, and field service engineers are growing fastest. Jobs created by the AI boom are concentrated in building and operating physical infrastructure such as chips, data centers, and hardware, not in software development.[^lenny-tech-job-market-growth-structure]
