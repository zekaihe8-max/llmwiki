# Instinct 个人 Agent：手机号注册与 A to A 体验

*Instinct Personal Agent: Phone Registration and A-to-A Experience*

帖文称，作者被 AJ 推荐的 Instinct 个人 Agent 说服，认为其细节体验出色，连简单的 A to A 交流都能带来 aha-moment，并建议不要一个人用。帖文还称该产品支持香港、美国等手机号注册，美国号可用 saily 的包年 11.99 美金 eSIM，只能接收短信不能发；注册后可绑定 iMessage、WhatsApp、Telegram 使用。若美国或香港手机号不能发信息，帖文称可把 iMessage 主号改成 Apple ID，哪怕使用国内账号。目前为邀请注册状态，评论区有邀请地址，能邀请 10 个人。[^vista8-instinct-personal-agent-phone-invite]

The post says the author was convinced by AJ's recommendation of Instinct, a personal agent product whose details feel impressive even in simple A-to-A exchanges, and suggests not using it alone. It also says the product supports registration with Hong Kong, US, and other phone numbers; a US number can use saily's $11.99-per-year eSIM that only receives SMS and cannot send; after registration it can bind iMessage, WhatsApp, and Telegram. If a US or Hong Kong phone number cannot send messages, the post says the iMessage main number can be changed to an Apple ID, even with a domestic account. It is currently invite-only, with an invite address in the comments that can invite 10 people.[^vista8-instinct-personal-agent-phone-invite]
