# Menlo 企业 AI 报告：支出 5.4 倍，Agent 与编程成主力

*Menlo Enterprise AI Report: Spend Up 5.4x, Agents and Coding Lead*

「帖文称」Menlo Ventures 发布第四份企业 AI 报告，企业 AI 支出一年增长 5.4 倍达到 1768 亿美元；Anthropic 企业 API 支出份额升至 55%，OpenAI 升至 31%，Google 跌至 9%。Agent 在 OpenRouter 上产生 80% 的 token，编程成为首个爆发场景，市场规模 340 亿美元，AI 原生公司拿下 96%。开源模型处理 19% 的 token 却只获 4% 支出，其中中国模型占开源支出 89%。[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]

The post cites Menlo Ventures' fourth enterprise AI report, saying enterprise AI spending grew 5.4x to $176.8 billion; Anthropic's share of enterprise API spending rose to 55%, OpenAI to 31%, and Google fell to 9%. Agents generate 80% of tokens on OpenRouter, coding is the first breakout scenario at $34 billion, and AI-native companies take 96% of that market. Open-source models handle 19% of tokens but receive only 4% of spending, with Chinese models accounting for 89% of open-source spending.[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 企业 AI 支出一年涨 5.4 倍，到 1768 亿美元；Anthropic、OpenAI、Google 三家拿走 69% 部署支出，共 1227 亿美元。 / Enterprise AI spending rose 5.4x to $176.8 billion; Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google took 69% of deployment spending, totaling $122.7 billion.[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]
- Agent 在 OpenRouter 上产生 80% 的 token，每周 130.9 万亿，约是人类使用量的 7 倍。 / Agents generate 80% of tokens on OpenRouter, 130.9 trillion per week, about 7x human usage.[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]
- 编程是 AI 应用市场最大场景，340 亿美元，增长 7.7 倍；Claude Code 与 Codex 合计 240 亿美元，占编程市场 70%。 / Coding is the largest AI application scenario at $34 billion, up 7.7x; Claude Code and Codex total $24 billion, 70% of the coding market.[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]
- 开源模型处理 19% 的 token，仅拿到 4% 支出，平均每百万 token 0.13 美元；中国模型占开源支出 89%（Kimi 61%、DeepSeek 15%、GLM 11%、Qwen 3%）。 / Open-source models process 19% of tokens but receive only 4% of spending, at $0.13 per million tokens; Chinese models account for 89% of open-source spending (Kimi 61%, DeepSeek 15%, GLM 11%, Qwen 3%).[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]
- 企业实际 AI 支出平均是预算的 4.7 倍，中位数 2.2 倍；每花 1 美元训练模型，企业约花 1.7 美元使用模型。 / Actual enterprise AI spending averaged 4.7x budget, median 2.2x; enterprises spend about $1.7 on inference for every $1 spent on training.[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]
- 使用代码沙箱的 AI 开发者从 11% 升至 40%，用搜索的从 5% 升至 30%，用浏览器基础设施的从 4% 升至 14%。 / AI developers using code sandboxes rose from 11% to 40%, search from 5% to 30%, and browser infrastructure from 4% to 14%.[^menlo-enterprise-ai-report]
