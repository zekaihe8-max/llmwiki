# Claude Ultra 改名与取消 5h 限额

*Claude Ultra Renamed and 5-Hour Limit Removed*

「帖文称」Anthropic 于 10 月 30 日宣布 Claude 两档 Max 套餐改名：每月 100 美元的 Max 5x 改为 Max，每月 200 美元的 Max 20x 改为 Ultra，价格不变且现有订阅自动更换名称。同时，已验证的 Ultra 账号不再有 5 小时限额，当天先对大部分现有订阅者生效，周限额不变；较新账号在验证前保留临时限额，取消时会收到邮件。[^claude-ultra-rename-cancel-5h-limit]

The post says Anthropic announced on October 30 that Claude's two Max plans are renamed: Max 5x at $100/month becomes Max, and Max 20x at $200/month becomes Ultra, with unchanged prices and automatic subscription migration. Verified Ultra accounts also lose the 5-hour limit, effective the same day for most existing subscribers, while the weekly quota stays the same; newer accounts keep a temporary limit until verification and then receive an email.[^claude-ultra-rename-cancel-5h-limit]
