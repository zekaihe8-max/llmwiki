# 用 Qwen4-Max 给侄子做会动的古诗立体书

*Making an Animated Pop-up Ancient Poem Book for a Nephew with Qwen4-Max*

「帖文称」作者用 Qwen4-Max 正式版和 VibeCoding 为小侄子制作了一本会动、会发声的古诗立体书，选了《望庐山瀑布》《江雪》《小池》《山行》四首，翻页时山水弹出，合上收回，点击可听朗诵并伴随诗句逐字高亮，整体按 8 分自动做、2 分人力调整完成。[^qwen4-max-ancient-poem-popup-book]

The post says the author used Qwen4-Max official version and VibeCoding to make a moving, sound-making pop-up book of ancient poems for his nephew, selecting four poems including "Viewing the Waterfall at Mount Lu," "River Snow," "Small Pond," and "Mountain Travel"; turning pages pops up landscapes that retract when closed, clicking plays recitation with character-by-character poem highlighting, completed at roughly 80% AI automation and 20% human adjustment.[^qwen4-max-ancient-poem-popup-book]
