# Amp 回应「人人都能推 main」：文化拦水货，Token 是新基建

*Amp Responds to "Anyone Can Push to Main": Culture Stops Slop, Tokens Are New Infrastructure*

帖文称，Amp 团队在 21 人公司规模下取消强制 PR 拦截，依靠高信任小团队、直接对客户负责与公开反馈来防止“水货”代码；大架构变更可在 Slack 线程和客户现场定稿，Agent 把适配器开发成本降至近乎零，Token 被视为新基建而非外挂。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]

The post says Amp, a 21-person company, removes mandatory PR gates and relies on high-trust small teams, direct customer accountability, and public feedback to prevent AI-era "slop" code; major architecture changes can be settled in a Slack thread and on-site customer visit, agents drive adapter development cost to near zero, and tokens are treated as new infrastructure rather than a cheat code.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Amp 团队仅 21 人，不靠强制 PR 拦截 AI 时代水货代码，而是靠小团队、皮肤在局、公开反馈与共同品质。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- Amp's 21-person team does not rely on mandatory PRs to block AI-era slop code, but on small teams, skin in the game, public feedback, and shared taste.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- 自托管 Orbs 的大架构变更在一条 Slack 线程和一次客户现场中定稿，新闻帖当设计文档反复迭代，最后一周上线。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- Major architecture changes for self-hosted Orbs were finalized in one Slack thread and one on-site customer visit, with news posts iterated as design documents and launched within a week.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- 适配器变便宜后，客户可用 Agent 半日写出自有 hypervisor 适配器，把最后 20% 留给客户自己补。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- After adapters became cheap, customers can use agents to write their own hypervisor adapters in half a day, leaving the last 20% for customers to complete themselves.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- 团队更看重 UI、图和端到端是否跑通，而非单测，用视频和截图当证据；人工评审未必拦住 bug，却可能拖五天。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- The team values whether UI, diagrams, and end-to-end flows run, rather than unit tests, using videos and screenshots as evidence; manual review may not stop bugs but can delay by five days.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- Token 不是外挂，是新基建；每月只肯花 50 刀 Token 却要和别人拼，本就不是公平赛场。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- Tokens are not a cheat code but new infrastructure; being unwilling to spend more than $50 a month on tokens while competing with others is inherently an unfair playing field.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- 文化是你奖励什么、开除什么；频道里实时表扬与叫停比被动攻击式 PR 评论更管用。[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
- Culture is what you reward and what you fire; real-time praise and call-outs in channels are more effective than passive-aggressive PR comments.[^amp-response-everyone-can-push-main]
