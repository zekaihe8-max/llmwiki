*Alibaba Cloud Token Plan Personal Edition Increases Quotas by About 74% to 78%, Prices Unchanged*

The post says Alibaba Cloud Token Plan Personal Edition Lite, Essential, and Standard tiers keep prices unchanged while quotas rise by about 74% to 78%; the Pro tier monthly fee drops from 499 yuan to 312 yuan and quota rises from 180,000 to 200,000 Credits, all four tiers' subscription cost per Credit drops by about 43%, and new quotas will roll out to existing users and top up the current subscription cycle.[^aliyun-token-plan-personal-quota-increase]
