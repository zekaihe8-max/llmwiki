# Antigravity 配额重置：关注 Varun 而非 Tibo

*Antigravity Quota Reset: Follow Varun, Not Tibo*

宝玉在 X 上发帖称，若关心 Antigravity 的配额重置，不应盯着 Tibo，而应关注 Varun。帖文引用 Varun Mohan 表示，Antigravity 正在大幅扩容缓存池，使 Neon 的缓存命中率提升约 4%，能让同等配额下可完成的任务量至少提升 20%；由于之前配额消耗过快，更新完成后会重置一次配额。[^antigravity-quota-reset-varun]

On X, 宝玉 (@dotey) said users concerned about the Antigravity reset should not watch Tibo but should follow Varun. The post quotes Varun Mohan saying Antigravity is massively increasing its cache pool size, raising Neon's cache hit rate by around 4% and enabling at least 20% more work under the same quota; because quota burned faster previously, a reset will be issued after the change is done.[^antigravity-quota-reset-varun]
