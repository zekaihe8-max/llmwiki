# 1000 个 Claude Fable 5.2 智能体重做费马大定理形式化证明

*1000 Claude Fable 5.2 Agents Redo Formal Proof of Fermat's Last Theorem*

帖文称，Anthropic 的玩具实验中，1000 个早期 Claude Fable 5.2 智能体没有 Prove2Me 平台，也没有研究员当场指挥，仅使用通用工具和共享数据库，便在 28.7 小时内重做了今年 9 月的费马大定理形式化证明；输出 token 约为当时的三分之一，写出的证明大约是七分之一长。中文全文预计阅读时长约 18 分钟，收录于 11.10，原文发布于 11.09。[^anthropic-fable-5-2-fermat-formal-proof]

The post claims that in Anthropic's toy experiment, 1000 early Claude Fable 5.2 agents had no Prove2Me platform and no researchers directing on-site; using only general tools and a shared database, they redid the September formal proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 28.7 hours, with output tokens about one-third and proof length about one-seventh of the original. The Chinese full text is estimated at 18 minutes reading time, collected on 11.10 and originally published on 11.09.[^anthropic-fable-5-2-fermat-formal-proof]
