# Anthropic IPO：ANT 首日上市市值约 1.8 万亿美元

*Anthropic IPO takeaways: ANT debuts with $1.8 trillion market cap in landmark moment for AI*

CNBC 报道称，Anthropic（股票代码 ANT）于 2026 年 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克首日交易中上涨 17%，收盘价 97.17 美元，完全稀释市值约 1.8 万亿美元，成为美国第七大贵重公司。该公司此次 IPO 筹资 813 亿美元，发行 9.8 亿股，每股 83 美元，开盘价 111.50 美元。Amazon 和 Google 合计持有约 28% 股份，Nvidia 亦通过私募配售购买约 100 亿美元的 Class N 普通股。[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]

CNBC reports that Anthropic (ticker ANT) jumped 17% in its Nasdaq debut on November 19, 2026, closing at $97.17 per share with a fully diluted market cap of roughly $1.8 trillion, making it the seventh most valuable U.S. company. The IPO raised $81.3 billion through the sale of 980 million shares at $83 each, opening at $111.50. Amazon and Google together own about 28% of shares, while Nvidia purchased roughly $10 billion of Class N common stock in a private placement.[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Anthropic 首日收盘市值约 1.8 万亿美元，位列美国第七大贵重公司，仅次于 Apple、Microsoft、Nvidia、Alphabet、Amazon 和 SpaceX。[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- Anthropic closed its first trading day with a roughly $1.8 trillion fully diluted market cap, ranking as the seventh most valuable U.S. company behind Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Amazon and SpaceX.[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- IPO 筹资 813 亿美元，发行 9.8 亿股，每股定价 83 美元，开盘价 111.50 美元，收盘价 97.17 美元，涨幅 17%。[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- The IPO raised $81.3 billion by selling 980 million shares at $83 each; the stock opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, up 17%.[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- Amazon 持有约 14.7%，Google 持有约 13.7%，两者合计持股约 28%。[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- Amazon owns about 14.7% and Google about 13.7%, together holding roughly 28% of outstanding capital stock.[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- Nvidia 紧随发行完成后通过私募配售购买约 100 亿美元的 Anthropic Class N 普通股。[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- Nvidia agreed to purchase roughly $10 billion worth of Anthropic's Class N common stock in a private placement immediately following the offering.[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- 公司称过去九个月营收 332.4 亿美元，同比增长 1200%；2021 年由 Dario Amodei 等前 OpenAI 研究人员创立。[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
- The company reported $33.24 billion in revenue for the nine months ended Sept. 30, up 1,200% year over year; it was founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei and other former OpenAI researchers.[^anthropic-ipo-ant-debut]
