# 思科总裁让 27 岁员工不限量 AI 重写 98% 老代码

*Cisco President Lets 27-Year-Old Use Unlimited AI to Rewrite 98% of Legacy Code*

帖文称，思科总裁兼首席产品官 Jeetu Patel 在 Fortune 晚宴上透露，将公司最大工程之一交给27岁的 EzDubs 创始人 Paddy，给予无限 AI Token，目标在24个月内重写思科98%的遗留代码；Patel 还主张招募更少经验的年轻人，让他们以“反向导师”身份教老员工用 AI 思考。[^cisco-27-year-old-ai-legacy-code-rewrite]

The post says Cisco President and Chief Product Officer Jeetu Patel revealed at a Fortune dinner that he entrusted one of the company's largest engineering projects to 27-year-old EzDubs founder Paddy, granting unlimited AI tokens with a goal to rewrite 98% of Cisco's legacy code within 24 months; Patel also advocates hiring younger, less experienced people to serve as “reverse mentors” teaching veteran employees to think with AI.[^cisco-27-year-old-ai-legacy-code-rewrite]

- Patel 给 Paddy 的条件是 AI Token 不限量，先用四周证明可行，最终目标是 24 个月内重写思科 98% 的遗留代码。[^cisco-27-year-old-ai-legacy-code-rewrite]
- Patel's condition for Paddy was unlimited AI tokens, with a four-week proof of concept and a final goal of rewriting 98% of Cisco's legacy code within 24 months.[^cisco-27-year-old-ai-legacy-code-rewrite]
- Paddy 2023 年与两个合伙人创办 EzDubs，做实时翻译 App，支持通话时将语音翻译成 30 多种语言并保留原声和语气。[^cisco-27-year-old-ai-legacy-code-rewrite]
