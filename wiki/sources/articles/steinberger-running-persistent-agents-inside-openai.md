# 斯坦伯格：在 OpenAI 内运行持久智能体

*Steinberger: Running Persistent Agents Inside OpenAI*

「帖文称」彼得·斯坦伯格（@steipete）表示其团队撰文介绍如何在 OpenAI 内部运行持久智能体；他还引用凯文·林（@kevins8）11 月 12 日的帖文，称像 Openclaw 或 Dots 这类持久智能体的部署对企业 IT 构成重大挑战，因为智能体更像承包商而非传统 SaaS 软件，能够在后台无需人工干预地主动采取行动，并有能力完成整项工作。[^steinberger-persistent-agents-openai]

The post says Peter Steinberger (@steipete) stated his team wrote about how they run persistent agents inside OpenAI; he also cited Kevin S Lin (@kevins8)'s November 12 post, noting that deploying persistent agents like Openclaw or Dots poses a major challenge for enterprise IT because agents are more like contractors than traditional SaaS software, able to take proactive actions in the background without human intervention and capable of fulfilling entire jobs.[^steinberger-persistent-agents-openai]
