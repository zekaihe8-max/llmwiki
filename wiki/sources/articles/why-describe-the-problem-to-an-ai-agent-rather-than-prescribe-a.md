# 为什么要向 AI 代理讲清问题而非方案：一个提升面谈质量的技巧

*Why describe the problem to an AI agent rather than prescribe a solution: one tip for better grilling*

帖文称，提升与 AI 代理“面谈（grilling）”质量的一个技巧是，多解释“为什么”，少谈“什么”。作者以管理员分析面板为例：直接提出带图表和筛选的解决方案，会让 AI 只停留在实现层面盘问；而先说明管理员目前无法查看重要数据概览这一问题，则能给 AI 更多发挥空间，从而产生更深入、更好的对话。[^a86d212a680036ca]

The post says one tip to improve your “grilling” of an AI agent is to explain more of the “why” rather than the “what.” Using an admin analytics panel as an example, the author argues that prescribing a solution with graphs and filters makes the agent focus only on implementation, whereas describing the problem—admins lacking an overview of important data—gives the agent license to be more creative and leads to a deeper, better conversation.[^a86d212a680036ca]
