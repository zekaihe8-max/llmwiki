# Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash 正式商用

*Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash Generally Available*

帖文称，Google 于 11 月 5 日发布 Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash 视频模型，单条最长可生成 15 秒带声音视频，720p 约 1 美元，比 1.1 版降价 30%，且是 Omni 首次正式商用（GA），可用于生产环境。开发者可在 Google AI Studio 试用并通过 Gemini API 接入，企业走 Google Cloud；普通用户可在 Google Vids 生成 15 秒片段，Gemini App 正逐步推送给 AI Ultra 订阅用户。帖文还称，从 12 月 3 日起调用 1.1 的请求将自动转给 1.2 并按 1.2 价格收费，API 免费层不可用；在 Runway 开发者平台上，Omni 1.2 以每秒 0.07 美元的价格低于 Veo、Seedance、可灵等模型。[^eebd9f1863fcf87b]

According to the post, Google released Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash on November 5, a video model generating up to 15-second clips with sound at about $1 per 720p video, 30% cheaper than version 1.1 and now generally available for production use. Developers can try it in Google AI Studio via the Gemini API, while enterprises use Google Cloud; regular users can generate 15-second clips in Google Vids at no extra charge, and the Gemini App is rolling out to AI Ultra subscribers. The post also says calls to 1.1 will be automatically routed to 1.2 starting December 3 at 1.2 pricing, the API free tier is unavailable, and on Runway’s developer platform Omni 1.2 at $0.07 per second is priced below Veo, Seedance, and Kling.[^eebd9f1863fcf87b]
