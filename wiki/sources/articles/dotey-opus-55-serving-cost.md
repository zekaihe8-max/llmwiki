# 宝玉谈 Opus 5.5 成本约 $0.6/$3.0

*Dotey on Opus 5.5 serving cost around $0.6/$3.0*

[@dotey](https://x.com/dotey) 称 Opus 5.5 的成本价大概是 $0.6/$3.0，比很多模型都便宜。数据来源是 Anthropic 于 2026 年 10 月 26 日向 SEC 提交的 S-1：上半年营收约 162.86 亿美元、收入成本约 23.80 亿美元，分析师据此推导约 85.4% 毛利率；同一数字也出现在 10 月中旬投资者会议材料中，由 Bloomberg 等报道为调整后或 GAAP 毛利率约 85%。帖内引用 [@petergostev](https://x.com/petergostev)：大厂推理效率被严重低估；Anthropic 毛利率 85%，故服务 Opus 5.5 成本约 $0.60 / $3.00——低于 OpenAI 对 6.1 Terra 的收费，也远低于 Muse、Gemini、Kimi 等。[^dotey-opus-55-serving-cost] 原帖 <https://x.com/dotey/status/2115286764294214011>。

[@dotey](https://x.com/dotey) says Opus 5.5’s cost price is roughly $0.6/$3.0—cheaper than many models. The figure comes from Anthropic’s S-1 filed with the SEC on 2026-10-26: H1 revenue about $16.286B and cost of revenue about $2.380B, from which analysts derive ~85.4% gross margin; the same number appeared in mid-October investor materials, reported by Bloomberg and peers as adjusted or GAAP gross margin around 85%. The post quotes [@petergostev](https://x.com/petergostev): big-lab inference efficiency is badly underestimated; with Anthropic’s 85% gross margin it costs about $0.60 / $3.00 to serve Opus 5.5—less than what OpenAI charges for 6.1 Terra and much less than Muse, Gemini, Kimi, and peers.[^dotey-opus-55-serving-cost] Post: <https://x.com/dotey/status/2115286764294214011>.
