# 手把手教你把Skill挂上支付宝挣钱，好手艺不该只有免费开源

*Step-by-Step Guide to Monetizing Skills on Alipay SkillPay: Good Craftsmanship Shouldn't Be Only Free and Open Source*

帖文称，支付宝于今年7月上线SkillPay技能交易平台，创作者可将Skill文件夹上架并设定买断价（0.01-50元）或订阅价（周/月/季，上限50元，免费试用最少5次），订阅制Skill保留在云端沙盒不对用户可见，平台暂不收服务费但即刻收服务收取1%手续费。[^5fd46825f0820c56]

The post claims Alipay launched the SkillPay skill trading platform in July this year, allowing creators to upload Skill folders and set one-time buyout prices (0.01-50 RMB) or subscription prices (weekly/monthly/quarterly, capped at 50 RMB, minimum 5 free trial uses); subscription Skills remain in a cloud sandbox invisible to users, the platform currently charges no service fee but the instant collection service takes a 1% cut.[^5fd46825f0820c56]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Skill本质是一个包含SKILL.md的文件夹，里面写明“知道什么、怎么判断、怎么做”，但内容全是明文，难以直接付费销售。[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- A Skill is essentially a folder containing SKILL.md specifying what to know, how to judge, and how to act, but its content is fully plaintext, making direct paid sales difficult.[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- 支付宝SkillPay提供买断制和订阅制两种卖法，买断定价区间为0.01-50元，订阅支持按周、月或季度收费，上限同样为50元。[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- Alipay SkillPay offers one-time buyout and subscription models; buyout pricing ranges from 0.01 to 50 RMB, while subscriptions support weekly, monthly, or quarterly billing, also capped at 50 RMB.[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- 订阅制Skill的完整内容保留在云端沙盒中，Agent只能收到本次任务所需的返回指令，看不到Skill明文。[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- For subscription Skills, the full content stays in a cloud sandbox; the Agent only receives the instructions needed for the current task and cannot see the Skill's plaintext.[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- 创作者上架前需签署SkillPay产品协议和即刻收产品协议，平台本身暂不收服务费，但即刻收服务收取1%费用。[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- Creators must sign the SkillPay product agreement and the Instant Collection product agreement before listing; the platform currently charges no service fee, but Instant Collection carries a 1% fee.[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- 购买后用户可在WorkBuddy、豆包工作、Codex、Claude Code等不同Agent平台使用同一Skill，且支持购买前试用。[^5fd46825f0820c56]
- After purchase, users can use the same Skill across Agent platforms such as WorkBuddy, Doubao Work, Codex, and Claude Code, and can try before buying.[^5fd46825f0820c56]
