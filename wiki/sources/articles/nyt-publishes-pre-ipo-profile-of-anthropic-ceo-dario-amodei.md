*NYT publishes pre-IPO profile of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei*

帖文称，纽约时报 11 月 5 日发布 Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei 的长篇人物稿，赶在 Anthropic 本月上市之前。由于 Dario 通过公司发言人拒绝受访，文章主要依据二十多位同学、朋友、老师和同事的回忆，以及早期邮件和 OpenAI 内部消息。帖文梳理了 Dario 在理性主义和有效利他主义圈子的早期经历、在 OpenAI 的立场变化、与妹妹 Daniela 共同创办 Anthropic 的过程，以及他对芯片出口管制、AI 监管和军事应用的公开主张。[^dotey-2119191652770226525]

The post says the NYT published a long profile of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei on November 5, ahead of Anthropic's IPO this month. Since Dario declined to be interviewed through a spokesperson, the article relies mainly on recollections from over two dozen classmates, friends, teachers, and colleagues, plus early emails and OpenAI internal messages. The post traces Dario's early involvement in rationalist and effective altruism circles, his evolving stance at OpenAI, co-founding Anthropic with his sister Daniela, and his public positions on chip export controls, AI regulation, and military applications.[^dotey-2119191652770226525]
