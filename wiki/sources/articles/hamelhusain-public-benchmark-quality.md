# Hamel Husain 谈公开榜质量差却被业界高度依赖

*Hamel Husain on poor public-benchmark quality vs industry reliance*

[@HamelHusain](https://x.com/HamelHusain) 称自己在看公开基准，对其质量之差相对业界依赖程度感到吃惊；[@xeophon](https://x.com/xeophon) 向他推荐独立第三方站点 [epoch.ai/benchmarks/](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/)，审榜并「看数据」。他举例若干大家常用的热门基准，并主张：采信前值得自行跑测，仔细检查 grader 判错的样例以感受质量。他仍在收集材料，打算日后写成文，并认为这是重要议题。[^hamelhusain-public-benchmark-quality] 原帖见 <https://x.com/HamelHusain/status/2114434195854684330>。

[@HamelHusain](https://x.com/HamelHusain) says he has been looking into public benchmarks and is astounded by the poor quality versus how much the industry relies on them; [@xeophon](https://x.com/xeophon) pointed him to the independent third-party site [epoch.ai/benchmarks/](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/), which scrutinizes benchmarks and “looks at the data.” He cites popular benchmarks people rely on and argues that before trusting a benchmark it is worth running them and carefully inspecting examples the grader failed, to sense quality. He is still collecting data and plans to write something up eventually, calling the subject important.[^hamelhusain-public-benchmark-quality] Post: <https://x.com/HamelHusain/status/2114434195854684330>.
