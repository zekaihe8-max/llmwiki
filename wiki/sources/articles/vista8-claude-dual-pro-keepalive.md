# 向阳乔木谈 Claude 保活：双 Pro 轮换与自有域名邮

*vista8 on Claude keepalive—dual Pro rotation and custom-domain email*

[@vista8](https://x.com/vista8)（向阳乔木）称感觉自己运气好，Claude 账号一个都没被封过，并强调不是凡尔赛。他推测可能是因为没开 200 刀账号、一直用 20 刀档，开两个换着用，并列出日常做法供参考。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive] 原帖见 <https://x.com/vista8/status/2115487808404230154>。

[@vista8](https://x.com/vista8) (向阳乔木) says he feels lucky—none of his Claude accounts have been banned, and he stresses it is not bragging. He guesses it may be because he never opened a $200 account and has stayed on $20 tiers, running two and alternating, and shares his routine for reference.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive] Post: <https://x.com/vista8/status/2115487808404230154>.

## 摘要 / Summary

- 档位策略：未开 200 刀档；一直用 **20 刀 Pro**，**两个账号轮换**。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Tier strategy: no $200 tier; stays on **$20 Pro**, **two accounts rotated**.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- 注册邮箱：用**自有域名邮箱**，经 **Cloudflare Email Routing** 路由到 Gmail；只需把域名绑到 Cloudflare，在 Email Service → Email Routing → Routing rules 创建邮箱。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Registration email: **custom-domain** address routed to Gmail via **Cloudflare Email Routing**; bind the domain to Cloudflare, then create the mailbox under Email Service → Email Routing → Routing rules.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- 网络：用自己 **Vultr** 服务器搭的代理 IP，称日常用此似乎也不会跳验证。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Network: a proxy IP from his own **Vultr** server; he says daily use on it seemingly does not trigger extra verification.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- 备用：用免费 Claude 账号正常对话，可多备 **2–3 个**。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Spares: use free Claude accounts normally; keep **2–3** extras.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- 支付：iOS 海外 App Store 账号下载 Claude 客户端，App Store **充值余额**用余额付 20 刀订阅；Android 手机绑定**众安银行卡**到 Google Play 直接升级。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Payment: overseas iOS App Store account for the Claude app, **top up App Store balance** to pay the $20 sub; Android binds a **ZhongAn Bank** card to Google Play to upgrade directly.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- 使用习惯：未刻意只用 Claude CLI，日常用官方客户端；**未改电脑时区、未改语言**，对话一直用中文。[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Usage habits: no deliberate CLI-only use—official client day to day; **did not change PC timezone or language**; chats stay in Chinese.[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- 作者自问：可能是**幸存者偏差**？[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
- Author asks: possible **survivorship bias**?[^vista8-claude-dual-pro-keepalive]
