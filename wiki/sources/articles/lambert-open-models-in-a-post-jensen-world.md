# Lambert · 没有老黄之后的开源模型

*Lambert · Open Models in a Post-Jensen World*

Nathan Lambert 在 X 上发帖称，开源模型世界在“后老黄”（post-Jensen）时代面临两大主要挑战：一是政治曝光，开源模型在华盛顿的盟友正在撤退；二是投资，开源模型构建者需要在没有其支持的情况下更早找到盈利产品。帖文称这种影响将比人们预期的更早到来。[^natolambert-post-jensen-open-models]

Nathan Lambert posted on X that the open model world faces two main challenges in a post-Jensen world: political exposure, as open models’ allies retreat in Washington, DC, and investment pressure, as open model builders must find profitable products sooner without his backstop. The post says the impact will be felt sooner than expected.[^natolambert-post-jensen-open-models]
