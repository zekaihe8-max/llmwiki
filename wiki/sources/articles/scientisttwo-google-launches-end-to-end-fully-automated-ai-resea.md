# ScientistTwo：Google 推出端到端全自动科研 AI 框架

*ScientistTwo: Google Launches End-to-End Fully Automated AI Research Framework*

「帖文称」Google 推出的 ScientistTwo 是一套端到端全自动科研 AI 框架，能自动挖掘顶会论文短板、提出新方案、按小数据集到全量数据节奏跑实验、做消融分析，并模拟同行评审与申诉答辩反复打磨论文。该帖还称在 NeurIPS、ICML、ICLR 等 107 个课题实测中，80.4% 任务超越人类顶尖水平，平均提升 25.2%，生成论文可复现且第三方 AI 评审工具给出 72% 以上顶会接收标准，平均耗时约 2.5 天。[^scientist-two-google-auto-research]

The post claims Google's ScientistTwo is an end-to-end fully automated AI research framework that automatically identifies shortcomings in top conference papers, proposes new solutions, runs experiments from small to full datasets, performs ablation analysis, and simulates peer review and appeal defense to iteratively polish papers. It also claims that in tests on 107 high-difficulty topics targeting NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, 80.4% of tasks surpassed human top-level performance with an average improvement of 25.2%; generated papers are reproducible and scored above 72% top-conference acceptance standards in third-party AI review tools, taking about 2.5 days per paper on average.[^scientist-two-google-auto-research]
