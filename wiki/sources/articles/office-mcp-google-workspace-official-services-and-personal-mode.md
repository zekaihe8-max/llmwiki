# 办公 MCP：谷歌 Workspace 官方服务与个人模式转正

*Office MCP: Google Workspace Official Services and Personal Mode Go GA*

「帖文称」谷歌已将 Gmail、云盘、文档、表格、演示文稿、日历、聊天、任务等 8 个官方 MCP 服务与命令行工具 gwscli 正式转正，并推出个人模式，免企业版和绑卡，每个产品每日白送 5000 次调用。用户可通过个人模式向导一键打通自有谷歌账号，跳过应用审核，再用 Claude、Antigravity 或终端助手 Claude Code、Codex 接入官方远程 MCP；发邮件、对外分享、删除等操作会触发人工确认锁。[^google-workspace-mcp-personal-mode]

The post says Google has generally availableed eight official Workspace MCP servers—Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat, and Tasks—plus the gwscli CLI, with a new 1-click Personal mode that skips app review and requires no Enterprise plan or billing card, offering 5,000 free requests per product per day. Sensitive actions such as sending mail, sharing externally, or moving items to trash pause for human confirmation.[^google-workspace-mcp-personal-mode]
