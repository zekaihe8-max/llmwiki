# Muse 视频生成：先稳单镜头人脸，再拆分镜头与运镜精修

*Muse Video Generation: Stabilize Single-Shot Faces First, Then Split Shots and Refine Camera Movement*

帖文称，使用 Muse 生成视频时换镜头容易导致人脸不稳，作者蓝哥AI建议不要急着重抽整条，而应先拿一张干净正面照做 6 秒单动作测试，脸稳后再加剧情；不满意时在原对话里一次只改一个点，避免同时改光线、表情和动作。30 秒短片建议拆成 6 个 5 秒镜头，复杂运镜先做动作预演，再拿参考脸精修。先把一个镜头做对，比反复赌整条片子更省心。[^muse-video-steady-shot-order]

The post says that changing shots in Muse-generated video often destabilizes the face; author Blue Brother AI advises against regenerating the whole clip and instead recommends starting with a clean front-facing photo for a 6-second single-action test, adding plot only after the face stabilizes. If unsatisfied, change one variable at a time in the original conversation rather than adjusting lighting, expression, and action together. A 30-second short should be split into six 5-second shots; for complex camera moves, rehearse the action first, then refine with a reference face. Getting one shot right is easier than repeatedly gambling on the entire video.[^muse-video-steady-shot-order]
