# Thinking Machines 发布 Inkling 2 Small 开源模型

*Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Open Model*

帖文称，OpenAI 前 CTO Mira Murati 创办的 Thinking Machines 发布新模型 Inkling 2 Small，Artificial Analysis 综合评分达 31 分，较上一代 Inkling-Small 的 13 分提升 18 分，成为美国得分最高的开源模型。该模型总参数 5940 亿、每次推理激活 170 亿，Agent 自动化任务通过率从 5% 提升至 46%，编程测试 FrontierCode 2.0 从 5% 提升至 25.4%，但仍低于 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 的 28.5%。帖文还称模型经 9 天强化学习训练、生成超 1700 万条训练轨迹，单项任务平均输出约 15 万 Token、评测成本从 0.20 美元涨至 1.18 美元、耗时约 21 分钟。目前仅在 Tinker 开放推理和微调，完整权重计划下周以 Apache 2.0 许可证开源。[^030fb114a196576f]

The post says Thinking Machines, founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, released Inkling 2 Small, scoring 31 on Artificial Analysis—up 18 from predecessor Inkling-Small's 13 and reportedly the highest-scoring open model in the U.S. The model has 594 billion total parameters with 17 billion activated per inference; agent automation task pass rate rose from 5% to 46%, and FrontierCode 2.0 from 5% to 25.4%, still below DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's 28.5%. The post also claims 9 days of RL training with over 17 million trajectories, average output of ~150k tokens per task, eval cost rising from $0.20 to $1.18, and ~21 minutes per task. Inference and fine-tuning are available on Tinker now, with full weights planned for Apache 2.0 release next week.[^030fb114a196576f]
