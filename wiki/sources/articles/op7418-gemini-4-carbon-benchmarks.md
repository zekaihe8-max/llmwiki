[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418) (歸藏) reacts with surprise to Google’s release and asks whether the scores are correct. He says Google shipped another Gemini 4 model, Gemini 4 Carbon; on **almost every eval it beats** Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5, with Terminal Bench 4.0 **far above** the other models he names.
