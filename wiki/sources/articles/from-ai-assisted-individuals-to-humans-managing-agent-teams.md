# 从 AI 辅助个人转向人类管理智能体团队

*From AI-Assisted Individuals to Humans Managing Agent Teams*

「帖文称」Aaron Levie 认为，越来越多角色的新工作描述将不再是“在 AI 辅助下提升个人产出”，而是“帮助智能体完成工作”，人人都将成为智能体团队的经理而非单纯的个人贡献者。这一转变最先影响编码、网络安全、法律、生物科技、工程等信息密集型岗位，并使杠杆与工时脱钩，智能体可并行执行任务，早期适应的个人与组织将获得复合收益。[^levie-manager-of-agents]

Aaron Levie argues that an increasing number of roles are shifting from “using AI to incrementally improve personal output” to “helping agents do their work,” turning everyone into a manager of a team of agents rather than an individual contributor with AI leverage. The change will hit information-heavy jobs such as coding, cybersecurity, legal work, biotech, and engineering first, decoupling leverage from hours worked and enabling agents to parallelize tasks; early adopters will see compounding gains.[^levie-manager-of-agents]
