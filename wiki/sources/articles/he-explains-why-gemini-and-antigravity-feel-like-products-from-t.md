# 他解释为什么 Gemini 和 Antigravity 用起来像两家公司的产品：三套班子挂在同一张会员卡上

*He explains why Gemini and Antigravity feel like products from two companies: three teams sharing one membership card*

帖文称，Google Gemini 与 Google Antigravity（AGY）用起来像两家公司的产品，根本原因是内部版图分裂：Gemini App 由原 Google Labs 掌门人 Josh Woodward 率领的谷歌原生产品团队负责，AGY 则由谷歌花 24 亿美元挖来的前 Windsurf 核心成员 Varun Mohan、Kevin Hou 等独立军团开发，计费又由 Google One 团队统一扣费，三套班子挂在同一张会员卡上，导致对话记忆、云盘、交互逻辑与配额校验完全隔离。[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]

The post says Google Gemini and Antigravity (AGY) feel like products from different companies because of an internal split: Gemini App is run by Google's native product team led by former Google Labs head Josh Woodward, AGY is developed by an independent team poached from Windsurf for $2.4 billion led by Varun Mohan and Kevin Hou, and billing is handled by Google One—three teams sharing one membership card, causing conversation memory, cloud storage, interaction logic, and quota validation to be fully isolated.[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Gemini App 归 Josh Woodward 统管，背负十亿月活大众入口指标，对话、长期记忆和办公全绑在谷歌统一云端账号图谱上。[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- Gemini App is led by Josh Woodward, carries the billion-MAU consumer entry metric, and binds all conversations, long-term memory, and office tools to Google's unified cloud account graph.[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- AGY 由前 Windsurf CEO Varun Mohan 和核心干将 Kevin Hou 带队，谷歌花 24 亿美元将其整建制挖入 DeepMind，四个月搓出 AGY 并退役吞并了谷歌原生 Gemini CLI。[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- AGY is led by former Windsurf CEO Varun Mohan and Kevin Hou; Google paid $2.4 billion to bring the whole team into DeepMind, built AGY in four months, and retired/absorbed Google's native Gemini CLI.[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- AGY 底座按本地工程沙箱设计，项目知识库、任务清单和录屏产物跟着本地目录走，未接入 Gemini 云端记忆系统，且获准在谷歌地盘内接入对手 Claude 5.5 模型。[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- AGY's base is designed around local engineering sandboxes; project knowledge bases, task lists, and recording artifacts follow local directories, it does not connect to Gemini's cloud memory system, and it is permitted to integrate rival Claude 5.5 models within Google.[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- 计费由 Google One 管扣费、Gemini 管日常权益、AGY 单独写配额校验，出现买了 Pro 在 Gemini 能用 Neon、进 AGY 却卡死在初始额度的情况。[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- Billing is split: Google One collects payment, Gemini manages daily benefits, and AGY writes its own quota checks, resulting in cases where Pro users can use Neon in Gemini but hit initial quota limits in AGY.[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- 帖文认为这种割裂是谷歌为抢速度故意留下的口子，避免新团队先打通十亿级产品云端底座，以换取没有大厂包袱的编程利器。[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
- The post argues this split is a deliberate opening Google left to move fast, avoiding forcing the new team to integrate billion-scale cloud infrastructure first, in exchange for a coding tool without corporate baggage.[^xiaomovps-gemini-antigravity-split]
