# UU远程公布12月1日会员收费方案：基础远控免费，VIP五折后119元/年

*UU Remote announces December 1 membership pricing: basic remote control free, VIP 119 yuan/year after discount*

「帖文称」UU远程今天正式官宣会员收费方案，12月1日上线；基础远控继续免费，不限连接次数、时长，不排队也不塞广告，桌面画质上限调至1080P 30帧。付费方面，VIP年付238元，老用户领五折券后119元/年，解锁4K 144帧与500Mbps码率；SVIP年付758元，老用户五折后379元/年，额外增加全球远控加速节点。鸿蒙版与Linux版内测期间付费功能全部免费。[^067c41b3820784f2]

The post says UU Remote officially announced its membership pricing plan effective December 1; basic remote control remains free with unlimited connections and duration, no queue or ads, and desktop quality capped at 1080P 30fps. VIP costs 238 yuan/year, or 119 yuan/year after a 50% old-user coupon, unlocking 4K 144fps and 500Mbps bitrate; SVIP costs 758 yuan/year, or 379 yuan/year after the discount, adding global remote-control acceleration nodes. All paid features are free during HarmonyOS and Linux beta tests.[^067c41b3820784f2]
