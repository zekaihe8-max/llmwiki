# 行动先于结果

*Action Before Results*

帖文称，行动永远先于结果；在结果尚未发生时，不应以先验态度评判，而应设计实验并等待结果。帖文还称，生活上应尽可能反其道而行之，因为实验越理性，浪漫主义的真实生活越珍贵，并认为 AI 时代毫无疑问是 ADHD 患者的黄金时代。[^action-before-results-1af9fcb695efeb63]

The post says that action always precedes results; before outcomes occur, one should not judge them with prior attitudes but design experiments and wait for results. It adds that life should run counter to this as much as possible, because the more rational the experiment, the more precious romantic real life becomes, and it calls the AI era undoubtedly a golden age for people with ADHD.[^action-before-results-1af9fcb695efeb63]
