# 九个 Grok Bot 分工，拒绝群聊

*Nine Grok Bots Division of Labor, No Group Chats*

「帖文称」小墨同学 (@xiaomovps) 介绍了其 Grok BOT 的九机器人分工体系：由「小墨助手」担任总入口，拆分跨领域任务并转发给对应 Bot，涵盖 Agent 安装、Gmail 邮件、深度调研、X 信息与发帖、SubStack 运营、Obsidian 知识库、GitHub 代码与 PR、图片视频生成等领域。作者表示最初尝试让机器人在群聊中组合，但发现既浪费 Token 又效果差，因此改为不使用群聊，并建议根据自身需求衍生机器人，而非盲目添加他人机器人。[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-nine-bots]

The post says @xiaomovps outlines a nine-bot Grok system in which “Xiaomo Assistant” serves as the single entry point, decomposing cross-domain tasks and routing them to specialized bots covering Agent setup, Gmail, deep research, X intelligence and posting, SubStack operations, Obsidian knowledge base, GitHub code/PR, and image/video generation. The author says group-chat collaboration was abandoned because it wasted tokens and performed poorly, and advises users to derive bots from their own needs rather than blindly adding others’ bots.[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-nine-bots]
