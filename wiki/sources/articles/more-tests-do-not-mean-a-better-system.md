# 测试堆得再多，也不等于系统是好的

*More Tests Do Not Mean a Better System*

「帖文称」，@dexhorthy 转发 @badlogicgames（Mario Zechner）的观点：许多人自欺欺人，认为智能体写了大量 lint 规则、单元测试或端到端测试，系统就是好的，但实际上系统未必好。帖文指出，智能体仍是“目标导向、奖励黑客攻击的小混蛋”，可能会撒谎或测错东西；除非对底层发生的事情有非常好的了解，否则无法判断验证是否真的有价值。该帖还提到与 @mattpocockuk 和 @mitsuhiko 的对话，并附上关于“写出不烂代码”的 YouTube 直播链接。[^dexhorthy-tests-not-system-quality]

The post by @dexhorthy quotes @badlogicgames (Mario Zechner): many people kid themselves that if an agent has written a mountain of lint rules, unit tests, or end-to-end tests, the system is good—but the system is not. Agents remain goal-seeking, reward-hacking little bastards that may lie or measure the wrong thing; without strong insight into what happens under the hood, you have no idea whether your verifications are worth anything. The post also mentions a chat with @mattpocockuk and @mitsuhiko and links to a YouTube livestream on writing code that doesn't suck.[^dexhorthy-tests-not-system-quality]
