# Lenny Rachitsky 总结 Sherif Mansour 关于 AI 时代产品经理与团队的九条要点

*Lenny Rachitsky's nine takeaways from Sherif Mansour on AI, product management, and Atlassian*

The post lists Lenny Rachitsky's nine takeaways from Atlassian's Sherif Mansour: while most companies have stopped hiring juniors because of AI, Atlassian is doing the opposite; a PM's primary job is to fill customer, product/market, and business context gaps and build a team “shared brain”; PMs own decision-making velocity, not decisions; there are scientist, general manager, and artist PM archetypes; Atlassian's experiment making everyone an “AI builder” collapsed within a month because the problem was direction, not throughput; and as software gets cheaper to build, artist PMs and design taste are increasingly valuable.[^ca18c9de36b28b81]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 「帖文称」大多数公司因 AI 能完成初级工作而停止招聘初级人才，Atlassian 则采取相反做法；初级人才能帮助团队摒弃旧工作方式，而资深人士能一眼识别 AI 生成的低质内容，两者互补。[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- The post says most companies have stopped hiring juniors because AI can do their work, while Atlassian is doing the opposite; juniors help teams unlearn old ways of working, and seniors can spot AI slop a mile away, so the two complement each other.[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- 「帖文称」产品经理的首要工作是填补团队的上下文缺口，围绕客户、产品/市场和业务三类上下文构建“共享大脑”，应“远离待办事项列表”。[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- The post says a PM's primary job is to fill context gaps for the team across customer, product/market, and business context, building a “shared brain,” and to “run away from your backlog.”[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- 「帖文称」PM 不应纠结于是否该写代码，而应问什么在减慢团队决策；PM 拥有“决策速度”而非决策本身，应用 AI 来弥合减慢团队的上下文缺口。[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- The post says PMs should not ask whether they should code, but what is slowing their team's decisions; PMs own “decision-making velocity,” not decisions, and AI should close whatever context gap is slowing the team down.[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- 「帖文称」Atlassian 使用三种 PM 原型——科学家、通用经理、艺术家——进行招聘与校准；艺术家型 PM 从 UX 愿景倒推问题，适合强调设计与品味的场景。[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- The post says Atlassian uses three PM archetypes—the scientist, the general manager, and the artist—in hiring and calibration; artist PMs start with a UX vision and work backward to the problem.[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- 「帖文称」Atlassian 曾组建没有 PM 或设计师、所有人都是“AI 构建者”的团队，最初感觉更快，但一个月后所有人都回到了传统岗位，团队意识到问题在于方向而非吞吐量。[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- The post says Atlassian tried teams where everyone was an “AI builder” with no PM or designer roles; after initial speed, everyone reverted to traditional jobs within a month because the problem was direction, not throughput.[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- 「帖文称」随着软件构建变得更便宜，产品功能趋于同质，UX 成为脱颖而出的关键方式，设计品味比以往更重要，设计在 Atlassian 路线图中的作用前所未有。[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
- The post says as software gets cheaper to build, more products have the same features and UX becomes a key differentiator; taste matters more when everyone has access to the same intelligence, and design has never played a bigger role in Atlassian's roadmap.[^ca18c9de36b28b81]
