# Aaron Levie：AI 编程杠杆正在拉大工程师产出差距

*Aaron Levie: AI coding leverage is widening engineer productivity gaps*

帖文称，AI 已经让部分编程场景的产出达到 AI 之前水平的一两个数量级以上。作者认为，这需要合适的智能体工作流、深度领域理解、相对新的代码库、多智能体协作流畅度以及低摩擦的测试与部署；该差距并未随时间缩小，反而因模型承担更大、更难问题而持续复利，目前最明显于软件工程，未来可能扩展到科学、AEC、金融、网安等计算机使用密集型领域。[^aaron-levie-ai-productivity-gap]

The post claims that AI has already enabled programming output one or two orders of magnitude above pre-AI levels in some scenarios. The author says this requires the right agent harness, deep domain understanding, a relatively new codebase, fluency with multiple agents, and low-friction testing and deployment; the gap is not narrowing but compounding as models handle larger, harder problems, and is most pronounced in software engineering before potentially spreading to science, AEC, finance, cyber, and other computer-use-sensitive domains.[^aaron-levie-ai-productivity-gap]
