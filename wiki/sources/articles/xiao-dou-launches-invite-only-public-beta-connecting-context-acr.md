# 小豆开启邀请制公测，上下文打通抖音飞书与手机本地能力

*Xiao Dou launches invite-only public beta, connecting context across Douyin, Feishu and phone local capabilities*

「帖文称」小豆今天开启邀请制公测，发文者在内测后分享了多个使用场景。该帖认为小豆最强的能力是上下文，已把抖音、飞书和手机本地能力几乎全部打通，可用于查找收藏的美食视频店铺、生成本地生活卡片、代拨电话询问排队、分析抖音账号数据、推送赛事比分、将聊天截图转为日程并同步到系统日历与飞书日历，以及接入 Apple 健康进行疾病和补剂分析。同时，帖文也指出该产品对话偏冗长、主动推送过频，但团队正在改进。[^x-op7418-xiao-dou-invite-beta]

The post says Xiao Dou launched an invite-only public beta today, and the author shared multiple usage scenarios after internal testing. The post argues that Xiao Dou's strongest capability is context, which has connected Douyin, Feishu and phone local capabilities, enabling use cases such as finding saved food video stores, generating local life cards, calling to ask about queue times, analyzing Douyin account data, pushing match scores, converting chat screenshots into calendar events synced to system and Feishu calendars, and accessing Apple Health for illness and supplement analysis. The post also notes that conversations are overly verbose and proactive pushes are too frequent, but the team is improving.[^x-op7418-xiao-dou-invite-beta]
