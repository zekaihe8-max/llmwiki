# 马天翼谈 Claude 额度燃尽实录

*fkysly on Claude access burnout*

[@fkysly](https://x.com/fkysly)（马天翼）称现在能用上 Claude 已感觉费尽心力：每天随时可能哪个账号就 G，然后要重新注册补货挂上去，再让 Grok Bot 去申请 Dispute；并说自己燃尽了。他强调最关键的是年初就是这局面，快年底仍是 Claude 一枝独秀。[^fkysly-claude-access-burnout] 原帖见 <https://x.com/fkysly/status/2115349799998529551>。

[@fkysly](https://x.com/fkysly) (马天翼) says keeping Claude usable already feels exhausting: any day an account may die (“G”), then he must re-register, restock, and hang it back up, and have Grok Bot apply for Dispute; he says he is burned out. He stresses that early year was already like this, and near year-end Claude is still the sole standout.[^fkysly-claude-access-burnout] Post: <https://x.com/fkysly/status/2115349799998529551>.
