# 外推：别因单次模型发布就过度判断 AI 走势

*Extrapolation: Don't over-judge AI trends from single model releases*

「帖文称」关注 AI 新闻的人容易从每个新模型发布过度外推；Anthropic Opus 在 5.5 之前经历过发布低谷，Google 也有长期空窗期，单一版本（如 6.2）表现平平并不能说明太多问题。另一条「帖文称」模型人格是基准无法捕捉的要素，Opus 4.7 到 5 曾失去“Claude 味”，而 5.5 又恢复了熟悉的感觉。[^emollick-extrapolate-ai-model-releases]

The post says people who closely follow AI news tend to over-extrapolate from each new model release; Anthropic's Opus went through a rough patch before 5.5 and Google had a long dry stretch, so a single mediocre version like 6.2 doesn't indicate much. Another post says model personality matters in ways benchmarks can't capture—Opus 4.7 to 5 lost its “Claude-y” feel, while 5.5 feels like working with old Claude again.[^emollick-extrapolate-ai-model-releases]
