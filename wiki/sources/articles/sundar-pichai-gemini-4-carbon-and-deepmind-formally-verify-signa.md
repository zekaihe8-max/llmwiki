# Sundar Pichai：Gemini 4 Carbon 与 DeepMind 使用 Lean 对 Signal 协议进行形式化验证

*Sundar Pichai: Gemini 4 Carbon and DeepMind Formally Verify Signal Protocol with Lean*

帖文称，Sundar Pichai 代表 Google 团队宣布，其将 Gemini 4 Carbon 投入一项重大安全挑战：对 Signal 协议底层代码进行数学证明。在 Google DeepMind 研究人员使用 AlphaProof Code 引导智能体的过程中，验证工作深入至编译后的设备代码，缩小了真实漏洞潜藏的差距。[^sundar-pichai-gemini-carbon-signal-formal-verifi]

The post states that Sundar Pichai, on behalf of Google teams, announced that Gemini 4 Carbon was applied to a major security challenge: mathematically proving the code underlying the Signal protocol. With Google DeepMind researchers guiding agents using AlphaProof Code, verification extended down to compiled device code, closing the gap where real bugs hide.[^sundar-pichai-gemini-carbon-signal-formal-verifi]
