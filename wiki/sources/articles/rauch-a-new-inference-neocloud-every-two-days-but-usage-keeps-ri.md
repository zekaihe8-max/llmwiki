# Rauch：推理新云两天来一家，但用量还在涨

*Rauch: A new inference neocloud every two days, but usage keeps rising*

「帖文称」Guillermo Rauch 平均每两天就被推销一家新的推理新云公司，他认真对待每位创业者，但难以想象这种局面可持续；与此同时，Vercel AI Gateway 的 Token 需求自年初以来每月环比复合增长约 40%，且没有停止迹象。他将算力比作新经济、Token 比作新能源单位，认为这更像持久的“石油热”而非短暂的“淘金热”，并预计短期内先扩张后整合。[^af7086424da5e749]

Guillermo Rauch says he is pitched a new inference neocloud company on average every two days and takes each entrepreneur seriously, but finds it hard to fathom this being sustainable. At the same time, AI Gateway token demand on Vercel has compounded at roughly 40% month-over-month since the beginning of the year with no signs of stopping. He likens compute to the new economy and tokens to the new energy unit, calling it more of a durable “oil rush” than a “gold rush,” and expects short-term expansion followed by consolidation.[^af7086424da5e749]
