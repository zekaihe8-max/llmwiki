# 阿里拟分拆平头哥赴港上市

*Alibaba Plans to Spin Off Pingtouge for Hong Kong IPO*

帖文称，阿里巴巴于11月10日宣布拟分拆旗下芯片公司平头哥，在香港独立上市；平头哥已于9月11日秘密递交申请，发行规模和上市时间尚未确定，上市后仍为阿里巴巴子公司。[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]

The post says Alibaba announced on November 10 plans to spin off chip unit Pingtouge for an independent Hong Kong listing; Pingtouge had secretly filed on September 11, with issuance scale and listing time undetermined, and it will remain an Alibaba subsidiary after listing.[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文披露，截至今年4月，平头哥真武系列AI芯片累计出货超过56万卡；3月，阿里CEO吴泳铭称其年化营收已达百亿元级别。[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]
- The post discloses that as of April this year, Pingtouge's Zhenwu series AI chips had cumulative shipments exceeding 560,000 cards; in March, Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu said its annualized revenue had reached the 10-billion-yuan level.[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]
- 根据IDC统计，2026年上半年平头哥占中国数据中心AI加速卡出货量的7%，在国产厂商中排名第二，仅次于华为的28%；英伟达以34%位居整体市场第一。[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]
- According to IDC, in H1 2026 Pingtouge accounted for 7% of China data center AI accelerator card shipments, ranking second among domestic vendors behind Huawei's 28%; NVIDIA led the overall market with 34%.[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]
- 已商用的真武M890可通过高速互联将64张芯片组成拥有9TB显存的超节点，可运行Kimi K3等超2万亿参数大模型；平头哥还于9月发布真武V900，官方称单芯片性能达M890的3倍，计划2027年第一季度量产。[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]
- The commercially available Zhenwu M890 can interconnect 64 chips into a super node with 9TB VRAM to run models such as Kimi K3 with over 2 trillion parameters; Pingtouge also released Zhenwu V900 in September, with official claims of 3x M890 single-chip performance and planned mass production in Q1 2027.[^alibaba-pingtouge-hk-ipo]
