# OpenAI 发布研究加速报告，主张 AI 实验室追踪递归自我改进

*OpenAI Releases Research Acceleration Report, Urges AI Labs to Track Recursive Self-Improvement*

帖文称，OpenAI 正在分享关于 AI 如何改变研究的新数据：研究人员的 AI 使用量继续呈指数级增长，智能体正在完成更长时间的任务，但人类参与仍处于核心地位；研究人员也在花更多时间在安全与对齐上，这些领域的人类工作量增长快于任何其他单一研究领域。该报告旨在提升研究加速的透明度，并为日益强大的 AI 的治理提供参考，OpenAI 希望 AI 实验室被要求追踪并公开报告递归自我改进（RSI）指标。[^openai-research-acceleration-report]

The post says OpenAI is sharing new data on how AI is changing research: researchers' AI use continues to rise exponentially, agents are completing longer tasks, but human involvement remains central; researchers are also spending more time on safety and alignment, with human effort in these areas growing faster than in any other individual research area. The report aims to increase transparency around research acceleration and inform the governance of increasingly capable AI, and OpenAI hopes AI labs will be required to track and publicly report indicators of recursive self-improvement (RSI).[^openai-research-acceleration-report]
