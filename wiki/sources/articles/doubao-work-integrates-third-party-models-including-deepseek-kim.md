# 豆包工作接入 DeepSeek、Kimi、智谱 GLM 等三方模型

*Doubao Work integrates third-party models including DeepSeek, Kimi, and Zhipu GLM*

「帖文称」豆包工作终于提供三方模型接入，新用户登录电脑版仍可领取 30 天会员；自动模式下简单提问和小任务免费，Doubao 2.1 Lite 与 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 均为 0.01 倍率，并新增 Kimi 与智谱 GLM。作者还以 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 配合视频剪辑 Skill 制作了一个 8 分钟视频，称积分折算约 0.12 元、耗时约 1 小时，快于其他对比工具。[^7e0a7c5695fa387e]

The author also used DeepSeek V4.1 Flash with a video-editing Skill to produce an 8-minute video, claiming it cost about 0.12 yuan in points and took about 1 hour, faster than other tools compared.[^7e0a7c5695fa387e]
