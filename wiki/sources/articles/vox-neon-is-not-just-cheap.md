# Vox · Neon 并不只是便宜

*Vox · Neon Is Not Just Cheap*

帖文称，尽管 Gemini 4 Neon 被当作廉价模型，但在两个仓库、105 个植入漏洞的修复测试中，Neon（xhigh）以 63 分略高于 GPT-6.2 Astra（62.7）和 Fable 5.2（58）；它还被描述为目前最拟人的文案模型，并在给定少量参考图片后，动作重现效果远优于 Opus 5.5。Google 在 Antigravity 中将其用于日常开发编排，SWE-sweep 成绩从最简单 harness 的 12.8% 提升至 Antigravity 下的 39.0%。[^vox-neon-not-just-cheap]

The post claims that although Gemini 4 Neon is treated as a cheap model, Neon (xhigh) scored 63 on a two-repo, 105-planted-bug fix test, slightly above GPT-6.2 Astra (62.7) and Fable 5.2 (58); it is also described as the most human-sounding writing model today, and with a few reference images it recreates motion far better than Opus 5.5. Google uses it in Antigravity for daily development orchestration, and SWE-sweep results rise from 12.8% on the simplest harness to 39.0% under Antigravity.[^vox-neon-not-just-cheap]
