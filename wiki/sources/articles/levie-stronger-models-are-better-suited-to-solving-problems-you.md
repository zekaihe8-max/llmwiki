# Levie · 更强的模型，更适合拿来解你自己也说不清的问题

*Levie · Stronger models are better suited to solving problems you can't even define*

帖文称，今年模型能力出现明显跃升，智能体不再需要用户精确指定每一步，反而更适合用来解决用户自己难以定义的问题。过去一年，用户需要非常清晰地告诉代理如何设计和开发产品想法，否则几乎不可能成功；现在，代理越来越能够提出比用户更好的实现方案，因为它们能连接远超人类的更大解决方案空间。这一趋势将在金融、法律、生命科学、网络安全等几乎所有知识工作领域显现，最终重塑人与 AI 的关系以及部署方式——从明确指令转向设定目标并给予足够自由。[^levie-agents-goals-not-instructions]

This year we’ve seen a clear jump in model capability: instead of telling agents precisely what to do, it’s better to use these systems to solve problems you can’t easily define yourself. A year ago, agents needed explicit instructions on how to design and develop a product idea to stand any chance of success. Increasingly, agents can devise better implementations than humans would have imagined by connecting dots across a vastly larger solution space. Levie expects this to reshape knowledge work in finance, legal, life sciences, cyber, and beyond, changing AI deployment from explicit instruction to goal-setting with freedom.[^levie-agents-goals-not-instructions]
