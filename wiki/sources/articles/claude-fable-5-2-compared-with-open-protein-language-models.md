# Claude Fable 5.2 对比开源蛋白质语言模型

*Claude Fable 5.2 compared with open protein language models*

帖文称，Claude Fable 5.2 在相同输入下击败了所有运行的开源蛋白质语言模型，包括仅用蛋白质序列训练的 ProGen3-3B、ProtGPT3-10B、ESM C 和 ESM3；同时，Opus 5.5 与 ProtGPT3-10B 处于同一水平。该帖附带一张图片，发布于 2026 年 11 月 17 日，获得 1,873 次浏览。[^claude-fable-5-2-vs-open-protein-lms]

The post claims that, with the same input for every model, Claude Fable 5.2 beats all open protein language models tested, including ProGen3-3B, ProtGPT3-10B, ESM C, and ESM3—models trained on nothing but protein sequences—and that Opus 5.5 is level with ProtGPT3-10B. The post includes an attached image, was published on November 17, 2026, and shows 1,873 views.[^claude-fable-5-2-vs-open-protein-lms]
