# 从美国就业看职业变化

*Job Shifts Seen Through US Employment*

帖文称，从美国就业数据看职业变化，软件行业基本停滞，部分职业消失趋势明显，AI 数据中心相关实体岗位大幅增加；AI 如同智能工业革命，对白领冲击巨大。[^ab8063efed4c0733]

The post says US employment data show the software industry is basically stagnant, some occupations are disappearing, and AI data-center-related physical jobs are surging; AI is like an intelligent industrial revolution, hitting white-collar workers hard.[^ab8063efed4c0733]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 科技公司有超过 281,000 个空缺职位，比一年前增长了 17%。[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- Tech companies have over 281,000 open roles, up 17% from a year ago.[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- 产品经理、工程和设计职位仅增长了 2%。[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- PM, engineering, and design roles grew only 2%.[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- 设计岗位缩减了 28%，用户体验研究缩减了 45%。[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- Design roles shrank 28% and UX research shrank 45%.[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- 硬件工程职位空缺翻了一番，供应链增长了 63%。[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- Hardware engineering openings doubled and supply chain grew 63%.[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- 增长最快的多为实体岗位，如数据中心技术员、安装工、现场服务工程师。[^ab8063efed4c0733]
- Many of the fastest-growing roles are physical jobs such as data center technicians, installers, and field service engineers.[^ab8063efed4c0733]
