# 阿里魔搭联合 AMD 送 100 小时免费云算力

*Alibaba ModelScope and AMD Give Away 100 Hours of Free Cloud Compute*

帖文称，阿里魔搭社区联合 AMD 推出免费云算力活动，赠送 100 小时额度，单卡配备 192GB 显存与 200GB 内存，浏览器打开即用，跑模型环境已提前装好，活动持续到今年 12 月 31 日。[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]

According to the post, Alibaba's ModelScope community has partnered with AMD on a free cloud compute promotion granting 100 hours of quota, with a single card featuring 192GB of VRAM and 200GB of RAM, ready to use in the browser with model environments pre-installed, running until December 31 this year.[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 单卡 192GB 显存，帖文称相当于 6 张 RTX 5090（每张 32GB），另配 200GB 内存。[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- A single card offers 192GB of VRAM, which the post claims equals six RTX 5090s (32GB each), plus 200GB of RAM.[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- 适用场景包括跑 Qwen、DeepSeek 等开源大模型、做模型微调、以及用 ComfyUI 整套跑生图与视频。[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- Use cases include running open-source LLMs like Qwen and DeepSeek, model fine-tuning, and running full ComfyUI image and video generation.[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- 领取分三步：登录魔搭进入我的 Notebook、跳转 AMD 开发者计划完成绑定、回到 Notebook 选 AMD GPU 环境开机。[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- Claiming takes three steps: log into ModelScope and open My Notebook, complete binding via the AMD developer program, then return to Notebook and start the AMD GPU environment.[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- 不用时点击停止即不扣时长；100 小时用完后可在魔搭发实战笔记换取算力积分续时。[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- Stopping the machine when idle avoids consuming time; after the 100 hours run out, posting hands-on notes on ModelScope earns compute credits for more time.[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- 活动全程免费、官方，持续到今年 12 月 31 日，入口为 modelscope.cn/events/247。[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
- The promotion is fully free and official, running until December 31 this year, with the entry at modelscope.cn/events/247.[^modelscope-amd-free-cloud-compute]
