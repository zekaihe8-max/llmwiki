# Google AI Ultra 容易被忽略的 6 项权益

*6 Easily Overlooked Benefits of Google AI Ultra*

## 摘要 / Summary

- Google Cloud 赠金：100 美元档每月 40 美元，200 美元档每月 100 美元，可抵扣符合条件的 Gemini API、Google AI Studio、Cloud Run、Firebase 等费用，需单独领取。[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Google Cloud credit: $40/month for the $100 tier and $100/month for the $200 tier, applicable to eligible Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Cloud Run, Firebase and other fees; must be claimed separately.[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Colab 计算点：每月 1000～2000 点，相当于云端 GPU 算力，可用于运行开源模型、训练 LoRA、生图、生视频。[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Colab compute points: 1,000–2,000 points per month, equivalent to cloud GPU compute, for running open-source models, training LoRA, image and video generation.[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Google Flow Music：每月额外赠送 30000 点独立积分，可用于生成歌曲、背景音乐和各种音频素材。[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Google Flow Music: an extra 30,000 independent points per month for generating songs, background music and various audio materials.[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Firebase Studio：提供 30 个云端工作区，可直接在云端开发和测试应用，并提供更高的 AI 辅助编程额度。[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Firebase Studio: 30 cloud workspaces for developing and testing applications in the cloud, with higher AI-assisted coding quotas.[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Chrome AI 自动浏览：支持让 Gemini 在浏览器里执行多步骤任务，如查找资料、比较商品、整理网页内容，目前有地区限制。[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Chrome AI browsing: lets Gemini execute multi-step tasks in the browser, such as finding information, comparing products and organizing web content; currently region-restricted.[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Google Photos AI 图片功能：可把照片生成短视频，或使用 Remix 修改图片风格，Ultra 拥有更高的生成额度。[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
- Google Photos AI features: turn photos into short videos or use Remix to modify image styles; Ultra receives higher generation quotas.[^google-ai-ultra-hidden-benefits]
