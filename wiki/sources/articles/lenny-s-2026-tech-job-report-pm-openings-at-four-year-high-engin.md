# Lenny 2026 科技就业报告：产品经理岗位四年来最多，工程师停止增长，设计岗位创新低

*Lenny's 2026 Tech Job Report: PM Openings at Four-Year High, Engineering Stalls, Design at Record Low*

帖文转发 Lenny Rachitsky 11 月 10 日发布的第五份科技就业报告，报道称 AI 时代产品经理岗位需求最旺，工程师需求停滞，设计师处境最难。报告区分 TrueUp 开放岗位与 Live Data Technologies 实际入职数据，并指出两套口径常常对不上。[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]

The post forwards Lenny Rachitsky's fifth tech employment report published November 10, saying product manager demand is strongest in the AI era, engineering demand has stalled, and designers face the toughest situation. The report distinguishes TrueUp job openings from Live Data Technologies actual hires and notes the two metrics often diverge.[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]

## 摘要 / Summary

- PM 开放岗位 7303 个，比 2023 年 3 月低点 4171 个多 75%，为近四年最多；过去 12 个月美国实际招约 3.26 万名 PM，比 2024 年初少 4%。[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- PM openings reached 7,303, up 75% from the March 2023 low of 4,171, the highest in four years; US actual hiring was about 32,600 PMs in the past 12 months, down 4% from early 2024.[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- 工程师开放岗位过去一年仅增长 2.5%，现有 65351 个，比今年 3 月更少；过去 12 个月美国招约 11.3 万名软件工程师，不到 2022 年高峰 23.9 万的一半。[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- Engineering openings grew only 2.5% over the past year to 65,351, below March levels; US hiring of about 113,000 software engineers in the past 12 months is less than half the 2022 peak of 239,000.[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- 产品设计开放岗位仅 1924 个，有记录以来最低，比一年前少 28%；过去 12 个月美国招约 1.26 万名产品设计师，不到 2022 年 2.76 万的一半。[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- Product design openings fell to 1,924, a record low down 28% year over year; US hiring of about 12,600 product designers in the past 12 months is less than half the 27,600 hired in 2022.[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- 招聘启事中 AI 技能出现频率上升：PM 启事里“LLM”排第一（11%），工程师启事里 Python 29%、LLM 12% 超过 Java 11%；设计启事里 Figma 37%、Claude 13% 超过 CSS。[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- AI skills appear more frequently in job postings: “LLM” ranks first in PM postings at 11%; for engineers Python is 29% and LLM 12% surpasses Java at 11%; for designers Figma is 37% and Claude 13% surpasses CSS.[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- 远程岗例占比降至 14.4%，为最低纪录，2022 年底曾为 21%；旧金山湾区占开放岗位 14.9%，创纪录，湾区 PM、工程、设计岗位已多于全球远程岗位。[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
- Remote roles fell to 14.4%, a record low from 21% at end of 2022; San Francisco Bay Area accounts for 14.9% of openings, a record, and Bay Area PM, engineering, and design openings now exceed global remote openings.[^lenny-2026-tech-job-market-report]
