# Demis Hassabis 称 Gemini 4 Carbon 逆向移植《主题公园》至 Web

*Demis Hassabis Claims Gemini 4 Carbon Reverse-Engineers Theme Park to Web*

帖文称，Demis Hassabis 宣布最新前沿模型 Gemini 4 Carbon 今日推出，并称 Gemini 3 到 Gemini 4 的进步发生在不到一年内。帖文还称 Carbon 能逆向他90年代编写的《主题公园》（Theme Park）原始二进制，新增 DeepMind 主题游乐设施，并一次性将其移植到 Web 上。在回复中，他还提到使用 Gemini 3 在 Google AI Studio 进行“vibe coding”，数小时内重现了该游戏的测试版，并允许玩家调整薯条上的盐量。[^demis-hassabis-gemini-4-carbon-theme-park]

The post claims Demis Hassabis announced the latest frontier model Gemini 4 Carbon is rolling out today, describing progress from Gemini 3 to Gemini 4 in less than a year. It says Carbon reverse-engineers his original Theme Park binaries from the 1990s, adds a DeepMind-themed ride, and ports it to the web in one shot. In a reply, he also mentioned vibe coding with Gemini 3 in Google AI Studio, recreating a Theme Park testbed in hours, including letting players adjust the amount of salt on chips.[^demis-hassabis-gemini-4-carbon-theme-park]
