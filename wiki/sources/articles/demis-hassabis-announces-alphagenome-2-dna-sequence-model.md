# Demis Hassabis 宣布 AlphaGenome 2 DNA 序列模型

*Demis Hassabis Announces AlphaGenome 2 DNA Sequence Model*

Demis Hassabis 在 X 上发帖称，Google DeepMind 推出 AlphaGenome 2，这是一种新的 DNA 序列模型，能够预测 DNA 变异在不同特定细胞类型中的影响，帮助科学家理解复杂疾病的驱动因素。该模型通过 API 面向学术研究开放。帖文还提到，心脏、大脑和免疫系统共享相同 DNA 序列，单字母变化可能在某些细胞中引发疾病而不影响其他细胞。[^demis-hassabis-alphagenome-2]

Demis Hassabis announced on X that Google DeepMind has launched AlphaGenome 2, a new DNA sequence model that predicts the impact of DNA variants in different specific cell types, helping scientists understand what drives complex diseases. The model is available for academic research via API. The post also notes that the heart, brain, and immune system share the same DNA sequence, and a single-letter change may cause disease in some cells while sparing others.[^demis-hassabis-alphagenome-2]
