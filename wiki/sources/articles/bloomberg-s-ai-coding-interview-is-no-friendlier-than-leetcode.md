# 彭博社的 AI Coding 面试并不比 LeetCode 更友好

*Bloomberg's AI Coding Interview Is No Friendlier Than LeetCode*

帖文称，彭博社第二轮 AI 代码库面试使用 Python Django 框架，面试者此前不了解 Django，且整个代码库非常庞大；行为面试占前 30 分钟，留给写代码的时间只剩约 30 分钟。AI 工具使用边界模糊，面试官未明确期望，边读 AI 生成内容边沟通几乎不可能，候选人虽通过几个测试用例仍被拒。作者称 LeetCode 至少题目明确，而 AI 面试需耗费大量时间摸懂代码库，更玄学且没有稳定通用应对方法。[^ecfa1b0378d44c14]

The post says Bloomberg's second-round AI codebase interview used Python Django; the candidate did not know Django and the codebase was huge. Behavioral questions took the first 30 minutes, leaving only about 30 minutes for coding. AI tool usage boundaries were vague, interviewers did not state expectations, and reading AI-generated content while communicating was nearly impossible; despite passing several test cases, the candidate was rejected. The author says LeetCode at least had clear problems, while AI interviews require much time to understand the codebase, are more mystical, and have no stable general coping method.[^ecfa1b0378d44c14]
