# 用 Qwen4-Max 按 ThreeUI 做杭州主题插画 Sketchbook

*Using Qwen4-Max with ThreeUI to Make a Hangzhou-Themed Illustration Sketchbook*

「帖文称」Ding 测试了昨晚正式上线的 Qwen4-Max，参考 Meng To 的 ThreeUI，用一句话提示词和详细提示词两种方式制作了杭州主题插画风格 Sketchbook；详细提示词版本在杭州场景、翻页、放大镜、墨色调节和导出上更完整，最终内容与交互明显更完整。帖文还列出三点感受：长任务和深度推理能力很强，大部分时候只需自由描述需求即可完成任务；缓存命中率提高、价格大幅降低，消耗 1931 万 token 后标准套餐还剩 98.3%，推算约 10–11 亿 token；提示词写法很重要，建议启动制作前先让模型多思考，有明确设计计划后再动手。[^dingyi-qwen4-max-threeui-hangzhou-sketchbook]

The post says Ding tested the newly launched Qwen4-Max, referencing Meng To's ThreeUI, and created a Hangzhou-themed illustration Sketchbook using one-sentence and detailed prompts; the detailed version was more complete in Hangzhou scenes, page turning, magnifying glass, ink tone adjustment, and export. It lists three takeaways: strong long-task and deep-reasoning ability, improved cache hit rate and much lower price (19.31 million tokens consumed with 98.3% of the standard plan remaining, implying roughly 1.0–1.1 billion tokens), and the importance of prompt writing—users should let the model think more before starting and have a clear design plan first.[^dingyi-qwen4-max-threeui-hangzhou-sketchbook]
