# 《影之刃零》主创：除代码外几乎不沾 AI，因 AI 易给人廉价感

*Shadow Blade Zero Creator: Almost No AI Beyond Coding Because AI Feels Cheap*

「帖文称」《影之刃零》的主创表示，该游戏除了写代码之外没有什么 AI 参与。主创认为 AI 制作天然给人廉价感，人们会觉得 AI 能做出来的东西不应该卖那么高的价钱，这在独立游戏品类中尤其明显，因为玩家很大程度上是在为偏执的品味买单。[^shadow-blade-zero-creator-ai-cheap-perception]

The post says the creator of Shadow Blade Zero stated that apart from writing code, the game has little AI involvement. The creator believes AI-made content naturally feels cheap, so people think things AI can produce should not be sold at high prices, especially in the indie game category, where players largely pay for obsessive taste.[^shadow-blade-zero-creator-ai-cheap-perception]
