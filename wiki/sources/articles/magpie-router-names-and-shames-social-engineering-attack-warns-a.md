# Magpie 路由器点名表扬社工攻击，提醒勿透露开发机 SSH 与密码

*Magpie Router Names and Shames Social Engineering Attack, Warns Against Sharing Dev Machine SSH and Passwords*

帖文称，Magpie 路由器发布提醒，指出 @xy862 在其推文下要求开发机开启 sshd、使用 frp 穿透、放行 22 端口，并发出 IP 地址与用户密码，以便“帮助优化代码”；Magpie 路由器将此认定为套取服务器权限的社工攻击，表示不会照做并已屏蔽该用户，而 @yetone 则评论称该平台具有“点名表扬”机制。[^magpie-router-social-engineering-alert]

According to the post, Magpie Router issued a reminder stating that @xy862 requested under its tweet to enable sshd on a development machine, use frp for NAT traversal, open port 22, and send the IP address and username/password to “help optimize code”; Magpie Router characterized this as a social engineering attack to obtain server permissions, said it would not comply and had blocked the user, while @yetone commented that the platform has a “naming and praising” mechanism.[^magpie-router-social-engineering-alert]
