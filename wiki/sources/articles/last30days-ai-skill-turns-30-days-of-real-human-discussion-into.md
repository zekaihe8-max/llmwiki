# 全网近 30 天真人讨论被做成 AI Skill

*Last30days AI Skill Turns 30 Days of Real Human Discussion into Research*

「帖文称」有人将全网过去 30 天的真人讨论做成了一个 AI Skill，近几天持续登上热榜，GitHub 已获 7.1 万星。该名为 last30days 的工具不依赖编辑和 SEO，而是让 AI 并行抓取 Reddit 高赞评论、X、Hacker News、GitHub、YouTube 字幕和 Polymarket 赔率，将同一事件合并后输出带出处的简报。v4 版本还能查询近一个月的美国新专利和官网招聘页，找出画风反常、可能藏有未发布业务的岗位和专利，并附上近 30 天股价；用户也可反向询问某个领域最近什么在起爆，按热度增速排出新兴话题。使用该 Skill 需要 API，作者建议思路可供借鉴并自行搭建。[^last30days-ai-skill]

The post claims that someone has turned real-person discussions from across the internet over the past 30 days into an AI Skill that has been trending and has 71,000 GitHub stars. Called last30days, the tool avoids editors and SEO by having AI run in parallel across Reddit top comments, X, Hacker News, GitHub, YouTube captions and Polymarket odds, merging the same events into a sourced brief. The v4 version also scans new U.S. patents and official hiring pages from the past month to surface roles and patents with unusual patterns that may hint at unreleased business, along with the past 30 days of stock price. Users can also ask reverse questions about what is taking off in a field, with results sorted by heat growth. Using the Skill requires an API, and the author suggests the approach is worth borrowing and rebuilding independently.[^last30days-ai-skill]
