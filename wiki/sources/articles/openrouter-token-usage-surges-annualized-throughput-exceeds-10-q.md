# OpenRouter Token 用量暴涨，年化吞吐首次突破 1 京

*OpenRouter Token Usage Surges, Annualized Throughput Exceeds 10 Quadrillion*

「帖文称」OpenRouter 在 10 月 26 日当周处理了约 194 万亿 Token，折算年化吞吐量首次突破 1 京（10¹⁶）；最近 9 周的处理量已经超过平台此前约 3 年 3 个月的总和。推动增长的主要力量之一是 AI Agent，其 Token 消耗于今年 2 月 6 日首次超过人工交互，到 9 月最后一周约为后者的 7 倍。[^openrouter-token-usage]

The post says OpenRouter processed about 1.94 quadrillion tokens in the week of October 26, with annualized throughput exceeding 10 quadrillion (10^16) for the first time; volume in the last nine weeks surpassed the platform's previous roughly 3 years and 3 months combined. AI agents are a major growth driver, with agent token consumption first exceeding human interaction on February 6 and reaching about 7 times that level by the last week of September.[^openrouter-token-usage]
