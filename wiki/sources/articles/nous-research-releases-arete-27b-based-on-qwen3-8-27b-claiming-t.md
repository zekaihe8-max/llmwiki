# Nous Research 发布基于 Qwen3.8-27B 的 Arete-27B，宣称强化西方价值观

*Nous Research releases Arete-27B based on Qwen3.8-27B, claiming to reinforce Western values*

帖文称美国 AI 实验室 Nous Research 发布 Arete-27B，直接以阿里 Qwen3.8-27B 作为底座，并用约 5300 万 Token 重新微调，使其认同「西方价值观」；模型权重、训练数据和评测题目均已公开。[^arete-27b]

The post says US AI lab Nous Research released Arete-27B, directly using Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B as the base and fine-tuning it with about 53 million tokens to align with "Western values"; model weights, training data, and eval questions are all public.[^arete-27b]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文称 Nous Research 承认中国开源模型实力最强，但认为它们在政治敏感问题上过于贴近中国官方立场。 / The post says Nous Research acknowledges Chinese open models are currently the strongest, but considers them too close to the Chinese official stance on politically sensitive issues.[^arete-27b]
- 团队自设 803 道测试题，覆盖言论自由、民主、隐私等七类议题，称「宣传」比例从 22% 降至 0.9%，通用能力基本没有下降。 / The team designed 803 test questions across seven categories including freedom of speech, democracy, and privacy, claiming the "propaganda" ratio dropped from 22% to 0.9% with general capabilities basically unchanged.[^arete-27b]
- 帖文指出该「宣传率」只统计是否认同中国官方叙事，不检查是否照搬美国或其他西方国家政治立场，测试题和评分标准由 Nous 制定，最后由马斯克旗下 xAI 的 Grok 4.7 当裁判。 / The post notes this "propaganda rate" only measures alignment with the Chinese official narrative and does not check whether the model copies US or other Western political stances; test questions and scoring criteria were set by Nous, with xAI's Grok 4.7 as the final judge.[^arete-27b]
- 目前模型权重、训练数据和评测题目均已公开。 / Model weights, training data, and eval questions are currently public.[^arete-27b]
