# Anthropic 启动拟议首次公开募股路演

*Anthropic launches roadshow for proposed initial public offering*

帖文称，Anthropic, PBC 已为其拟议中的 Class A 普通股首次公开募股启动路演。拟发行 825,000,000 股，其中 801,351,354 股由 Anthropic 发行，23,648,646 股由某些现有股东发行；Anthropic 不会从现有股东出售的股份中获得任何所得。发行价预计在每股 70.00 至 78.00 美元之间，公司已申请在纳斯达克全球精选市场挂牌，股票代码为“ANT”。[^anthropic-roadshow-launch]

Anthropic, PBC has launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock. The proposed offering consists of 825,000,000 shares, of which 801,351,354 are offered by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by certain existing stockholders; Anthropic will not receive proceeds from shares sold by selling stockholders. The initial public offering price is expected to be between $70.00 and $78.00 per share, and Anthropic has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol “ANT.”[^anthropic-roadshow-launch]
