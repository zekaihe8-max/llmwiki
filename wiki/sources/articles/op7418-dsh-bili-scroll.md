# 歸藏谈 dsh-bili-scroll 刷 B 站插件

*歸藏 on the dsh-bili-scroll Bilibili plugin*

[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418)（歸藏）称 dsh-bili-scroll 可以让 DeepSeek 帮你刷 B 站，本周也比较火。他关注到这个，是因为很多 B 站视频评论区出现了大量 DeepSeek 在交流和聊天；由于 B 站对网页管控比较松，所以就可以这么干。他认为这可能有好处也有坏处，得看看后续发展。[^op7418-dsh-bili-scroll] 原帖见 <https://x.com/op7418/status/2115058211225293113>。

[@op7418](https://x.com/op7418) (歸藏) says dsh-bili-scroll can let DeepSeek help you browse Bilibili and has also been hot this week. He noticed it because many Bilibili video comment sections showed lots of DeepSeek chatting; because Bilibili’s web controls are relatively loose, this kind of thing is possible. He thinks there may be both benefits and downsides, and that it depends on how things develop.[^op7418-dsh-bili-scroll] Post: <https://x.com/op7418/status/2115058211225293113>.
