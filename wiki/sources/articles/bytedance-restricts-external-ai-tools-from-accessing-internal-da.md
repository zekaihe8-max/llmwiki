# 字节限制外部 AI 工具访问内部数据

*ByteDance Restricts External AI Tools from Accessing Internal Data*

帖文称，字节跳动计划从 11 月起大范围限制 Claude、Codex 等外部 AI 工具访问内部数据，全公司 L4 级代码仓库和飞书文档将禁止外部 AI 访问，Seed 部分场景例外，员工个人购买的 AI 账号也将无法继续用于访问受限内容。[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]

The post claims ByteDance plans to broadly restrict external AI tools such as Claude and Codex from accessing internal data starting in November, banning external AI access to all company L4 code repositories and Feishu documents except some Seed scenarios, and disabling personally purchased AI accounts for restricted content.[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 字节跳动计划从 11 月起，大范围限制 Claude、Codex 等外部 AI 工具访问内部数据。 / ByteDance plans to broadly restrict external AI tools such as Claude and Codex from accessing internal data starting in November.[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]
- 全公司 L4 级代码仓库和飞书文档将禁止外部 AI 访问，Seed 部分场景例外。 / All company L4-level code repositories and Feishu documents will prohibit external AI access, with exceptions for some Seed scenarios.[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]
- 员工个人购买的 AI 账号也将无法继续用于访问这些受限内容。 / AI accounts personally purchased by employees will also no longer be able to access such restricted content.[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]
- 字节技术副总裁洪定坤称，L3 及以上内容一旦泄露后果严重，但禁止其他选择并非推广自家产品的好方式。 / ByteDance technical VP Hong Dingkun said leaks of L3 and above content would have serious consequences, but banning alternatives is not an effective way to promote its own products.[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]
- 2025 年 5 月，字节曾因员工使用个人账号导致业务数据沉淀在个人账户，限制 Cursor、Windsurf 等第三方工具。 / In May 2025, ByteDance restricted third-party tools such as Cursor and Windsurf after employees used personal accounts, causing business data to reside in personal accounts.[^byte-dance-restricts-external-ai]
