# Anthropic 11 月 12 日新规：Claude 反代与订阅转卖纳入打击范围

*Anthropic Nov 12 Rules: Claude Proxy and Subscription Resale Targeted*

帖文称，Anthropic 即将于 11 月 12 日生效的最新 Claude 使用政策，将中转站反代行为纳入铁拳打击范围，特别点名两类行为：将 Claude Pro、Max 等消费订阅额度包装成第三方 API 服务对外收费，以及伪装实际使用的产品或客户端（如让第三方工具请求冒充官方客户端）以绕过访问限制。[^anthropic-11-12-proxy-crackdown]

The post says Anthropic's latest Claude usage policy, effective November 12, brings reverse-proxy behavior by forwarding stations under enforcement scope, specifically targeting repackaging Claude Pro/Max consumer quotas into paid third-party APIs and disguising actual products or clients—such as third-party tool requests impersonating official clients—to bypass access restrictions.[^anthropic-11-12-proxy-crackdown]

- 新规定于 11 月 12 日生效。 / The new rules take effect on November 12.[^anthropic-11-12-proxy-crackdown]

- 禁止伪装实际使用的产品或客户端，例如让第三方工具请求冒充官方客户端以绕过访问限制。 / Bans disguising the actual product or client, such as third-party tool requests impersonating official clients to bypass access restrictions.[^anthropic-11-12-proxy-crackdown]
