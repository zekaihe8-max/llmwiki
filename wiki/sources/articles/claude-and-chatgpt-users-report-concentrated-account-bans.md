# Claude 与 ChatGPT 用户集中反映账号被封

*Claude and ChatGPT users report concentrated account bans*

「帖文称」最近不少 Claude 和 ChatGPT 用户反映账号突然被封。Claude 用户频繁遇到身份验证，有人提交中国护照后立即被封；ChatGPT 则从 11 月 7 日前后出现集中封号投诉，波及免费用户、Plus、每月 200 美元的 Pro 用户乃至企业账号。帖文还提到，Anthropic 禁止中国大陆等未支持地区使用 Claude，并通过 Persona 平台要求身份验证，验证失败会封号；OpenAI 则限制同一银行卡或设备关联过多订阅，并禁止转卖 API 额度或共享账号。两家公司近期亦更新使用政策，分别于 11 月 12 日和 11 月 20 日生效。[^claude-chatgpt-ban-wave]

The post says many Claude and ChatGPT users recently reported sudden account bans. Claude users frequently encountered identity verification, with some banned immediately after submitting Chinese passports; ChatGPT saw concentrated ban complaints around November 7, affecting free, Plus, $200/month Pro, and even enterprise accounts. The post also says Anthropic prohibits use from mainland China and other unsupported regions, requires identity verification via Persona, and bans on verification failure; OpenAI restricts multiple subscriptions tied to the same bank card or device, and prohibits reselling API credits or sharing accounts. Both companies recently updated usage policies effective November 12 and November 20 respectively.[^claude-chatgpt-ban-wave]
