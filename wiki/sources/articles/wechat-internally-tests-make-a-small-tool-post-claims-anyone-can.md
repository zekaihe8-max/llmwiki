# 微信内测「做个小工具」，有嘴就能造个人小程序

*WeChat internally tests “Make a Small Tool”; post claims anyone can build a personal mini-program just by speaking*

「帖文称」微信正在内测“做个小工具”个人小程序开发工具，发布者@0xlangeai 称已拿到内测资格并测试了一把，觉得相当惊艳。他用该工具做了一个注意力管理工具，被取名为“番茄专注”，并整理了包含的功能。帖文还称生成的小程序可以推送到微信，在聊天列表直接弹出，也出现在“最近常使用的小程序”里；发布者表示以后自己要用的小程序都可以自己造，而且非常简单。[^4bf490172506b986]

The post says WeChat is internally testing “Make a Small Tool,” a personal mini-program development tool, and that poster @0xlangeai has obtained internal-test access and tried it. He reportedly built an attention-management tool named “Tomato Focus” and organized its functions. The post claims the generated mini-program can be pushed to WeChat, popping up directly in the chat list and appearing under “recently used mini programs”; the poster says personal mini-programs can now be self-made very easily.[^4bf490172506b986]
