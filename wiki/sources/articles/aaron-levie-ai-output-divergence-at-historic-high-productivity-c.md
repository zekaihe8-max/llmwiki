# Aaron Levie：AI 产出分化达历史最高，生产力上限跃升至 5-10 倍

*Aaron Levie: AI output divergence at historic high, productivity ceiling jumps to 5-10x*

「帖文称」AI 产出分化可能是历史最高，尽管采用率也最广；几年前编程等领域的最佳生产力提升仅约 30-50%，而现在部分领域上限可能达到 5-10 倍，前沿与非前沿使用者的产出差距可能是一个数量级。[^levie-ai-output-divergence]

The post says AI output divergence may be the highest ever even as adoption is broadest; a few years ago top gains in fields like coding were only 30-50%, while now some fields may reach 5-10x, with frontier versus non-frontier users differing by an order of magnitude.[^levie-ai-output-divergence]

- 当前模型可在较少人工干预下完成更长周期任务，编排工具支持可处理更大任务的智能体工作，且可同时协作多个智能体。 / Current models can complete longer tasks with limited human interaction, harnesses support agentic work on larger tasks, and systems support working with many agents at once.[^levie-ai-output-divergence]
