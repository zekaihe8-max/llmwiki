# 第二场面试更像付费咨询

*The second interview felt more like paid consulting*

帖文称，第二轮面试甚至没有自我介绍环节，三位面试官直接进入具体场景提问，内容涉及 MiniMax H3 私有化部署成本、生成效率、是否需要专职运维、100+ 用户并发的 GPU 配置与最低配置，以及云服务折扣；还询问基于 MiniMax H3 做特定场景强化学习所需的数据量、内容要求、是否一次获取完整、RL 周期、效果保障、替代方案及成本。面试官并询问若要在一个月内上线，两人（含受访者）是否足够，团队中已有一名实习生。作者表示整个过程更像接项目并与甲方做项目前期沟通，甚至开玩笑想顺便谈报价和工期；此前他还发帖称正集中面试不同阶段、规模与行业的公司，以补充对行业和团队的理解。[^3a07f887fb23c5b2]

The post says the second interview skipped self-introduction and went straight to scenario-specific questions about MiniMax H3 private deployment cost, generation efficiency, dedicated ops needs, GPU configuration and minimum specs for 100+ concurrent users, and cloud discounts. It also covered RL data volume, content requirements, one-time completeness, cycle, effect guarantees, alternatives, and cost for scenario-specific RL based on MiniMax H3. The interviewer asked whether two people including the author, plus an existing intern, could launch within one month. The author said it felt like taking a project and doing preliminary client communication, even joking about quoting price and timeline; he previously said he was interviewing companies at different stages, sizes, and industries to broaden his understanding.[^3a07f887fb23c5b2]
