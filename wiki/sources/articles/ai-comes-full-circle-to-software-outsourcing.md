# AI 干回软件外包

*AI Comes Full Circle to Software Outsourcing*

帖文称，AI 行业狂奔三年后竟干回了软件外包：2023 年会调 OpenAI API 即被视为 AI 人才，2024 年会 RAG 与向量数据库身价涨一截，2025 年人人研究 Agent 与多智能体协作，而到 2026 年会搭 Agent 已不值钱，竞争重心转向业务理解。网友总结 AI 用三年走完了传统软件行业二三十年的路，最终最稳定的商业模式仍是了解客户需求、开发系统、交付项目、驻场维护，兜兜转转又回到了软件外包。[^ai-returns-to-software-outsourcing]

The post says that after three years of rapid growth, the AI industry has come full circle to software outsourcing: in 2023, calling the OpenAI API made one an AI talent; in 2024, RAG and vector databases raised one's value; in 2025, everyone studied Agents and multi-agent collaboration; by 2026, building Agents was no longer valuable, and competition shifted to business understanding. Netizens summarized that AI took three years to traverse a path the traditional software industry took twenty to thirty years, and the most stable business model remains understanding customer needs, developing systems, delivering projects, and on-site maintenance—back to software outsourcing.[^ai-returns-to-software-outsourcing]
