# 组织力

*Organizational Power*

「帖文称」小邓同学在 X 上表示，用 Grok Bot 搭建自动化运营系统后发现，Agent 已具备独立干活能力，却远未形成自主运转的组织能力；真正难点在于规划、分工、协作、监督、纠错及持续运行，人类文明依靠无数人协作而非单个天才，AI 亦然，未来最有价值的是能让成千上万个 Agent 自主协作的系统，而非最强 Agent。[^heydengai-agent-organizational-power]

The post says that after building an automated operations system with Grok Bot, the author found Agents can work independently but lack autonomous organizational capability; the real challenges are planning, division of labor, collaboration, supervision, error correction, and continuous operation. Like human civilization, AI relies on collaboration rather than a single genius, and the future's most valuable asset is a system enabling thousands of Agents to collaborate autonomously, not the strongest Agent.[^heydengai-agent-organizational-power]
