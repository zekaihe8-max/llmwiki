# Chollet：教育别只给答案，AI 应助学生思考

*Chollet: Don't Just Give Answers in Education*

帖文称，对话式 AI 将改变教育并实现大规模个性化辅导，但必须正确构建。当前大多数 AI 教育工具优化错了目标，即“得到答案”，而家庭作业的意义在于通过过程学会思考。我们应构建帮助学生发展自身思考、而非将思考外包给 AI 的 AI。帖文还提到 Gakku 正在这样做，并祝贺 Annie 融资。[^chollet-education-dont-just-give-answers]

The post says conversational AI will transform education and deliver personalized tutoring at scale, but it must be built right. Most current AI education tools optimize for the wrong thing: getting the answer. The point of homework is learning to think through the process. We should build AI that helps students develop their own thinking rather than outsourcing it to AI. The post also notes Gakku is doing this and congratulates Annie on the raise.[^chollet-education-dont-just-give-answers]
