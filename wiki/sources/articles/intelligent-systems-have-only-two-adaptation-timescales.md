# 智能系统只有两套适应时间尺度

*Intelligent systems have only two adaptation timescales*

「帖文称」所有智能系统都恰好拥有两套适应时间尺度。第一套是即时情境建模，人类称为流体智能，现代 AI 通过推理链生成或程序生成实现；第二套是离线挖掘长期经验缓冲区以提取可复用抽象，人类称为长期可塑性或连接体学习，现代 AI 对应预训练与后训练。帖文还称，截至 2024 年底 AI 仅具备第二阶段，没有流体智能，只能存储和检索训练时见过的模式。该内容引自作者在伯克利最近一次演讲的幻灯片。[^fe1720373ea79def]

The post states that all intelligent systems have exactly two adaptation timescales: on-the-fly situation modeling (fluid intelligence in humans; reasoning chain or program generation in AI) and offline mining of past experiences to extract reusable abstractions (long-term plasticity in humans; pre-training and post-training in AI). It also claims that until late 2024, AI had only the second phase, with no fluid intelligence, only storage and retrieval of training-time patterns. The content is from a slide from a recent talk at Berkeley.[^fe1720373ea79def]
