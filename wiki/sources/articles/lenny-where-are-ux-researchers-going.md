# Lenny · 用户研究员去哪了

*Lenny · Where Are UX Researchers Going?*

「帖文称」，在 UXR 岗位减少的背景下，许多用户研究员正流向其他岗位或行业。根据文中引用的 2,779 名美国用户研究员 2024-25 年转岗数据，49% 继续从事用户研究，17% 转入设计，9% 离开该领域，约 2% 成为产品经理；与此同时，近一半转岗研究员加入了科技公司之外的机构，银行、金融公司和保险公司吸纳了大约七分之一。[^lenny-uxr-where-did-they-go]

The post asks where UX researchers are going as UXR roles decline. Citing data on 2,779 U.S. user researchers who changed jobs in 2024-25, it says 49% took another user research job, 17% moved into design, 9% left the field, and about 2% became PMs. It also notes that research jobs are moving outside tech, with nearly half of researchers who took another research job joining non-tech companies, and banks, financial firms, and insurers hiring about one in seven.[^lenny-uxr-where-did-they-go]
