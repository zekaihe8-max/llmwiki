# Antigravity Panel（反重力面板）

*Antigravity Panel*

「帖文称」Antigravity Panel 是一款适用于 Google Antigravity IDE 的实时 AI 额度监控与缓存管理侧边栏扩展，可追踪 Gemini、Claude 和 GPT 的使用情况，可视化消耗趋势，并管理对话缓存。该扩展支持智能额度监控、14 天使用趋势图表、对话上下文窗口 80% 预警、Google One AI 积分显示、缓存浏览与清理、免提自动接受模式、本地或 Claude 提交信息生成，以及连接诊断与日志查看，界面支持 15 种语言，采用 Apache License 2.0 开源发布。[^antigravity-panel]

Antigravity Panel is a real-time AI quota monitor and cache manager sidebar extension for Google Antigravity IDE. It tracks Gemini, Claude, and GPT usage, visualizes consumption trends, and manages conversation cache. Features include smart quota monitoring with configurable thresholds, 14-day usage history, context window warnings at 80%, Google One AI credit tracking, cache browsing and cleanup, optional hands-free auto-accept, local or Claude commit message generation, and connectivity diagnostics. The extension works on Windows, macOS, and Linux in 15 languages and is licensed under Apache License 2.0.[^antigravity-panel]
