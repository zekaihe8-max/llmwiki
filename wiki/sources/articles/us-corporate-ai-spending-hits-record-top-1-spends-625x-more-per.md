*US Corporate AI Spending Hits Record, Top 1% Spends 625x More Per Employee Than Typical Firm*

AI adoption rose from 45.5% in January to a record 56.8% in October. The post also notes the top 1% now spends 625 times more per employee than the typical company, with corporate AI spending accelerating across the board.[^kobeissi-letter-ramp-ai-spending-oct-2026]
