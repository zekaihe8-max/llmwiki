# 别给任何一家 AI 当精神股东

*Don't Be a Spiritual Shareholder for Any AI Company*

帖文称，一位月花大几千的重度 AI 用户将 Claude 用于方案与前端、Codex 用于大量代码与 Computer Use、Gemini 用于文案与视频拆解、Grok Bot 用于统一派活，并辅以豆包、ElevenLabs、即梦等细分工具；核心主张是预算拆给各家、低频按量调用、已用各领域最佳则效果不佳先反思自身用法，不要给任何一家 AI 当精神股东。[^438f7023e46cee1c]

The post says a heavy AI user spending thousands per month assigns Claude to planning and frontend, Codex to heavy coding and Computer Use, Gemini to copywriting and video breakdown, and Grok Bot to unified dispatch, supplemented by Doubao, ElevenLabs, and JiMeng for niche tasks; the core advice is to split budgets across providers, use pay-per-use for low frequency, and if results are poor despite using the best in each field, reflect on one's own usage rather than becoming a "spiritual shareholder" of any single AI company.[^438f7023e46cee1c]
