# 铁柱AGI谈 Grok Bot 京东换开发票

*cgnot996 on Grok Bot reissuing JD.com invoices*

[@cgnot996](https://x.com/cgnot996)（铁柱AGI）称 Grok Bot 又帮了大忙：前阵在河南驻场，许多设备是自己先买再在项目里用、回来报销，发票要换开成公司抬头。他把京东登录到 Bot 的浏览器，让 Bot 翻订单、找出地址为驻场园区的商品，去掉 E 卡和外卖订单，剩下的项目物料由 Bot 换开好；接收发票的邮箱用 Bot 的，完成后打包发回给他。帖文引用 Elon Musk「Grok Bot is like hiring an awesome employee」，并附截图。[^cgnot996-grok-bot-jd-invoice-reissue] 原帖见 <https://x.com/cgnot996/status/2115345115137053105>。

[@cgnot996](https://x.com/cgnot996) says Grok Bot helped a lot again: while on residency in Henan, he had bought much equipment himself for project use and needed invoices reissued under the company letterhead for reimbursement. He logged JD.com into the bot’s browser, had it dig through orders for items shipped to the residency park, drop E-card and food-delivery orders, and reissue the remaining project materials; invoice email used the bot’s mailbox, then the bot packaged and sent everything back. The post quotes Elon Musk—“Grok Bot is like hiring an awesome employee”—and attaches screenshots.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-jd-invoice-reissue] Post: <https://x.com/cgnot996/status/2115345115137053105>.

- Outcome: company-letterhead invoices reissued, received on the bot’s email, packaged back to the author; quotes Musk that it is like hiring an awesome employee.[^cgnot996-grok-bot-jd-invoice-reissue]
