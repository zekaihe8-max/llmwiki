*On the Eve of the AI Bubble Bursting*

The author questions whether AI has truly created incremental value: AI coding boosts code productivity without proportional revenue growth; falling token prices squeeze middlemen; giants like Microsoft and ByteDance lay off staff or pour money into AI investment. The article defines AI as a "self-pleasure economy," arguing that before the bubble bursts, AI has yet to prove it can create value beyond mere substitution.[^2bc7338254318615]
