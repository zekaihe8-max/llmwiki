# François Chollet 称 LLM 写作把每句话都当金句

*François Chollet says LLM writing turns every sentence into a punchline*

「帖文称」François Chollet 在 X 上发文指出，LLM 写作最令人反感之处在于试图把每句话都变成金句或机智口号，完全不顾哪些句子需要强调、哪些不需要，并称这是一种“疲惫且过度使用的公式”。[^dc765b0a57fbf0c0]

In a post on X, François Chollet says the most grating aspect of LLM writing is that it tries to turn every sentence into a punchline or witty slogan, with no regard for which sentences need emphasis and which ones don't, calling it “a tiring formula, overused.”[^dc765b0a57fbf0c0]
