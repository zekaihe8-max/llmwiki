# 推出面向医疗保健的 Gemini

*Introducing Gemini for healthcare*

「帖文称」Google Cloud 于 2026 年 11 月 11 日宣布推出面向医疗保健的 Gemini，这是一款面向付款方和提供方的行业解决方案，扩展了在 Gemini at Work 上发布的通用 Gemini 智能体。文章指出，医疗数据分散在电子病历、理赔系统和行政工具中，临床医生每周花费近 28 小时手动核对信息。Gemini for healthcare 通过医疗技能、系统连接器、开放合作伙伴生态和受治管平台四大组件，在保留现有身份与权限边界的前提下编排多步骤工作流，并当日起以预览版提供，构建用于支持 HIPAA 合规。[^gemini-for-healthcare]

Google Cloud announced Gemini for healthcare on November 11, 2026, an industry solution for payers and providers that extends the universal Gemini agent introduced at Gemini at Work. The article says healthcare data is fragmented across EHRs, claims systems, and administrative tools, with clinicians spending nearly 28 hours a week manually reconciling information. Through four components—healthcare skills, system connectors, an open partner ecosystem, and a governed platform—Gemini for healthcare orchestrates multi-step workflows while preserving existing identity and permission boundaries, available in preview starting the same day and built to support HIPAA compliance.[^gemini-for-healthcare]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Gemini for healthcare 是 Google Cloud 推出的面向医疗保健行业的目的构建解决方案，扩展了在 Gemini at Work 上发布的通用 Gemini 智能体，当日起以预览版提供。[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- Gemini for healthcare is Google Cloud's purpose-built industry solution for healthcare, extending the universal Gemini agent introduced at Gemini at Work, available in preview starting the same day.[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- 方案包含四大核心组件：医疗保健工作技能、连接临床财务运营系统的连接器、开放医疗保健合作伙伴生态系统，以及支持开放模型选择的安全受治管平台。[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- It includes four core components: healthcare skills, connectors to clinical/financial/operational systems, an open healthcare partner ecosystem, and a secure governed platform with open model choice.[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- 平台构建用于支持 HIPAA 合规，提供企业级身份管理、细粒度访问控制、端到端审计日志和可验证引用，供审核人员检查答案来源。[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- The platform is built to support HIPAA compliance with enterprise-grade identity management, fine-grained access control, end-to-end audit logging, and verifiable citations for reviewers.[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- 连接器涵盖 Epic EHR、Oracle Health EHR、InterSystems FHIR Server、Optum、TriZetto、GoodRx、Salesforce、ServiceNow 和 Microsoft 365 等，并继承各源系统的现有角色访问控制。[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- Connectors cover Epic EHR, Oracle Health EHR, InterSystems FHIR Server, Optum, TriZetto, GoodRx, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Microsoft 365, inheriting each source system's existing role-based access controls.[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- 临床场景可从碎片化来源构建患者纵向时间线、就诊前准备病历，并提取与付款方政策对齐的审计就绪授权资料包。[^gemini-for-healthcare]
- Clinical scenarios include synthesizing longitudinal patient timelines from fragmented sources, preparing charts before visits, and extracting audit-ready authorization packets aligned with payer policy.[^gemini-for-healthcare]
