# OpenAI 发布 LifeVal 日常任务个人助手基准

*OpenAI Releases LifeVal Benchmark for Everyday Personal Assistant Tasks*

帖文称，OpenAI 为让通用人工智能惠及全人类，构建了 LifeVal 新基准，该基准依据数千名 ChatGPT 用户反馈而成，聚焦用户最希望个人助手提供帮助的日常事务，包括寻找托儿服务、获取政府服务和管理个人财务；OpenAI 称过去一年一直使用 LifeVal 衡量并改进模型在这些日常任务上的表现。[^openai-lifeval-benchmark]

The post says OpenAI built LifeVal, a new benchmark shaped by feedback from thousands of ChatGPT users on where they most want help from a personal assistant—from finding childcare to accessing government services to managing personal finances—and has used it over the past year to measure and improve how well its models handle everyday tasks.[^openai-lifeval-benchmark]

## 摘要 / Summary

- OpenAI 发布名为 LifeVal 的新基准，用于评估个人助手在日常任务上的能力。 / OpenAI released a new benchmark called LifeVal to evaluate personal assistant capabilities on everyday tasks.[^openai-lifeval-benchmark]
- 基准设计参考了数千名 ChatGPT 用户关于最希望获得帮助的任务的反馈。 / The benchmark design incorporates feedback from thousands of ChatGPT users on the tasks they most want help with.[^openai-lifeval-benchmark]
- 覆盖场景包括寻找托儿服务、获取政府服务以及管理个人财务。 / Covered scenarios include finding childcare, accessing government services, and managing personal finances.[^openai-lifeval-benchmark]
- OpenAI 表示过去一年已使用 LifeVal 衡量并改进模型处理日常任务的水平。 / OpenAI said it has used LifeVal over the past year to measure and improve model performance on everyday tasks.[^openai-lifeval-benchmark]
