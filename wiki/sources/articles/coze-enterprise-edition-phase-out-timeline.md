# 扣子企业版下线时间表

*Coze Enterprise Edition Phase-Out Timeline*

「帖文称」字节跳动旗下的 AI Agent 平台扣子（Coze）将分阶段下线现售企业版：11 月 30 日起企业标准版和旗舰版停止接受新客户购买，火山引擎旧版套餐于今年 12 月 31 日下线，新版企业套餐则于 2027 年 12 月 31 日全面下线；现有企业客户仍可续费和增购席位，但服务期限不得超过该日期。帖文还引用 IDC 数据称，2025 年扣子在中国公有云智能体开发平台市场收入份额为 19.3%，位居第一，但该细分市场全年软件收入仅约 2.5 亿元，扣子对应规模粗算约 4800 万元。此前 8 月，字节已将扣子与 TRAE 团队并入豆包体系，并推出办公 Agent「豆包工作」，相关能力开始向豆包集中。[^d4885f14c8981e13]

The post says ByteDance's AI Agent platform Coze will phase out its current enterprise edition in stages: Enterprise Standard and Flagship editions stop accepting new customers from November 30, legacy packages on Volcano Engine go offline on December 31, and new enterprise packages are fully discontinued by December 31, 2027. Existing enterprise customers can still renew and add seats, but service terms cannot exceed that date. The post also cites IDC data that in 2025 Coze held a 19.3% revenue share, ranking first in China's public cloud intelligent agent development platform market, though the segment's total annual software revenue was only about 250 million yuan, implying roughly 48 million yuan for Coze. Previously in August, ByteDance merged the Coze and TRAE teams into the Doubao system and launched the office Agent "Doubao Work," with related capabilities concentrating toward Doubao.[^d4885f14c8981e13]
