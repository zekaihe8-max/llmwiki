# Lenny · 科技岗位在向湾区集中

*Lenny · Tech jobs are concentrating in the Bay Area*

「帖文称」Lenny Rachitsky 在 X 上发布的最新招聘趋势观察显示，旧金山湾区正在吸纳越来越多的开放科技岗位，远程工作机会则在快速收缩。该帖文指出，目前约 15% 的开放科技岗位位于湾区，创下历史新高；产品与设计岗位中，每四个就有一个在湾区，工程岗位中则有五分之一。与此同时，提供远程选项的科技岗位仅占 14%，为有记录以来最低水平，且自 2022 年底以来显著下降。帖文还称，去年某个时候，湾区的产品、工程和设计岗位总和已超过全球远程岗位，而三年前远程岗位几乎是前者的两倍。[^lenny-tech-jobs-bay-area-concentration]

Lenny Rachitsky's latest X post on hiring trends says the San Francisco Bay Area is absorbing a growing share of open tech roles while remote opportunities shrink. The post states about 15% of open tech roles are now in the Bay Area, a record high; one in four open product and design roles and one in five engineering roles are there. Meanwhile, only 14% of open tech roles offer remote options, the lowest on record and down from 21% in late 2022. The post also claims that sometime last year, Bay Area product, engineering, and design roles combined overtook remote roles worldwide, whereas three years ago remote roles led nearly two to one.[^lenny-tech-jobs-bay-area-concentration]
