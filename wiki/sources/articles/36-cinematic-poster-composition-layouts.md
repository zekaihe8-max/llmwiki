The post "36 Cinematic Poster Composition Layouts" by VaneAi888 proposes selecting poster compositions from 36 layouts based on a film’s title, genre, mood, one-line logline, available assets, and publishing scenario. Composition is defined as the spatial relationship among subject, title, and information, not merely color or style changes. It can deliver executable composition instructions and text hierarchy, or directly generate and revise posters. Chinese fonts are chosen by subject—Song, Hei, handwriting, or calligraphy—and calligraphy is not the default. Comparisons should offer two or three clearly different directions instead of cramming all layouts into one poster. The author is a designer and self-media creator; the related GitHub project has 1.5K stars and is licensed under MIT.[^vane-cinematic-poster]
