# dusk.bot：每个机器人拥有自己的电脑

*dusk.bot: Each bot gets its own computer*

「帖文称」dusk.bot 为每个机器人配备独立的浏览器和桌面，机器人可自行登录并点击操作；用户能够实时观看，并随时接管鼠标。示例中，名为 nova 的机器人在 Google 机票页面搜索孟买到里斯本的最便宜航班，并显示多个已 hold 的选项。[^dusk-bot]

The post says dusk.bot equips each bot with its own browser and desktop so it can sign in and click through tasks; users can watch live and take back the mouse at any time. In the example, a bot named nova searches Google Flights for the cheapest Mumbai to Lisbon flights and displays several held options.[^dusk-bot]
