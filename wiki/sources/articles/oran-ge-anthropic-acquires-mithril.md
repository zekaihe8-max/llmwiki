## 摘要 / Summary

- Mithril：创始人利用 Claude 与 11 个外部实验室合作；不到 18 个月，将针对某种肌肉萎缩症的潜在疗法从构想推进到小鼠中取得令人鼓舞的结果；预计最早 2028 年底可有药物准备开始人体临床试验。[^oran-ge-anthropic-acquires-mithril]
- Mithril: its founder used Claude with 11 external labs; in under 18 months a potential therapy for a form of muscular dystrophy moved from idea to encouraging mouse results; a drug could be ready to start human clinical trials as early as end of 2028.[^oran-ge-anthropic-acquires-mithril]
- Anthropic 收购节奏：此前很少收购，但 2026 年半年内完成四笔——四月收购 Coefficient Bio（不到 10 人，约 4 亿美元全股票）；五月收购 Stainless（SDK 与 MCP server 生成器，据报道 3 亿美元以上）。[^oran-ge-anthropic-acquires-mithril]
- Anthropic’s deal pace: rare acquisitions before, but four in the first half of 2026—April’s Coefficient Bio (under 10 people, ~$400M all-stock); May’s Stainless (SDK and MCP server generator, reported above $300M).[^oran-ge-anthropic-acquires-mithril]
