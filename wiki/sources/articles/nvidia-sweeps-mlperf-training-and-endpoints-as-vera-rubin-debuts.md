# NVIDIA 在 MLPerf 训练与端点基准中全面领先，Vera Rubin 首秀展现领先性能

*NVIDIA Sweeps MLPerf Training and Endpoints as Vera Rubin Debuts With Leading Performance*

帖文称，NVIDIA Vera Rubin 平台在 MLPerf 基准中首次亮相，便横扫训练 v6.1 全部基准，并在首次提交 MLPerf 端点时，代理式 AI 每瓦吞吐量实现超过 10 倍的代际提升。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]

The official article says NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform debuts in MLPerf benchmarks, sweeping every Training v6.1 benchmark and delivering more than 10x generational gains in throughput per watt for agentic AI in its first MLPerf Endpoints submission.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Vera Rubin NVL72 在 MLPerf 训练 v6.1 首秀中，每 GPU 性能最高比经高度优化的 GB300 NVL72 系统高出多达 2 倍。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- In its MLPerf Training v6.1 debut, Vera Rubin NVL72 delivered up to 2x more performance per GPU than the highly optimized NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 system.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- 在 DeepSeek-V3 基准上，Vera Rubin NVL72 从 256 块 GPU 扩展到 1,024 块，效率约 97%，训练时间从 16 分钟缩短至约 4 分钟。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- On the DeepSeek-V3 benchmark, Vera Rubin NVL72 scaled from 256 to 1,024 GPUs with about 97% efficiency, cutting training time from 16 minutes to about four minutes.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- 在临时 Kimi K3 端点结果中，Vera Rubin NVL72（32 个加速器）在相同响应速度下，每瓦吞吐量超过 GB300 NVL72（48 个加速器）的 10 倍。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- In a provisional Kimi K3 MLPerf Endpoints result, Vera Rubin NVL72 with 32 accelerators delivered more than 10x the throughput per watt of GB300 NVL72 with 48 accelerators at the same response speed.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- 20 家组织（包括 ASUS、Azure、Cisco、Dell、Google Cloud、HPE、Oracle 等）提交了基于 NVIDIA 平台的 MLPerf 训练结果。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- NVIDIA partners from 20 organizations, including ASUS, Azure, Cisco, Dell Technologies, Google Cloud, HPE and Oracle, submitted MLPerf Training results.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- NVIDIA 还在 10,240 块 GB300 GPU 上运行了基准，为本轮最大规模；微软 Azure 以 10,240 块 GB200 GPU 匹配该规模。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- NVIDIA ran the benchmark on 10,240 GB300 GPUs, the largest scale in the round, while Microsoft Azure matched that scale with 10,240 GB200 GPUs.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- 官方还称，该平台在 SemiAnalysis AgentX 和 Artificial Analysis AgentPerf 等独立基准上也表现领先。[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
- The official article also says the platform delivers leading performance on independent benchmarks including SemiAnalysis AgentX and Artificial Analysis AgentPerf.[^nvidia-vera-rubin-mlperf-sweep]
