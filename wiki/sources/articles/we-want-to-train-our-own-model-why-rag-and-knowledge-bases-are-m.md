# 面试里总听到「我们想训自己的模型」：为何 RAG 与知识库更现实

*"We want to train our own model": Why RAG and knowledge bases are more realistic*

帖文称，最近几次面试反复听到「某某模型不行，不懂我们这个行业，我们想训自己的模型」，但面试方说不清具体哪里不行、要提升哪方面能力，也无法说明现有可用数据；作者随后只回应「哦，好」，并质疑企业为何轻看知识库、RAG 和实时检索，认为对绝大部分场景这些才是最现实可行的路径，动不动就训模型可能未想清楚区别。[^cac6cfe1e2b6f6de]

The post says that in recent interviews, the author repeatedly heard "some model doesn't work, it doesn't understand our industry, we want to train our own model," but the other side could not specify which model failed, what capability to improve, or what data was available. The author later just replied "oh, good," and questioned why companies overlook knowledge bases, RAG, and real-time retrieval, arguing these are the most realistic paths for most scenarios, and that rushing to train models may reflect a failure to understand the differences.[^cac6cfe1e2b6f6de]
