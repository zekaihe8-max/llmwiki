# LLM「常识」15 个月保质期

*LLM "Common Sense" 15-Month Shelf Life*

帖文称，斯坦福研究者用 20 个新模型重新测试了 50 项被广泛引用的 LLM 结论：44% 已失效，34% 明显减弱，只有 16% 仍然成立。编造引用、幻觉和刻板印象正在减少，但迎合用户和政治立场偏左依然存在。研究提示，模型版本和测试日期可能与结论本身同样重要。[^llm-common-sense-15-months]

The post says Stanford researchers retested 50 widely cited LLM conclusions using 20 new models: 44% are invalidated, 34% significantly weakened, and only 16% still hold. Fabricated citations, hallucinations, and stereotypes are decreasing, but sycophancy and left-leaning political stance persist. The study suggests model version and test date may be as important as the conclusion itself.[^llm-common-sense-15-months]
