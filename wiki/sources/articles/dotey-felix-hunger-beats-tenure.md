# 宝玉转述 Felix：取消编程面试与冲劲胜过资历

*Dotey relays Felix—dropping coding interviews and hunger beats tenure*

[@dotey](https://x.com/dotey) 译介 Anthropic 工程经理 Felix Rieseberg 的博客：Felix 自称从本月起面试不再考传统编程题——Claude 比人更会做这些题，考一道小 React 题与考汇编一样没意义；团队里最出成果的人，与这类测试考得好不好关系不大。帖文标明这是他自己的面试环，未说 Anthropic 全公司是否跟进。他对「AI 让初级更难找工作」持乐观看法：有了 AI 写代码，个人能做出多大成果，不再那么取决于攒了多少年框架经验；一些入行不久的工程师跑赢了代码更好、资历更老的人。标题「冲劲胜过资历」（Hunger beats tenure）；「初级」一词不准，实写给公司里没人脉、没地位、没话语权的工程师。并举 Claude Code 桌面版：两位入职不久的工程师用一个周末做出 GUI 原型，约八天后首个公开版上线。原文博客 <https://felixrieseberg.com/hunger-beats-tenure-advice-for-junior-engineers/>。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure] 原帖 <https://x.com/dotey/status/2115218749888634988>。

[@dotey](https://x.com/dotey) relays Anthropic eng manager Felix Rieseberg’s blog: Felix says that starting this month he no longer gives classic coding puzzles in interviews—Claude beats humans at them, so a small React quiz is as pointless as an assembly quiz; his strongest shippers do not track how well they do on those tests. The post notes this is his own interview loop, not a company-wide Anthropic policy. Against the “AI makes juniors unemployable” story he is optimistic: with AI writing code, how much one person can ship depends less on years of framework tenure; some early-career engineers outrun better coders with more seniority. The title is “Hunger beats tenure”; “junior” is a misnomer—the piece is for engineers without network, status, or voice inside the company. Example: Claude Code’s desktop GUI—two recent hires built a prototype over a weekend, with a first public release about eight days later. ## 摘要 / Summary

- 面试：本月起 Felix 去掉传统编程题；理由是 Claude 更会做题，且出成果与考题表现关系不大；替代方案尚未定，目前看简历与作品集里能显品味、同理心与有原则思考的小原型。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- Interviews: Felix removed classic coding puzzles this month—Claude aces them, and shipping strength barely tracks puzzle scores; he has not settled on a replacement and currently reads resumes/portfolios for small prototypes that show taste, empathy, and principled thinking.[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- 主张：AI 下「冲劲」可胜过资历；新人短板是没人听，但把东西做出来已不贵；过去公司靠少数人拍板点子，新人得先熬地位。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- Claim: with AI, hunger can beat tenure; juniors’ bottleneck is that nobody listens, but shipping is now cheap—firms used to gate ideas through a few deciders after juniors earned status.[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- 新人视角：不知公司「规矩」反而是本钱（Notion 离线模式面试题上核实「CEO 不会接受」的未验证常识）；老员工会劝阻自己试过却失败的点子（大电视看板故事）。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- Fresh eyes: not knowing company “rules” is an asset (on a Notion offline-mode interview question he checked the unverified “CEO won’t accept” lore); tenure can talk people out of ideas one already failed at (the big-TV dashboard story).[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- 五条建议：盯业务影响；想一个大胆点子；用周末做成能跑的原型以跳过需求/对齐/排期；一原型一好点子；用好新视角（用户产品上更懂外部用户，基建上还没学会「为什么做不到」）。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- Five tips: bind to business impact; pick one bold idea; ship a working prototype over a weekend to skip docs/alignment/queues; one good idea per prototype; use the newcomer lens (user products—feel like outsiders; infra—haven’t learned “why it’s impossible”).[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
- 实例：Claude Code 原为终端；Anthony Morris 与 Alice Zhao 周末做出图形界面原型，约八天后公开首版，现很受欢迎。[^dotey-felix-hunger-beats-tenure]
