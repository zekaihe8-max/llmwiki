*Official: Thinking Machines Releases 26B-Parameter Inkling 2 Mini Small Model*

「帖文称」Thinking Machines 官方账号 @thinkymachines 发布推文，回应用户对 Inkling 2 Small（594B 参数）过大的反馈，推出 Inkling 2 Mini。该模型为循环变换器，仅 26B 参数，号称小到可在笔记本电脑上运行，且在 AA Index 上得分 20.1，足以胜任 Agentic 工作。[^thinkymachines-inkling-2-mini]

The post says Thinking Machines' official account @thinkymachines announced Inkling 2 Mini in response to feedback that Inkling 2 Small (594B parameters) was too large.
