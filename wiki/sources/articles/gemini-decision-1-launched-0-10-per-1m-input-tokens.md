# Gemini Decision 1 发布：每百万输入 tokens 0.10 美元

*Gemini Decision 1 Launched: $0.10 per 1M Input Tokens*

「帖文称」Logan Kilpatrick 代表 Google DeepMind 推出 Gemini Decision 1，作为 Gemini API 中的新型前沿决策模型，定价为每 100 万输入 tokens 0.10 美元且无额外费用，原生支持图像、音频、视频与 PDF，上下文窗口达 256K，并可处理 1000 个问题，用户可在 API 中免费试用。[^gemini-decision-1-api]

The post says Logan Kilpatrick, on behalf of Google DeepMind, launched Gemini Decision 1 as a new frontier decision model in the Gemini API, priced at $0.10 per 1 million input tokens with no additional cost, natively supporting images, audio, video, and PDF, with a 256K context window and support for 1,000 questions, available for free trial in the API.[^gemini-decision-1-api]
