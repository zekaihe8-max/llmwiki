# 实测 GPT-6.2 Sol Ultra Fast 剪辑 8 分钟视频：27 分钟完成，峰值 252 token/s

*Real-world test: GPT-6.2 Sol Ultra Fast edits an 8-minute video in 27 minutes, peaking at 252 token/s*

「帖文称」@op7418 试用了 GPT-6.2 Sol 新上线的 Ultra Fast 模式，让其剪辑一段 8 分钟视频；全程耗时 27 分钟，实际模型响应约 14 分钟，平均速度 126 token/s，最快一次达到 252 token/s。用户此前还提到，GPT-6.2 Astra 反馈不佳，但 Sol 模式明显更快，开启 Fast 后任务完成迅速。[^gpt-6-2-sol-ultra-fast-video-edit-test]

The post says @op7418 tested the newly launched Ultra Fast mode of GPT-6.2 Sol by having it edit an 8-minute video; the whole process took 27 minutes, with about 14 minutes of actual model response time, averaging 126 token/s and peaking at 252 token/s. The user previously noted that GPT-6.2 Astra received poor feedback, but Sol is noticeably faster, and enabling Fast mode made tasks complete quickly.[^gpt-6-2-sol-ultra-fast-video-edit-test]
