# 长文实测 MAGI-2：画质接近 Seedance 2.3，1080P 仅 0.5 元/秒，便宜约 90%

*Long-form hands-on test of MAGI-2: visuals near Seedance 2.3, 1080P at 0.5 yuan/second, about 90% cheaper*

「帖文称」Sand.ai 的全新视频模型 MAGI-2 已发布，作者实测后认为其画质可媲美 Seedance 2.3，1080P 画质下每秒仅 0.5 元，约为 Seedance 2.5 的十分之一，便宜约 90%；模型为 480B 全模态视频模型，支持多模态参考与局部编辑，最长生成 30 秒，但音频表现仅约等于 Seedance 2.0 水平，且计划后续开源。[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]

The post claims Sand.ai's new video model MAGI-2 has been released; after hands-on testing, the author says its quality rivals Seedance 2.3, costing only 0.5 yuan per second at 1080P—about one-tenth of Seedance 2.5 and roughly 90% cheaper. MAGI-2 is a 480B full-modal video model supporting multimodal references and local editing, with up to 30-second generation, though audio is only around Seedance 2.0 level, and open-sourcing is planned.[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]

## 摘要 / Summary

- MAGI-2 是 480B 全模态视频模型，单次支持最多 20 个多模态素材参考，最长支持 30 秒生成。[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- MAGI-2 is a 480B full-modal video model supporting up to 20 multimodal reference inputs per generation and up to 30 seconds of output.[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- 1080P 画质下价格为每秒 0.5 元，约为 Seedance 2.5 的 1/10，便宜约 90%，甚至低于 Seedance 2.5 Flash。[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- At 1080P, the price is 0.5 yuan per second, about one-tenth of Seedance 2.5, roughly 90% cheaper, and even below Seedance 2.5 Flash.[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- 作者实测认为画面质感逊于 Seedance 2.5，但在运镜、提示词遵循和物理表现上较强，废片率极低。[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- Hands-on testing found visual texture below Seedance 2.5, but camera movement, prompt following, and physics performance are strong, with very low failure rates.[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- 音频效果一般，大约只有 Seedance 2.0 的水平，带有明显 AI 味，是目前最大瑕疵。[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- Audio quality is average, around Seedance 2.0 level with obvious AI artifacts, currently the biggest flaw.[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- 模型目前独家上线 LibTV，Sand.ai 表示接下来会直接开源。[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
- The model is currently exclusive to LibTV, and Sand.ai says it will be open-sourced next.[^magi-2-video-model-hands-on-test]
