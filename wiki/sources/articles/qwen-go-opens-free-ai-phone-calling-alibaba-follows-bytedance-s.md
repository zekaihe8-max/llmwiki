# 千问 Go 免费开放 AI 代打电话，阿里跟进字节小豆

*Qwen Go Opens Free AI Phone Calling, Alibaba Follows ByteDance's Xiao Dou*

帖文称，字节的个人助理“小豆”凭代打电话功能刷屏，全网抢邀请码；今日中午阿里直接跟进，千问 App 内的千问 Go 免费开放代打电话，无需抢码，人人可用。用户在千问 App 切换至千问 Go，说明打给谁及办理事项并授权后，AI 即可真实外拨，适用于订餐厅包间、酒店失物询问、快递进度查询及定时自动外呼等场景；占线或未接听最多重拨两次，通话后会发送录音和对话记录。帖文还指出，大平台可靠软件接口打通，但线下餐厅、酒店前台、快递员多无接口，只认电话，谁先让 AI 打通电话，谁就能绕过无接口的死结，接管更多线下活；同时也有声音担忧对方直接挂断或 AI 办砸的风险。[^lxfater-qwen-go-free-phone-calling]

The post claims that ByteDance's personal assistant "Xiao Dou" went viral for its phone-calling feature, with users scrambling for invitation codes; Alibaba followed up at noon today by making Qwen Go's phone calling free within the Qwen App, no invitation code required. After switching to Qwen Go in the Qwen App, specifying the recipient and task, and authorizing, the AI can make real outbound calls for scenarios such as booking restaurant private rooms, inquiring about hotel lost-and-found items, checking delivery progress, and scheduled automatic outbound calls. If the line is busy or unanswered, it redials at most twice, and after the call it sends the recording and conversation transcript. The post also argues that while big platforms can connect via software interfaces, offline restaurants, hotel front desks, and couriers often lack interfaces and only accept phone calls; whoever lets AI make calls first can bypass the no-interface deadlock and take over more offline activities. Some worry about the other party hanging up or the AI making mistakes.[^lxfater-qwen-go-free-phone-calling]
