# Google AI Studio 发布自定义域名与 Android 应用构建更新

*Google AI Studio releases custom domain and Android app build updates*

「帖文称」Google AI Studio 今日推出三项更新：用户可在 AI Studio 内直接购买自定义域名；在美国可构建 Android 应用并通过 Google Play 商店下载到手机；还可在 AI Studio 中安全地为 Android 应用添加 AI，构建入口为 ai.studio/build。[^officiallogank-ai-studio-updates]

The post says Google AI Studio today rolled out three updates: users can buy custom domains directly in AI Studio; in the US they can build Android apps and download them to phones via the Google Play Store; and they can securely add AI to Android apps in AI Studio, with the build entry at ai.studio/build.[^officiallogank-ai-studio-updates]
