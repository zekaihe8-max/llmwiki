# 山姆·阿尔特曼警告三星高管：AI需求被重复计算，短期将出现波动

*Sam Altman warns Samsung executives: AI demand double-counted, short-term volatility expected*

《金融新闻》独家报道称，OpenAI 首席执行官山姆·阿尔特曼在与三星电子高管会面时警告，AI、存储器和数据中心需求存在短期波动，市场出现需求重复计算现象；他同时预计长期 AI 使用将激增，导致芯片和数据中心短缺，并呼吁加大供应链投资。[^3883c04e2348f3b0]

Financial News exclusively reported that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, in a meeting with Samsung Electronics executives, warned of short-term fluctuations in AI, memory, and data-center demand and cited double-counted demand in the market; he also projected a long-term surge in AI usage causing chip and data-center shortages, and called for greater supply-chain investment.[^3883c04e2348f3b0]

- 阿尔特曼于10月31日在京畿道龙仁市三星人力资源开发中心与约50名三星关联社社长会面。[^3883c04e2348f3b0]
- Altman met about 50 Samsung affiliate presidents on October 31 at the Samsung HR Development Center in Yongin, Gyeonggi Province.[^3883c04e2348f3b0]
