# 土区 Google AI Ultra 订阅价格失效，早订阅者赶上末班车

*Turkey-region Google AI Ultra subscription price ended, early subscriber caught the last bus*

「帖文称」土区 Google AI Ultra 5× 原先约 1990 土耳其里拉（折合人民币 270 元左右）的订阅价格已不再，现涨至每月 4750 里拉（约 630 元人民币）。帖文称等待党输得最惨，而自称“铁柱”的用户总能赶上末班车，并引用了 @rwayne 关于谷歌 AI Ultra 土区 Bug 失效、涨价的帖子。[^google-ai-ultra-turkey-price-hike]

The post says the Turkey-region Google AI Ultra 5× subscription, previously about 1,990 Turkish lira (around 270 RMB), is no longer available at that price and has risen to 4,750 lira per month (about 630 RMB). It claims the wait-and-see crowd lost the most, while a user calling himself “Iron Zhu” caught the last bus, citing @rwayne’s post about the Google AI Ultra Turkey-region bug ending and the price increase.[^google-ai-ultra-turkey-price-hike]
