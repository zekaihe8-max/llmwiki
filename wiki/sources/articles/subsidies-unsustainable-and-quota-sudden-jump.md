# 补贴扛不住与额度跳变

*Subsidies Unsustainable and Quota Sudden Jump*

「帖文称」马斯克已顶不住，无法持续补贴 Grok Bot；此前以 Opus 5.5 驱动，但近期路由到该模型的比例越来越低，且许多用户发现使用额度一夜之间下降 20%、30% 甚至 40%，原本用不完的额度突然没有了。帖文还称 Grok Bot 成本相对于收入太高、不可持续，个人 Agent 的具体收益难以计算，Meta 的 Muse 免费模式也并不好受。[^grok-bot-subsidy-quota-jump]

The post says Musk can no longer sustainably subsidize Grok Bot; although it was previously driven by Opus 5.5, the share of tasks routed to that model has recently dropped, and many users found their usage quotas suddenly fall by 20%, 30% or even 40% overnight, with previously ample quotas exhausted. The post also says Grok Bot costs are too high relative to revenue and unsustainable, that Personal Agent revenue is hard to calculate, and that Meta's free Muse model is not easy either.[^grok-bot-subsidy-quota-jump]
