# 纳森·兰伯特：AI 扩散依赖人类专家，知识工作比评测更具人性

*Nathan Lambert: AI Diffusion Depends on Human Experts, Knowledge Work Is More Human Than Evals Suggest*

「帖文称」AI 将知识工作带给长尾领域的益处可能被严重低估，更多数据公司应打造专家帮助 AI 公司改进工具的品牌，而非让专家被替代。纳森·兰伯特指出，许多知识工作深具人际性、混乱且依赖情境，AI 采纳更可能成为现有员工的生产力红利，而非替代所有知识工作的 ASI 叙事。他还披露自己担任 Mercor 顾问，认为该公司可在 AI 扩散难题中居中心位置。[^lambert-ai-diffusion-human-expertise]

The post claims that the benefits of AI for the long tail of knowledge work may be vastly underestimated, and more data companies should build brands around experts helping AI companies improve tools rather than being replaced. Nathan Lambert says much knowledge work is deeply interpersonal, messy, and context-driven, making AI adoption a productivity boon for existing employees rather than an ASI replacement of all knowledge work. He discloses advising Mercor and sees it as central to the diffusion puzzle.[^lambert-ai-diffusion-human-expertise]
