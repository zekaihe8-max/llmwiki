# Leaf Yeah!：用 Antigravity 自动跑 Colab 超分，榨干 Google AI Pro 200 算力单元

*Leaf Yeah!: Automating Colab Video Upscaling with Antigravity to Squeeze Google AI Pro Compute Units*

帖文称，Leaf Yeah! 在 X 上分享其使用 Antigravity Agent 接管已登录的 Chrome，在 Google Colab 上运行视频超分脚本，将 23MB 的 480P 数字人口播视频升分至 1080P，全程约 15 分钟，实际渲染约 153 秒，仅消耗 1.06 个 Google AI Pro 算力单元。该帖还称 Google AI Pro 每月赠送 200 个 Colab 算力单元，未用完可累计滚动最多 3 个月，上限 600 个，因此作者决定暂不升级 Google AI Ultra，先榨干 Pro 权益，并主张应给 Antigravity 更多浏览器与工具操作权限。[^leaf-sanren-google-ai-pro-colab-antigravity]

The post claims Leaf Yeah! shared on X how the Antigravity Agent took over a logged-in Chrome browser to run a video super-resolution script on Google Colab, upscaling a 23MB 480P digital avatar video to 1080P in about 15 minutes, with actual rendering around 153 seconds and consuming only 1.06 Google AI Pro compute units. The post also claims Google AI Pro grants 200 Colab compute units per month, which can roll over for up to three months to a maximum of 600, so the author plans to defer upgrading to Google AI Ultra and fully exploit Pro benefits first, while advocating giving Antigravity broader browser and tool operation permissions.[^leaf-sanren-google-ai-pro-colab-antigravity]
