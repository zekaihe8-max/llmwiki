# 他分享了一个相对省钱的 AI MV 和剧情视频做法

*He shares a relatively cost-effective method for AI MVs and narrative videos*

「帖文称」最近做 AI MV 或带剧情视频有一个相对省钱的方式：让 Opus 5.5 负责写代码、剪辑、写转场，以及制作非场景和非人物的动画；再用图片模型负责场景和人物，由 Opus 5.5 通过简单动画让图片动起来，这样一个视频里用到视频模型的可能只有一两个镜头。帖文还提到，刷抖音和小红书能发现新奇 AI 玩法，例如有人用 AI 制作像素动画风格 MV，从 10 月 25 日开始发布，一个月涨了 10 万粉。[^ef81ceb59a7fad60]

The post claims a relatively cost-effective way to make AI MVs or narrative videos: Opus 5.5 handles coding, editing, transitions, and non-scene/non-character animation, while image models generate scenes and characters; Opus 5.5 then simply animates the images, so the video may use video models for only one or two shots. It also mentions that browsing Douyin and Xiaohongshu can reveal novel AI tricks, such as someone gaining 100,000 followers in a month with an AI-made pixel-animation MV starting October 25.[^ef81ceb59a7fad60]
