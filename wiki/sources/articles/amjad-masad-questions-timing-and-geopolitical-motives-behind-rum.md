# 阿玛德·马萨德质疑英伟达CEO免职传闻的时机与地缘政治动机

*Amjad Masad Questions Timing and Geopolitical Motives Behind Rumored Nvidia CEO Ouster*

帖文称，《华尔街日报》报道英伟达董事会正准备因CEO黄仁勋两段婚外情（其中一段涉及疑似中国间谍）而将其免职。Replit联合创始人阿玛德·马萨德在X上发帖质疑，这则并未严密掩盖的旧闻为何突然成为国际与地缘政治新闻，并指出时机可疑，可能与美国中期选举及即将进行的IPO有关；他还表示，若开放模型的主要倡导者黄仁勋被免职，可能有利于希望AI保持封闭、集中和受控的势力。[^amasad-questions-nvidia-ceo-ouster-timing]

The post cites a Wall Street Journal report that Nvidia's board is preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang over two extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy. Replit co-founder Amjad Masad asks on X why an old story that was not well covered up has suddenly become international and geopolitical news, calls the timing suspicious, and suggests it may relate to the midterms and upcoming IPOs; he adds that removing Jensen, a premier champion of open models, could benefit those who want AI closed, centralized, and controlled.[^amasad-questions-nvidia-ceo-ouster-timing]
