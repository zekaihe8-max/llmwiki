# 五个可免费体验的国内 Agent

*Five Domestic Agents Available for Free Trial*

「帖文称」Grok Bot 额度出现异常后，作者整理了腾讯 LightVela、字节豆包工作、阿里千问 Go!、腾讯 WorkBuddy、百度搭子 DuMate 五个可免费体验的国内 Agent，部分产品还赠送云电脑或积分，但多数为限时活动或邀请测试，不能无限使用。[^e2a728c617483763]

The post says that after Grok Bot encountered quota issues, the author compiled five domestic Agents available for free trial—Tencent LightVela, ByteDance Doubao Work, Alibaba Qianwen Go!, Tencent WorkBuddy, and Baidu DuMate—some of which include cloud computers or credits, but most are limited-time offers or invitation-only tests and cannot be used without limits.[^e2a728c617483763]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 腾讯 LightVela 官网显示新用户可免费体验 1 个月，赠送 4500 AI 积分，并提供至少 2 核 8G 的云电脑，底层使用 Hermes Agent。 / Tencent LightVela's website shows new users can try free for 1 month, receive 4,500 AI credits, and get a cloud computer with at least 2 cores and 8G RAM, built on Hermes Agent.[^e2a728c617483763]
- 字节豆包工作新用户首次登录可领 30 天免费体验套餐，新老用户签到 30 天最多领 8800 额度，但不包含生视频和 Pro 模型。 / ByteDance Doubao Work offers new users a 30-day free trial package on first login, and new and existing users can earn up to 8,800 quota by checking in for 30 days, excluding video generation and Pro models.[^e2a728c617483763]
- 阿里千问 Go! 目前为邀请测试，需在千问 App 内申请体验资格，支持淘宝购物、航班理赔、预约，并增加创建个人应用能力。 / Alibaba Qianwen Go! is currently invitation-only and requires applying for trial access within the Qianwen App; it supports Taobao shopping, flight claims, appointments, and recently added personal application creation.[^e2a728c617483763]
- 腾讯 WorkBuddy 定价页面有免费体验版，每月提供 500 积分，支持创建个人助理、自动任务和项目空间。 / Tencent WorkBuddy's pricing page offers a free trial version with 500 credits per month, supporting personal assistants, automated tasks, and project spaces.[^e2a728c617483763]
- 百度搭子 DuMate 官网提供 0 元个人体验版，可通过每日签到领取积分，侧重数字人和自媒体运营等现成工具套件。 / Baidu DuMate's website offers a 0-yuan personal trial version, with credits available via daily check-ins, focusing on ready-made toolkits such as digital humans and self-media operations.[^e2a728c617483763]
- 作者表示这些免费体验多为限时活动或需申请资格，赠送积分不能直接与 Grok Bot 额度对比，自己仍主要使用 Grok Bot。 / The author notes that these free trials are mostly limited-time offers or require application, gifted credits cannot be directly compared with Grok Bot quotas, and he still mainly uses Grok Bot.[^e2a728c617483763]
