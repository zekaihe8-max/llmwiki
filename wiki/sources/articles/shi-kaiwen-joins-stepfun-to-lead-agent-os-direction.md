# 施凯文加入阶跃星辰，负责 Agent OS 方向

*Shi Kaiwen Joins StepFun to Lead Agent OS Direction*

帖文称，前飞书产品副总裁施凯文已加入阶跃星辰，担任合伙人并直接向董事长印奇汇报，负责 Agent OS（智能体操作系统）方向；该消息最早由虎嗅在9月中旬报道后删除，阶跃至今未公开确认。施凯文6月底离开飞书准备创业做端侧 Agent，团队来自字节跳动和上海交大，曾传红杉中国、锦秋基金参投且估值传闻高达5亿美元，但不到3个月便终止创业转而加入大模型公司。[^42a46aba0c0a7c0f]

The post says former Feishu product VP Shi Kaiwen joined StepFun as a partner reporting directly to chairman Yin Qi, leading the Agent OS direction; the report first surfaced from Huxiu in mid-September and was later deleted, with StepFun still unconfirmed. Shi left Feishu in late June to start an edge-side Agent venture with a team from ByteDance and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with Sequoia China and Jinqiu Fund rumored as early investors and a rumored $500M valuation, but he ended the venture in under three months to join a large model company.[^42a46aba0c0a7c0f]
