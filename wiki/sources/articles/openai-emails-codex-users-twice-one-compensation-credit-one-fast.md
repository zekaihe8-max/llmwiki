# OpenAI 连续两天给 Codex 用户发邮件：一封补偿积分，一封预告额度消耗加快

*OpenAI Emails Codex Users Twice: One Compensation Credit, One Faster Quota Consumption Notice*

「帖文称」OpenAI 于 11 月 24 日承认 11 月 16 日至 22 日期间部分 Codex 用量统计出错，并自动向符合条件的用户发放补偿积分；作者称自己收到三千多积分，有效期至 12 月 31 日。次日，OpenAI 又发邮件称 GPT-6.2 Astra 和 Sol 此前部分请求少扣了额度（含 Ultrafast 极速模式），错误已修复，今后同样的使用量可能会消耗更多额度。[^openai-codex-compensation-quota]

The post says OpenAI acknowledged on November 24 that some Codex usage statistics were erroneous between November 16 and 22, and automatically issued compensation credits to eligible users; the author says they received over 3,000 credits valid through December 31. The next day, OpenAI emailed that some GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol requests had previously been undercharged, including Ultrafast mode, the error has been fixed, and the same usage may consume more quota going forward.[^openai-codex-compensation-quota]
