# 腾讯推出独立 AI 搜索网站「元宝搜索」对标百度

*Tencent Launches Independent AI Search Site "Yuanbao Search" to Challenge Baidu*

「帖文称」腾讯于11月11日正式推出独立搜索网站「元宝搜索」（so.qq.com），默认开启 AI 搜索并保留传统网页搜索，同时提供公众号、视频号、图片和学术等专门入口；腾讯还开始将搜狗流量导向元宝，并支持在 QQ 浏览器中将其设为默认搜索引擎。文章回顾了腾讯从2006 年「搜搜」、2013 年并入搜狗、2021 年全资收购搜狗的历史，指出腾讯自2024 年起整合微信搜一搜与搜狗做元宝 AI 搜索，2025 年面向企业和开发者开放 API、MCP 和 Skill，10 月更名为「元宝搜索」，拥有千亿级索引并支撑50 多项内部业务，准备在2026 年带着 AI 和微信生态再抢百度搜索生意。[^tencent-yuanbao-search-launch]

The post says Tencent officially launched the independent search site "Yuanbao Search" (so.qq.com) on November 11, enabling AI search by default while retaining traditional web search and offering dedicated entry points for Official Accounts, Channels, images, and academic content. Tencent is also redirecting Sogou traffic to Yuanbao and supporting it as the default search engine in QQ Browser. The article reviews Tencent's search history from SOSO in 2006, the merger into Sogou in 2013, and full acquisition in 2021, noting that Tencent integrated WeChat Search and Sogou into Yuanbao AI Search starting in 2024, opened API, MCP, and Skill access to enterprises and developers in 2025, renamed it "Yuanbao Search" in October, and claims a trillion-level index supporting over 50 internal businesses, preparing to challenge Baidu's search business again in 2026 with AI and the WeChat ecosystem.[^tencent-yuanbao-search-launch]
