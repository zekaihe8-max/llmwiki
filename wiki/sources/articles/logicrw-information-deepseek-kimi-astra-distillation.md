## 摘要 / Summary

- **Browser Use 网页任务**：新版 DeepSeek 在浏览器操作任务上尤其突出；**Browser Use** 的 **180 项真实网页任务**测试中，得分从旧版 **70.9** 提高到 **75.0**，接近 Astra 的 **77.9**；每题模型调用成本约 **0.22 美元**，Astra 约 **10.77 美元**，相差近 **49 倍**。[^logicrw-information-deepseek-kimi-astra-distillation]
- **Browser Use web tasks**: DeepSeek’s new version stands out on browser-operation tasks; on **Browser Use**’s **180 real web tasks**, score rose from **70.9** to **75.0**, near Astra’s **77.9**; per-task model call cost about **$0.22** vs Astra **$10.77**, roughly **49×** apart.[^logicrw-information-deepseek-kimi-astra-distillation]
- **蒸馏目标转移**：此前 Claude 是国内 AI 公司蒸馏的主要目标；Anthropic 近两个月不断收紧防护，提取推理过程难度大增，国内实验室于是把目光转向 Astra。[^logicrw-information-deepseek-kimi-astra-distillation]
- **Distillation target shift**: Claude was the main distillation target for Chinese AI firms; Anthropic tightened defenses over roughly two months, making extraction of reasoning traces much harder, so labs turned toward Astra.[^logicrw-information-deepseek-kimi-astra-distillation]
- **DeepSeek 内部态度**：创始人梁文锋原本不愿在 V4 研发中依赖蒸馏；看到智谱 GLM 等国内竞争对手借助蒸馏取得进展后，最终向竞争压力妥协。[^logicrw-information-deepseek-kimi-astra-distillation]
- **DeepSeek stance**: founder Liang Wenfeng originally did not want V4 R&D to rely on distillation; after domestic rivals such as Zhipu GLM made progress with distillation, he compromised under competitive pressure.[^logicrw-information-deepseek-kimi-astra-distillation]
