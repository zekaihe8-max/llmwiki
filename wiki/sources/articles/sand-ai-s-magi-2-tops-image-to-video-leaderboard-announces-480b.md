# 三呆科技 MAGI-2 登顶图生视频榜，宣布 4800 亿参数模型将开源

*Sand.ai's MAGI-2 Tops Image-to-Video Leaderboard, Announces 480B-Parameter Open Weights*

帖文称，三呆科技（Sand.ai）发布新一代视频生成模型 MAGI-2，在 Artificial Analysis 图生视频榜单中以 1165 分险胜字节 Seedance 2.5 的 1161 分，登顶全球第一；生成 1080P 视频每秒仅需 0.5 元，30 秒约 15 元，且公司表示接下来将开源这款 4800 亿参数模型的权重。[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]

The post says Sand.ai has released its next-generation video generation model MAGI-2, edging out ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 with 1165 points versus 1161 on the Artificial Analysis image-to-video leaderboard to rank first globally; 1080P video costs just 0.5 yuan per second, about 15 yuan for 30 seconds, and the company says it will open-source the 480-billion-parameter model weights next.[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]

## 摘要 / Summary

- MAGI-2 在人工智能分析图生视频榜单以 1165 分险胜 Seedance 2.5 的 1161 分，登顶全球第一。[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- MAGI-2 scores 1165 on the Artificial Analysis image-to-video leaderboard, beating Seedance 2.5's 1161 to rank first globally.[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- MAGI-2 生成 1080P 视频每秒 0.5 元，30 秒约 15 元，比同分辨率的 Seedance 2.5 便宜约 87%。[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- MAGI-2 generates 1080P video at 0.5 yuan per second, about 15 yuan for 30 seconds, roughly 87% cheaper than Seedance 2.5 at the same resolution.[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- 正式版总参数量从 1140 亿扩大到 4800 亿，预训练数据约扩大 3 倍；MoE 架构每个 Token 仅激活约 280 亿参数。[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- The formal model expands from 114 billion to 480 billion parameters with about 3x more training data; its MoE architecture activates only about 28 billion parameters per token.[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- 单次可生成最长 30 秒 1080P 有声视频，支持最多 20 份图片/视频/音频参考，并可控制运镜、角色动作、镜头切换时间及局部修改。[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- It supports up to 30 seconds of 1080P video with sound, up to 20 reference images/videos/audio, and precise control of camera movement, character actions, shot timing, and local video editing.[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- 三呆科技表示接下来将开源 4800 亿参数模型权重；8 月已开源 MAGI-2 Preview，当时位居图生视频榜全球第六。[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
- Sand.ai says it will open-source the 480-billion-parameter model weights next; MAGI-2 Preview was open-sourced in August and ranked sixth globally.[^3a6baa5ccbef92d6]
