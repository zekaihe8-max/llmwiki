# 温泉助理

*Hot Spring Assistant*

「帖文称」郭宇最近做了十几二十个垂直化个人助理，其中一个放在山月夜公司，做全日本温泉旅行地图和各种温泉旅馆的卖店。他提到当地人其实很难理解 SaaS，也不太会使用各种平台，只会通过电话和邮件联系，所以 AI 就像拼多多一样，反而普惠了很多完全不会使用电脑或手机的人。[^6efca77da66841a9]

The post says Guo Yu recently built over ten verticalized personal assistants, including one for Shanyueye providing Japan-wide hot spring travel maps and inn listings. He said local operators find SaaS hard to understand, do not know how to use various platforms, and contact only by phone and email, so AI, like Pinduoduo, instead makes services accessible to many people who cannot use computers or phones at all.[^6efca77da66841a9]
