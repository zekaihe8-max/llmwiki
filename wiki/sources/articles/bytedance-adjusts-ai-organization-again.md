# 字节再次调整 AI 组织架构

*ByteDance Adjusts AI Organization Again*

帖文称，字节跳动再次调整 AI 组织架构：新设 Seed Model-MaaS Infra 二级部门，原火山方舟团队更名为 Seed MaaS，两部门均由吴迪负责，分别向 Seed 基础设施负责人项亮和火山引擎负责人谭待汇报业务。帖文还称，火山方舟是字节收入规模最大的 AI 业务，上半年火山引擎 MaaS 业务收入达 48 亿元、毛利率超过 55%；豆包日均 Token 调用量突破 180 万亿，火山引擎在中国公有云 MaaS 市场份额达 49.5% 并位居第一。[^byte-ai-org-adjustment]

The post says ByteDance has adjusted its AI organization again, creating the Seed Model-MaaS Infra department and renaming the Volcano Ark team to Seed MaaS, both led by Wu Di. It also states that Volcano Ark is ByteDance's largest AI business by revenue, with H1 Volcano Engine MaaS revenue of 4.8 billion yuan and gross margin above 55%; Doubao's daily token calls exceeded 180 trillion, and Volcano Engine holds a 49.5% share of China's public cloud MaaS market, ranking first.[^byte-ai-org-adjustment]
