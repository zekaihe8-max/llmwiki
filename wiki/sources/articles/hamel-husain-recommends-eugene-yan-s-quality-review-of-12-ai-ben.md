# Hamel Husain 推荐 Eugene Yan 对 12 个 AI 基准的质量审查

*Hamel Husain recommends Eugene Yan's quality review of 12 AI benchmarks*

帖文称，Hamel Husain 欢迎更多人审视热门 AI 基准，并推荐 Eugene Yan 对 12 个公开评测的深度拆解。Eugene 发现的问题包括错误的评分器、配置错误的测试框架、与真实使用场景不符的不切实际规格，以及环境问题；他还论述了每任务成本以及已被 Goodhart 化的指标。[^hamel-husain-eugene-yan-benchmark-scrutiny]

The post says Hamel Husain welcomes growing scrutiny of popular AI benchmarks and recommends Eugene Yan's deep dive into 12 public evals. Eugene's findings include erroneous graders, misconfigured harnesses, unrealistic specifications that don't resemble real AI use, and environment problems, plus arguments about cost per task and Goodhart-nerfed metrics.[^hamel-husain-eugene-yan-benchmark-scrutiny]
