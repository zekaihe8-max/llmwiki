# ChatGPT 推出 GPT-6.2 Sol 版本

*ChatGPT Rolls Out GPT-6.2 Sol Version*

「帖文称」OpenAI 正在 Chat 标签页中推出 GPT-6.2 Sol 版本，针对日常对话优化，并称其在处理复杂请求、遵循详细指令以及联网查找信息方面表现更好。更新还包括更精准的照片细节识别、基于价格或位置等因素的在线选项缩小，以及更合时的后续提醒（如价格下跌通知）。该版本当天开始向 Plus、Pro、Business 和 Enterprise 用户推送。[^chatgpt-gpt-6-2-sol-rollout]

According to the post, OpenAI is rolling out GPT-6.2 Sol in the Chat tab, optimized for everyday conversation. The post claims it handles complex requests better, especially with detailed instructions or online information lookup. Improvements include finer photo detail recognition, narrowing online comparisons by factors such as price or location, and better-timed follow-up offers such as price-drop alerts. It is rolling out today to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users.[^chatgpt-gpt-6-2-sol-rollout]
