# Chase · 目标工程

*Chase · Goal Engineering*

「帖文称」，随着智能体从简单文本生成发展到执行完整任务，设计目标范围变得至关重要；哈里森·蔡斯与 Actively AI 合作者认为，智能体失效常因缺乏未写下来的情境，这类情境需通过人类专家反馈提供，因此目标工程应让人类尽可能轻松地给予高质量反馈。[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]

The post argues that as agents evolve from simple text generation to executing full tasks, designing the scope of goals becomes crucial. Harrison Chase and collaborators from Actively AI note that agents often fail due to missing unwritten context, which must be supplied through human expert feedback, so goal engineering should make it as easy as possible to give high-quality feedback.[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 文章由 LangChain 创始人 Harrison Chase 与 Actively AI 的 @mgarimella 和 Anshul Gupta 共同撰写。[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- The article is co-written by LangChain founder Harrison Chase together with @mgarimella and Anshul Gupta from Actively AI.[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- 智能体已从简单文本生成发展到工具调用、决策制定和执行完整任务，未来将面向开放式目标工作。[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- Agents have progressed from simple text generation to tool calling, decision making, and executing full tasks, and will increasingly work toward open-ended goals.[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- Actively AI 最初为每个客户账户配置一个智能体，后来改为按“动作”（motion）组织智能体，如外呼或早期交易推进，以便销售人员集中审阅并反馈。[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- Actively AI initially assigned one agent per account, then shifted to “motions” organized by specific jobs such as outbounding or early-stage deal progression so salespeople can review and give feedback more efficiently.[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- 智能体最难提供的情境是未写在任何文档中的内容，需要人类专家以反馈形式传递。[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- The hardest context to give an agent is knowledge not written down anywhere, which must be conveyed to the agent as feedback from a human expert.[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- 作者认为提示工程和上下文工程可能会消失，但目标工程将长期存在。[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
- The authors suggest that prompt engineering and context engineering may disappear, but goal engineering will remain.[^goal-engineering-how-to-design-goals]
