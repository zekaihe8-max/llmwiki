# 邵萌谈 Kimi 英推 KOL 宣发怪

*Meng Shao on the odd Kimi English-Twitter KOL promo*

[@shao__meng](https://x.com/shao__meng) 称 Kimi K3.1 Pro 这次宣发里所谓「英推 AI KOL 正义揭露」怎么看怎么怪，每个环节都很怪：以该「KOL」关注≈被关注与过往内容看，若 Kimi KOL 运营选了他可原地辞职，实习生都不行；X 推出 Paid Partnership 后更快遵守的是 KOL，国内不少小 AI 团队仍存侥幸绕规，而 Kimi 的段位与宣发预算格局不该这么小，要遵守并学习平台规则；若模型真强，最好的宣发是让人用起来——尤其面向 AI 开发者——可借鉴 OpenRouter 上玩的老办法，不要规定奇怪话术，扎堆刷屏都会变垃圾。[^shao-meng-kimi-en-kol-promo-weird] 帖文见 <https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2114185431277527220>。

[@shao__meng](https://x.com/shao__meng) says the so-called “English-Twitter AI KOL justice expose” around this Kimi K3.1 Pro launch looks odd at every step: from that “KOL”’s follow≈follower balance and past posts, if Kimi KOL ops chose them they should resign on the spot—even an intern would not; after X shipped Paid Partnership, KOLs complied faster because they know platform rules, while many small Chinese AI teams still try to dodge them—and at Kimi’s tier and promo budget that should not be so petty: follow and learn each platform’s rules; if the model is truly strong, the best promo is letting people use it—especially for AI-developer-facing models—borrowing the long-standing OpenRouter play, without prescribing weird talking points, because pile-on spam becomes garbage.[^shao-meng-kimi-en-kol-promo-weird] The post is at <https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2114185431277527220>.
