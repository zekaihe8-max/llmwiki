帖文称 Jim Cramer 在最新采访中向英伟达临时 CEO Colette Kress 追问：鉴于总统肯定黄仁勋对美科技的价值，董事会是否会考虑让他以 CTO 或形象大使等不触碰监管红线的身份回归。Kress 给出毫无回旋余地的回应：黄仁勋已辞职、董事会已接受辞呈并明确决定不恢复其职位，这是最终决定。Kress 强调公司重心是全力向前看、实现业务增长，集中精力服务员工、客户与股东。后续帖文提到 Jeff Bezos 建议董事会从内部选人接班，称英伟达太复杂，外来 CEO 成功机会极低。[^cramer-kress-nvidia-huang-no-return]

Per the post, Jim Cramer asked NVIDIA interim CEO Colette Kress whether the board would consider bringing Jensen Huang back as CTO or ambassador given the President's endorsement of his value to US tech. Kress gave an unequivocal response: Huang has resigned, the board accepted his resignation and decided not to restore his position — this is final. Kress emphasized the focus is moving forward, driving growth, and serving employees, customers, and shareholders.
