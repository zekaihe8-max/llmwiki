# Motion Prompt：建立 AI 动效与镜头标准词典

*Motion Prompt: A Standard Dictionary for AI Motion Effects and Camera Work*

帖文称，Motion Prompt 项目整理了涵盖动效、运镜、界面组件与光影风格的 192 个专业词汇，每个词条均配备动态演示、单句释义与提示词范例，旨在解决创作者因叫不出专业术语而无法精确指挥 AI 生成的问题；配套插件支持在可视化画廊点选效果，直接将代码规范接入视频或网页项目。[^motion-prompt]

The post says the Motion Prompt project compiles 192 professional terms covering motion effects, camera movement, UI components, and lighting styles, each with dynamic demos, one-sentence definitions, and prompt examples, aiming to solve creators' difficulty in precisely directing AI generation due to lack of terminology; the companion plugin lets users select effects in a visual gallery and integrate code specifications into video or web projects.[^motion-prompt]
