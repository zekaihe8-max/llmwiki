# 姚顺雨谈用户黏性：护城河在产品层而非模型层

*Yao Shunyu on User Stickiness: Moats Lie at the Product Layer, Not the Model Layer*

帖文称，清华大学讲席教授张亚勤向腾讯首席AI科学家姚顺雨提问，DeepSeek、腾讯、Anthropic、OpenAI等的用户黏性何在。姚顺雨回应称，护城河更多在产品层而非模型层，一是拥有他人没有的私有数据和上下文，二是用户是否信任AI了解其数据与上下文并采取行动。他以微信内使用AI为例，指出模型不必最聪明，但若知道用户日程、朋友和活动，就难以被替代；更聪明的模型若不了解用户，则无法取代。张亚勤随即开玩笑说Anthropic可能不值两万亿美元甚至一万亿美元，终将变成同质化商品，并提醒不要听他的建议买股票。[^yao-shunyu-user-stickiness]

The post reports that Tsinghua University distinguished professor Zhang Yaqin asked Tencent Chief AI Scientist Yao Shunyu where user stickiness lies for DeepSeek, Tencent, Anthropic, OpenAI, and others. Yao replied that moats are more at the product layer than the model layer: first, possessing private data and context others lack; second, whether users trust AI to know their data and context and act on it. He cited AI within WeChat as an example—the model need not be the smartest, but if it knows the user's schedule, friends, and activities, it is hard to replace; a smarter model that does not know the user cannot replace it. Zhang then joked that Anthropic might not be worth two trillion or even one trillion dollars and would eventually become a homogenized commodity, adding not to follow his advice on stock purchases.[^yao-shunyu-user-stickiness]
