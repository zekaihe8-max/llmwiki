# Claude Meetings 测试版推出：AI 可加入视频会议并实时协作

*Introducing Claude Meetings*

「帖文称」Anthropic 推出 Claude Meetings 测试版，让 Claude 作为可见参与者加入视频通话，跟随对话并在会议期间开始工作。团队成员可通过语音或聊天向 Claude 提问，Claude 默认以文字回答，并在获许可后使用连接的工具。Claude 还可实时协作构建文档、白板和其他成果，并撰写会议记录与行动项。该功能目前在 Google Meet 上提供，正在 Team 和 Enterprise 计划中逐步推出，后续将支持更多视频会议平台。[^779abe288b1c01da]

Anthropic has launched Claude Meetings in beta, letting Claude join video calls as a visible participant, follow the conversation, and start work during the meeting. Team members can ask Claude questions out loud or in chat; Claude answers in writing by default and uses connected tools only with permission. Claude also collaborates live on docs, whiteboards, and other artifacts while teammates refine them, and writes notes and action items. It is rolling out on Team and Enterprise plans, starting with Google Meet, with other platforms coming soon.[^779abe288b1c01da]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Claude 加入通话后会以参与者身份出现，并在通话聊天中发布通知；默认情况下，如果有人从组织外受邀，Claude 会保持不加入，若有人中途加入则会离开。[^779abe288b1c01da]
- When Claude joins, it appears as a participant and posts a notice in the call’s chat; by default it stays out of calls when someone from outside the organization is invited, and leaves if an outsider joins partway through.[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Claude 保存转录文本、摘要和屏幕描述，不保存音频；每个会议在 claude.ai 上都有独立页面，包含笔记、转录和 Claude 制作的内容，组织内受邀人员可查看和编辑。[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Claude saves transcripts, summaries, and screen descriptions, not audio; each meeting gets a separate page on claude.ai with notes, transcript, and artifacts, viewable and editable by people from your organization on the invite.[^779abe288b1c01da]
- 测试期间，Team 与 Enterprise 计划的笔记和转录免费，通话中的回答也免费，除非答案使用了连接工具，那样会计入计划用量；文档、白板和其他成果免费至 2027 年 2 月 10 日。[^779abe288b1c01da]
- During the beta, notes and transcripts are free on Team and Enterprise plans, as are answers in calls unless an answer uses a connected tool, which counts toward plan usage; docs, whiteboards, and other artifacts are free through February 10, 2027.[^779abe288b1c01da]
- 目前仅支持 Google Meet，其他视频会议平台即将支持；Team 计划默认开放，管理员可关闭；Enterprise 计划需管理员为全部或部分组织开启。[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Support is currently available for Google Meet, with other platforms coming soon; on Team plans it is available by default and an admin can turn it off, while on Enterprise plans an admin turns it on for all or part of the organization.[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Claude 首次使用某人连接工具时会请求许可，同意后持续有效；口头提问时每次都会确认说话人身份，聊天中的回答对通话所有人可见。[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Claude uses each person’s connected tools with that person’s permission, asking the first time with yes applying going forward; when someone asks out loud, Claude asks each time to confirm who’s speaking, and answers posted in chat are visible to everyone on the call.[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Claude 正与 Gong、Attention、Granola、Fireflies.ai 和 Otter 等合作伙伴合作，未来可将会议转录发送到团队已用的工具。[^779abe288b1c01da]
- Claude is working with partners like Gong, Attention, Granola, Fireflies.ai, and Otter so meeting transcripts can soon be sent to the tools teams already use.[^779abe288b1c01da]
