# Grok Bot 电话通话功能上线

*Grok Bot Phone Call Feature Launched*

「帖文称」Grok Bot 电话通话功能正式上线，用户可直接通过机器人拨打电话，全程可实时查看字幕，通话结束后可查看录音。[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-phone-calls]

The post says Grok Bot's phone call feature is officially live, allowing users to make calls directly through the bot with real-time subtitles during the call and recordings available afterward.[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-phone-calls]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 每天最多拨打 10 通电话，同一个号码最多 4 次。 / Up to 10 calls per day, max 4 calls to the same number.[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-phone-calls]
- 单次通话最长 85 分钟，每次拨号前都需要用户确认。 / Single call up to 85 minutes, user confirmation required before each dial.[^xiaomovps-grok-bot-phone-calls]
