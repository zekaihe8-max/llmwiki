# 10 月 Grok Bot 五大更新（0.66→0.78）

*Five Major Grok Bot Updates in October (0.66 → 0.78)*

「帖文称」Grok Bot 近一个月从 0.66 一路升级到 0.78，作者小墨同学盘点了十月最值得关注的五个大更新，涵盖手机端系统应用调用、X 连接器与独立邮箱升级、Pages 和视频生成、导入其他 AI 记忆、以及购物和订阅管理等功能。[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]

The post says Grok Bot upgraded from 0.66 to 0.78 over the past month; author @xiaomovps summarizes five major October updates, including mobile system app invocation, X connector and standalone email upgrades, Pages and video generation, importing memory from other AIs, and shopping/subscription management.[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 手机端可调用日历、提醒事项、联系人、位置等系统功能，并支持 iPhone Action Button 启动语音对话。[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- Mobile can access calendar, reminders, contacts, location and other system functions, and supports iPhone Action Button to start voice conversation.[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- X 连接器无需单独申请开发者账号，可直接搜索帖子、整理内容、管理 List；独立邮箱支持邮件转发、附件处理及 Routine 定时检查。[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- X connector no longer requires a separate developer account and can search posts, organize content, and manage Lists; standalone email supports forwarding, attachment handling, and scheduled Routine checks.[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- Pages 可生成带图表、表格的报告和数据看板并分享，还支持视频生成。[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- Pages can generate reports and data dashboards with charts and tables for sharing, and video generation is supported.[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- 可将 ChatGPT、Claude、Hermes Agent、OpenClaw 等的长期记忆整理后导入，并新增 1Password 登录授权与 Vault 管理。[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- Long-term memory from ChatGPT, Claude, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, etc. can be organized and imported; 1Password login authorization and Vault management are added.[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- 0.78 支持购物、支付、物流跟踪，并提供查找闲置订阅、监控续费价格、管理免费试用的 Bot 模板。[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
- 0.78 supports shopping, payment, and logistics tracking, and provides Bot templates for finding idle subscriptions, monitoring renewal prices, and managing free trials.[^grok-bot-october-five-updates]
