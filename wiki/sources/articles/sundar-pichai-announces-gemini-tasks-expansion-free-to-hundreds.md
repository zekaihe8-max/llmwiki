# Sundar Pichai 宣布 Gemini Tasks 免费面向六国数亿用户扩展

*Sundar Pichai Announces Gemini Tasks Expansion Free to Hundreds of Millions in Six Countries*

帖文称，Sundar Pichai 宣布 Gemini Tasks 将免费向六国数亿用户开放，由 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 驱动，用户可将日常生活事务交给 AI 代理处理，包括转发邮件至日历、代拨本地商家比价查库存、通过 Google Pay 结账以及使用 Google Password Manager 登录，并与主要网站和安全供应商合作。[^sundar-pichai-gemini-tasks-expansion]

The post states that Sundar Pichai announced Gemini Tasks will be opened free of charge to hundreds of millions of users in six countries, powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen, allowing users to hand off everyday life admin to AI agents, including forwarding emails to calendars, having Gemini call local businesses to compare prices and check inventory, checking out via Google Pay, and signing in via Google Password Manager, while partnering with major websites and security providers.[^sundar-pichai-gemini-tasks-expansion]
