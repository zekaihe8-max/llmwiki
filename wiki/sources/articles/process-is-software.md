# 流程即软件

*Process Is Software*

「帖文称」Matt Pocock 指出，AI 的一个奇特之处在于，如果你能用清晰、一致的语言描述业务流程，就已经构建了一半自动化该流程所需的软件，领域驱动设计（DDD）从此更具威力。Jonathan Malkin 回复询问这种方法是否适用于非编程工作，并表示正考虑对整个业务仓库进行 DDD。[^matt-pocock-ddd-process-software]

Matt Pocock observes that AI has a quirk: if you can describe your business processes in clear, consistent language, you have already half-built the software needed to automate the process, making domain-driven design (DDD) more powerful than ever. Jonathan Malkin replies asking how well this works for non-coding work and says he is thinking of DDD'ing his entire business repository.[^matt-pocock-ddd-process-software]
