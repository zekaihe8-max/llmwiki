# 国产 Personal Agent 已经有几款能看的了

*Several domestic Personal Agents are now worth looking at*

「帖文称」国内目前完成度最高的Personal Agent是蚂蚁灵光2.0，它可在独立云主机离线运行，并直连支付宝账单与出行，还能通过MCP/CLI接入钉钉、飞书、12306和米家；字节小豆处于邀请制公测，接入抖音、飞书和日历，并能代打电话订餐厅与酒店；Today AI由齐俊元带队，主打常驻云端电脑与个人记忆可视化。帖文还提到，阿里千问Go!与腾讯小微声量偏低，Qclaw、有道龙虾、DuMate等更偏向本地电脑效率助手。作者认为国内Personal Agent最大的问题是微信、淘宝、美团、抖音等高频服务互不开放接口，导致Agent难以真正跨平台替用户办事。[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]

The post claims that Ant Lingguang 2.0 is currently the most mature domestic Personal Agent, running offline on an independent cloud host and connecting directly to Alipay bills and travel, while also accessing DingTalk, Feishu, 12306, and Mi Home via MCP/CLI. ByteDance Xiaodou is in invitation-only public testing, integrating Douyin, Feishu, and calendar, and can place phone calls to book restaurants and hotels. Today AI, led by Qijunyuan, emphasizes an always-on cloud computer and visualizable personal memory. The author also notes that Qclaw, Youdao Longxia, and DuMate lean toward local-computer efficiency assistants rather than always-on life managers, and that China's closed super-app ecosystems—WeChat, Taobao, Meituan, and Douyin—remain the main barrier to cross-platform Personal Agents.[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 蚂蚁灵光2.0：独立云主机离线跑，直连支付宝账单和出行，通过MCP/CLI接钉钉、飞书、12306和米家，可盯机票降价、盯班级群并连打印机打作业。[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- Ant Lingguang 2.0 runs offline on an independent cloud host, connects directly to Alipay bills and travel, and accesses DingTalk, Feishu, 12306, and Mi Home via MCP/CLI to monitor flight prices, watch class groups, and print homework via a connected printer.[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- 字节小豆：豆包旗下，刚开启邀请制公测，接入抖音、飞书和日历，支持盯降价和长期目标跟进，还能代打电话订餐厅和酒店。[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- ByteDance Xiaodou, under Doubao, has just launched invitation-only public testing, integrating Douyin, Feishu, and calendar; it supports price-drop monitoring and long-term goal tracking, and can make phone calls to book restaurants and hotels.[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- Today AI：齐俊元带队，国内上线最早，常驻云端电脑，最大亮点是个人记忆可视化，用户可直接查看和修改它记住的内容。[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- Today AI, led by Qijunyuan and launched earliest in China, runs as an always-on cloud computer; its main highlight is visualizable personal memory that users can directly view and edit.[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- 国内Personal Agent最大瓶颈是超级App生态封闭：微信、淘宝、美团、抖音互不开放接口，各家只能在自家生态里打转，难以真正跨平台替用户把事办成。[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- The biggest bottleneck for domestic Personal Agents is the closed super-app ecosystem: WeChat, Taobao, Meituan, and Douyin do not open interfaces to each other, so agents are largely confined to their own ecosystems and struggle to complete tasks across platforms.[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- Qclaw、有道龙虾、DuMate等工具接管本地电脑，但更偏向效率助手，离常驻管生活还有距离。[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
- Tools such as Qclaw, Youdao Longxia, and DuMate take over local computers but are more efficiency assistants than always-on life managers.[^xiaomovps-china-personal-agents]
