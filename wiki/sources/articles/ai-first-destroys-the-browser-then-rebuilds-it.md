# AI 先废掉浏览器再重造

*AI First Destroys the Browser, Then Rebuilds It*

帖文称，1999 年仅需 10 行 HTML 即可实现滚动、复制、搜索和键盘操作，而 2026 年 AI 编写的 1 万行 React 页面虽然架构先进、动画丝滑，却连 Tab 键都失效；作者认为当前网页开发正在先废除浏览器能力，再让 AI 重新发明一遍。[^ff10ca7ce914ad14]

The post claims that in 1999, just 10 lines of HTML enabled scrolling, copying, searching, and keyboard operation, while in 2026 an AI-written 10,000-line React page with advanced architecture and smooth animations still fails to respond to the Tab key; the author argues that web development today is disabling browser capabilities first, then asking AI to reinvent them.[^ff10ca7ce914ad14]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 1999 年：10 行 HTML，滚动、复制、搜索、键盘操作全都有。 / 1999: 10 lines of HTML provided scrolling, copying, searching, and keyboard operation.[^ff10ca7ce914ad14]
- 2026 年：AI 写了 10000 行 React，架构先进，动画丝滑。 / 2026: AI wrote 10,000 lines of React with advanced architecture and smooth animations.[^ff10ca7ce914ad14]
- 用户反映 Tab 键没反应，AI 建议引入一套键盘导航系统。 / Users reported the Tab key had no response; AI suggested introducing a keyboard navigation system.[^ff10ca7ce914ad14]
- 作者总结：现在写网页，是先把浏览器的能力废掉，再让 AI 重新发明一遍。 / The author concludes: writing webpages now means first disabling browser capabilities, then letting AI reinvent them.[^ff10ca7ce914ad14]
