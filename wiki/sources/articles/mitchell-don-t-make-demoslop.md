# Mitchell · 别做 demoslop

*Mitchell · Don't Make Demoslop*

帖文称，Mitchell Hashimoto 代表 Superlogical 表示，团队厌倦了感觉像 demoslop 的软件，希望向世界展示精心打磨软件的质感；帖文还称，他们当然使用 AI，但要展示的是“毫无品味的提示词”与“经验丰富的工匠借助 AI”之间的区别。Jordan Singer 在另一条帖文中称赞 Superlogical 的 Rex 软件 fit and finish 出色，像是 AI 时代之前打造的产品。[^mitchell-demoslop]

In the post, Mitchell Hashimoto, speaking for Superlogical, says the team is tired of software that feels like demoslop and wants to show what meticulously crafted software feels like again. The post states they use AI, but to demonstrate the difference between tasteless prompting and experienced craftspeople enhanced by AI. Jordan Singer's earlier post praised Rex by Superlogical as phenomenal software with tremendous fit and finish, feeling like it was built pre-AI.[^mitchell-demoslop]
