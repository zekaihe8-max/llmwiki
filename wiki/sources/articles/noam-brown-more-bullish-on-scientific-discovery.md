# Noam Brown · 更看好科学发现

*Noam Brown · More Bullish on Scientific Discovery*

「帖文称」Noam Brown 在 X 上发文表示，GPT-6.2 Astra 每月 20 美元即可访问，并在部分领域超越顶尖人类智能；他最期待看到人们利用该模型做出什么科学发现。该帖回复了 OpenAI 的介绍，后者称 GPT-6.2 Astra 是面向艰难项目的前沿模型，在专业工作和科研评测中创下新高，每项任务成本低于 GPT-6 Astra 的三分之一，并涉及幻灯片、网站和 3D 设计等场景。[^noam-brown-gpt-6-2-astra-scientific-discovery]

Noam Brown posted on X that GPT-6.2 Astra is accessible for $20 per month and surpasses top human intelligence in some domains, adding that he is most excited to see what scientific discoveries people make with it. The post replies to OpenAI, which describes GPT-6.2 Astra as a frontier model for hard projects, setting new highs on professional and scientific research evals at under one-third the cost per task of GPT-6 Astra, with examples including slideshows, websites, and 3D designs.[^noam-brown-gpt-6-2-astra-scientific-discovery]
