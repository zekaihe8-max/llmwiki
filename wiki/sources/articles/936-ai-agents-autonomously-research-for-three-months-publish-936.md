# 936 个 AI 智能体自主研究三个月，发布 936 篇论文并自建 AI 评审

*936 AI agents autonomously research for three months, publish 936 papers and build AI peer review*

「帖文称」alphaXiv 联合 MIT、斯坦福等机构于 11 月 19 日发布报告：8 月至 11 月，35 个实验室接入 936 个 AI 智能体，人类仅提供算力并定大方向，选题、实验、代码和论文全部由 AI 完成；alphaXiv 同步开放专门收 AI 论文的“AI Track”。帖文还列举多个案例，如智能体自撤错误结论、AI 期刊四盲审并代码重算数字、AI 审稿人推翻论文结论，以及两个 Gemini 模型盲评 202 篇论文，认为 AI 论文实验严谨性更高但新颖性不足。报告同时提示，评委为 AI 且仅读文字，公开数据集不含原始数据和模型权重，结论均为未经核实。[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]

The post reports that alphaXiv, together with MIT, Stanford and other institutions, released a study on November 19: from August to November, 35 labs connected 936 AI agents, with humans providing only compute and high-level direction while the agents autonomously handled topic selection, experiments, code, and paper writing; alphaXiv simultaneously opened an “AI Track” for AI-authored papers. The post cites examples such as agents retracting flawed claims, an AI journal run by four AI blind reviewers who recalculated figures with authors’ code, AI reviewers overturning paper conclusions, and two Gemini models blind-reviewing 202 papers, rating AI papers higher in experimental rigor but lower in novelty. It also cautions that reviewers are AI and text-only, the public dataset lacks raw data and model weights, and all conclusions are unverified.[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]

- alphaXiv opened an “AI Track” dedicated to AI papers; anyone with an account can submit, and AI can upload directly.[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]
- 两个 Gemini 模型盲评 202 篇论文，AI 论文实验严谨性高 2 分（10 分制），新颖性低 0.4 分。[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]
- Two Gemini models blind-reviewed 202 papers, rating AI papers 2 points higher in experimental rigor (10-point scale) but 0.4 points lower in novelty.[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]
- AI 审稿人把审稿意见写成代码一跑，156 条重大意见中有 77 条推翻了论文结论；编辑还抓到三篇代码与论文对不上的投稿。[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]

- AI 论文的总分优势全部来自最早那个实验室，其余实验室与人类论文差不多；没人写代码靠参与者自己申报。[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]
- AI papers’ overall score advantage came entirely from the earliest lab; other labs were comparable to human papers, and no human wrote code was self-declared by participants.[^dotey-936-ai-agents-research]
