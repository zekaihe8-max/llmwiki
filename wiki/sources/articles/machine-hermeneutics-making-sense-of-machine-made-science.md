# 机器诠释学：读懂机器做出的科学成果

*Machine Hermeneutics: Making Sense of Machine-Made Science*

宝玉（@dotey）转述 Anthropic 联合创始人 Jack Clark 的观点：AI 加快科学研究之后，人类需要投入更多精力去理解机器做出的科学成果，这会催生一门新学问，他称之为「机器诠释学」（Machine Hermeneutics）。帖文还附上一篇设定在 2033 年的虚构短篇，出自他写的 AI 周报 Import AI 第 482 期。[^machine-hermeneutics]

Baoyu (@dotey) relays Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark's view that as AI accelerates scientific research, humans must invest more effort to make sense of machine-driven advances, giving rise to a new discipline he calls 'Machine Hermeneutics.' The post also includes a fictional short set in 2033, from issue 482 of his Import AI newsletter.[^machine-hermeneutics]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Jack Clark 曾任 OpenAI 政策总监，更早是科技记者，每周撰写 AI 周报 Import AI，汇总研究进展并在末尾常附一篇短篇小说；机器诠释学的观点出自该周报第 482 期。[^machine-hermeneutics]
- Jack Clark, formerly OpenAI's policy director and earlier a tech journalist, writes the weekly Import AI newsletter that summarizes AI research and often appends a short story; the Machine Hermeneutics idea comes from issue 482.[^machine-hermeneutics]
- 「诠释学」原本研究如何读懂《圣经》、法律条文这类难懂的经典，Jack Clark 借它指专门研究如何读懂机器做出的科学成果。[^machine-hermeneutics]
- 'Hermeneutics' originally studies how to read difficult classics like the Bible and legal texts; Clark borrows the term to mean the study of how to read machine-made scientific results.[^machine-hermeneutics]
- 帖文称现实依据是今年出现大量由机器写出的数学论文，人类读懂它们花的时间可能是机器写出所用时间的几十倍到几百倍，他认为「奇点已经开始」。[^machine-hermeneutics]
- The post cites as its real-world basis the many machine-written math papers this year, which humans may take tens to hundreds of times longer to read than the machines took to write, leading Clark to say 'the singularity has begun.'[^machine-hermeneutics]
- 配套科幻短篇设定在 2033 年，开头引用丘吉尔 1943 年的话「我们塑造建筑，之后建筑塑造我们」。[^machine-hermeneutics]
- The accompanying sci-fi short is set in 2033 and opens with Churchill's 1943 line, 'We shape our buildings; thereafter they shape us.'[^machine-hermeneutics]
- 小说设定人类规定机器的任何新发明都必须先由人类彻底解释清楚原理才能获准使用；面对像「外星科技」的成果，人类发展出「机器诠释学」与让人化身电子、量子比特或等离子体的「教学建筑学」。[^machine-hermeneutics]
- In the story, humans require any new machine invention to be fully explained before approval; facing 'alien-technology'-like results, they develop Machine Hermeneutics and 'Teaching Architecture,' in which experts embody electrons, qubits, or plasma.[^machine-hermeneutics]
- 核心反转是：人类倾力理解机器造物时，也在潜移默化中被改造，不知不觉学会像机器一样思考和行事。[^machine-hermeneutics]
- The core twist: as humans exhaust themselves to understand machine creations, they are subtly reshaped, unknowingly learning to think and act like machines.[^machine-hermeneutics]
