# Obsidian 官方支持 Grok Bot 连接器

*Obsidian Officially Supports Grok Bot Connector*

「帖文称」Obsidian 官方现已支持 Grok Bot 连接器，用户可将 Grok Bot 接入个人笔记库，实现搜索笔记、修改链接、将网页内容存入笔记，以及创建 Bases 数据库和 Canvas 画布；配合 Obsidian Sync，修改可同步至各台设备，免去来回复制粘贴。[^10a7bbbb9d84c489]

The post claims Obsidian now officially supports a Grok Bot connector, letting users connect Grok Bot to their personal vault to search notes, edit links, save web content into notes, and create Bases databases and Canvas canvases; with Obsidian Sync, edits are synchronized across devices, eliminating manual copy-pasting.[^10a7bbbb9d84c489]
