# 自媒体工作台：用三个开源 Skill 串联 AGY 创作流程

*AI Self-Media Workbench: Chaining Three Open-Source Skills in AGY*

「帖文称」小墨同学近期尝试将 Google Antigravity 打造成个人 AI 自媒体工作台，仅依赖 Agent Reach、wewrite、guizang-social-card-skill 三个开源 Skill，就把选题收集、内容创作与视觉排版串联成一套本地工作流，避免在十几个网页和软件之间切换。[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]

The post says Xiaomovps recently tried turning Google Antigravity into a personal AI self-media workbench, relying solely on three open-source Skills—Agent Reach, wewrite, and guizang-social-card-skill—to connect topic collection, content creation, and visual layout into a single local workflow, avoiding switching among over a dozen web pages and apps.[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Agent Reach 负责抓取 X、YouTube、GitHub、Reddit 及小红书等平台的最新动态，并整理开源项目和视频字幕，用于筛选选题。[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- Agent Reach is responsible for grabbing the latest updates from X, YouTube, GitHub, Reddit, and Xiaohongshu, and organizing open-source projects and video subtitles for topic selection.[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- wewrite 负责整理大纲、写初稿或拆解视频脚本，能读取本地旧文章学习表达习惯，并进行审稿去除 AI 腔调。[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- wewrite handles outlining, drafting, or breaking down video scripts, can read local old articles to learn writing habits, and performs proofreading to remove AI-like tone.[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- guizang-social-card-skill 负责将文字制作成公众号头图、信息长图及小红书图文卡片，适合多平台分发。[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- guizang-social-card-skill turns text into WeChat official account cover images, information long images, and Xiaohongshu graphic cards, suitable for multi-platform distribution.[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- 三个 Skill 全部在 AGY 同一个本地工作区里完成，兼容通用 SKILL.md 标准和 MCP。[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- All three Skills run in the same AGY local workspace and are compatible with the general SKILL.md standard and MCP.[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- 作者表示不管是运营 X、公众号、小红书还是视频平台，自媒体流程都差不多，关键是把找选题、攒素材、写内容、做配图、发布等环节顺畅接起来。[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
- The author says that whether operating X, official accounts, Xiaohongshu, or video platforms, the self-media process is similar; the key is to smoothly connect finding topics, gathering materials, writing content, making images, and publishing.[^xiaomovps-agy-self-media-workbench]
