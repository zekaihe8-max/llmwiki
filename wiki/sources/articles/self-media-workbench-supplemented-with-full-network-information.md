# 自媒体工作台补全全网信息获取与多平台分发

*Self-media workbench supplemented with full-network information acquisition and multi-platform distribution*

「帖文称」小墨同学此前已用 DeepSeek Harness 搭建包含 3 个插件的自媒体工作台，现补充全网信息获取和多平台分发两个关键环节，分别接入开源项目 OpenBiliClaw 与 social-auto-upload，试图串联从热点收集、内容创作到多平台发布的完整工作流，并提醒自动发布存在账号登录与平台风控风险。[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]

The post says Xiao Mo Tongxue previously built a self-media workbench with three DeepSeek Harness plugins, and now adds two key links—full-network information acquisition and multi-platform distribution—by integrating the open-source projects OpenBiliClaw and social-auto-upload, attempting to connect a full workflow from hotspot collection and content creation to multi-platform publishing, while warning that automatic publishing involves account login and platform risk-control risks.[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 文章提到 OpenBiliClaw 可从 X、B 站、小红书、抖音、YouTube 等平台收集热点，并支持按兴趣筛选，已适配 DeepSeek Harness 插件。[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
- The article says OpenBiliClaw can collect hotspots from X, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, YouTube and other platforms, supports filtering by interest, and has been adapted as a DeepSeek Harness plugin.[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
- social-auto-upload 支持抖音、小红书、B 站、视频号、YouTube 等平台的视频自动上传，但并非 DeepSeek Harness 原生插件，需自行对接。[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
- social-auto-upload supports automatic video upload to Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, Video Account, YouTube and other platforms, but is not a native DeepSeek Harness plugin and requires separate integration.[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
- 帖文提醒自动发布涉及账号登录和平台风控，建议先用小号测试，不能保证不被封号。[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
- The post reminds that automatic publishing involves account login and platform risk control, suggests testing with a secondary account first, and cannot guarantee no ban risk.[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
- 整个工作流涵盖全网热点收集、内容创作、排版配图、多平台分发，适用于 X、小红书、抖音等运营场景。[^xiaomovps-self-media-workbench-distribution]
