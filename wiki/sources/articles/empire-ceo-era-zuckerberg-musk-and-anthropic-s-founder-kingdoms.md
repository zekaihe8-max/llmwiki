*"Empire CEO" Era: Zuckerberg, Musk, and Anthropic's Founder Kingdoms*

The post says The Wall Street Journal portrays Meta's Zuckerberg, SpaceX's Musk, and Anthropic's Dario Amodei as ancient monarchs, declaring we are living in an "Empire CEO" era. These tech giants increasingly resemble kingdoms centered on founder will, able to bet tens of billions on near-sci-fi tech visions and, even after going public, avoid the direct shareholder constraints faced by traditional executives.[^empire-ceo-era]
