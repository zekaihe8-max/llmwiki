# 吉列尔莫·劳奇：跨时主动服务是智能体杀手级应用

*Guillermo Rauch: "Doing stuff for you over time" is the killer app of agents*

「帖文称」：「为你随时间办事」是智能体的杀手级应用。Muse 与 Instinct 会在夜间整合记忆，早晨提醒事项，并在上次互动过去 N 天后主动发消息。Vercel Schedules 类似“类固醇版 Cron”，可在运行时定义、无限扩展，并置于沙箱之外，以按需成本效率调用计算。Vercel Schedules 现已进入公开测试，让智能体和应用自行安排与管理工作，支持在代码中一次定义静态任务、运行时创建动态任务，并在包括预览环境在内的任意环境运行。[^rauchg-vercel-schedules-agents-killer-app]

The post says “doing stuff for you over time” is the killer app of agents. Muse and Instinct consolidate memories at night, remind you in the morning, and message you after N days since your last interaction. Vercel Schedules are like Cron on steroids, definable at runtime, infinitely scalable, and outside the Sandbox for cost-efficient compute invocation. Vercel Schedules is now in public beta, letting agents and applications schedule and manage their own work, with static jobs defined in code, dynamic ones created at runtime, and execution in any environment including preview.[^rauchg-vercel-schedules-agents-killer-app]
