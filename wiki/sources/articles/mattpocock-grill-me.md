# Matt Pocock 谈 /grill-me

*Matt Pocock on /grill-me*

[@mattpocockuk](https://x.com/mattpocockuk) 说他有时仍会用 `/grill-me about this, one question at a time`——通常是他其实不知道自己在建什么、问题严重欠规格、而一轮问答就可能把工作带向截然不同方向的时候。[^mattpocock-grill-me] 原文见 <https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2113278944594325761>。

[@mattpocockuk](https://x.com/mattpocockuk) says he still sometimes uses `/grill-me about this, one question at a time`—usually when he really has no idea what he is building, the problem is hugely underspecified, and a single Q&A round could send the work in massively different directions.[^mattpocock-grill-me] The post is at <https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2113278944594325761>.

## 摘要 / Summary

- 当问题严重欠规格时，用一次只问一个问题的 `/grill-me`。[^mattpocock-grill-me]
- Use `/grill-me` with one question at a time when the problem is hugely underspecified.[^mattpocock-grill-me]
- 一轮问答就能把欠规格的构建带向截然不同的方向，此时顺序提问更稳妥。[^mattpocock-grill-me]
- A single Q&A round can send an underspecified build in massively different directions, so sequential questions are safer then.[^mattpocock-grill-me]
