# Matt Pocock on /grill-me

[@mattpocockuk](https://x.com/mattpocockuk) says he still sometimes uses `/grill-me about this, one question at a time`—usually when he really has no idea what he is building, the problem is hugely underspecified, and a single Q&A round could send the work in massively different directions.[^mattpocock-grill-me] The post is at <https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2113278944594325761>.

## Summary
