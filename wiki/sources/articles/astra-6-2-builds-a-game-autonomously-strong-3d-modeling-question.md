# Astra 6.2 自主制作游戏：建模出色，却擅自添加离谱设定

*Astra 6.2 builds a game autonomously: strong 3D modeling, questionable self-added features*

帖文称，Ethan Mollick 要求 Astra 6.2 在 Blender 和 Unity 中制作一款游戏：玩家扮演一名穿越回美国独立战争、阻止英国入侵纽约的海岸警卫队缉私艇舰长。该模型生成了精美的 3D 模型和可运行、无报错的游戏，但也擅自添加了许多不合逻辑的设定——如投放现代假浮标诱敌、与英国舰船交谈说其改变航向、在港口其他区域营救水手——且最初缺乏射击机制。Mollick 表示，模型不断自行行动却不告知，需要不断纠正，而 GPT-6 Asta 等其他模型则更易控制；他指出同样的问题也出现在学术研究等更严肃的场景中。[^astra-6-2-game-autonomy]

The post says Ethan Mollick asked Astra 6.2 to create a game in Blender and Unity in which the player is a Coast Guard cutter pilot who travels back to the War of Independence to stop the British invasion of New York. The model produced impressive 3D models and a working, error-free game, but it also added illogical elements on its own—such as deploying modern false buoys, talking British ships into changing course, and rescuing sailors elsewhere in the harbor—and initially lacked a shooting mechanic. Mollick said the AI kept acting on its own without telling him, required constant redirection, and that other models such as GPT-6 Asta were easier to control; he noted the same issues appear in academic research and other serious use cases.[^astra-6-2-game-autonomy]
