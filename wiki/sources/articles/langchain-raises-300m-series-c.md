# LangChain 获得 3 亿美元 C 轮融资

*LangChain Raises $300M Series C*

「帖文称」LangChain 已筹集 3 亿美元 C 轮融资，估值达 30 亿美元，由 CapitalG 领投，GIC、DFJ Growth、IVP 和 Sapphire Ventures 参投，旨在推进 LangSmith 智能体平台，让每家公司都能拥有自己的智能。[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]

LangChain announced it raised $300M at a $3B valuation, led by CapitalG with participation from GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures, to advance its LangSmith agent platform and enable every company to own its intelligence.[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 融资规模为 3 亿美元，估值 30 亿美元，由现有投资者 CapitalG 领投，GIC 和 DFJ Growth 为新投资者，IVP 和 Sapphire Ventures 为现有投资者跟投。[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- The round is $300M at a $3B valuation, led by existing investor CapitalG, with new investors GIC and DFJ Growth and existing investors IVP and Sapphire Ventures.[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- 超过 7,000 个组织依赖 LangSmith，包括 Harvey、Rippling、Listen Labs 以及 NVIDIA、Toyota、Salesforce 等财富 500 强企业。[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, including startups like Harvey, Rippling, and Listen Labs and Fortune 500 enterprises like NVIDIA, Toyota, and Salesforce.[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- LangSmith 围绕 Agent Runtime、Agent Improvement 和 Agent Governance 三大支柱构建，涵盖 LangGraph、LangSmith Agent Server、Observability、Fine-Tuning 及 LLM/Tool Gateways 等能力。[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- LangSmith is built on three pillars—Agent Runtime, Agent Improvement, and Agent Governance—covering LangGraph, LangSmith Agent Server, Observability, Fine-Tuning, and LLM and Tool Gateways.[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- 公司将于 11 月 20 日下周五举行特别发布活动，分享多项新的 LangSmith 能力。[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- On Friday, November 20, LangChain will hold a special release event to share several new LangSmith capabilities.[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- 除领投方外，Salesforce Ventures、Insight Venture Partners、QuantumLight 和 MongoDB Ventures 加入，Sequoia、Amplify 和 Benchmark 在此轮中追加投资。[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
- Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight, and MongoDB Ventures joined as new investors, while Sequoia, Amplify, and Benchmark doubled down in this round.[^cdf9beb5c6bc899b]
