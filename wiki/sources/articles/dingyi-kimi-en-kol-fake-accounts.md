# Ding 谈 Kimi 不投中推与英推假大号

*Ding on Kimi skipping CN KOLs and fake English accounts*

[@dingyi](https://x.com/dingyi)（Ding）引用并认同一种甲方操盘说法：不必给中推花一分钱，只要花小钱买通上游英推大 V，中推为了吃流量会免费搬运、高潮、吹爆——「既然自带干粮，何必给钱？」他接着称，放着那么多优质中文博主，Kimi 包括很多国内公司就是不愿意花钱，非要找海外；但很多所谓海外大号其实是中国人/香港/新加坡运营起来的假账号，互相刷起来，背后有 MCN 拿着一堆自己刷出来的英文大号对接甲方，甲方「kuku 送钱」却毫无效果。至于产品侧，他称 Kimi 已完全沦落到国产模型第二梯队，并建议试试最新的 DeepSeek 4.1 flash、MiniMax 3.1 flash——又快又好、根本用不完；反观 Kimi 的套餐，几句话就把额度用光。[^dingyi-kimi-en-kol-fake-accounts] 原帖见 <https://x.com/dingyi/status/2114227612654698798>。

[@dingyi](https://x.com/dingyi) (Ding) quotes and agrees with a client-side playbook: spend nothing on Chinese-Twitter KOLs—buy English-Twitter upstream big accounts cheaply, and Chinese accounts will free-carry, hype, and blow the product up for traffic—“why pay if they bring their own rations?” He adds that despite many quality Chinese bloggers, Kimi and many domestic firms refuse to spend and insist on overseas; but many so-called overseas big accounts are fake accounts run by people in China/Hong Kong/Singapore, mutual-engagement farmed, with an MCN pitching clients a pile of English accounts it farmed itself—clients throw money with no effect. On the product side, he says Kimi has fully fallen to the second tier of Chinese models, and suggests trying the latest DeepSeek 4.1 flash and MiniMax 3.1 flash—fast, good, and hard to exhaust—while Kimi’s package burns the quota in a few sentences.[^dingyi-kimi-en-kol-fake-accounts] Post: <https://x.com/dingyi/status/2114227612654698798>.
