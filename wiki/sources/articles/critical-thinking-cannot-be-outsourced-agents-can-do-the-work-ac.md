# 批判性思考交不出去：Agent 能干活，对结果负责不能交

*Critical Thinking Cannot Be Outsourced: Agents Can Do the Work, Accountability Cannot*

帖文称，Claude Code 团队提醒：模型越好、输出越顺眼，就越没人逼你再想一遍；批判性思考不会自动发生，得自己放进日常习惯。事情可以交给工具来做，对结果负责这件事交不出去。[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]

The post cites the Claude Code team's reminder that better models and polished outputs reduce the pressure to rethink; critical thinking does not happen automatically and must be built into daily habits. Work can be delegated to tools, but responsibility for results cannot be outsourced.[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 帖文列出三种认知滑坡：认知卸载（活交给 AI，结果仍归你）、把判断往后拖、认知投降（AI 说的悄悄变成自己的话，也不想再核）。 / The post lists three cognitive slippage patterns: cognitive offloading (handing work to AI while results remain yours), deferring judgment, and cognitive surrender (AI output quietly becoming your own words without checking).[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]
- 低风险事务如从三家差不多酒店里选场地可全权交给 AI；投资、给孩子择业建议、做什么产品、能不能上线等影响他人的事，推理还得留在自己手里。 / Low-risk tasks such as choosing among similar hotels can be fully delegated to AI, but reasoning for investment, children's career advice, product decisions, and deployment must remain in human hands when others are affected.[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]
- 作者建议动手前先立哪怕很差的假设，自问“是在请它帮你想，还是替你想”，并尝试证明模型错、让另一模型唱反调、当着维护者面为结果担保。 / The author suggests forming even a poor hypothesis before acting, asking whether you are asking AI to help think or to think for you, trying to prove the model wrong, having another model dissent, and being able to vouch for the result before maintainers.[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]
- 绿灯只证明一件事，测试和功能可能犯同一错；需先约定“怎样才算验证过”，先看命令和输出再看摘要，并抽查原文和关键数字。 / A green light proves only one thing; tests and functions may share the same error. Teams should define what counts as verified, inspect commands and output before summaries, and spot-check originals and key figures.[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]
- 不敢署上自己名字的就不发出去；产出速度快过敢署名的速度就是编排成本，此时要收紧检查或放慢，而不是再开一轮会话。 / Do not ship anything you cannot sign your name to; if output speed exceeds the speed at which you can take responsibility, tighten checks or slow down rather than starting another session.[^cfcc2f607d62f8fd]
