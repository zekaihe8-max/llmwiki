# Meta 和 Sierra 的个人智能体协议 Poppy 1.0

*Meta and Sierra's Personal Agent Protocol Poppy 1.0*

「帖文称」Meta 与 Sierra 联合发起的个人智能体协议 Poppy 发布 1.0 版本，开始从草案走向大规模应用；Shopify 已为平台数百万商家自动发布接入文件，Meta 的个人 AI 智能体 Muse 也已全面启用这套协议。[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]

The post says Meta and Sierra's Personal Agent Protocol Poppy has released v1.0, moving from draft to large-scale application; Shopify has automatically published access documentation for millions of merchants, and Meta's personal AI agent Muse has fully enabled the protocol.[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Shopify 已为平台数百万商家自动发布 Poppy 接入文件，无需店主额外操作。[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Shopify has automatically published Poppy access documentation for millions of merchants on its platform, requiring no extra action from store owners.[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Meta 的个人 AI 智能体 Muse 已全面启用 Poppy 协议，每次访问网站都会优先查找 Poppy。[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Meta's personal AI agent Muse has fully enabled the Poppy protocol and prioritizes looking for Poppy on every website visit.[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Poppy 与 Shopify 购物协议 UCP 打通，前者负责身份识别和授权，后者负责商品搜索、加购物车和结账。[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Poppy is integrated with Shopify's shopping protocol UCP, with Poppy handling identity identification and authorization while UCP handles product search, add-to-cart, and checkout.[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Mercury 允许 AI 查询银行余额、整理交易记录并发起转账，但资金划转仍需经过账户审批。[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Mercury lets AI query bank balances, organize transaction records, and initiate transfers, though fund transfers still require account approval.[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- 此次又有 60 家企业（包括 Salesforce、Zoom、达美航空）加入协议设计，合作伙伴总数超过 100 家。[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
- Another 60 companies, including Salesforce, Zoom, and Delta Air Lines, joined the protocol design, bringing total partners to over 100.[^poppy-1-0-personal-agent-protocol]
