# OpenAI 私有智能

*OpenAI Private Intelligence*

OpenAI 在其企业页面“OpenAI Private Intelligence”中表示，企业可将先进智能用于最敏感的工作，并强调隐私保护应与智能同步提升。页面列出访问限制、数据所有权、智能体动作和数据安全四大保护维度，并提及零数据保留、传输中加密、私有推理和机密计算等机制。[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]

On its "OpenAI Private Intelligence" enterprise page, OpenAI says businesses can bring advanced intelligence to their most sensitive work, with privacy advancing as fast as intelligence. The page lists four protection dimensions—access restrictions, data ownership, agent actions, and data security—and mentions mechanisms such as zero data retention, encryption in transit, private inference, and confidential computing.[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 企业拥有其业务数据，包括输入和输出。[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- Enterprises own their business data, including inputs and outputs.[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- OpenAI 对业务内容的访问设有严格限制，并为合格工作流提供零数据保留和私有推理。[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- OpenAI sets strict limits on access to business content and provides Zero Data Retention and Private Inference for eligible workflows.[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- 用户可决定智能体能访问什么、可执行哪些动作，以及何时需要批准。[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- Users decide what agents can access, which actions they can take, and when approval is required.[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- 数据通过加密、严格访问控制和持续威胁检测受到保护，并涉及机密计算与可验证控制。[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
- Data is protected with encryption, strict access controls, and ongoing threat detection, involving confidential computing and strict verifiable controls.[^c1f89fa9e22b7c2c]
