# Mollick · 还没有 Fable 级的开源模型

*Mollick · No Fable-class open model yet*

帖文称，自 Fable/Mythos 首次公开以来已过去 8 个月，仍未出现同级的开源模型，这与许多人的预期相悖。文中还引用 Artificial Analysis 的信息，指出阿里 Qwen4 Max 在智能指数上较 Qwen3.8 Max 提升 9 分，得分 43，落后 Kimi K3.1 Pro 3 分，且每任务成本低约 11%。[^d052900d6fb11ec7]

The post says that, contrary to many expectations, no Fable-class open model has emerged more than 8 months after Fable/Mythos was first made public. It also cites Artificial Analysis, noting that Alibaba's Qwen4 Max improved 9 points over Qwen3.8 Max on the Intelligence Index to score 43, trailing Kimi K3.1 Pro by 3 points while costing about 11% less per task.[^d052900d6fb11ec7]
