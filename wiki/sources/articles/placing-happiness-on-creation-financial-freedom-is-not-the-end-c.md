# 把幸福的重心放在创造上：财务自由不是终点，创造才是乐趣来源

*Placing Happiness on Creation: Financial Freedom Is Not the End, Creation Is the Source of Joy*

「帖文称」郭宇转发了王立波关于财务自由的讨论，并配文表示，没有什么比创造并在创造过程中带来财富更让人幸福；创造过程本身有强大乐趣，其副作用是加强人与人联系，而财富只是创造的结果，只能保障享乐，却无法比拟创造带来的万分之一乐趣。王立波在原文中表示，虽然客观上并未拥有太多财富，但凭借“不再需要通过主动劳动获取收入，也能长期维持期望生活水平”的定义，他某种程度上已经实现财务自由。[^turingou-creation-happiness]

The post says Guoyu reposted Wang Libo's discussion on financial freedom, stating that nothing is happier than creating and bringing wealth through creation; the creative process itself is deeply enjoyable, its side effect strengthens human connections, and wealth is merely the result of creation—able to secure pleasure but unable to match even one ten-thousandth of the joy creation brings. Wang Libo wrote in the original post that although he does not objectively possess much wealth, by the definition of “no longer needing active labor for income while long maintaining one's desired standard of living,” he has to some extent achieved financial freedom.[^turingou-creation-happiness]
