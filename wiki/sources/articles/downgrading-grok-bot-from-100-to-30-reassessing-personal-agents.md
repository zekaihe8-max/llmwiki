The post says that xiaomovps decided to downgrade the Grok Bot subscription from $100 to $30, not due to insufficient usage but because of poor overall experience. The post also mentions free Muse and Hermes Agent are available, and evaluates OpenBot and OpenMausBot as open-source alternatives, stressing that a Personal Agent should not rely solely on one vendor and that knowledge base, memory, and work data should ideally remain in one's own hands.[^grok-bot-downgrade-100-to-30]
