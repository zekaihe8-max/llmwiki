# Alex · Fable 用法

*Alex · Claude Fable 5.2 Usage Tips*

帖文称 Alex Albert 很喜欢使用 Fable 5.2，认为其在大型开放式工作上显著优于 Opus 5.5，并给出四条使用建议：先询问构建中存在的问题，再让模型开始构建；从中高等努力级别开始，将最高级别留给最难问题；预先告知需要审批的操作（如删除内容），其余交由模型决定；交付完整项目而非单一步骤，并让其运行数小时。帖文还引用 Anthropic 官方介绍，称 Claude Fable 5.2 与 Claude Mythos 5.2 旨在应对艰巨技术挑战，在编程、推理和视觉等方面有所提升。[^alex-fable-usage]

The post says Alex Albert loves using Claude Fable 5.2, finding it a major step above Opus 5.5 for big, open-ended work, and shares four tips: ask what is wrong with an idea before building, start at medium/high effort and reserve the highest effort for the hardest problems, specify upfront what needs approval such as deleting things, and hand over whole projects to run for hours. It also quotes Anthropic's official introduction that Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 are designed to tackle daunting technical challenges, with improvements in areas like coding, reasoning, and vision.[^alex-fable-usage]
