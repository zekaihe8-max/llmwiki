# 铁柱AGI谈小铁柱群聊日报可雇

*cgnot996 on hireable Xiao Tiezhu group-chat digests*

[@cgnot996](https://x.com/cgnot996)（铁柱AGI）称答疑群运维助手「小铁柱」的群聊日报发布后反响热烈，已给 [@bbkirstry](https://x.com/bbkirstry) 小晚老师的答疑群也加上，很好解决了群友没时间刷群和爬楼的痛点。他发掘可变现点：让小铁柱替自己打工——可雇佣小铁柱去你的群里发群聊日报；并让 Grok Bot 为小铁柱做了宣传落地页，放在 Grok Bot 周边，落地页网址见评论区；有需求可看，也可聊完全定制开发。帖文附截图，并引用前一日上线说明：架构为接收群聊消息存储 → Grok Bot 处理 → 微信通信渠道发送；不设计成所有人都能与 Bot 沟通（以免群聊更乱），只作自动发送渠道、定时发资讯与日报整理，且只响应作者 @ 它的消息。[^cgnot996-xiao-tiezhu-group-digest-hire] 原帖见 <https://x.com/cgnot996/status/2115317431019430249>。

[@cgnot996](https://x.com/cgnot996) says the Q&A-group ops assistant “Xiao Tiezhu” group-chat digests were warmly received after launch and were also added to [@bbkirstry](https://x.com/bbkirstry) teacher Xiaowan’s Q&A group—well solving members’ lack of time to scroll and crawl the chat. He spots a monetization angle: let Xiao Tiezhu work for him—you can hire Xiao Tiezhu to post group-chat digests in your group; he had Grok Bot build a promo landing page placed among Grok Bot peripherals, with the URL in the replies; people with that need can look, or talk full custom development. Screenshots attach, and he quotes the prior-day launch note: architecture is receive/store group messages → Grok Bot process → WeChat channel send; not designed so everyone can talk to the bot (that would clutter the chat)—only an auto-send channel for scheduled news/digests, responding only when the author @-mentions it.[^cgnot996-xiao-tiezhu-group-digest-hire] Post: <https://x.com/cgnot996/status/2115317431019430249>.
