# AI 原生公司定义补充：信息反馈与商业模拟环境

*Supplement to AI Native Company Definition: Information Feedback and Business Simulation Environments*

帖文称，AI 原生公司的一个有趣定义并非关于人与 Agent 的协作方式（即 harness 设计），而是关于信息反馈以及如何为商业本身构建模拟环境的语言结构。原帖还预测，到 2030 年，每家公司都将成为强化学习（RL）环境公司。[^turingou-ai-native-company-definition]

The post states that an interesting definition of AI Native companies is not about how humans collaborate with Agents (i.e., harness design), but about information feedback and the language structure for building simulation environments for business itself. It also predicts that by 2030, every company will be an RL environment company.[^turingou-ai-native-company-definition]
