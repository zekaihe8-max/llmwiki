# A 社封号经济学

*The Economics of Account Bans at Company A*

帖文称，A 社因封号损失了 5% 到 7% 的收入，路透社估计损失约 20 亿美元；该社称封号是为了反蒸馏，以维持模型代际差带来的定价能力。帖文还引用 2026 年上半年数据：收入 162.86 亿美元，推理毛利率高达 85%，但研发投入 97.41 亿美元，导致经营亏损 6.93 亿元。[^oran-ge-a-she-feng-hao-ji-xue]

The post claims Company A lost 5–7% of revenue from account bans, estimated by Reuters at about $2 billion, and says bans are enforced for anti-distillation to preserve the pricing power of model generational gaps. It cites 2026 first-half figures: $16.286 billion revenue, up to 85% inference gross margin, but $9.741 billion R&D spending resulting in a $693 million operating loss.[^oran-ge-a-she-feng-hao-ji-xue]
