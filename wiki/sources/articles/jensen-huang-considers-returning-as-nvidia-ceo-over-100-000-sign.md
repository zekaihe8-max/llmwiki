# 黄仁勋考虑回归英伟达 CEO，超 10 万人签公开信支持

*Jensen Huang considers returning as Nvidia CEO, over 100,000 sign open letter in support*

帖文称，英国《金融时报》11 月 15 日报道，黄仁勋正在考虑重新出任英伟达 CEO，并已与几位亲信讨论回归可能；此前英伟达于 11 月 12 日宣布其辞去 CEO 和董事职务，CFO Colette Kress 任临时 CEO，而独立调查尚未结束。超 10 万人签署“Stand with Jensen”公开信，马斯克、Tim Sweeney、Garry Tan、Amjad Masad 等在列；Altimeter Capital 与 Atreides Management 两位投资人亦致信董事会，建议审慎处理接班事宜并保留黄仁勋回归选项。Kalshi 上其复职赔率随报道升至 20%。[^356b2e3768b94a9f]

The post cites the Financial Times' November 15 report that Jensen Huang is considering returning as Nvidia CEO and has discussed a comeback with close associates; Nvidia announced on November 12 that he resigned as CEO and board member, with CFO Colette Kress serving as interim CEO while an independent investigation remains unfinished. More than 100,000 people signed the "Stand with Jensen" open letter, including Musk, Tim Sweeney, Garry Tan, and Amjad Masad; investors Brad Gerstner and Gavin Baker also wrote to the board urging careful succession planning while keeping Huang's return as an option.
