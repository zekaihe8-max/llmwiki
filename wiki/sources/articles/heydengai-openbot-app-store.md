# 小邓同学谈 OpenBot 上架 App Store

*Heydengai on OpenBot shipping on the App Store*

[@heydengai](https://x.com/heydengai)（小邓同学）称 OpenBot 正式上架苹果 App Store：连接自己的服务器，即可通过 iPhone 随时随地访问与操控 AI Agent；支持 Claude Code、Codex、Gemini、Grok、Cursor、OpenCode、Cline 等，并可接入 Ollama、LM Studio 本地模型；支持多 Agent 协作与任务交接，也能直接使用已有的 AI 订阅套餐——相当于把电脑上的 AI Agent 工作台装进手机。帖中转引 [@OpenBot_](https://x.com/OpenBot_)：已上架、告别 TestFlight，下载后连服务器即可随身带着 agents；站点 [openbot.run](https://openbot.run/)。[^heydengai-openbot-app-store] 原帖见 <https://x.com/heydengai/status/2115367414368202876>。

[@heydengai](https://x.com/heydengai) (小邓同学 / Heydengai) says OpenBot is now on the Apple App Store: connect your own server and access and control AI agents from an iPhone anytime; it supports Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Grok, Cursor, OpenCode, Cline, and peers, plus local models via Ollama and LM Studio; multi-agent collaboration and task handoff are supported, and existing AI subscription plans can be used directly—framed as putting the desktop AI-agent workbench into the phone. The post quotes [@OpenBot_](https://x.com/OpenBot_): on the App Store, no more TestFlight; download, connect to your server, and take your agents with you; site [openbot.run](https://openbot.run/).[^heydengai-openbot-app-store] Post: <https://x.com/heydengai/status/2115367414368202876>.
