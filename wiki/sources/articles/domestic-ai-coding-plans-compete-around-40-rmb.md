# 国内 AI Coding 套餐卷到 40 元左右

*Domestic AI coding plans compete around 40 RMB*

帖文称，国内 AI Coding 套餐现在卷到 40 元左右就能用上不少旗舰模型。作者浏览了火山方舟、小米 MiMo、MiniMax 三家套餐，发现大厂开始转向 Credits、Token 等额度计费，更适合 Claude Code、OpenCode、OpenClaw 等 Agent 工具。火山方舟 Agent Plan Small 40 元/月，支持多家模型并按 AFP 积分扣除；小米 MiMo Token Plan Lite 39 元/月，提供 41 亿 Credits 且无 5 小时额度限制；MiniMax M Plan Plus 49 元/月，侧重长期运行 Coding Agent，支持 3～4 个 Agent 并发。作者认为喜欢切换模型可优先考虑火山方舟，在意连续跑 Agent 可尝试小米 MiMo，业务需求不高但需要大量 Token 可考虑国内厂商套餐。[^f64cff2816f81a0f]

The post says domestic AI coding plans now cost around 40 RMB and provide access to many flagship models. The author reviewed Volcano Ark, Xiaomi MiMo, and MiniMax plans, finding that major vendors are shifting to Credits/Token quota billing, better suited to Agent tools such as Claude Code, OpenCode, and OpenClaw. Volcano Ark Agent Plan Small costs 40 RMB/month and supports multiple models with AFP points; Xiaomi MiMo Token Plan Lite costs 39 RMB/month with 4.1 billion shared Credits and no 5-hour quota limit; MiniMax M Plan Plus costs 49 RMB/month and targets long-running Coding Agents with 3–4 concurrent Agents. The author suggests Volcano Ark for model switching, Xiaomi MiMo for continuous Agent runs, and domestic vendor plans for users with low business needs but high Token consumption.[^f64cff2816f81a0f]
