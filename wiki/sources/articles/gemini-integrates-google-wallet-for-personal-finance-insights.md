# Gemini 接入 Google Wallet，提供个人理财洞察

*Gemini Integrates Google Wallet for Personal Finance Insights*

「帖文称」Google 宣布 Gemini 的个人智能（Personal Intelligence）现已连接 Google Wallet，用户可在 Gemini App 中基于消费习惯获得更个性化的理财回复，包括生成现金流与资产配置动态图表、发现被遗忘订阅并标记涨价，以及依据去年礼物与旅行支出设定节日预算；用户还可选择通过 Plaid 将信用卡与银行账户安全关联至 Wallet 以解锁更深入洞察。[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]

Google announced that Gemini's Personal Intelligence now connects to Google Wallet, allowing users to receive more personalized finance-related responses in the Gemini App based on spending habits, including dynamic cash flow and asset allocation charts, forgotten subscription detection with price-increase flags, and holiday budgeting based on last year's gift and travel spending; users may also optionally link credit cards and bank accounts to Wallet via Plaid for deeper insights.[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Gemini 个人智能新增 Google Wallet 作为可连接应用，用于围绕个人理财问题提供更个性化回复。[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- Gemini Personal Intelligence adds Google Wallet as a connectable app to provide more personalized responses around personal finance questions.[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- 用户可在对话中直接生成现金流、资产配置和预算的动态图表，以可视化支出。[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- Users can generate dynamic charts tracking cash flow, asset allocation, and budgets directly in chat to visualize spending.[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- Gemini 可查找关联账户中的被遗忘订阅和定期费用，并会标记任何价格上涨。[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- Gemini can find forgotten subscriptions and recurring charges across linked accounts and flag any price increases.[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- 用户可询问去年礼物和旅行支出，Gemini 会依据过去一年交易建议定制节日预算。[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- Users can ask about last year's gift and travel spending, and Gemini will suggest a tailored holiday budget based on transactions from the last year.[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- 用户可选择通过 Plaid 将信用卡和银行账户安全关联至 Google Wallet，以解锁更深入的理财洞察。[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
- Users can optionally securely link financial accounts from credit cards and banks to Wallet via Plaid to unlock deeper insights.[^google-gemini-wallet-personal-intelligence]
