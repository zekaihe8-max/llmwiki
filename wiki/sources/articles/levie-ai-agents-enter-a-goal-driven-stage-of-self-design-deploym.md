# Levie：智能体进入“给目标、自主设计并持续改进”的新阶段

*Levie: AI agents enter a goal-driven stage of self-design, deployment, and continuous improvement*

帖文称，AI 智能体正进入又一个新时代：从快速回答和任务编排，发展到给定目标后由智能体自行设计最佳方案、基于持续执行进行改进，并部署、优化所构建的内容。这一趋势最先在工程领域出现，但将扩展到客户入职、风险降低和安全态势等知识工作，带来人类此前无法实现的成果与新的生产力水平。[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]

The post says AI agents are entering yet another era, moving from quick answers and orchestrated tasks to a stage where users give agents goals and the agents design the best solution, continuously improve it through ongoing execution, test and iterate, deploy, and optimize. It is expected to start in engineering before expanding to knowledge work such as client onboarding, risk reduction, and security posture, enabling outcomes humans could not previously achieve.[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]

- Box 联合创始人 Aaron Levie 在 X 上发文称，AI 智能体正从快速回答和任务编排进入又一个新时代。[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- Box co-founder Aaron Levie posted on X that AI agents are moving from quick answers and task orchestration into yet another era.[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- 帖文称，用户很快能给智能体一组目标，智能体可为该目标设计最佳方案，并基于持续执行不断改进。[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- The post says users will soon be able to give agents a set of goals, and agents can design the best solution for that goal while continuously improving it based on ongoing execution.[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- Levie 表示这一变化最先从工程领域开始，因为几乎每个智能体新时代的最佳实践都最先在工程中出现。[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- Levie says this will start with engineering because that is where best practices emerge first in almost every new era of agents.[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- 文中举例包括：让智能体按目标改进客户入职流程、持续降低业务风险、持续提升安全态势。[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- The post lists examples such as improving client onboarding workflows to a targeted goal, reducing business risk, and improving security posture on an ongoing basis.[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
- 自主系统将承担目标、设计结果、测试迭代、部署，并对所构建内容持续优化。[^levie-agent-goals-outcome-engineering]
