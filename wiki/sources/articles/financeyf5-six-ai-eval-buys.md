# AI Will 谈一年收购 6 家 AI 评测公司

*AI Will on six AI-eval acquisitions in a year*

[@FinanceYF5](https://x.com/FinanceYF5)（AI Will）问硅谷大厂一年收了 6 家 AI 评测公司、买的到底是什么；并称做 AI 评测和监控的 23 家公司今年被买走 6 家——Dynatrace 以 9.15 亿美元买 Arize、思科买 Galileo、ServiceNow 买 Traceloop、ClickHouse 买 Langfuse 等——最值钱的 Arena 没卖，估值 31 亿美元。其判断：大厂买的不是软件，是 AI 干活记录流进来的那道门；被买公司门后的数据是客户的，Arena 的数据是它自己的，中立才值钱，所以买不走（作者标明后一句为推断）。[^financeyf5-six-ai-eval-buys] 帖文见 <https://x.com/FinanceYF5/status/2113804141403549762>。

[@FinanceYF5](https://x.com/FinanceYF5) (AI Will) asks what big Silicon Valley firms were really buying when they acquired six AI-eval companies in a year; among 23 AI eval-and-monitoring firms, six were bought this year—Dynatrace paid $915M for Arize, Cisco bought Galileo, ServiceNow bought Traceloop, ClickHouse bought Langfuse, and others—while the most valuable, Arena, did not sell and is valued at $3.1B. The take: firms are not buying software but the door AI work records flow through; behind that door at acquired companies the data is the customer’s, while Arena’s data is its own—neutrality is what makes it valuable, so it resists being bought (the author flags that last clause as inference).[^financeyf5-six-ai-eval-buys] The post is at <https://x.com/FinanceYF5/status/2113804141403549762>.
