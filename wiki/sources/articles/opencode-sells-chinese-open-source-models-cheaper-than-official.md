# OpenCode 把国产开源模型卖得比官方还便宜

*OpenCode sells Chinese open-source models cheaper than official prices*

「帖文称」海外平台 OpenCode 宣布智谱 GLM 5.3 和 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 降价 60%，按四折出售：每 100 万 token，GLM 5.3 官方要 1.4 美元，OpenCode 只要 0.56 美元；DeepSeek V4.1 Flash OpenCode 只卖 0.12 美元，而官方低谷半价时段也要 0.15 美元。帖文认为，这两款模型文件免费公开，OpenCode 无需承担训练成本，只需租显卡运行即可低价转卖；国产厂商开源是因为显卡紧张，需外部平台分担流量，且海外用户不愿为国产模型付高价，开源有助于抢占工具生态，后续再靠企业服务变现，但如何回收训练成本仍未明。[^lxfater-opencode-cheaper-models]

The post says overseas platform OpenCode cut prices for Zhipu GLM 5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash by 60%, selling at 40% of the official rate: per 1 million tokens, GLM 5.3 costs $1.4 officially but only $0.56 on OpenCode, while DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is $0.12 on OpenCode versus $0.15 even in the official off-peak half-price period. It argues that because the two models' files are publicly free, OpenCode pays no training cost and can simply rent GPUs to run and resell them cheaply; Chinese vendors open-source models because GPUs are scarce and external platforms help absorb traffic, while overseas users are unwilling to pay high prices for domestic models, so open source helps capture tools and ecosystems before monetizing enterprise services—though how to recoup training costs remains unclear.[^lxfater-opencode-cheaper-models]
