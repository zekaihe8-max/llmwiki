# AI 科学发现报告：研究人员如何使用 Claude

*AI for Scientific Discovery: How Researchers Use Claude*

据 Anthropic 发布的报告《AI for Scientific Discovery: How Researchers Use Claude》称，研究团队通过隐私保护基础设施，对 2026 年 8 月 2 日至 9 月 30 日期间的 207,049 个 Claude 和 Claude Code 研究会话进行了分析。报告指出，相对于研究人员数量，计算机科学、数学和统计学等形式科学使用 Claude 更为密集，而健康与实验室型自然科学使用较少；数据分析约占 Claude 会话的三分之一，Claude Code 会话的近一半。报告还以简单模型推演，若现有 AI 能力被广泛采用，部分领域研究产出可能翻倍；若 AI 能承担所有非物理研究工作，计算机科学与统计学产出最高可提升 15 倍，化学与生物学等基准科学可能提升三到四倍。[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]

According to Anthropic's report 'AI for Scientific Discovery: How Researchers Use Claude,' the research team analyzed 207,049 Claude and Claude Code research sessions from August 2 to September 30, 2026 using privacy-preserving infrastructure. The report finds that relative to researcher numbers, formal sciences such as computer science, mathematics, and statistics use Claude more intensively, while health and lab-based physical sciences use it less; data analysis accounts for about one-third of Claude sessions and nearly half of Claude Code sessions. The report also models scenarios in which widespread adoption of current AI capabilities could double research output in some fields, and full automation of non-physical research work could increase output by up to 15-fold in computer science and statistics, and three- to four-fold in bench sciences such as chemistry and biology.[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 样本包含 207,049 个研究会话，其中 128,914 个来自 Claude（Chat 与 Cowork），78,135 个来自 Claude Code，时间为 2026 年 8 月 2 日至 9 月 30 日。 / The sample includes 207,049 research sessions—128,914 from Claude (Chat and Cowork) and 78,135 from Claude Code—between August 2 and September 30, 2026.[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]
- 相对于各领域研究人员规模，计算机科学、数学和统计学等形式科学的 Claude 使用相对更高；健康科学和实验室型自然科学使用相对较低。 / Relative to researcher numbers, formal sciences such as computer science, mathematics, and statistics show higher relative Claude use, while health sciences and lab-based physical sciences show lower relative use.[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]
- 数据分析约占 Claude 会话的三分之一，并占 Claude Code 会话的近一半；科研人员主要用 Claude 进行数据分析和写作，而用于构思假设和收集数据的比例较低。 / Data analysis accounts for about one-third of Claude sessions and nearly half of Claude Code sessions; scientists primarily use Claude for data analysis and writing, with lower shares for idea generation and data gathering.[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]
- 报告称，AI 理论能力最强的前 20% 任务获得的 Claude 使用量是研究时间预测值的两到四倍，而能力最弱的后 20% 任务使用量极低。 / The report states that the top 20% of tasks best suited to AI receive two to four times as much Claude use as time spent would predict, while the bottom 20% receive very little use.[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]
- 在现有 AI 能力广泛采用的情景下，部分领域研究产出可能翻倍；若 AI 能承担所有非物理研究工作，计算机科学与统计学产出最高可提升 15 倍，化学与生物学可能提升三到四倍。 / Under widespread adoption of current AI capabilities, research output in some fields could double; if AI carried out all non-physical research work, output in computer science and statistics could rise up to 15-fold, while chemistry and biology could rise three- to four-fold.[^ai-scientific-discovery-claude-research]
