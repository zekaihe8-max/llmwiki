# Vibe Coding 是另一种奶头乐

*Vibe Coding Is Another Form of 'Naitou Le'*

The post claims Vibe Coding is another form of 'naitou le' (mindless entertainment), differing from watching Hongguo short dramas only in that the latter is free while Vibe Coding requires users to pay for tokens themselves. It also quotes replies comparing endless Agent interactions and building useless wheels to short dramas, and a remark that not watching Hongguo short dramas could become a core competitiveness in the new era.[^oran-ge-vibe-coding-naitou-le]
