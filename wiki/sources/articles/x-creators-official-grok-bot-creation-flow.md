# X Creators 官方 Grok Bot 创作流

*X Creators Official Grok Bot Creation Flow*

帖文称，X Creators 官方账号分享了一套基于 Grok Bot 的内容创作流程，建议创作者把过去作品、喜欢案例和修改意见交给 AI，让其逐渐理解风格；拆解收藏视频的开头、节奏和受众适配方法；在脚本完成后让 AI 指出开场拖沓、表达冗余及观众可能划走的位置；把原始素材交给 AI 先看剪辑方案，再审片、反馈、修改；发布后结合数据复盘，将经验用到下一条。文中还提到 Ethan 一次交给 Grok Bot 10 份视频素材，约两小时完成含动画和配乐的剪辑，并经历几轮反馈。[^x-creators-grok-bot-creation-flow]

The post says X Creators' official account shared a Grok Bot-based content creation flow, suggesting creators feed past works, favorite cases, and revision feedback to AI so it gradually learns their style; deconstruct collected videos for hooks, pacing, and audience fit; ask AI to flag slow openings, redundant expressions, and potential drop-off points after scripting; hand raw materials to AI for editing plans before review, feedback, and revision; and after publishing, combine data for review to apply lessons to the next piece. It also mentions Ethan gave Grok Bot 10 video materials and completed editing with animation and music in about two hours, with several feedback rounds.[^x-creators-grok-bot-creation-flow]
