# 伊森·莫利克评 GDPx 智能体基准

*Ethan Mollick on GDPx Agent Benchmark*

The post calls GDPx a successor to GDPval for evaluating AI work; Ethan Mollick says it still needs human baselines like GDPval, notes GPT-6 Pro spotted grading errors, and says 0% scores hide economically meaningful differences in success rates, though it remains a valuable benchmark. SemiAnalysis released GDPx to measure agentic capabilities, with the best model scoring just 12% (1/7).[^emollick-gdp-x-agent-benchmark]
