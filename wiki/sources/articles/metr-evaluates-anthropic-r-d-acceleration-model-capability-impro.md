# METR 评估 Anthropic 研发加速：模型能力提升速度翻倍，AI 辅助研发成主力

*METR Evaluates Anthropic R&D Acceleration: Model Capability Improvement Doubles, AI-Assisted R&D Primary Driver*

帖文称第三方评估机构 METR 受邀深入 Anthropic 内部，独立测算 AI 对自身研发的加速效应。核心结论包括：至 2026 年 10 月，Anthropic 在定义明确任务上的模型能力提升速度已达 2024 年 3 月至 2025 年 10 月期间的约 2 倍；目前超一半加速归功于 AI 辅助人类研究员，而非 AI 完全自主研发；未来 12 个月内未发现算法、训练算力、实验算力、数据等明显瓶颈；若进一步自动化 AI 研发任务、消除人为操作瓶颈，或将引发新一轮更迅猛加速。报告另暗示 Anthropic 内部至少还有 Model 3、Model 4 两个领先于 Opus 5.5 的模型。[^metr-anthropic-rd-acceleration-2026]

The post reports that third-party evaluator METR was invited into Anthropic to independently measure AI's acceleration of its own R&D. Key findings: by Oct 2026, Anthropic's model capability improvement speed on well-defined tasks reached ~2x the Mar 2024–Oct 2025 rate; over half of acceleration comes from AI assisting human researchers, not fully autonomous AI R&D; no obvious bottlenecks (algorithms, training compute, experiment compute, data) for the next 12 months; further automation of AI R&D tasks could trigger a new, faster acceleration wave. The report also implies Anthropic has at least two models ahead of Opus 5.5 (Model 3 and Model 4).[^metr-anthropic-rd-acceleration-2026]
