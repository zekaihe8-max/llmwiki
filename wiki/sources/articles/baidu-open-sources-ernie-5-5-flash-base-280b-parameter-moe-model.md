# 百度开源 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base，280B 参数 MoE 模型

*Baidu Open-Sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, 280B-Parameter MoE Model*

帖文称，百度开源发布了 ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base，一个 MoE 架构模型，拥有 280B 总参数与每 Token 14B 激活参数。Baidu Research 表示该模型已在同规模中达到 SOTA，且完整版 ERNIE 5.5 Flash 即将推出。帖文还指出，此前对比未使用各家最新模型是因为希望同参数量级对比，并列举了 DeepSeek、Hy、MiMo 等模型的对应版本供参考。[^ernie-5-5-flash-base-open-source]

The post says Baidu has open-sourced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, an MoE architecture model with 280B total parameters and 14B active parameters per token. Baidu Research states it already matches SOTA at its size, and the full ERNIE 5.5 Flash is coming soon. The post also notes that earlier benchmarks did not compare against the latest models from other vendors because it wanted same-parameter-scale comparisons, and lists corresponding DeepSeek, Hy, and MiMo model versions for reference.[^ernie-5-5-flash-base-open-source]
