# 手机上先把 Antigravity 加到主屏幕

*Add Antigravity to Your Phone Home Screen First*

帖文称，Antigravity 目前还没有手机 App，但据说很快发布；在此之前，用户可通过打开 antigravity.google.com 并登录，点击“查看更多”后点分享按钮，选择“添加到主屏幕”，从而在手机上直接远程操控并回复所有 Antigravity 对话。[^antigravity-mobile-home-screen]

The post states that Antigravity has no mobile app yet, though one is reportedly coming soon; in the meantime, users can open antigravity.google.com, log in, tap “See more” then the share button, choose “Add to Home Screen,” and thereby remotely control and reply to all Antigravity conversations directly from their phone.[^antigravity-mobile-home-screen]
