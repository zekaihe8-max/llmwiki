# 谷歌模型突然变强的背后：夏季大换血与工程交付转向

*Behind Google's Sudden Model Strength: Summer Overhaul and Shift to Engineering Delivery*

帖文称，谷歌近期密集发布 Argon、Carbon、Hydrogen、Neon 等模型，写代码和长任务的稳健度明显提升。作者认为这源于六月至八月的大换血：Jeff Dean、Noam Shazeer、Oriol Vinyals 三位联创出走，Hassabis 卸任 DeepMind CEO，Transformer 论文八位作者彻底清零。随后重组让 Koray Kavukcuoglu 单线统管 DeepMind，Sergey Brin 每周回归坐班三四天，团队与 TPU 算力第一次全押在交付上。后训练由 Melvin Johnson 负责，用内部十万工程师真实踩坑的报错做 RL，并用更严评分标准卡死代码边界。去年花 24 亿美元挖来的 Windsurf 团队也交出 Antigravity，把真实工程交互接回模型训练，跑通数据飞轮。作者总结，谷歌已从发论文的研究院变成只盯交付的工程机器。[^xiaomovps-2125484340998213921]

The post claims Google recently released models including Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen, and Neon in quick succession, with coding and long-task stability markedly improved. The author attributes this to a June–August overhaul in which co-founders Jeff Dean, Noam Shazeer, and Oriol Vinyals departed, Hassabis stepped down as DeepMind CEO, and all eight Transformer paper authors left. After reorganization, DeepMind was placed under Koray Kavukcuoglu for single-line management, Sergey Brin returned to work on-site three or four days a week, and teams plus TPU compute were fully focused on delivery. Post-training is led by Melvin Johnson, using real bug reports from 100,000 internal engineers for RL and stricter scoring to lock code boundaries. The Windsurf team hired last year for $2.4 billion delivered Antigravity, feeding real engineering interaction back into model training to close the data flywheel. The author concludes Google has shifted from a paper-publishing research institute to an engineering delivery machine.[^xiaomovps-2125484340998213921]
