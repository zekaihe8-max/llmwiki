# 李彦宏在百度世界 2026 推出「词元所」Token Exchange

*Robin Li Launches "Token Exchange - 词元所" at Baidu World 2026*

帖文称，百度创始人李彦宏在百度世界 2026 开幕式上宣布正式推出「Token Exchange - 词元所 - 模型的交易所」，整合百度模型与国内开源文本、图像及音视频模型；发文者指出其主打“可信”卖点，包括真实性、稳定达成、性能容量达成和能力达成，但怀疑这只是换了更高大上的名字的中转站，未看到本质区别，目前不太看好。[^baidu-token-exchange-2026]

The post says Baidu founder Robin Li officially launched "Token Exchange - 词元所 - Model Exchange" at the Baidu World 2026 opening ceremony, integrating Baidu models and domestic open-source text, image, and audio/video models; the author notes its main selling point is "trustworthiness," including authenticity, stable delivery, performance/capacity delivery, and capability delivery, but suspects it is merely a relay station with a fancier name and sees no essential difference, currently not optimistic.[^baidu-token-exchange-2026]
