# 当智能体执行工作时保持批判性思维

*Critical thinking when agents do the work*

「帖文称」随着代理工作流让问答之间的空间被压缩至秒级，人们越来越容易将思考外包给 AI，甚至滑向认知投降，失去对输出结果的所有权与解释能力。[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]

The post argues that as agentic workflows compress the space between question and answer to seconds, people increasingly offload thinking to AI and may slide into cognitive surrender, losing ownership and the ability to explain outputs.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 作者将 AI 使用描述为认知外卸、递让与认知投降的光谱，认知投降是指 AI 输出悄然成为自己的观点，且无需核查。 / The author describes AI use as a spectrum from cognitive offloading to deferral and cognitive surrender, where AI output quietly becomes one's own view without checking.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]
- 认知投降一词由沃顿商学院的 Steven Shaw 和 Gideon Nave 提出，Margaret-Anne Storey 还写过团队共享系统理论侵蚀中的认知债。 / Cognitive surrender is a term coined by Wharton's Steven Shaw and Gideon Nave; Margaret-Anne Storey has written about cognitive debt eroding shared system theory in teams.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]
- 作者提出自我提问清单，包括是否先形成假设、是在请求 AI 帮助思考还是外包思考、什么条件下结论会错误、是否听过反方观点等。 / The author proposes a self-questioning checklist including whether a hypothesis was formed first, whether AI is helping thinking or replacing it, what would have to be true for the conclusion to be wrong, and whether counter-arguments were heard.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]
- 验证应作为习惯，定义“已验证”的标准，绿色测试不代表功能未损坏，审查代码时应像 AI 未编写过一样阅读 diff，并能为所交付内容辩护。 / Verification should be a habit; define "verified" upfront, green tests do not guarantee unbroken features, review diffs as if AI did not write them, and understand shipped work well enough to defend it.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]
- 当无法向需维护系统的人解释答案，甚至借助代理也无法说明时，就已放弃所有权；低风险场景可完全委派，高风险决策应保留推理。 / When you cannot explain an answer to the person who must maintain the system, even with your agent's help, you have ceded ownership; low-stakes decisions can be delegated entirely, but high-stakes decisions should retain reasoning.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]
- 代理让验证变得廉价，但判断什么是良好验证很困难；当生成的工作量超过你能背书的量时，就不再是审查，而是在寄希望于结果。 / Agents make verification cheap, but knowing what good verification looks like is difficult; when output exceeds what you can stand behind, you are no longer reviewing, just hoping.[^addy-osmani-critical-thinking-agents]
