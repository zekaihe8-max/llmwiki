# FrontierHarness v1.1：21 套 Harness × 5 模型 × 62 任务，规模扩大 72 倍

*FrontierHarness v1.1: 21 Harnesses × 5 Models × 62 Tasks, 72× Scale-Up*

「帖文称」Guanlan Dai 团队正在投入最高 100B Token、完成 26,040 次 Agent 运行，探讨一个问题：当你切换模型时，是否也应该切换 Harness？FrontierHarness v1.0 曾在单一模型上对 Harness 进行排行；v1.1 将规模扩大到 21 个 Harness × 5 个模型 × 62 个任务 × 4 次尝试，是 v1.0 的 72 倍，结果正在实时公布。[^frontier-harness-v1-1]

According to the post, Guanlan Dai's team is spending up to 100B tokens across 26,040 agent runs to ask whether switching models should also mean switching harnesses. FrontierHarness v1.0 ranked harnesses on a single model; v1.1 expands to 21 harnesses × 5 models × 62 tasks × 4 attempts, 72× the size of v1.0, with results coming in live.[^frontier-harness-v1-1]
