# 岗位涨跌：科技招聘需求分化

*Job Market Shifts: Diverging Tech Hiring Demand*

帖文称，过去一年科技岗位开放职位出现明显分化：前端工程师下降 51%、iOS/Android 工程师下降 46%、UX 研究员下降 45%、产品设计师降至历史最低并下降 28%；与此同时，AI 工程师上升 46%、安全工程师上升 34%、硬件工程师翻倍、前瞻部署工程师增长 4.6 倍。数据基于开放职位的同比百分比变化。[^e399ac6a9467cf82]

The post says tech job openings diverged over the past year: front-end engineers down 51%, iOS/Android engineers down 46%, UX researchers down 45%, and product designers at a record low down 28%; meanwhile AI engineers rose 46%, security engineers up 34%, hardware engineers doubled, and forward-deployed engineers grew 4.6x. Figures are based on percentage change in open roles over the past year.[^e399ac6a9467cf82]
