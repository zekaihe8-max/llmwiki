# No AI Slop 宣言

*No AI Slop Manifesto*

「帖文称」，由 AI Engineer Code Summit 2026 演讲者和参会者共同撰写的《No AI Slop 宣言》以 2001 年敏捷宣言为参照，提出五组对照，并定义 slop 为“投入的心思少于要求接收者花在理解上的精力”。宣言强调，使用 AI 本身不等于 slop，但将模型产出转手发出且不对质量和读者体验负责才会构成 slop；人类判断不能外包，审核才是关键差别。帖文还提到，宣言已登上 Hacker News 首页，签名者包括来自 Baseten、SpaceX、OpenAI、PostHog、Shopify、Glean、ZoomInfo 等团队的工程师，GitHub Gist 上已列出 504 个签名。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]

The post says the "No AI Slop Manifesto," co-written by speakers and attendees of AI Engineer Code Summit 2026, references the 2001 Agile Manifesto and proposes five contrasts, defining slop as putting in less thought than you ask the receiver to spend understanding. It stresses that using AI is not automatically slop, but handing off model output without taking responsibility for quality and reader experience is; human judgment cannot be outsourced, and review is the key difference. The post also says the manifesto reached Hacker News' front page, with signers from Baseten, SpaceX, OpenAI, PostHog, Shopify, Glean, and ZoomInfo, and 504 signatures listed on its GitHub Gist.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 宣言提出五组对照：结果重于产量，理解重于速度，自己的思考重于模型的第一稿，更好的工作重于更省事的工作，改进系统重于修补输出。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- The manifesto proposes five contrasts: outcomes over output, understanding over speed, one's own thinking over the model's first draft, better work over easier work, and improving systems over patching outputs.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 帖文称 slop 的定义是：你投入的心思，少于你要求接收者花在理解上的精力；用了 AI 并不自动等于 slop。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- The post defines slop as investing less thought than you ask the receiver to spend understanding, and notes that using AI does not automatically equal slop.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- Agent 一天产出可能超过一个人一辈子审得完的量，生产成本趋近于零，但审核成本几乎没降，产出还会进入知识库和代码被下一个 Agent 模仿。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- An agent can produce in a day more than one person can review in a lifetime; production cost approaches zero while review cost hardly drops, and such output enters knowledge bases and code to be imitated by the next agent.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 宣言强调判断不能外包，AI 只能放大判断（包括判断力的不足），意图、品味和后果仍由人承担。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- The manifesto stresses that judgment cannot be outsourced; AI only amplifies judgment, including its deficiencies, and intent, taste, and consequences remain human responsibilities.[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
- 帖文称 Horthy 发帖时已有 300 多个签名，宣言登上 Hacker News 首页，GitHub Gist 已列出 504 个签名，HN 讨论约 222 分、133 条评论。[^no-ai-slop-manifesto]
