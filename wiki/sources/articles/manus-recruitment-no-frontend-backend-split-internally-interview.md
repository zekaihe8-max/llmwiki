# Manus 招聘：内部不分前后端，面试改用 Manus 实战四小时

*Manus Recruitment: No Frontend/Backend Split Internally, Interviews Use Four-Hour Manus Practice*

帖文称，Manus 招聘页面仍挂着前端工程师、后端工程师等岗位，但联合创始人张涛在 Web Summit 上表示，公司内部已经不分这些角色；产品经理都在往代码库提交代码，设计师也在写代码，按岗位写招聘启事只是“写给外界看的”，因为“外面的世界还没跟上”。变化从今年 3 月前后开始，AI 本身很会做事，会做事的人不再稀缺，Manus 现在要找的是能和 AI 一起干活的人。工程师面试不再考传统编程题，改为“用 Manus 花四小时解决一个问题”，候选人带回完整的人机来回记录与面试官复盘，考察是否已习惯和 AI 一起工作。张涛还说，对外官网仍按前端、后端等岗位招人，对内角色已撤销，理由是有了 AI“每个人都能做所有事”。[^manus-recruitment-ai-native]

The post says Manus' recruitment page still lists frontend and backend engineer roles, but co-founder Zhang Tao stated at Web Summit that the company no longer divides such roles internally; product managers submit code to repositories and designers also write code, with role-based job postings written only for outsiders because "the outside world hasn't caught up." The change began around March this year; since AI is already good at getting things done, people who can get things done are no longer scarce, and Manus now seeks people who can work with AI. Engineer interviews no longer test traditional programming questions, instead asking candidates to "use Manus to solve a problem in four hours" and bring back the full human-AI interaction record for review with the interviewer, to assess whether they are accustomed to working with AI. Zhang Tao added that externally the official website still hires by frontend/backend roles, while internally roles have been abolished because with AI "everyone can do everything."[^manus-recruitment-ai-native]
