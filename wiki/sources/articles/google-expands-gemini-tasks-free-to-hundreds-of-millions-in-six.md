# Google 免费向六国数亿用户扩展 Gemini Tasks

*Google Expands Gemini Tasks Free to Hundreds of Millions in Six Countries*

「帖文称」Google 从当天起，将 Gemini Tasks 免费扩展到美国、日本、韩国、加拿大、澳大利亚和印度的数亿更多用户。用户只需在 Gemini App 中将切换项从“聊天”切换到“任务”，描述需求，即可让 Gemini 在后台处理较长项目，并支持设置定期重复的待办，例如更新家庭日历或查找未使用的订阅优惠。[^google-gemini-tasks-expansion]

According to the post, Google is expanding Gemini Tasks free of charge to hundreds of millions more people in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India starting that day. Users can flip the toggle from “Chat” to “Task” in the Gemini App, describe what they need, and let Gemini handle longer projects in the background, with optional recurring schedules for ongoing to-dos.[^google-gemini-tasks-expansion]
