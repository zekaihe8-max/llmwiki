# Grok Bot 3D Campus 开源：多 Bot 3D 虚拟办公园区

*Grok Bot 3D Campus Open-Source: 3D Virtual Office Campus for Multiple Bots*

帖文称，Grok Bot 社区有人将多个 Bot 搭成了一座 3D 虚拟办公园区，并已开源整个项目，包含对应 Skill 与部署教程，可在 Vercel 上私有部署；演示站点使用模拟数据，真实管理需自行配置连接与权限，刷新状态会消耗 Bot 额度。[^grok-bot-3d-campus-open-source]

The post says the Grok Bot community built a 3D virtual office campus for multiple bots and open-sourced the project with Skills and deployment instructions for private Vercel deployment; the demo uses mock data, real management requires self-configured connections and permissions, and refreshing status consumes bot quota.[^grok-bot-3d-campus-open-source]
