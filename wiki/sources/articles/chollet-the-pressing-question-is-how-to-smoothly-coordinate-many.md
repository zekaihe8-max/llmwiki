# Chollet：眼下的难题是怎样让许多 agent 顺畅协作

*Chollet: The pressing question is how to smoothly coordinate many agents*

「帖文称」François Chollet 指出，当下的大问题是如何顺畅协调许多智能体的工作。一种备受关注的做法是模仿人类组织，为智能体设定研究员、产品经理、测试员等特定角色，每个智能体维护其角色的上下文；这些智能体在云端 24/7 运行，且可通过 Slack 接收指令。他还推荐查看 MyClaw 3.0 的相关介绍。[^fchollet-multi-agent-coordination]

François Chollet says the big question of the moment is how to smoothly coordinate the work of many agents. One approach gaining traction is to mirror human organizations by assigning agents specific roles such as researcher, PM, or tester, with each agent maintaining context for its role. The agents run in the cloud 24/7 and can receive instructions from Slack; he also points to MyClaw 3.0 as worth checking out.[^fchollet-multi-agent-coordination]
