# Cognition 发布基于 Kimi K3 的 SWE-2.1 编程模型

*Cognition releases SWE-2.1 coding model trained on Kimi K3*

「帖文称」Cognition 推出最新编程模型 SWE-2.1，该模型在 Kimi K3 上通过纯强化学习训练，在多项基准测试中较 SWE-2 取得显著提升，并将在未来两周内免费向 Devin Pro、Max 和 Teams 用户开放。[^cognition-swe-2-1]

The post states that Cognition has launched its latest coding model SWE-2.1, trained via pure reinforcement learning on Kimi K3, delivering large gains over SWE-2 across benchmarks, and free for Devin Pro, Max, and Teams users for the next two weeks.[^cognition-swe-2-1]

- SWE-2.1 是 Cognition 的最新编程模型。[^cognition-swe-2-1]
