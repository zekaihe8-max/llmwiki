## 摘要 / Summary

- SpaceXSI 团队成员 Lauren Tan 和 Roman Ugarte 在 YC Lightcone 做客一小时访谈，称 Grok Bot 从第一行代码到上线仅用七周。[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- SpaceXSI team members Lauren Tan and Roman Ugarte appeared on YC Lightcone for a one-hour interview, saying Grok Bot went from first line of code to launch in seven weeks.[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- 团队设定三条原则：工作以 Bot 为基本单位；Bot 使用和人一样的工具，包括云端电脑；Bot 始终在线并主动找人。[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- The team set three principles: work takes Bot as the basic unit; Bots use the same tools as people, including cloud computers; Bots are always online and actively seek people.[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- 文中称 Lauren 每月合入约 4000 个拉取请求，并在 Slack 线程中与同事和 Bot 协作完成功能，全程未打开 Cursor；这些均为访谈自述，未经独立审计。[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- The article says Lauren merges about 4,000 pull requests per month and completes features with colleagues and Bots in Slack threads without opening Cursor; these are interview self-reports, not independently audited.[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- 同一账户下的多个个人 Bot 共用一台云端电脑、登录状态和文件；邮箱为每个用户一个 mail.grokbot.com 地址，而非每个 Bot 一个。[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- Multiple personal Bots under the same account share one cloud computer, login state, and files; email is one mail.grokbot.com address per user, not per Bot.[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- Lauren 将「Bot 有自己的电话号码和信用卡」描述为对 2027 年的期望，而非已落地功能；Team Bots 更像部门共用同事，可连接个人或共用管理员账号。[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- Lauren described "Bots having their own phone numbers and credit cards" as an expectation for 2027, not a launched feature; Team Bots are more like department-shared colleagues that can connect personal or shared admin accounts.[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- 文章提出 Bot 被钓鱼、超预算或写出 bug 时责任归属问题，现有审批说明只回答了一部分；执行变便宜后，判断力和方向成为稀缺资源。[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
- The article raises accountability questions when Bots are phished, exceed budgets, or introduce bugs; existing approval guidance answers only part; as execution becomes cheaper, judgment and direction become scarce resources.[^grok-bot-yc-lightcone-interview]
