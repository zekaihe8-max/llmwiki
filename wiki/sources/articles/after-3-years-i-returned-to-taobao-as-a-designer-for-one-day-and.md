# 时隔3年，我重新入职淘宝当了一天设计师，也看见了这个行业最真实的进化。

*After 3 Years, I Returned to Taobao as a Designer for One Day and Saw the Industry's Real Evolution*

「帖文称」作者数字生命卡兹克时隔三年重新入职淘宝设计团队一天，亲身体验内部AI设计工具TaoDesign，从需求会到评审会，观察AI如何接管执行与标注，并通过与负责人奇维、承一的访谈，探讨AI时代设计师的核心能力与价值。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]

The post says that blogger Digital Life Kha'Zix returned to Taobao's design team for one day after three years, experiencing the internal AI design tool TaoDesign from requirement review to design review, observing how AI takes over execution and annotation, and discussing the core capabilities and value of designers in the AI era through interviews with leaders Qiwei and Chengyi.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]

## 摘要 / Summary

- 淘宝设计团队将AI应用分为创意、知识和主力设计工具三块，其中主力工具为自研的TaoDesign。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- Taobao's design team applies AI in three areas—creativity, knowledge, and its primary design tool—with the in-house tool TaoDesign as the main production platform.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- TaoDesign支持从模板和Prompt生成可点击、可滑动、带动效的设计稿，并可在画布中手动编辑或通过对话让AI修改。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- TaoDesign generates clickable, scrollable, animated design drafts from templates and prompts, editable on canvas or adjustable via AI chat.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- 作者入职第一天接到冬季促销服饰频道全链路设计需求，约2.5小时完成并参与下午5点评审。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- On his first day, the author received a full-chain winter promotion clothing channel design task, completing it in about 2.5 hours for the 5 PM review.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- 评审中，负责人指出AI已足够强于执行与出方案，设计师更核心的能力是描述与判断，包括审美、逻辑和用户行为判断。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- In the review, leaders said AI is already strong enough for execution and proposals, so designers' core capabilities are description and judgment—aesthetic, logical, and user-behavior judgment.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- 淘天设计约15%的TaoDesign用户是产品经理，团队方向从“交付设计”转向“交付设计的能力”。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- About 15% of TaoDesign users at Taotian are product managers; the team's direction has shifted from delivering designs to delivering design capabilities.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- 技术负责人认为，AI+Design真正难的不是像素到代码，而是从设计意图对接复杂业务系统，技术的作用是把不可能转为可能。[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
- Technical leads said the real difficulty in AI+Design is not pixel-to-code but connecting design intent to complex business systems, with technology turning the impossible into possible.[^taobao-ai-designer-one-day]
