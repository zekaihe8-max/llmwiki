The post says Andrej Karpathy, annoyed by YouTube's recommendations, built HackerTube in a weekend hack; its videos come from 20 years of repeatedly recommended Hacker News links, filtered by Claude from 43 million comments down to 6,454 videos in about two hours, and he reportedly uses Claude Opus 5.5 medium reasoning intensity for daily use.[^karpathy-hackertube]
