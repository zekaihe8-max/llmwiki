*Bao Yu: Software engineers should shift to “human problems” and raise ambition in the AI era*

「帖文称」宝玉转译 Scott Bolinger 的《软件工程师现在该怎么办》，指出每发布一个新模型，纯技术技能价值就会缩水，自己越来越像管理 AI 的产品经理，工作技能正从“技术”变得越来越“人性化”。他引用《无穷的开始》，主张人类创新没有尽头，工程师应把格局放到国家级、全球级甚至宇宙级，并跳出技术圈理解产品、市场营销和销售。他正在学习 AI 帮不上忙的“人的问题”，包括产品定位、创造力、打动人心、心理学和市场营销。[^dotey-software-engineer-ai-era]

The post says Bao Yu translates Scott Bolinger’s “What a software engineer should do now,” noting that every new model release shrinks the value of pure technical skills and that he increasingly feels like a product manager managing AI, with work shifting from “technical” to “human.” Citing The Beginning of Infinity, he argues human innovation has no end and engineers should raise ambition to national, global, or even cosmic scale, stepping outside tech to understand product, marketing, and sales. He is focusing on “human problems” AI cannot solve: product positioning, creativity, delighting humans, psychology, and marketing.[^dotey-software-engineer-ai-era]
