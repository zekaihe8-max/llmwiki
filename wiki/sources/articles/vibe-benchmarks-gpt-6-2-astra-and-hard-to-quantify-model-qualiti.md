# 手感基准：GPT-6.2 Astra 与难以量化的模型品质

*Vibe Benchmarks: GPT-6.2 Astra and Hard-to-Quantify Model Qualities*

帖文称，GPT-6.2 Astra 的早期测试者（包括发文者本人）的评价，印证了“手感基准”在数字基准之外的持续价值；帖文还指出，人格、毅力、创造力、写作清晰度与品味等方面缺乏良好衡量手段，但这些因素很重要。文中嵌入的 Epoch AI 帖文称，GPT-6.2 Astra 在 Epoch 能力指数（ECI）上得分 174，与 Fable 5.2 持平，并高于推理模型此前的预期趋势；该测试基于发布前仅使用自有基准进行的评估，且 ECI 持续更新。[^emollick-gpt-6-2-astra-vibe-benchmarks]

The post says early tester reviews of GPT-6.2 Astra, including the author's own, vindicate the continuing value of vibe benchmarks alongside numeric benchmarks, while noting that personality, perseverance, creativity, writing clarity, and taste lack good measures but matter. It also embeds an Epoch AI post stating GPT-6.2 Astra scored 174 on the Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI), on par with Fable 5.2 and above the pre-Astra trend for reasoning models, based on pre-launch testing using only its own benchmarks; the ECI is continually updated.[^emollick-gpt-6-2-astra-vibe-benchmarks]
