# NVIDIA AI 发布 Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B

*NVIDIA AI announces Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B*

[@NVIDIAAI](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI) 宣布 NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB 现已可用：最新小体积开放模型，面向本地助手、互动角色，以及能持续对话并用工具办事的机器人；并以 Reachy Mini 演示——Bolt 负责对话、NVIDIA Cosmos 负责视觉，二者均在 Jetson Orin Nano 8GB 本地运行。[^nvidia-ai-nemotron-bolt-e4b] 帖文见 <https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2113657112408785153>；了解更多 <https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB/>。

[@NVIDIAAI](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI) announces NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB is available now: the newest small open model, built for local assistants, interactive characters, and robots that can carry on a conversation and use tools to get things done; a Reachy Mini demo has Bolt handling conversation and NVIDIA Cosmos handling vision, both running locally on Jetson Orin Nano 8GB.[^nvidia-ai-nemotron-bolt-e4b] Post: <https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2113657112408785153>; learn more: <https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB/>.
