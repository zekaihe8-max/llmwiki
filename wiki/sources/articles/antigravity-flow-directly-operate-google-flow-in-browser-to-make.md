# Antigravity Flow：在浏览器中直接操作 Google Flow 制作视频

*Antigravity Flow: Directly Operate Google Flow in Browser to Make Videos*

帖文称，Antigravity Flow 插件将 Google 的 AI 影视创作工具 Google Flow 接入 Antigravity，实现从剧本分镜到角色创建、视频生成与剪辑的全流程自动化；插件直接调用 Antigravity 内置的浏览器执行操作，无需额外配置外部服务或本地自动化环境，并提供默认确认与全自动两种运行模式，在消耗积分操作前提示费用，遇到登录验证或额度不足时自动暂停交由人工处理。[^antigravity-flow-google-flow-video]

The post says the Antigravity Flow plugin integrates Google’s AI filmmaking tool Google Flow into Antigravity, enabling end-to-end automation from script and storyboard to character creation, video generation, and editing. It uses Antigravity’s built-in browser execution, requiring no external services or local automation setup, and offers default-confirmation and fully automatic modes with cost prompts before credit-consuming operations and automatic pauses for login verification or insufficient quota.[^antigravity-flow-google-flow-video]
