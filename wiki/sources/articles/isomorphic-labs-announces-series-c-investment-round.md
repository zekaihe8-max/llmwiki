# 异构实验室宣布 C 轮融资

*Isomorphic Labs announces Series C investment round*

「帖文称」异构实验室已筹集 50 亿美元 C 轮融资，由 Greenoaks 与 Sequoia Capital 共同领投，Andreessen Horowitz（a16z）及多家新投资者参与，Alphabet 继续大额投资，老股东 Thrive Capital、MGX、GV、CapitalG、Temasek 与英国主权 AI 基金全部跟投。公司聚焦利用 AI 设计与开发新药，并将扩展专有药物设计引擎 IsoDDE 及 AI 设计的治疗候选物管线。[^2cce17f778912ab3]

The official announcement says Isomorphic Labs has raised $5 billion in Series C funding, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital alongside Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and several other new investors, with continued significant investment from Alphabet and participation from all existing investors including Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company focuses on designing and developing new medicines with AI and is scaling its proprietary drug design engine (IsoDDE) and AI-designed therapeutic candidate pipeline.[^2cce17f778912ab3]
