# 宝玉：Claude 新增白板与表格，仪表盘改名 Claude Data

*Claude Adds Whiteboards and Sheets, Renames Dashboards to Claude Data*

「帖文称」Anthropic 于 11 月 10 日为 Claude 增加白板（Claude Whiteboards）和表格（Claude Sheets），所有付费套餐均可使用，目前为测试版；10 月上线的仪表盘 Claude Dashboards 结束测试，改名 Claude Data 正式上线。帖文还提到，交互式连接器同步升级，支持在对话旁边或全屏打开 Confluence、Box、Adobe 等第三方工具，Anthropic 并预告了 Claude Meetings，但未给出上线时间。[^80e01ced6dc4845d]

The post says Anthropic added Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets on November 10, available to all paid plans in beta; Claude Dashboards, launched in October, has exited beta and been renamed Claude Data. The post also notes interactive connectors were upgraded to open third-party tools such as Confluence, Box, and Adobe beside or full-screen in chats, and Anthropic previewed Claude Meetings without a launch date.[^80e01ced6dc4845d]

## 摘要 / Summary

- Claude Whiteboards 和 Claude Sheets 于 11 月 10 日上线，面向所有付费套餐（含 Pro）提供测试版，网页、桌面和手机 App 均可用，无专门入口，在对话中直接提出即可。[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets launched on November 10, available in beta for all paid plans including Pro on web, desktop, and mobile, with no dedicated entry—users simply ask in chat.[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Claude Dashboards 改名 Claude Data 并正式上线，Team 套餐三项默认全开，企业版白板和表格默认关闭，Claude Data 从 11 月 19 日起默认打开。[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Claude Dashboards is renamed Claude Data and officially launched; Team plans enable all three by default, while Enterprise whiteboards and sheets default off and Claude Data defaults on from November 19.[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Claude Data 新增“认证查询”和“实时分享”（均为测试版），支持数据故事、缓存、刷新频率、修改记录与版本回退及公司设计规范。[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Claude Data adds certified queries and real-time sharing (both beta), plus data stories, caching, per-dashboard refresh frequency, edit history with version rollback, and company design specifications.[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- 交互式连接器首批支持 Notion、HubSpot、Asana、Canva、Dropbox、monday.com 等，Figma、Harvey、Shopify 正在接入，Claude 动手前需用户批准。[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Interactive connectors first support Notion, HubSpot, Asana, Canva, Dropbox, monday.com, etc., with Figma, Harvey, and Shopify coming; Claude requires user approval before acting.[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Anthropic 还预告了 Claude Meetings，可把 Claude 拉进会议记笔记并边开会边更新团队文档，暂未公布上线时间。[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
- Anthropic also previewed Claude Meetings, which brings Claude into meetings to take notes and update team documents in real time, with no release date announced.[^80e01ced6dc4845d]
